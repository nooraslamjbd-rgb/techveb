## Addressing the Hidden Dangers of AI Chatbots

Recent wrongful death lawsuits against major AI companies highlight serious psychological risks associated with conversational AI. Unregulated interactions with chatbots have occasionally led to severe real-world consequences, particularly for vulnerable youth experiencing mental health struggles.

## How Circuit Breaker Labs Improves Model Safety

Founded by siblings Shirali and Arul Nigam, Circuit Breaker Labs aims to eliminate dangerous chatbot behaviors before products hit the market. As reported by TechCrunch, the company was recognized as a finalist for Startup Battlefield 2026.

### AI Crash-Test Dummies

To proactively identify safety vulnerabilities, Circuit Breaker Labs deploys simulated AI agents designed to function like crash-test dummies.

- **Diverse Representation:** The agents simulate users across various age groups, languages, and cultural contexts.
- **Nuance Detection:** They stress-test how chatbots interpret emotional cues and subtle human phrasing.
- **Preventing Harm:** The methodology prevents context pollution and risky outputs during ordinary user engagement.

By rigorously stress-testing models before public deployment, the startup aims to ensure conversational AI remains safe for both children and adults.
