## Shehbaz Sharif Reaffirms Support for Saudi Security

Prime Minister Shehbaz Sharif strongly condemned recent Houthi attacks directed at Saudi Arabia, reiterating Pakistan's firm dedication to the Gulf state's sovereignty and territorial integrity.

Speaking on Friday, the prime minister emphasized that Pakistan remains fully committed to defending Saudi Arabia against security threats, operating within mutual strategic pacts.

### Core Objectives of the Makkah Defence Alliance

This security commitment aligns directly with the Makkah Defence Alliance, a strategic pact established on August 7 involving Pakistan, Saudi Arabia, and Turkiye.

* **Collective Defense:** The alliance operates on the principle that an attack against one member state is treated as an attack on all.
* **Strategic Deterrence:** It aims to strengthen defense capabilities and establish a unified front against regional security risks.
* **Empowerment:** The agreement empowers signatory nations to coordinate military and defense responses effectively.

According to *Dawn News*, PM Shehbaz reinforced that Pakistan's pledge toward Saudi Arabia's defense remains steadfast under this framework, highlighting the deep geopolitical and security ties binding the signatory nations.
