## Police Officer Hospitalized After Nottingham Shooting

A Nottinghamshire Police officer is currently receiving medical treatment in hospital after being shot in Nottingham city centre during the early hours of Sunday morning. 

According to reports from BBC News, the incident took place shortly before 04:50 BST on Angel Row when the officer approached two individuals. During the encounter, the officer sustained a gunshot wound to his foot.

## Investigation and Crime Scene Details

Following the shooting, a major police response was deployed to the area. Detectives confirmed that a firearm was recovered at the scene. 

Key details of the ongoing investigation include:
* **Suspects at large:** A man and a woman fled the scene immediately after the discharge.
* **Street closures:** Angel Row and parts of St James's Street remain cordoned off.
* **Forensic teams:** Forensic officers are currently examining evidence left along the street.

## Official Responses and Public Appeal

Assistant Chief Constable Claire Rukas expressed her shock regarding the event, calling it a sobering reminder of the hazards emergency service personnel encounter daily. She urged members of the public who may have CCTV, dashcam footage, or any relevant information to contact Nottinghamshire Police without delay.

In a statement shared on social media, the East Midlands Ambulance Service extended its heartfelt thoughts and best wishes to the injured officer, his family, and his colleagues, hoping for a full and swift recovery.
