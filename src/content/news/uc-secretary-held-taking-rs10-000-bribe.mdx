MULTAN, (APP - UrduPoint / Pakistan Point News - 29th Aug, 2026) The Anti-Corruption Establishment (ACE) [Multan](https://www.urdupoint.com/education/board-of-education-multan.html) Region arrested a Union Council secretary red-handed while allegedly receiving a Rs10,000 bribe during a trap raid on Saturday.

According to an ACE spokesperson, the operation was carried out by a team led by Circle Officer Ali Hassan under the supervision of Regional Director ACE Multan Basharat Nabi and a judicial magistrate.

The accused, identified as Akhtar, secretary of Union Council No.

54, Rashidabad, Multan, was allegedly caught receiving Rs10,000 from complainant Muhammad Ayaz, son of Muhammad Murad. The marked [Currency](https://www.urdupoint.com/business/open-market-currency-rates-in-pakistan.html) notes were recovered from his possession and taken into custody as evidence.

The spokesperson said the accused had earlier allegedly received Rs5,000 from the complainant in exchange for issuing a birth certificate.

A case was registered against the accused under the relevant law, while further investigation was underway.
