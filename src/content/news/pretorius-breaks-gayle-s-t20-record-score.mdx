South African batter Lhuan-dre Pretorius has rewritten cricket history by shattering Chris Gayle's long-standing record for the highest individual score in men's T20 cricket. Playing for the Titans against the Knights in the CSA T20 Challenge, the 20-year-old smashed an unbeaten 188 off just 79 deliveries.

## A Record-Breaking Knock in Bloemfontein

Pretorius displayed sensational power-hitting at the Mangaung Oval in Bloemfontein. His explosive innings contained 13 sixes and 15 fours, operating at an astonishing strike rate of 237.97.

### Match Milestones:
- **50 Runs:** Reached off 24 balls
- **100 Runs:** Reached off 40 balls (just 16 balls after his half-century)
- **150 Runs:** Reached off 58 balls
- **Final Score:** 188* off 79 balls

## Surpassing Chris Gayle's Iconic IPL Record

The historic 188* eclipses the previous men's T20 world record of 175 not out, set by West Indies legend Chris Gayle for Royal Challengers Bangalore against Pune Warriors in the 2013 Indian Premier League (IPL).

Driven by Pretorius's onslaught, Titans posted a colossal 267-3 in their 20 overs. In response, Knights were dismissed for 143 in 17.2 overs, wrapping up a massive 124-run victory for Titans. Pretorius, who has already earned international caps for South Africa across Tests, ODIs, and T20s, continues to prove himself as one of modern cricket's premier young talents. *(Source: BBC News)*
