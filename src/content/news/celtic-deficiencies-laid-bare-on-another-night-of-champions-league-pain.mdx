## Celtic's European Nightmares Continue

Celtic suffered another heartbreaking defeat in the UEFA Champions League, squandering a 4-0 advantage against LASK. The capitulation exposed deep-seated tactical and physical deficiencies that continue to haunt the Scottish champions on the continental stage.

## McGregor Slams Teammates After Collapse

Following the match, Celtic captain Callum McGregor delivered an exceptionally stern assessment of his team's performance. In a scathing post-match statement, he described the squad as passive and scared during critical phases of the contest.

"Now we'll see what people are made of," McGregor said, sending a direct message to the dressing room after one of the most painful nights of his long tenure at the club.

### A Troubling History in Europe

As reported by BBC News, this latest failure adds to a long list of European humiliations for Celtic in recent seasons. Despite domestic dominance, the team has repeatedly fallen short in European competitions.

Key European setbacks in recent years include:
- Heavy defeats to Borussia Dortmund (7-1) and Atletico Madrid (6-0)
- Disappointing play-off exits against Midtjylland, Ferencvaros, and Cluj
- Penalty shootout heartbreak against Kairat Almaty

## Moving Forward

Celtic must re-evaluate its European strategy and mental approach if it wants to restore credibility on the big stage. Without fundamental changes in defensive discipline and squad mindset, dark nights in Europe will remain a recurring theme.
