-   **[Smart Systems Stage](https://techcrunch.com/2026/07/27/power-up-your-ai-infrastructure-a-first-look-at-the-smart-systems-stage-agenda-at-techcrunch-disrupt-2026/):** Every AI breakthrough depends on the infrastructure behind it.
