## Adiala Inmates Challenge IHC Ruling in Federal Constitutional Court

Three inmates from Adiala jail have filed petitions before the Federal Constitutional Court (FCC) challenging the Islamabad High Court's (IHC) dismissal of their requests for private hospital transfers. The prisoners are seeking medical relief similar to measures directed for former Prime Minister Imran Khan, according to Dawn News.

### Interpretation of Rule 197 of Pakistan Prison Rules

Advocate Akhtar Cheema, representing prisoner Muhammad Ilyas Khan, highlighted Rule 197 of the Pakistan Prison Rules, 1978. The rule dictates the framework for transferring sick prisoners to civil medical facilities.

The legal counsel argued that government authorities misinterpret "civil hospital" to mean exclusively public institutions. They contend the rule distinguishes between military and civilian facilities, allowing inmates with severe ailments to receive private care paid for by their families.

### Precedent and Health Concerns

The legal action follows an August 18 Supreme Court order directing the transfer of Imran Khan to a private hospital facility. Although Khan was ultimately taken to a public hospital, inmates argue the legal precedent supports private treatment options.

One petitioner has experienced severe internal bleeding, requiring eight emergency hospital visits within two months. Defense counsel expressed concerns over the adequacy of public healthcare facilities for complex medical conditions.
