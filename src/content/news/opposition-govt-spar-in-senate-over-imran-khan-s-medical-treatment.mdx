Tensions flared in the Senate on Tuesday as the government and opposition exchanged fierce accusations over the medical treatment of former Prime Minister Imran Khan. Both factions accused each other of violating Supreme Court directions regarding the Pakistan Tehreek-e-Insaf (PTI) founder's healthcare, according to a report by Dawn News.

## Supreme Court Directives and Medical Panel
On August 18, the apex court issued clear instructions directing the government to transfer Imran Khan to Shifa International Hospital in Islamabad. In addition to the hospital transfer, the court mandated the formation of a dedicated medical board.

To ensure transparency, the Supreme Court specifically ordered the inclusion of Khan’s personal physician and his sister on the medical panel. Despite these directives, parliamentary members from both sides claim the opposing party is hindering implementation and committing contempt of court.

## Senate Clashes Over Healthcare Standards
During the session, opposition lawmakers expressed deep concern over Khan's health conditions and accused the administration of delaying required medical access. Conversely, government representatives maintained that all legal protocols are being strictly observed, pushing back against claims of negligence.

The ongoing debate highlights persistent political friction surrounding the former premier's incarceration and access to adequate healthcare facilities.
