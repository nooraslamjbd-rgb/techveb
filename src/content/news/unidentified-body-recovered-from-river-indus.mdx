HYDERABAD, (APP - UrduPoint / Pakistan Point News - 31st Aug, 2026) A body of an unidentified man was recovered from the River Indus near Bhutto Park in the Hussainabad area of [Hyderabad](https://www.urdupoint.com/education/board-of-education-hyderabad.html) here on Monday.

A Rescue 1122 team retrieved the body from the water and shifted to hospital in the presence of police for further investigation.

According to the rescue team, the identity of the deceased could not be ascertained so far.
