## Ishaq Dar Wraps Up UK Tour, Arrives in Turkiye

Pakistan's Deputy Prime Minister and Foreign Minister Ishaq Dar has concluded a high-level diplomatic visit to the United Kingdom. He has now departed for Istanbul, Turkiye, to attend the upcoming Regional 4 (R-4) Foreign Ministers' Meeting, according to a statement from the Foreign Office (FO).

### Highlights of the UK Diplomatic Visit

During his stay in London, DPM Dar engaged in strategic discussions with key British leaders and international delegation heads. His key engagements included high-profile meetings with:

- **Ed Miliband**: UK Foreign Secretary
- **Stephen Doughty**: Minister of State for Pakistan
- **Jonathan Powell**: National Security Adviser
- **Arsenio Dominguez**: IMO Secretary General
- **Shirley Ayorkor Botchwey**: Commonwealth Secretary General

Additionally, Dar interacted with British parliamentarians of Pakistani origin and met members of the Pakistani diaspora in the UK.

## Focus on Regional Security at the R-4 Summit in Istanbul

Following an invitation from Turkiye's Foreign Minister Hakan Fidan, Dar accepted the opportunity to represent Pakistan at the R-4 summit in Istanbul. The Regional 4 framework—comprising Pakistan, Turkiye, Saudi Arabia, and Egypt—was formed in March to foster regional stability and facilitate mediation between the US and Iran.

The upcoming talks in Istanbul come at a critical juncture as negotiations between key international parties remain stalled. Previous sessions were held in Riyadh, Islamabad, and Cairo, where participating member states reaffirmed their commitment to coordinated regional peace.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Why is Pakistan DPM Ishaq Dar visiting Istanbul?

Ishaq Dar is visiting Istanbul to participate in the Regional 4 (R-4) Foreign Ministers' Meeting alongside representatives from Turkiye, Saudi Arabia, and Egypt.

### What is the Regional 4 (R-4) framework?

The R-4 framework is a diplomatic initiative comprising Pakistan, Turkiye, Saudi Arabia, and Egypt aimed at promoting regional peace, security, and US-Iran mediation.
