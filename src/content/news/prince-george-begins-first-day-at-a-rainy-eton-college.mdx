## Prince George Starts First Day at Eton College

Prince George has officially arrived for his first day at Eton College, marking a major milestone in his academic journey. Accompanied by his parents, the Prince and Princess of Wales, the 13-year-old royal arrived on a rainy morning to begin his tenure as a boarding student.

The royal family was welcomed at the school yard near Tudor landmark Lupton's Tower by head teacher Simon Henderson and provost Sir Nicholas Coleridge. Henderson greeted Prince George with a handshake, wishing him good luck as he stepped inside to start his modern school life.

## Following Royal and Historic Traditions

Prince George follows directly in the footsteps of his father, Prince William, who also attended the historic institution. For his official arrival, George wore Eton's signature uniform, featuring a black tailcoat, waistcoat, and stiff white collar.

### Key Facts About Eton College:

* **Legacy:** The elite private school has educated 20 British prime ministers.
* **Location:** Located in Windsor, a short distance from the young prince's home at Forest Lodge.
* **Tuition Fees:** Annual fees for boarders at Eton are currently estimated at £65,000.

According to reports originally published by BBC News, Prince George will reside on campus as a boarder throughout his time at the historic school.
