## Pakistan Named Chair of IMF and World Bank Boards for 2027

In a major diplomatic development, Pakistan has been selected to chair the Boards of Governors of the International Monetary Fund (IMF) and the World Bank Group (WBG) for 2027. This milestone marks Pakistan's return to the leadership position after 74 years, according to a report by Dawn News.

### Leadership Announcement

Minister for Economic Affairs Ahad Khan Cheema was officially announced as the incoming chair during the annual meetings held in Bangkok on October 16. The role highlights Pakistan's active diplomatic participation in international financial institutions.

### Scope and Impact of the Chairmanship

While the position carries significant prestige, its scope is strictly defined:

- **Procedural Authority:** The chair presides over the annual joint meetings and manages organizational proceedings.
- **Diplomatic Engagement:** It offers a prominent platform to highlight multilateral economic issues facing developing nations.
- **Lending Governance:** The role does not confer extra voting power or direct decision-making authority over loan programs and fiscal policy.

This appointment underscores Pakistan's renewed visibility within international economic governance frameworks.
