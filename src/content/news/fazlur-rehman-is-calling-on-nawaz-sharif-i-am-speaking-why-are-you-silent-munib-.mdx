Munib Farooq says Fazlur Rehman is upset because promises made to him were not fulfilled, while Nawaz Sharif is unlikely to issue a strong statement as his brother and daughter are in government.

LAHORE, (UrduPoint / Pakistan Point News - 9th Sep, 2026) Journalist Munib Farooq said that Fazlur Rehman is calling on his old associate [Nawaz Sharif](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/nawaz-sharif.html), asking: “I am speaking, so why are you silent?” He said the fact is that promises made to Fazlur Rehman were not fulfilled, which has hurt him and prompted him to speak. In his vlog, he said Fazlur Rehman was calling on his old associate [Nawaz Sharif](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/nawaz-sharif.html), saying that if he was speaking, why was [Nawaz Sharif](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/nawaz-sharif.html) remaining silent.

He added that the promises made to Fazlur Rehman had not been fulfilled, and that he was speaking because he was distressed.

> 

“All the promises made to [Nawaz Sharif](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/nawaz-sharif.html) have been fulfilled.

His brother’s [government](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/government.html) is in [Islamabad](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/islamabad.html) and his daughter’s [government](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/government.html) is in [Punjab](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/punjab.html), so he is currently unlikely to issue a strong statement,” he said. He added that there was a significant difference in the political circumstances of [Nawaz Sharif](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/nawaz-sharif.html) and Fazlur Rehman.

The [government](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/government.html) was running confidently, so would [Nawaz Sharif](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/nawaz-sharif.html) simply come forward and issue a strong statement? He said anything could happen in the country, but for now there were no such signs and nothing indicated that a direct confrontation was about to take place. Therefore, [Nawaz Sharif](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/nawaz-sharif.html) was also waiting and might even be pleased after [hearing](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/hearing.html) Fazlur Rehman’s statements.
