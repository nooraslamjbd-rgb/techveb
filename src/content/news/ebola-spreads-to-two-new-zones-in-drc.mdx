KINSHASA, (UrduPoint / Pakistan Point News / WAM - 29th Aug, 2026) The fastest-spreading Ebola outbreak in history has reached two new health zones in eastern [Democratic Republic of the Congo](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/democratic-republic-of-the-congo.html) (DRC), with a total of 60 areas now affected, according to the latest [government](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/government.html) figures.

The two new zones affected are Biena and Manguredjipa in DRC’s North Kivu province, where the fatality rate is much higher than the overall rate of 48%, partly due to delayed response efforts, according to data by the Ministry of Health released on Friday.

The ministry said the outbreak in DRC has recorded 5,794 confirmed cases, including 2,[786](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/786.html) deaths, and is spreading at an unprecedented speed – faster than efforts to track and slow it.
