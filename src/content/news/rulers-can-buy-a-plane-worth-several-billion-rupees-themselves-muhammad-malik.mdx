Many business figures own private planes, but the rulers are even wealthier; still, travelling for free can be described as insensitivity, says anchorperson Muhammad Malik.

LAHORE, (UrduPoint / Pakistan Point News - 12th Sep, 2026) [tv](https://www.urdupoint.com/tv/) anchorperson Muhammad Malik said that the rulers have enough [money](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/money.html) to [buy](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/buy.html) a plane worth several [billion](https://www.urdupoint.com/business/prize-bonds.html) rupees themselves. Speaking on Geo [news](https://www.urdupoint.com/urdu-news)’ programme, he said, “When we [travel](https://www.urdupoint.com/travel/), we pay [from](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/from.html) our own pockets.

Travelling for free does not remain a hobby; it becomes a habit. [God](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/god.html) has blessed [all](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/all.html) of them with so much [money](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/money.html) that they can [buy](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/buy.html) their own planes. Buying a plane worth several [billion](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/billion.html) rupees is no problem for them.

Many [business](https://www.urdupoint.com/business/prize-bonds.html) figures in [Pakistan](https://www.urdupoint.com/pakistan/) own their own planes; these people are even wealthier than those businesspeople. Yet, travelling for free can be called insensitivity—what else can we say? More than insensitivity, it is the attitude that if I do it, no one will question me.”

> 

He added, “When we report something, we think about whether it could [lead](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/lead.html) to a defamation case or require a response, so we report carefully because there will be consequences.

But if we know that no one will question us and we can report whatever we want, we do not know what kind of outrageous claims we might make.”
