## Tragic Fire Incident at PIMS Hospital Islamabad

A devastating fire broke out early Wednesday in the gynaecology ward of the Pakistan Institute of Medical Sciences (PIMS) in Islamabad, resulting in the tragic deaths of fourteen newborn infants. Emergency response teams rushed to the scene to contain the blaze and assist in evacuations.

### Emergency Response and Rescue Operations

Rescue personnel used ladders to shatter upper-level windows to gain access to the smoke-filled ward. Broadcast footage showed emergency workers navigating challenging conditions to clear debris and reach trapped individuals.

Outside the facility, distressed family members gathered as security personnel managed access to allow rescue operations to proceed efficiently, as originally reported by Dawn News.

### Official Inquiry and Safety Review

Hospital management and local authorities have initiated an inquiry to establish the cause of the fire. Further updates are expected as safety audits and forensic reviews continue at the facility.
