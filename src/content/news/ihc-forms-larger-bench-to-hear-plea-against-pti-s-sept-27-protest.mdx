## IHC Sets Up Larger Bench for PTI Protest Case

The Islamabad High Court (IHC) has decided to constitute a larger bench to hear a constitutional petition filed against the Pakistan Tehreek-e-Insaf's (PTI) upcoming nationwide protest on September 27. According to a report by Dawn News, Chief Justice Sardar Muhammad Sarfaraz Dogar highlighted the matter's sensitivity and its broader constitutional implications during the hearing.

### Business Community Raises Concerns Over Disruptions

The petition was moved by Islamabad-based trader Waqas Ahmed. His legal counsel argued that the planned demonstration would severely impact daily business activities, commercial transport, and normal public routine across the federal capital.

Key points raised by the petitioner's legal counsel include:
- **Impact on Trade:** Blockades and protests directly harm local shopkeepers and traders in Islamabad.
- **Leadership of Protest:** Khyber Pakhtunkhwa Chief Minister Sohail Afridi plans to lead the rally into Islamabad.
- **Judicial Integrity:** Demanding relief through street pressure sets a risky precedent for pending court cases.

### Court Issues Notices to Police Chiefs

Recognizing the potential law and order challenges, the IHC issued official notices to the police chiefs of all four provinces. The court directed officials to ensure attendance at the upcoming hearing scheduled for September 10.

### Background of PTI's Protest Call

The PTI announced its nationwide demonstration for September 27 to demand the release of party founder Imran Khan, who remains incarcerated at Rawalpindi's Adiala jail. The party stated the protest aims to mobilize citizens for the supremacy of the Constitution.
