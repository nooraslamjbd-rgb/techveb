## India Recovers to Post 351/5 Against West Indies

India staged a dramatic recovery in New Chandigarh to set West Indies a steep target of 352 runs. After a disastrous start that saw the host nation reduced to 4 for 2 by West Indies bowler Jayden Seales, India's batting lineup showed impressive resilience.

### KL Rahul Leads the Fightback

KL Rahul played a stellar, unbeaten knock of 129 runs to anchor the Indian innings. Navigating the initial spell of high-quality pace bowling, Rahul accelerated seamlessly in the middle and death overs to push the score past the 350-run mark.

### Crucial Fifties from Rohit and Gaikwad

Supporting Rahul's masterclass were captain Rohit Sharma and Ruturaj Gaikwad, both of whom registered solid half-centuries. Their partnerships stabilized the top order and laid a firm foundation for late-order power hitting.

* **Jayden Seales:** Stripped early momentum from India with two quick wickets.
* **Key Partnerships:** Rohit and Gaikwad rebuilt before Rahul took control.
* **Final Target:** West Indies require 352 runs to win the match.

According to reports initially published by *ESPN Cricinfo*, India's strong total places them in a dominant position as West Indies begin their chase.
