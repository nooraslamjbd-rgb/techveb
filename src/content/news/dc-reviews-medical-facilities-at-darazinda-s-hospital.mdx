DERA ISMAIL KHAN, (APP - UrduPoint / Pakistan Point News - 25th Aug, 2026) Deputy Commissioner Hikmatullah Wazir paid a surprise visit to type-D hospital Darazinda and reviewed the medical facilities, cleanliness and other arrangements at the facility.

Accompanied by Assistant Commissioner and Tehsildar Darazinda, the deputy commissioner inspected various sections of the hospital, including the pharmacy, OPD, laboratory, medical ward, washrooms, drinking water facility and waiting area for patients’ attendants.

He also met patients under treatment and sought their feedback regarding the medical facilities and services being provided to them.

He directed the hospital administration to ensure the provision of timely and quality healthcare services to patients and maintain proper standards of cleanliness.

He also reviewed issues related to electricity supply at the hospital and issued necessary directions for their permanent resolution.

He stressed that uninterrupted electricity supply should be ensured so that patients and medical staff do not face difficulties in receiving and providing healthcare.
