## Miraculous Survival Amid Devastating Nepal Floods

A 97-year-old woman has been successfully pulled from the debris of her care home following catastrophic flash floods in Nepal. Guna Maya Bohara is currently recovering in a hospital after surviving the severe ordeal.

Her great-nephew, Dipak Bohara, traveled directly from Kathmandu to assist emergency teams. Speaking to BBC Nepali, he described his great-aunt as looking like "a warrior" who had returned victorious from battle.

### The Four-Hour Rescue Mission

The complex extraction operation took emergency crews four hours of intensive effort to clear heavy rubble and reach Bohara. Her survival stands as a rare highlight of resilience amid widespread devastation.

### Environmental Trigger and Impact

The disastrous flash floods have resulted in hundreds of confirmed fatalities across the Himalayan country. Scientists attribute the sudden flood disaster to a major glacier collapse along the border region with Tibet.
