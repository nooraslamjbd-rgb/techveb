ASTORE, (APP - UrduPoint / Pakistan Point News - 25th Aug, 2026) A sudden fire broke out at the house of a man identified as Abdul Haleem in Shugart village of Astore District, triggering panic among local residents.

Upon receiving information about the incident, a Rescue 1122 Astore team immediately rushed to the scene and launched firefighting and relief operations.

With the assistance of local residents, Rescue 1122 personnel promptly brought the fire under control, preventing it from spreading further. The rescue team also prevented further damage to the affected house and protected nearby houses and other property from the flames.

The timely joint efforts of rescue personnel and local residents helped contain the fire and avert the risk of major loss of life and property. No casualties were reported.

According to Rescue 1122 officials, the exact cause of the fire could not immediately be ascertained. Further information and facts regarding the incident are being collected.

Local residents appreciated the timely response of Rescue 1122, saying the quick action helped prevent the fire from spreading to the nearby populated area.
