## Field Marshal Asim Munir Arrives in Tehran for Strategic Talks

Chief of Defence Forces and Chief of Army Staff Field Marshal Asim Munir is scheduled to visit Tehran today for high-level bilateral discussions with Iranian leaders. The critical diplomatic visit focuses on regional security and diplomatic cooperation.

According to reports originally published by Dawn News, Pakistani officials and Iranian media confirmed the scheduled meeting as part of ongoing diplomatic engagements between both nations.

## Key Focus: Strait of Hormuz and Regional De-escalation

The talks follow earlier diplomatic meetings held during the summer aimed at restoring stability across key trade and maritime routes.

### Core Agenda Points
- **Strait of Hormuz:** Reopening and securing the critical maritime chokepoint for international trade.
- **De-escalation Efforts:** Establishing practical frameworks to prevent further military tension in the region.
- **Bilateral Security:** Enhancing border management and diplomatic alignment between Pakistan and Iran.

This high-profile visit underscores Pakistan's active diplomatic role in mediating regional stability and ensuring critical economic corridors remain open.
