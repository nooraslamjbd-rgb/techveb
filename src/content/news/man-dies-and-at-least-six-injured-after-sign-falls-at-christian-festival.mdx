## Tragic Incident at Big Church Festival Leaves One Dead and Six Injured

A fatal incident occurred at the Big Church Festival in Wiston Park, near Steyning, West Sussex. According to Sussex Police, emergency services were dispatched to the site at approximately 15:38 BST on Friday following reports that a large sign had collapsed onto attendees.

Tragically, a 41-year-old man from Eastbourne died at the scene. Emergency teams treated multiple victims, with at least six individuals transported to nearby hospitals for further medical care.

## Emergency Response and Festival Cancellation

Following the tragedy, organizers confirmed that the multi-day event would be brought to an immediate end to allow emergency crews to operate and ensure public safety.

* **Location:** Wiston Park, Washington Road, West Sussex
* **Casualties:** 1 fatality (41-year-old male), at least 6 hospitalized
* **Agencies Responding:** Sussex Police, Health and Safety Executive (HSE), local authority

Organizers expressed deep condolences, stating their immediate focus remains on supporting those impacted and helping thousands of visitors clear the site safely.

## Police Investigation Launched: Operation Igloo

Sussex Police have initiated a formal inquiry titled Operation Igloo to determine the structural cause of the sign failure. Det Ch Supt Richard McDonagh acknowledged the immense distress the event has caused among visitors.

Investigators are working alongside the Health and Safety Executive (HSE) and local authorities. Police urge anyone with eyewitness accounts or relevant video footage to report details online or by calling 101, referencing Operation Igloo.
