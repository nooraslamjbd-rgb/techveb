CALIFORNIA, (UrduPoint / Pakistan Point News - 9th Sep, 2026) US [technology](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/technology.html) [company](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/company.html) [Apple](https://www.urdupoint.com/mobile/apple-mobiles-prices-in-pakistan.html) has unveiled its first foldable smartphone, the iPhone Duo, after a long wait. It is being described as the biggest design change in the iPhone lineup in several years.

The new iPhone takes the form of a relatively compact phone when folded, while opening it provides users with a large, nearly tablet-sized display.

The iPhone Duo can be opened and closed like a book.

It features a 5.4-inch outer display and a 7.6-inch inner display when unfolded, the largest screen ever offered on an iPhone. The larger inner display has also been designed for using multiple apps and multitasking.

The new foldable iPhone is built primarily with a titanium frame and uses Ceramic Shield glass for protection.

It also carries an IP68 rating, intended to provide better resistance against [water](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/water.html) and dust. The iPhone Duo is available in Star White and the deep blue Night Sky colors.

> 

The iPhone Duo uses [Apple](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/apple.html)'s new A20 Pro chip and C2 modem, while two 48-megapixel rear cameras are among its key features.

With the new device, [Apple](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/apple.html) has finally entered the foldable smartphone [market](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/market.html), where [samsung](https://www.urdupoint.com/mobile/samsung-mobiles-prices-in-pakistan.html), [Google](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/google.html), [Huawei](https://www.urdupoint.com/mobile/huawei-mobiles-prices-in-pakistan.html) and other companies have already introduced their foldable phones.

The iPhone Duo is considerably more expensive than [Apple](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/apple.html)'s conventional iPhones.

Its starting [price](https://www.urdupoint.com/business/prize-bonds.html) in the [United States](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/united-states.html) has been set at approximately $2,000 or more, while supplies are expected to begin in [October](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/october.html).

A notable aspect of [Apple](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/apple.html)'s new phone is that the [company](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/company.html) entered the foldable [market](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/market.html) considerably later than its rivals.

However, the arrival of the iPhone Duo is being described as a significant development for the foldable smartphone [market](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/market.html), and [Apple](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/apple.html)'s entry is expected to intensify competition in the sector.

Apple has also introduced its new iPhone 18 series, including new models such as the iPhone 18 Pro and iPhone 18 Pro Max.

These phones focus on next-generation processors, improved camera systems, artificial intelligence-related features and enhanced battery performance. However, the iPhone Duo, the [company](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/company.html)'s first foldable iPhone, attracted the most attention at [Apple](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/apple.html)'s [event](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/event.html) this year.
