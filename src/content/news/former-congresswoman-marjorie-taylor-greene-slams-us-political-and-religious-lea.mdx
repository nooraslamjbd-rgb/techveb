WASHINGTON, (UrduPoint / Pakistan Point News - 8th Sep, 2026) Former US Congresswoman Marjorie Taylor Greene strongly criticized political leaders who continue providing military and financial aid to [Israel](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/israel.html), as well as [Christian](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/christian.html) religious leaders who have remained silent over the killing of children in [Gaza](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/gaza.html), warning that they would have to answer for their silence. According to a report, Greene raised serious questions on X over the silence of the US [government](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/government.html) and [Christian](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/christian.html) religious circles regarding children being killed and [injured](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/injured.html) in [Gaza](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/gaza.html).

She said thousands of children had been killed and many had been left permanently [injured](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/injured.html), disabled or orphaned, yet elected US leaders continued sending [money](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/money.html) and military aid, while American [Christian](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/christian.html) churches remained largely silent. The former congresswoman said, “I don’t think this is going to end well.” She did not clarify what she meant by “this day,” although her statement has been interpreted in a religious context as a reference to Judgment Day or the hereafter. Her central objection was that despite the large-scale killing and lifelong disability of children in [Gaza](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/gaza.html), US political and religious leaders were not responding to the issue as, in her view, they should. Greene’s criticism carries particular significance in light of her former political position.

She served as a member of the US House of Representatives [from](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/from.html) 2021 and was regarded for several years as a prominent supporter of [Donald Trump](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/donald-trump.html) and his “MAGA movement” [politics](https://www.urdupoint.com/politics/). However, serious differences emerged in her relationship with [Trump](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/donald-trump.html) in late 2025, and she resigned [from](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/from.html) [Congress](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/congress.html) on 5th Jan, 2026. Her disagreements also included US support for [Israel](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/israel.html), foreign policy, healthcare and other issues.

Greene’s position on [Gaza](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/gaza.html) has hardened over time. Even before leaving [Congress](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/congress.html), she had been questioning the [Trump](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/donald-trump.html) administration’s foreign policy and objecting to overseas military operations and spending in the name of “America First.” Her current criticism is also part of her broader position advocating that the US [government](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/government.html) focus on domestic issues instead of spending resources on wars abroad. The former congresswoman’s latest statement came as Palestinian deaths continued in Israeli military operations in [Gaza](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/gaza.html) despite a ceasefire.

According to [Gaza](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/gaza.html)’s Health Ministry, 1,344 Palestinians have been killed and 4,464 [injured](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/injured.html) in Israeli operations since the ceasefire took effect on 10th Oct, 2025. Overall, Palestinian officials say the death toll in the war that began in [October](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/october.html) 2023 has exceeded 73,500, while the number of [injured](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/injured.html) has surpassed 17,400.
