LAHORE, (APP - UrduPoint / Pakistan Point News - 7th Sep, 2026) Punjab Chief Minister Maryam Nawaz Sharif on Monday paid rich tribute to the martyrs and veterans of the [Pakistan](https://www.urdupoint.com/pakistan/) Air Force (PAF) and saluted its Shaheens and valiant personnel on Air Force Day.

In her special message, the chief minister offered prayers for the elevation of the ranks of the martyrs and for the strength and well-being of their bereaved families.

CM Maryam Nawaz paid tribute to the professional capabilities and exemplary courage of the PAF’s Shaheens, saying that PAF heroes, including Shaheed Rashid Minhas, had written remarkable chapters of courage, bravery and valour in the skies.

She said that in May this year, the Pakistan Air Force demonstrated its professional excellence by shooting down eight enemy fighter aircraft, once again making the nation proud and raising the green and white flag with honour.

The chief minister said Air Force Day was a symbol of national unity, unwavering resolve and the spirit of patriotism. She said the sacrifices and services of the Pakistan Air Force in safeguarding the country’s airspace would always be remembered, adding that the nation saluted its martyrs, veterans and brave personnel for their invaluable services to Pakistan.
