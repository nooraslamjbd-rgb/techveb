## Ambassador Asim Iftikhar Ahmad Appointed as Foreign Secretary

The Pakistani government has appointed Ambassador Asim Iftikhar Ahmad as the country's next foreign secretary. As the top career diplomat, he takes charge of the bureaucratic head of Pakistan's diplomatic apparatus.

However, analysts note that the state of the office he inherits has diminished significantly in recent years. Despite capable leadership from previous foreign secretaries, the office's policy influence has noticeably weakened.

## The Declining Stature of Pakistan's Top Diplomatic Office

Historically, the foreign secretary was central to framing and delivering the nation's external policy. Recently, institutional authority has shifted, leaving top diplomats with a lower public profile on critical international issues.

### Shifting Decision-Making Power

Major geopolitical initiatives are increasingly managed by political and military principals rather than senior career diplomats. For instance, during sensitive negotiations like the US-Iran mediation, the Foreign Office played a peripheral public role despite the execution of significant agreements like the Islamabad Memorandum of Understanding.

## Institutional Challenges in Foreign Policy Execution

When diplomatic craft and institutional memory are excluded from central decision-making, foreign policy continuity suffers. Re-empowering the Foreign Office remains crucial for Pakistan's strategic clarity on global stages.
