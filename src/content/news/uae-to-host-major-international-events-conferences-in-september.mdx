ABU DHABI, (UrduPoint / Pakistan Point News / WAM - 31st Aug, 2026) As [September](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/september.html) 2026 unfolds, the [UAE](https://www.urdupoint.com/arabic/uae.html) reinforces its standing as an international catalyst for economic and cultural [progress](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/progress.html) by hosting a formidable slate of global summits, expos, and trade forums.

The lineup brings together [world](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/world.html) leaders, investors, and pioneers across energy, [technology](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/technology.html), [media](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/media.html), trade, and [education](https://www.urdupoint.com/education/), showcasing the nation's operational excellence and high-capacity infrastructure.

Abu Dhabi will host the 35th [Abu Dhabi](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/abu-dhabi.html) International Book Fair [from](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/from.html) 13th to 18th [September](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/september.html) at the [Abu Dhabi](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/abu-dhabi.html) National Exhibition Centre (ADNEC). Organised by the [Abu Dhabi](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/abu-dhabi.html) [Arabic](https://www.urdupoint.com/arabic/) Language Centre under the theme “Turn the Page, Read the World”, the fair will feature exhibitors [from](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/from.html) 95 countries and around 1,500 events, reflecting its standing as a global platform for culture, knowledge and the creative industries.

On 15th and 16th [September](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/september.html), [Abu Dhabi](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/abu-dhabi.html) will host Schneider Electric’s Innovation Summit [middle](https://www.urdupoint.com/education/middle-result.html) East & [Africa](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/africa.html) at ADNEC under the theme “Advancing Energy Tech to Power Intelligence”. The summit will bring together global leaders, [government](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/government.html) officials, and energy and [technology](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/technology.html) experts to discuss the future of energy systems and digital transformation.

Education Interface will organise the 12th edition of its annual exhibition on 22nd and 23rd [September](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/september.html) at ADNEC Centre [Abu Dhabi](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/abu-dhabi.html) under the theme “Education and the Family”. More than 600 universities and educational institutions [from](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/from.html) the [UAE](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/uae.html) and abroad will participate, providing students and parents with opportunities to explore academic programmes, scholarships, future specialisations and career pathways.

Al Ain will host the inaugural Al Ain Future [business](https://www.urdupoint.com/business/prize-bonds.html) Forum on 28th and 29th [September](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/september.html) at ADNEC Centre Al Ain. Organised by the [Abu Dhabi](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/abu-dhabi.html) Department of Economic Development under the theme “Invest, Develop, Thrive”, the forum will address investment, economic growth, talent development, research and development, higher [education](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/education.html), agricultural [technology](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/technology.html), advanced manufacturing, smart transportation, renewable energy and sustainable tourism.

Meanwhile, the 23rd [Abu Dhabi](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/abu-dhabi.html) International Hunting and Equestrian Exhibition (ADIHEX) will continue at ADNEC Centre [Abu Dhabi](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/abu-dhabi.html) until 6th [September](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/september.html). The exhibition features 15 sectors, more than 40 cultural and entertainment activities, and eight falcon auctions, bringing together Emirati heritage and innovation across falconry, equestrianism, hunting, camping, and land and marine tourism.

Dubai will also host a packed programme of specialised international exhibitions and conferences throughout [September](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/september.html), reflecting the diversity of sectors attracted to the emirate and its ability to bring together investors, experts and decision-makers [from](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/from.html) around the [world](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/world.html).

Middle East Energy 2026 will take place at [Dubai](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/dubai.html) [World](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/world.html) Trade Centre [from](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/from.html) 1st to 3rd [September](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/september.html), with more than 1,900 exhibitors [from](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/from.html) over 150 countries showcasing the latest products and solutions across the energy and electrical infrastructure sectors. The [event](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/event.html) will also feature five expert-led conferences.

From 7th to 9th [September](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/september.html), [Dubai](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/dubai.html) will host the International Property Show (IPS 2026) and AIM [Congress](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/congress.html) at [Dubai](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/dubai.html) [World](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/world.html) Trade Centre. IPS will bring together investors, developers, [government](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/government.html) entities and financial institutions to showcase real estate projects and investment opportunities, while AIM [Congress](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/congress.html) will convene investors, decision-makers and experts to discuss sustainable investment, economic diversification, digital transformation and international partnerships.

Dubai will host the 25th [Dubai](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/dubai.html) [World](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/world.html) Dermatology and Laser Conference & Exhibition ([Dubai](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/dubai.html) Derma) [from](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/from.html) 8th to 10th [September](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/september.html) at [Dubai](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/dubai.html) [World](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/world.html) Trade Centre, bringing together specialists and companies operating in dermatology, aesthetics, laser technologies and healthcare through a specialised scientific and training programme.

The [Arab](https://www.urdupoint.com/arabic/) [Media](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/media.html) Summit 2026 will take place in [Dubai](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/dubai.html) [from](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/from.html) 15th to 17th [September](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/september.html). The [event](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/event.html) will feature eight specialised forums and bring together more than 200 [media](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/media.html) figures [from](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/from.html) outside the [UAE](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/uae.html) and around 10,000 [media](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/media.html) professionals and [industry](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/industry.html) stakeholders [from](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/from.html) the [UAE](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/uae.html) and across the [Arab](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/arab.html) [world](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/world.html).

From 16th to 18th [September](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/september.html), [Dubai](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/dubai.html) Exhibition Centre at Expo City [Dubai](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/dubai.html) will host GISEC GLOBAL 2026. The cybersecurity [event](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/event.html) will bring together [government](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/government.html) entities, companies, information security leaders and experts to discuss cyber threats and the future of digital security, alongside solutions spanning infrastructure protection, cloud security and artificial intelligence.

Sharjah, meanwhile, will host the fifth [Sharjah](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/sharjah.html) Booksellers Conference on 19th and 20th [September](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/september.html) at Expo Centre [Sharjah](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/sharjah.html). Hundreds of distributors, booksellers and e-commerce platforms [from](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/from.html) 98 countries will participate in discussions on the global book distribution landscape, supply-chain development, stronger partnerships between distributors and publishers, and the evolving digital landscape.
