SINGAPORE, (UrduPoint / Pakistan Point News / WAM - 10th Sep, 2026) [Oil](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/oil.html) [prices](https://www.urdupoint.com/business/prize-bonds.html) held firm on Thursday after Brent crude breached $100 a barrel, with traders remained concerned about further supply disruptions.

Brent crude futures inched lower by 0.1 percent to $101.10 a barrel by 02:56 GMT. US West Texas Intermediate crude was at $96.24 a barrel, up 0.2 percent.
