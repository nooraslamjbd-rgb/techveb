## King Charles Reaffirms Non-Working Royal Status

A spokesperson for the Duke and Duchess of Sussex stated that Prince Harry and Meghan Markle were surprised by a recent letter sent on behalf of King Charles III. The official communication clarified that the couple remains non-working royals and private citizens following their move back to the UK from California.

The letter explicitly confirmed that the couple will not be able to use the titles "His and Her Royal Highness."

## Short Notice Sparks Frustration

While royal sources maintain the couple was informed prior to the public release, representatives for the Sussexes revealed they only saw the letter about an hour before it hit the media. Due to the tight timeline, Prince Harry's team had virtually no opportunity to review or respond to the statement before publication.

## UK Security Arrangements Under Assessment

With Prince Harry, Meghan, and their children—Archie and Lilibet—now residing in the UK, the family's security framework is under active review.

Key points regarding their protective arrangements include:

* **Ravec Evaluation:** The Royal and VIP Executive Committee, alongside Home Office representatives, is assessing taxpayer-funded security levels.
* **Security Complaints:** Prince Harry has previously voiced serious concerns regarding insufficient police protection during his UK stays.
* **Official Stance:** The Home Office declined to share specific details, citing strict policies to maintain security integrity.
