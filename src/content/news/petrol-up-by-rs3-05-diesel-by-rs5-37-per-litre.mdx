The federal government has announced new petroleum product prices and issued a notification.

ISLAMABAD, (UrduPoint / Pakistan Point News - 10th Sep, 2026) The [Federal](https://www.urdupoint.com/education/board-of-education-federal.html) [government](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/government.html) has announced new [prices](https://www.urdupoint.com/business/prize-bonds.html) for petroleum products. The [price](https://www.urdupoint.com/business/prize-bonds.html) of [petrol](https://www.urdupoint.com/business/petroleum-prices-in-pakistan.html) has been increased by Rs3.05 per litre, bringing its new [price](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/price.html) to Rs370.80.

According to the notification, diesel has become more expensive by Rs5.37 per litre, with its new [price](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/price.html) set at Rs398.04. OGRA issued the notification for the new prices applicable [from](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/from.html) 11 [September](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/september.html). The new prices will take effect at midnight tonight.
