 Published August 30, 2026 | 08:20 PM

KOHAT, (APP - UrduPoint / Pakistan Point News - 30th Aug, 2026) District Police Officer (DPO) [Kohat](https://www.urdupoint.com/education/board-of-education-kohat.html), Rai Mazhar Iqbal, conducted inspections of Lachi and Mills police stations on Sunday and directed staff to ensure accurate record keeping.

During his visit, DPO personally engaged with visitors and complainants, listening to their grievances and promising merit based resolutions to their issues.

He said that delivering timely justice, offering better facilities to citizens, and upgrading overall policing standards remain the department's [Primary](https://www.urdupoint.com/education/primary-result.html) focus.

Meanwhile, Traffic Police initiated a targeted crackdown against vehicles utilizing tinted windows, non-standard tinting, and unauthorized fancy number plates.

Traffic police officers set up checkpoints along major highways and key intersections across district, issuing challans to non-compliant drivers and removing illegal number plates on the spot.
