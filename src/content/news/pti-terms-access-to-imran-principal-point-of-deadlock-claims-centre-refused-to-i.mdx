## PTI Talks Deadlock Over Imran Khan Access

Negotiations between Pakistan Tehreek-e-Insaf (PTI) and the federal government ended in a stalemate after the Centre rejected terms regarding access to jailed founder Imran Khan.

According to reports originally published by Dawn News, hours of intense dialogue between opposition representatives and the government coalition failed to produce a mutual consensus.

### Key Breakdown in Negotiations

The opposition delegation, composed of PTI and Tehreek-i-Tahafuz-i-Ayin-i-Pakistan (TTAP), met with a joint team representing the government and the Pakistan Peoples Party (PPP).

* **Primary Issue:** PTI insisted on formal terms granting regular access to Imran Khan.
* **Government Stance:** The Centre refused to embed visitation terms into the official draft agreement.
* **Outcome:** Both sides departed without signing an agreement, leading to a complete deadlock.

### Political Implications

PTI leaders reaffirmed that establishing communication with Imran Khan remains the central hurdle in negotiations. Without clear commitments regarding their party leader's access, future talks between the opposition and government coalition remain uncertain.
