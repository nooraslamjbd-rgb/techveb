## England Take Control of Third Test After Pakistan Collapses

England dominated the opening day of the third Test against Pakistan at Edgbaston, putting themselves on course for a series whitewash. The visiting side suffered another dramatic batting collapse, getting bowled out for just 133 runs in 33.5 overs.

### Pace Attack Dismantles Pakistan Batting

England's four-man pace attack proved relentless from the start. Fast bowler Josh Tongue led the charge with figures of 4-42, while Ollie Robinson (3-15) and Jofra Archer (3-25) provided lethal support.

Key highlights from the first innings include:
* **Early Wickets:** Pakistan slipped to 26-4 inside the first hour of play.
* **Top Scorer:** Saud Shakeel offered sole resistance, top-scoring with 43 off 47 balls.
* **Milestone:** Ollie Robinson claimed his 100th Test wicket by trapping Saim Ayub lbw.

### Debutant Razaullah Shines Amid Squad Shake-up

Following a dismal second Test at Lord's, Pakistan introduced three debutants—Razaullah, Ghazi Ghori, and Mohammad Imran—after seven squad members were sent home and head coach Sarfaraz Ahmed was dismissed.

Debutant fast bowler Razaullah made an immediate impact by dismissing England superstar Joe Root for a duck with only his fourth delivery in Test cricket.

### England Secure First-Innings Lead

Despite Root's early exit, a century partnership between Emilio Gay and Harry Brook stabilized England's reply. By the close of play on Day 1, England reached 156-4, establishing a 23-run lead over Pakistan.
