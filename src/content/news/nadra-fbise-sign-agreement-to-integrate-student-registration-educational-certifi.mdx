 Published September 01, 2026 | 06:00 PM

ISLAMABAD, (APP - UrduPoint / Pakistan Point News - 1st Sep, 2026) The National Database and Registration Authority (NADRA) and the [Federal](https://www.urdupoint.com/education/board-of-education-federal.html) [board](https://www.urdupoint.com/education/boards-of-education.html) of Intermediate and Secondary Education (FBISE) have signed an agreement to integrate the student registration system for Matriculation and Intermediate examinations.

Under the agreement, a mutual data-exchange mechanism has been established. This will enable NADRA to provide FBISE with key student details directly from its registry, including full name, father's name, and [date](https://www.urdupoint.com/islam/today-islamic-date.html) of birth.

The initiative aims to minimise data discrepancies and errors during student registration, whilst enhancing administrative efficiency through the issuance of accurate and verifiable academic credentials.

The partnership also includes the development of a secure Digital Wallet, allowing students to access, store, and digitally share their educational certificates.

This collaboration forms part of NADRA's broader initiative to integrate national identity system and educational records.

Under this programme, NADRA is progressively working to streamline digital data-sharing across secondary and higher secondary [education](https://www.urdupoint.com/education/) boards, universities, and the Higher Education Commission (HEC) nationwide.

The signing ceremony took place today at the NADRA Headquarters in Islamabad, attended by the Chairman of FBISE, Dr Ikram Ali Malik, and the Chairman of NADRA.
