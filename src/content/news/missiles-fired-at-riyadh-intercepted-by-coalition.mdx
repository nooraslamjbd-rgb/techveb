## Saudi Defense Forces Neutralize Aerial Threats Over Riyadh

Saudi Arabia’s air defense systems successfully intercepted multiple ballistic missiles and armed drones launched by Yemen’s Houthi movement toward Riyadh, according to statements from the Saudi-led military coalition reported by Dawn News.

Coalition spokespersons confirmed that military defenses detected and destroyed two combat drones aimed directly at the capital region before they could reach their intended targets.

### Cross-Border Escalation Despite Global Condemnation

The latest aerial strike highlights a continuing escalation of cross-border attacks despite widespread international calls for a ceasefire and peaceful resolution to the Yemeni conflict.

### Operational Highlights

* **Targeted Area:** Metropolitan Riyadh and surrounding security zones.
* **Defense Strategy:** Early detection and interception using advanced surface-to-air missile defense systems.
* **Attacker:** Yemen-based Houthi militia forces.

Coalition forces reaffirmed their commitment to taking necessary operational measures to protect civilians and critical infrastructure from hostile drone and missile threats.
