## Key Takeaways

- Jose Mourinho brought printed screenshots to protest refereeing decisions after Real Madrid lost 2-1.
- Mourinho claimed Atletico Madrid should have received two first-half red cards during the match.
- This derby defeat marks Real Madrid's second loss of the current football season.
- Atletico manager Diego Simeone urged coaches to respect referees and avoid crossing professional lines.
- The controversial incident involved a 37th-minute clash between Jude Bellingham and Cristian Romero.

Real Madrid head coach Jose Mourinho made headlines following his side's 2-1 derby defeat against Atletico Madrid by bringing printed evidence to his post-match press conference. As reported by BBC News, Mourinho presented an A4 sheet containing screenshots of two key first-half incidents that he argued deserved straight red cards for Atletico players.

## Unpacking the Refereeing Controversy

Mourinho expressed strong frustration over the officiating, claiming key errors severely impacted the outcome of the match. The primary clash highlighted occurred in the 37th minute between Real Madrid's Jude Bellingham and Atletico Madrid's Cristian Romero.

- **Unpunished Incidents:** Mourinho insisted Atletico should have played over 50 minutes with nine men.
- **Match Outcome:** The 2-1 defeat represents Real Madrid's second loss of the season, following an earlier defeat to Real Betis.
- **Team Praise:** Despite the result, Mourinho praised his squad's character and level of performance under challenging conditions.

## Diego Simeone Responds to Mourinho

In contrast, Atletico Madrid manager Diego Simeone celebrated the victory while offering a calm counter-perspective on Mourinho's complaints. Speaking to the media, Simeone emphasized the public responsibility carried by high-profile managers.

Simeone noted that figures like himself, Mourinho, Hansi Flick, and Manuel Pellegrini must maintain professional standards. He stressed that crossing the line regarding respect for match officials and colleagues ultimately harms the sport as a whole.
