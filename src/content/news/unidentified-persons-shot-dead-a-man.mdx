PESHAWAR, (APP - UrduPoint / Pakistan Point News - 29th Aug, 2026) Unidentified assailants here on Saturday killed a man by indiscriminate firing on OTS Road [Kohat](https://www.urdupoint.com/education/board-of-education-kohat.html), police said.

According to police sources, unknown gunmen opened fire on a man identified as Abdul Khaliq on OTS Road near the Jungle Khel area.

The victim succumbed to wounds on the spot while attackers managed to flee the scene.

Meanwhile, teams of Rescue 1122 rushed to the spot and shifted the body to nearby hospital. Police has started probe into the incident.

Police authorities have also started a operation to clear the area of anti-social elements. Police arrested two and recovered hashish from their possession during raid in Gambat area.
