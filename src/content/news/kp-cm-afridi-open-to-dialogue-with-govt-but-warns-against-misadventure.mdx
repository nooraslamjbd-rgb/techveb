## Dialogue Proposal Amid Political Tensions
Khyber Pakhtunkhwa Chief Minister Sohail Afridi has expressed willingness to engage in dialogue with Pakistan's federal government. Addressing key political tensions, the Chief Minister highlighted the importance of negotiation over political conflict, offering a conciliatory stance to address mutual disputes peacefully.

## Warning Against Federal Misadventure
Despite offering talks, Chief Minister Afridi delivered a stern warning regarding potential heavy-handed tactics by federal authorities. He stated that any misadventure against the provincial administration or peaceful demonstrators would trigger serious repercussions.

### Key Highlights of the Situation
* **Primary Demand**: The immediate release of Pakistan Tehreek-e-Insaf (PTI) founder Imran Khan.
* **Protest Strategy**: Planned marches toward Islamabad from various provinces.
* **Current Status**: Imran Khan remains incarcerated in Rawalpindi's Adiala jail.

According to a report by Dawn News, the political climate remains tense as PTI supporters gear up for widespread demonstrations. The provincial leadership maintains that dialogue is the preferred path forward, provided the federal government respects democratic norms and constitutional rights.
