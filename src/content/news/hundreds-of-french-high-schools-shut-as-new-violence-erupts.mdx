## French High School Protests Escalate Nationwide

A growing student protest movement across France has forced the closure or blockade of around 400 high schools. Final-year students at lycées have taken to the streets, igniting fires outside educational institutions and clashing with police forces.

According to a report by Dawn News, the unrest shows no immediate signs of abating as demonstrations continue to spread through major cities and regional towns alike.

## Escalating Clashes and Campus Blockades

The demonstrations have turned increasingly volatile in several municipalities. Protesting pupils have used trash bins, barricades, and makeshift obstacles to block entrances to school grounds, preventing staff and peers from entering.

Key disruptions observed during the unrest include:

* **Fires set outside school facilities:** Demonstrators lit fires near entrance gates to enforce blockades.
* **Riot police intervention:** Security forces were deployed to disperse crowds using tear gas and crowd-control tactics.
* **Disrupted national examinations:** Academic schedules and preparations for final secondary school exams face major delays.

## Movement Spreads from Paris Region

What began as localized student discontent in the Greater Paris region last week quickly galvanized nationwide support. Final-year high school students cite growing frustration over recent educational reforms and government policies, fueling the rapid mobilization.

French educational authorities are working alongside local law enforcement to restore order and reopen affected lycées, but student groups indicate that further actions may be planned in the coming days.
