NEW YORK, (UrduPoint / Pakistan Point News - 11th Sep, 2026) A US [court](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/court.html) has sentenced Pakistani-origin [woman](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/women.html) Zakia Khan to 6 years and 4 months in prison for defrauding the US [government](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/government.html) of tens of [millions](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/million.html) of [Dollars](https://www.urdupoint.com/business/currency-rate/dollar-to-pkr.html). A female judge at the [Federal](https://www.urdupoint.com/education/board-of-education-federal.html) [court](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/court.html) in [New York](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/new-york.html) announced the verdict, sentencing Zakia Khan for fraud and deception against the US [government](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/government.html).

The [court](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/court.html) also ordered her to pay $56 [million](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/million.html) and ruled that assets worth $5 [million](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/million.html) would be forfeited. According to prosecutors, Zakia Khan established a daycare center for senior citizens and used it as a cover to allegedly obtain a large sum [from](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/from.html) the US [government](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/government.html) through fraud.

The fraud caused approximately $64 [million](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/million.html) in losses to the US [government](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/government.html). Zakia Khan was arrested in the case in 2024. During the trial, she also pleaded guilty. The case was decided at a federal [court](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/court.html) in [New York](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/new-york.html) yesterday, with the [court](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/court.html) imposing prison and financial penalties after taking the nature of the crime and the financial loss into account. Under the [court](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/court.html)’s decision, Zakia Khan must pay $56 [million](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/million.html) to the US [government](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/government.html), while assets worth $5 [million](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/million.html) will also be forfeited.
