No court will allow armed groups to march on Islamabad; protests may be permitted if three basic conditions are met, the prime minister’s adviser on political affairs says.

ISLAMABAD, (UrduPoint / Pakistan Point News - 9th Sep, 2026) [Prime Minister](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/prime-minister.html)’s Adviser Senator [Rana Sanaullah](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/rana-sanaullah.html) has said that intelligence agencies have reported that the [Pakistan](https://www.urdupoint.com/pakistan/) Tehreek-e-Insaf ([PTI](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/pakistan-tehreek-e-insaf.html)) is planning to repeat a mistake bigger than [May](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/may.html) 9 on Sept 27, adding that no [court](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/court.html) will allow armed groups to [march](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/march.html) on [Islamabad](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/islamabad.html).

Speaking on GTV [news](https://www.urdupoint.com/urdu-news), he said the [government](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/government.html) would comply if a [court](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/court.html) ordered that [PTI](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/pakistan-tehreek-e-insaf.html) be allowed to hold a [protest](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/protest.html) and provided security. However, he said he was certain that no [court](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/court.html) could issue such an order.

He said no [court](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/court.html) could allow an armed group to [attack](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/attack.html) its [government](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/government.html) or the capital.

He said the group wanted to [march](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/march.html) on [Islamabad](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/islamabad.html) in an armed formation, adding that if [Islamabad](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/islamabad.html) were attacked, it would become a subject of ridicule across the [world](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/world.html) and the lives and property of everyone, including foreign diplomats, would be at risk.

He said neither the Constitution nor the law allowed such an armed group to [attack](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/attack.html) the capital, and no [court](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/court.html) would permit it; such activities should be prevented.

Permission for a [protest](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/protest.html) could be granted subject to three basic conditions: no person would be armed; an assurance would be given that the [protest](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/protest.html) would remain peaceful; and the terms of the [protest](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/protest.html) [long march](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/long-march.html) would be settled in consultation with the [Islamabad](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/islamabad.html) administration.

However, he asked who would trust a party with [May](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/may.html) 9 to its credit. He said the reports by the agencies were being further verified and that [PTI](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/pakistan-tehreek-e-insaf.html) was going to repeat a mistake bigger than [May](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/may.html) 9 on Sept 27, something it itself did not realize. He said it would be better for [PTI](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/pakistan-tehreek-e-insaf.html) to review its conduct.
