## Fire Breaks Out at Karachi Mayor's KMC Office

A minor fire broke out on Tuesday morning at the office of Karachi Mayor Murtaza Wahab, located on the first floor of the historic Karachi Metropolitan Corporation (KMC) building. Emergency responders and fire tenders promptly reached the scene and brought the blaze under control.

### Cause and Immediate Impact

According to officials speaking to Dawn News, the incident occurred around 9:15 AM, likely triggered by a spark or short circuit in an air conditioner's outer unit. The presence of false ceilings and wooden furniture accelerated the fire within the mayor's personal room.

Key details regarding the incident include:
* **Casualties:** Zero injuries or casualties reported.
* **Structural Damage:** Mayor Wahab's main room was damaged, but the adjacent conference room and chamber were saved.
* **Record Safety:** No official municipal records or files were destroyed.

### Official Statement from Mayor Wahab

Speaking to media outside the KMC building, Mayor Murtaza Wahab clarified that government documents remain safe, as active files are processed and immediately dispatched to relevant departments.

He reassured the public that city operations would continue without delay. Wahab stated he would resume his official duties immediately from another room within the historic KMC building and proceed with scheduled city field visits.
