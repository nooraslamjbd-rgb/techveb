LAHORE, (UrduPoint / Pakistan Point News - 18th Sep, 2026) A video allegedly showing a [police](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/police.html) officer publicly assaulting a woman in front of her two minor sons during a train journey over an alleged lack of a ticket has surfaced on [social media](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/social-media.html), prompting strong [condemnation](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/condemnation.html). Anchorperson Adil Nizami shared the video of the incident on his X account.

The video purportedly shows the police officer mistreating and assaulting the woman, while her two minor children are also present.

The children appear frightened during the incident, while the woman can be seen repeatedly pleading with the police officer to stop hitting her.

Social media users and various circles have raised questions about the police officer’s conduct.

Commenting on the incident, journalist Adil Nizami said that amid [billions](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/billion.html) of rupees in [corruption](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/corruption.html), how much damage would the country have suffered if the woman and her two children had travelled on the train without tickets? Critics, meanwhile, said that if the woman and her children did not have tickets, the matter could have been dealt with according to law, and that violence could never be a solution. The incident has also sparked debate about police officers’ conduct toward the public and passengers’ rights.

However, complete details of the incident and an official police statement are still awaited.
