BISHKEK, Kyrgyzstan, (UrduPoint / Pakistan Point News / WAM - 02nd Sep, 2026) [UN](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/united-nations.html) Secretary-General António Guterres urged leaders of the [Shanghai](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/shanghai.html) Cooperation Organisation ([SCO](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/sco.html)) to help reshape global governance for a changing [world](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/world.html), arguing that a more multipolar international order can strengthen multilateralism, advance fairness and better serve developing countries.

Addressing the [SCO](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/sco.html) summit in [Bishkek](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/bishkek.html), [Kyrgyzstan](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/kyrgyzstan.html), on Tuesday, the Secretary-General said shifting global power dynamics present an opportunity to build more representative international institutions and a [United Nations](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/united-nations.html) that works "at the service of [all](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/all.html) without double standards in line with the Charter."

The [Shanghai](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/shanghai.html) Cooperation Organisation is a Eurasian political, economic and security grouping founded in 2001 that today brings together 10 member States, including [China](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/china.html), [India](https://www.urdupoint.com/islam/india-prayers-timings.html), [Pakistan](https://www.urdupoint.com/pakistan/), [Russia](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/russia.html) and several Central [Asian](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/asia.html) countries. It is one of the [world](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/world.html)'s largest regional organizations by population and geographic scope.

“Our [world](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/world.html) is increasingly multipolar,” Guterres said.

“That is a good thing and must be strengthened.”

He described multipolarity as “a condition for justice in global governance and international relations” and “the [best](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/best.html) support for effective multilateralism.”

The [UN](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/united-nations.html) chief noted that while global power relations have evolved significantly, many international institutions continue to reflect the geopolitical realities of 1945.

“We must reform our multilateral institutions, including the [United Nations](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/united-nations.html) and the Bretton Woods system, to reflect today's realities, and increase the representation and influence of developing countries to build a just [world](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/world.html) order,” he said.

He also praised the longstanding partnership between the [UN](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/united-nations.html) and the [SCO](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/sco.html), describing the organisation as “an important pillar of multilateralism.”

The Secretary-General outlined four areas where he believes cooperation between the [UN](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/united-nations.html) and [SCO](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/sco.html) can be strengthened: Global security, Financing for development, Climate action and AI.
