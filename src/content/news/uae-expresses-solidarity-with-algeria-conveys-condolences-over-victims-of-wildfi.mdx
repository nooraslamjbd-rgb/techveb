ABU DHABI, (UrduPoint / Pakistan Point News / WAM - 28th Aug, 2026) The United [Arab](https://www.urdupoint.com/arabic/) Emirates has expressed its solidarity with the People's Democratic Republic of [Algeria](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/algeria.html) over the victims of wildfires in the northeast of the country, which resulted in a number of deaths and injuries.

In a statement, the Ministry of Foreign Affairs (MoFA) conveyed its sincere condolences and sympathy to the families of the victims, and to the People's Democratic Republic of [Algeria](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/algeria.html) and its people over this tragedy, as well as its wishes for a speedy recovery for the [injured](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/injured.html).
