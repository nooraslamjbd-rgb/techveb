SUKKUR, (APP - UrduPoint / Pakistan Point News - 11th Sep, 2026) The Department of [English](https://www.urdupoint.com/dictionary/) Language and Literature at Shaikh Ayaz University, Shikarpur, has announced admissions to its four-year [BS](https://www.urdupoint.com/education/bs-result.html) English programme for the 2027 academic session, inviting aspiring students to build strong communication, critical-thinking and professional skills.

The BS English programme is designed to provide students with a comprehensive understanding of the English language and literature while preparing them for diverse academic and professional opportunities, said a press release on Thursday.

The programme will help students to develop strong communication skills, literary and critical thinking, creative abilities, confidence and personal development.

It will also equip students with digital and professional skills needed in today’s competitive environment.

Graduates of BS English can pursue career opportunities in teaching, media, publishing, communication and other fields, while proficiency in English can also provide an advantage in pursuing opportunities in an increasingly globalized world.

The programme further offers students a pathway to higher [education](https://www.urdupoint.com/education/), including [MS](https://www.urdupoint.com/education/ms-result.html), MPhil and PhD programmes, enabling them to continue their academic and research careers.

The university has announced that the last [date](https://www.urdupoint.com/islam/today-islamic-date.html) to apply is October 10, 2026. The admission test will be held on November 7, 2026 followed by the announcement of [results](https://www.urdupoint.com/education/results.html) on November 9. The first merit list will be displayed on November 11, while regular classes are [scheduled](https://www.urdupoint.com/business/prize-bonds-draw-schedule.html) to commence on January 4, 2027.

The Department of English Language and Literature has encouraged eligible students to take advantage of the opportunity and apply for admission to the four-year BS English programme at Shaikh Ayaz University, Shikarpur.
