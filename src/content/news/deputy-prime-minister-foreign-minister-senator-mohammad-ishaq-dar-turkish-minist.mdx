ISLAMABAD, (APP - UrduPoint / Pakistan Point News - 31st Aug, 2026) Deputy Prime Minister/Foreign Minister Senator Mohammad Ishaq Dar met with Türkiye’s Minister of National Defence Yasar Güler, on the sidelines of the inaugural meeting of the Strategic Political and Defence Committee in Istanbul, established under the Makkah Joint Defence Agreement.

The two sides discussed bilateral defence cooperation and matters of mutual interest.
