ISLAMABAD, (UrduPoint / Pakistan Point News - 11th Sep, 2026) Public outrage is growing in [Pakistan](https://www.urdupoint.com/pakistan/) over the continued increase in petroleum product prices, while former [Federal](https://www.urdupoint.com/education/board-of-education-federal.html) Minister [Fawad Chaudhry](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/fawad-chaudhry.html) has also criticised the [government](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/government.html). Sharing a [Pakistan Television](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/ptv.html) ([ptv](https://www.urdupoint.com/tv/sports-channels/ptv-sports-live.html)) video, [Fawad Chaudhry](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/fawad-chaudhry.html) expressed his reaction to the Shehbaz [government](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/government.html)’s measures on the [social media](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/social-media.html) platform. He wrote, “Shehbaz [government](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/government.html)’s vision!” The [government](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/government.html) claims that the deserted [petrol](https://www.urdupoint.com/business/petroleum-prices-in-pakistan.html) pumps resulting [from](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/from.html) higher [petrol](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/petrol.html) and diesel [prices](https://www.urdupoint.com/business/prize-bonds.html) are actually a major success. Fawad Chaudhry adopted a sarcastic tone regarding the [government](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/government.html)’s stance and also wrote in [English](https://www.urdupoint.com/dictionary/), “Can you believe that…”

> 

It [may](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/may.html) be noted that the increase in petroleum product prices has caused serious difficulties for citizens.

The rise in [petrol](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/petrol.html) and diesel prices is also expected to increase the prices of essential commodities, including transport costs.
