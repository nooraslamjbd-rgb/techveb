Updated: Aug 21, 2026 4:42pm UTCThe 4K Blu-ray releases for Toy Story 5 went up for preorder this morning.

Unfortunately for any collectors, however, the previously announced limited edition steelbook has already sold out across retailers.

A standard Blu-ray and DVD are still up for preorder with the same release date of September 22, and there's a small chance we see a restock of the steelbook between now and then.

Otherwise, I wouldn't be surprised to eventually see it for up-marked prices on eBay.

Toy Story 5's Limited Edition 4K Steelbook Is Already Sold OutIt's not too hard to see why this particular steelbook got snapped up so fast.

The cover art is pretty top of the line, and celebrates Toy Story as a whole instead of being too specific to the new sequel.

Sold out at Amazon and Walmart.

All of the artwork is in a unique watercolor style, with the main Toy Story trio as well as some of the new character additions on the front.

On the back you'll see a certain emotionally devastating tire swing tree from Toy Story 2 that makes its return in the new sequel.

Aside from being so darn pretty, the other reason this steelbook likely sold out so quick is because there doesn’t appear to be any standard 4K UHD release.

In other words, anyone who was looking to grab the movie in 4K UHD was forced into the steelbook option.

Standard Blu-ray and DVD Still AvailableOut September 22Toy Story 5 (Blu-ray)The standard DVD and Blu-ray are still available at multiple retailers.

These feature the same cover art that matches the primary Toy Story 5 poster, showing the team of familiar toys gawking in fear at the sequel's new villain, LilyPad.

The original press release also claimed there would be a new collected set of all five Toy Story movies, but no listings have popped up for that one yet.
