ISLAMABAD, (APP - UrduPoint / Pakistan Point News - 2nd Sep, 2026) Prime Minister Muhammad Shehbaz Sharif, Wednesday, arrived in [Pakistan](https://www.urdupoint.com/pakistan/) after his three-day official visit of Kyrgyzstan.

Earlier in Bishkek, the prime minister was seen off by President of the Kyrgyz Republic Sadyr Nurgozhoevich Japarov.

During his visit, the prime minister participated in the Council of Heads of State of the Shanghai Cooperation Organization (SCO) meeting and addressed it, PM Office Media Wing said in a press release.

He also interacted with the SCO leaders and discussed regional and international issues.
