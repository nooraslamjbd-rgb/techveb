## Bishkek Summit Marks 25 Years of Shanghai Cooperation Group

World leaders are gathering in Bishkek, Kyrgyzstan, for a crucial two-day Shanghai Cooperation Organization (SCO) summit. The event brings together key figures from Russia, China, India, Pakistan, and Iran to discuss regional stability and economic partnership.

### Key Attendees and Leaders Present
Host President Sadyr Japarov will lead the plenary sessions. Major participants include:
* **Vladimir Putin** (President of Russia)
* **Xi Jinping** (President of China)
* **Masoud Pezeshkian** (President of Iran)
* **Shehbaz Sharif** (Prime Minister of Pakistan)
* **Narendra Modi** (Prime Minister of India)

### High-Stakes Bilateral Meetings
Russian President Vladimir Putin is scheduled to conduct high-level bilateral discussions with Iranian President Pezeshkian and Chinese President Xi Jinping. The meeting comes at a critical time as international sanctions push Moscow and Tehran into deeper economic and military cooperation.

### Navigating Global Geopolitical Challenges
The summit takes place against the backdrop of ongoing trade tensions and international conflicts. While officially declared as a non-aligned economic group, the SCO continues to grow as a strategic diplomatic counterweight to Western influence in Eurasia.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the Shanghai Cooperation Organization (SCO) summit?

The SCO summit is a regional diplomatic meeting bringing together leaders from Russia, China, India, Pakistan, Iran, and Central Asian nations to discuss economic and security cooperation.

### Who is attending the Bishkek SCO summit?

Russian President Vladimir Putin, Chinese President Xi Jinping, Iranian President Masoud Pezeshkian, Indian Prime Minister Narendra Modi, and Pakistani Prime Minister Shehbaz Sharif are among the attendees.

### Where is the SCO summit taking place?

The summit is taking place in Bishkek, the capital city of Kyrgyzstan.
