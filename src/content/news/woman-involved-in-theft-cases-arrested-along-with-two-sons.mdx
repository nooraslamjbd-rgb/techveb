LAHORE, (APP - UrduPoint / Pakistan Point News - 29th Aug, 2026) Kot Lakhpat police arrested a woman and her two sons involved in theft cases from the Chungi Amar Sidhu area.

According to the police spokesperson, the accused used to commit crimes under the pretext of shifting goods in a rickshaw.

The accused had stolen gold worth 1.8 million from a woman named Zainab a few days ago.

The police spokesperson said that the stolen gold has been recovered from the accused.

According to SP Model Town Asad Ali, the arrested accused include Razia Bibi, Sufyan and Faizan. Further investigation is underway with the accused.
