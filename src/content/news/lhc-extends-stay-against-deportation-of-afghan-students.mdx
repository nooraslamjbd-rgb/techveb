## LHC Prevents Immediate Deportation of Afghan Medical Trainees

The Lahore High Court (LHC) has extended an interim stay order preventing the expulsion and deportation of foreign medical trainees in Pakistan. Justice Khalid Ishaq granted the extension during Friday's court proceedings, giving the Pakistan Medical and Dental Council (PMDC) additional time to submit its official response, as reported by Dawn News.

### Background of the Legal Challenge

The legal action was initiated by over 30 Afghan medical students, including lead petitioner Syed Mubeenul Fatti. The petitioners approached the court to challenge recent PMDC notifications that jeopardized their medical education and residency status in Pakistan.

### Key Highlights of the Court Order

* **Temporary Relief:** The LHC's extended stay prevents regulatory bodies and law enforcement from deporting the affected students.
* **Time Granted to PMDC:** The court accepted the PMDC's request for extra time to prepare a formal reply to the constitutional petitions.
* **Protection of Educational Rights:** The petition highlights legal concerns regarding the sudden alteration of regulations for foreign doctors enrolled in local institutions.

### Implications and Future Outlook

This extension provides critical temporary relief to foreign students navigating complex regulatory environments. The court will resume hearing the arguments once the PMDC submits its comprehensive written reply regarding student licensing and deportation guidelines.
