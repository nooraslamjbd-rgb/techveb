## Severe Fire Breaks Out at CFB Wainwright Base

A major fire near Canadian Forces Base (CFB) Wainwright in Alberta has left several people dead and multiple others injured. Defense Minister David McGuinty confirmed the incident, describing the active situation as delicate while emergency crews work to manage the site.

CFB Wainwright is one of Canada's premier military training facilities. According to preliminary reports from local media citing military sources, the fire erupted after a gas line or gas well was inadvertently struck during a live-fire training exercise.

### Extreme Weather Accelerates Blaze

The Municipal District of Wainwright reported that the fire spread rapidly after tearing through the base perimeter fences. Strong winds with gusts reaching up to 100 km/h (62 mph) severely compounded the spread before emergency responders managed to contain the flames.

* **Location:** CFB Wainwright, Alberta, Canada
* **Weather Impact:** Wind gusts up to 100 km/h forced rapid spread
* **Containment:** Local fire crews have since contained the main blaze

### Healthcare Systems Trigger Code Orange Protocol

In response to the sudden influx of victims, the Royal Alexandra Hospital in Edmonton declared a Code Orange disaster response. Emergency officials noted that the scale of casualties threatened to overwhelm standard regional healthcare capacity, marking it as a rare disaster event for the province.

Prime Minister Mark Carney expressed sympathies for those affected, noting that a serious incident had created a major emergency. Investigative teams and local authorities continue to assess the site to confirm the final casualty numbers and exact sequence of events.
