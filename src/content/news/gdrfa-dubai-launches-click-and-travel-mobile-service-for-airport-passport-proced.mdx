DUBAI, (UrduPoint / Pakistan Point News / WAM - 07th Sep, 2026) The General Directorate of Identity and Foreigners Affairs – [Dubai](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/dubai.html) (GDRFA [Dubai](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/dubai.html)) has launched [Click](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/click.html) and Travel, described as the [world](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/world.html)’s first [mobile](https://www.urdupoint.com/mobile/) service enabling passport control officers to complete passengers’ entry and departure procedures at their location within [airport](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/airport.html) terminals during peak periods and emergencies.

The service uses [mobile](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/mobile.html) devices integrated with GDRFA [Dubai](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/dubai.html)’s systems and databases, alongside biometric verification, to verify identities and complete transactions in approximately 12 to 15 seconds.

During pilot operations [from](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/from.html) [May](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/may.html) to 31st [August](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/august.html) 2026, the service completed 31,373 transactions.

By allowing officers to operate beyond fixed passport control points, [Click](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/click.html) and Travel gives [airport](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/airport.html) teams greater flexibility to manage passenger flows, allocate resources and respond to increased [traffic](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/traffic.html) and operational pressures.

The departure service is available to [all](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/all.html) passengers, including [UAE](https://www.urdupoint.com/arabic/uae.html) citizens, residents and visitors. For arrivals, the first phase covers [UAE](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/uae.html) citizens and residents, with expansion planned in line with operational requirements.

Lieutenant General Mohammed Ahmed Al Marri, Director-General of Identity and Foreigners Affairs – [Dubai](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/dubai.html), said the initiative reflects a shift [from](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/from.html) improving procedures to redesigning the passenger experience.

“We place people at the heart of the development process and harness advanced technologies to create an easier and more efficient [travel](https://www.urdupoint.com/travel/) experience,” he said.

Al Marri added that completing more than 31,000 transactions demonstrates the service’s operational readiness and supports its continued development and expansion.

Major General Talal Ahmed Al Shanqiti, Assistant Director-General for Air Ports at GDRFA [Dubai](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/dubai.html), said bringing passport services directly to passengers enables faster responses to operational needs, reduces waiting points and improves passenger flow while maintaining security and efficiency.

Colonel Expert Khalid Bin Mediya Al Falasi, Assistant Director-General for Digital Services at GDRFA [Dubai](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/dubai.html), said the service combines digital systems with field operations, allowing officers to securely access and verify passenger information and complete procedures on the spot.

He added that it was designed to support further development and the expansion of services delivered through [mobile](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/mobile.html) devices.

GDRFA [Dubai](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/dubai.html) is exploring additional [mobile](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/mobile.html) services beyond entry and departure procedures, subject to approved operational and security requirements. The initiative forms part of its efforts to develop smart ports, improve operational efficiency and make travel services more accessible and flexible.
