LAHORE, (APP - UrduPoint / Pakistan Point News - 29th Aug, 2026) The City Traffic Police [Lahore](https://www.urdupoint.com/education/board-of-education-lahore.html) (CTPL) on Saturday organized a Women’s Scooter Walk at Liberty Market as part of its ongoing efforts to promote women’s mobility,confidence and independence under the vision of Chief Minister(CM),Punjab Maryam Nawaz Sharif.

The event was organized under the Women on Wheels initiative and featured women who had successfully completed motorcycle and scooter riding training at Lahore Traffic Police Driving Schools.

CTO Lahore Syed Abdul Raheem Shirazi and SP Headquarters Fakhar Bashir Raja participated in the activity and appreciated the enthusiasm and achievements of the participating women.

Speaking on the occasion,CTO Lahore Syed Abdul Raheem Shirazi said the CTPL was committed to creating opportunities for women to acquire essential driving and riding skills and become more confident, independent and self-reliant.

He said driving and riding skills provided women with greater mobility and enabled them to independently pursue [education](https://www.urdupoint.com/education/), employment and other social and professional opportunities.

According to the CTO,24,420 candidates had so far received training at Lahore Traffic Police Driving Schools, including 15,256 women.

Of these,7,888 women were trained in [car](https://www.urdupoint.com/cars/) driving, while 7,178 received motorcycle and scooter riding training. He said 1,472 women had received driving and riding training during the current year,reflecting the department’s continued focus on promoting women’s mobility and independence.

“Thousands of women have become confident drivers through Lahore Traffic Police Driving Schools.Our objective is not merely to teach women how to drive,but to equip them with the confidence and skills necessary to become more independent in their everyday lives,” he added.

CTO Syed Abdul Raheem Shirazi said Women-to-Women training services were also being provided at scooter schools, offering female learners a comfortable and supportive environment to acquire riding skills.

He said 12 driving schools had been established across Lahore to facilitate women seeking training in car driving as well as motorcycle and scooter riding.

Under the Women on Wheels project, Lahore Traffic Police was providing free motorcycle and scooter riding training to women to enhance their mobility,confidence and independence.

The CTO said empowering women could contribute significantly to positive social transformation, adding that an empowered woman contributed not only to her own progress but also to the development of her family and society.

“Active participation of women, alongside men, is essential for sustainable social and economic development,”he said.

Syed Abdul Raheem Shirazi said that the CTPL would continue expanding initiatives aimed at women’s empowerment,mobility,road safety and skills development in line with the vision of the Punjab government.
