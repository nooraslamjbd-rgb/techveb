ISLAMABAD, (APP - UrduPoint / Pakistan Point News - 28th Aug, 2026) President Asif Ali Zardari on Friday expressed deep sorrow over the passing of King Harald V of Norway, who passed away earlier in the day at the age of 89.

The president, in a statement, extended heartfelt condolences to Queen Sonja, Crown Prince Haakon, the Royal Family and the people of Norway.

He also paid tribute to King Harald’s long and dedicated service to Norway and towards strengthening relations between [Pakistan](https://www.urdupoint.com/pakistan/) and Norway.
