LAHORE, (UrduPoint / Pakistan Point News - 8th Sep, 2026) A [Faisalabad](https://www.urdupoint.com/education/board-of-education-faisalabad.html) resident received an [electricity](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/electricity.html) bill of Rs50,000. When he went to sell his motorcycle to pay it, he discovered e-challans worth Rs70,000. According to a [media](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/media.html) report, the resident said that his household had received an [electricity](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/electricity.html) bill of Rs70,000. Anyone can [visit](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/visit.html) our home and see that there is nothing there. Only three fans are used, which every household has. I decided to sell my bike and pay the bill. There, I found out that there were 35 challans worth Rs70,000. He said [all](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/all.html) the challans were for helmet violations and that he had committed no other offence.

I appeal to the [government](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/government.html) to warn [poor](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/poor.html) people and let them go. I work repairing motorcycles at a shop and earn Rs1,000 per day. I did not receive a single message about the 35 challans.

If I had received a message, I would have paid them. I did not wear a helmet previously, but now I wear one even when I go to [buy](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/buy.html) vegetables. There is a bypass [road](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/road.html) next to my home, which is why the challans were issued.
