## Mourinho Clarifies Alexander-Arnold's Midfield Role

Real Madrid manager Jose Mourinho has addressed Trent Alexander-Arnold's tactical position following the club's 2-1 Champions League victory over Inter Milan. Despite starting the 27-year-old in central midfield, Mourinho insisted that the former Liverpool player is not a natural midfielder.

### Temporary Tactical Adjustment

Alexander-Arnold's selection in the center of the pitch came out of necessity rather than a permanent tactical shift. With key players Eduardo Camavinga, Arda Guler, and Bernardo Silva sidelined due to suspensions, Mourinho deployed Alexander-Arnold alongside Federico Valverde.

"He has played midfield with England and Liverpool... but he is not a midfielder," Mourinho stated after the match, as reported by BBC News. "He has interpreted what he needed to really well, tactically."

### Match Performance Against Inter Milan

During his second start of the season, Alexander-Arnold demonstrated both his playmaking strengths and defensive limitations:

- **Chances Created:** 2
- **Crosses Attempted:** Highest among Real Madrid players
- **Passing Accuracy:** 83.3%
- **Tackles Won:** 0

Alexander-Arnold was substituted in the 77th minute shortly after Inter Milan scored. While Mourinho expressed gratitude for the player's adaptability, he signaled that the defender will likely return to his primary right-back role as regular midfielders return from suspension.
