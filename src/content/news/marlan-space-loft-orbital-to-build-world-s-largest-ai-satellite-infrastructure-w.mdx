PARIS, (UrduPoint / Pakistan Point News / WAM - 10th Sep, 2026) Marlan Space, a [UAE](https://www.urdupoint.com/arabic/uae.html) based investor and operator in space [technology](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/technology.html) and a subsidiary of [IHC](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/ihc.html), along with Loft Orbital are leading a consortium in a US$1 [billion](https://www.urdupoint.com/business/prize-bonds.html) investment in [France](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/france.html) to build Altair-Next Gen, the [world](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/world.html)’s largest artificial intelligence (AI) satellite infrastructure.

The plan was announced and signed today at the International Space Summit in [Paris](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/paris.html) by Marlan Space and Loft Orbital, in the presence of Emmanuel Macron, president of the French Republic.

With Altair-Next Gen, [France](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/france.html) and the United [Arab](https://www.urdupoint.com/arabic/) Emirates set out to [lead](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/lead.html) the [world](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/world.html) in artificial intelligence in space.

The initial phase comprises fifty [satellites](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/satellites.html) carrying radar, optical and other sensors, built to serve the needs of [government](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/government.html) users, including French, European and Emirati, along with serving a global base of commercial and sovereign customers.

Altair-Next Gen [satellites](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/satellites.html) analyse what they observe while still in orbit and transmit the [event](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/event.html), not the raw imagery.

The user gets an [alert](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/alert.html) within seconds. Conventional systems deliver data hours later, once it has been downloaded and processed on the ground.

Fifty [satellites](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/satellites.html) is a threshold at which this becomes a permanent capability rather than periodic observation: with a fleet of this size, one satellite is always close to where an [event](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/event.html) is unfolding, so an [alert](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/alert.html) delivered in seconds arrives while there is still time to act.

Services run through an AI application store: users pick the models they need, [from](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/from.html) several providers, and run them on shared [satellites](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/satellites.html), so one constellation serves many programmes at the [same](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/same.html) time.

First applications include maritime domain awareness, wildfire detection and disaster response, and the security of ports and critical infrastructure.

Altair-Next Gen will build on a growing customer base which already includes various commercial and [government](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/government.html) customers in [France](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/france.html) and in the [UAE](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/uae.html).

This satellite infrastructure, held in a dedicated French [company](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/company.html), pairs Emirati ambition, capital and manufacturing strength with European supply chain and space heritage.

The [satellites](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/satellites.html) are produced by Orbitworks at its purpose-built facility in KEZAD, [Abu Dhabi](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/abu-dhabi.html), the region's largest high-volume [site](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/site.html) for commercial constellations, which also hosts satellite operations and gives the programme the industrial capacity to scale [from](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/from.html) ten [satellites](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/satellites.html) to fifty.

Orbitworks was formed in 2024 as a joint venture between Marlan Space and Loft Orbital.

The announcement follows [IHC](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/ihc.html)’s [agreement](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/agreement.html) to acquire an 80 percent stake in Marlan Space, announced on 8th [September](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/september.html) 2026, further strengthening the Group’s investment in deeptech and the new space economy.

Syed Basar Shueb, CEO of [IHC](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/ihc.html), said, “Altair-Next Gen represents the kind of ambitious, technology-led platform we want to build through Marlan Space. By bringing together advanced satellite infrastructure, AI, sovereign capabilities and world-class international partners, this investment positions the [UAE](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/uae.html) and [France](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/france.html) at the forefront of a rapidly evolving space economy. For [IHC](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/ihc.html), it is also a powerful example of how we can connect capital, [technology](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/technology.html) and global partnerships to build platforms with long-term strategic and commercial relevance.

”

Dr. Hamdullah Mohib, Chief Executive Officer, Marlan Space and Orbitworks \[signing on behalf of Marlan Space\], added, "When we set out to build Altair, this is the future we bet on: that intelligence would move into orbit, and that the [UAE](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/uae.html) would be one of the places building it. This is where the [industry](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/industry.html) is going, and we are proud to be at the front of it. Fifty [satellites](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/satellites.html), in groups with different capabilities working as one system, make this one of the most advanced constellations in the [world](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/world.html), and we are leading it, not just taking part in it. Altair and Altair-Next Gen turns a factory into a national capability and the [UAE](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/uae.html) [from](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/from.html) a buyer to a seller of satellite services - with engineering skills, intellectual property, and a supply chain that stays in the country long after the [satellites](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/satellites.html) are in orbit.”

Loft Orbital brings the technological innovations, products and operational experience at the heart of the programme, drawing on its global teams and capabilities across satellite platforms, onboard AI and software, space mission infrastructure, deployment and constellation operations.

Loft Orbital already operates more than 100 payloads and 20 AI missions in orbit. Most publicly, working with NASA's Jet Propulsion Laboratory, it demonstrated a [world](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/world.html) first: a satellite that runs a vision-language model onboard, understands what it sees, and can be tasked in plain language.

Pierre-Damien Vaujour, Chief Executive Officer, Loft Orbital, commented, "For the past few years, we have been working on deploying AI applications in space and orchestrating workflow. With this constellation, we are truly giving our partners developing agentic AI workflows in space a true world-class satellite infrastructure. I am excited to see the first of those [satellites](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/satellites.html), built in [Abu Dhabi](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/abu-dhabi.html), launch in the next few weeks and to start deploying solutions that have never been possible in the space [industry](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/industry.html).”

Mistral joins the consortium to bring frontier AI to space: its models, deployed directly onboard next to the sensors, give the constellation the ability to reason about what it observes and to be tasked in natural language, and will power services in the AI application store.

In addition to other European Aerospace and AI leaders that will be announced later this year, BlackSky, a US [company](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/company.html), is joining the team, investing in [France](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/france.html) to take part in this endeavour and will provide high-resolution optical [satellites](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/satellites.html) at the highest performance commercially available in the [world](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/world.html).

Blacksky, with a majority of its supply chain in [Europe](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/europe.html), has a long history of collaboration between the [UAE](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/uae.html), the US and [France](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/france.html), dating back to building satellite buses for Loft Orbital through Leostella, its previous joint venture with Thales Alenia Space.

Brian O’Toole, CEO, Blacksky, said, “We’re proud to be selected as a key partner and the very high-resolution optical provider on this program. The proven, on-orbit performance of our [technology](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/technology.html) stands as an essential capability behind the [world](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/world.html)’s first AI enabled constellation”

Finally, MaiaSpace has been selected as the preferred launch partner as its launch services become commercially available, anchoring [France](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/france.html)'s [lead](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/lead.html) in the new generation of European launchers
