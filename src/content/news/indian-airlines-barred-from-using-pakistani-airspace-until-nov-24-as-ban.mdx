## Pakistan Extends Airspace Restrictions for Indian Flights

Pakistan has officially extended its airspace ban prohibiting Indian aircraft from using its flight corridors until November 24. The extension was confirmed through a Notice to Airmen (NOTAM) released by the Pakistan Airports Authority (PAA), as originally reported by Dawn News.

The previous restriction was scheduled to lapse on October 24. With the newly issued notice, the prohibition remains fully active for another month across Pakistani airspace.

### Scope of the Airspace Prohibition

According to the official NOTAM, the restriction applies broadly across multiple aviation sectors. The order explicitly restricts:

* Indian-registered commercial and private aircraft
* Flights operated, owned, or leased by Indian operators
* All categories of Indian military aircraft

### Operational Consequences for Flight Routes

The ongoing airspace closure forces commercial carriers to reroute several long-haul international flights. Routing around Pakistani airspace adds flight duration and fuel expenses for airlines operating flights between India, Europe, the Middle East, and North America.
