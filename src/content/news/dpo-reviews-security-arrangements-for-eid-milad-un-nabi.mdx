MANSEHRA, (APP - UrduPoint / Pakistan Point News - 25th Aug, 2026) District Police Officer (DPO) Mansehra Muhammad Azhar Khan chaired a meeting to review security arrangements across the district for 12 Rabi-ul-Awwal, Eid Milad-un-Nabi.

The meeting was attended by all SPs, DSPs, SHOs, police post in-charges and other concerned police officers. The DPO directed officers to ensure foolproof security arrangements across the district for the occasion.

He ordered special surveillance along the routes of religious gatherings and processions as well as at sensitive locations.

Officers were directed to keep a close watch on suspicious individuals and activities and further intensify search operations, snap checking and police patrolling.

Muhammad Azhar Khan said providing citizens with a peaceful and secure environment during Eid Milad-un-Nabi was the police’s top priority.

He directed all officers to utilise available resources and maintain close coordination to ensure effective security arrangements and prevent any untoward incident during the religious observances.
