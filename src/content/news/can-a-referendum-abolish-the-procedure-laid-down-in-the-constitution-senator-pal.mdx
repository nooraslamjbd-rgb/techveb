ISLAMABAD, (UrduPoint / Pakistan Point News - 9th Sep, 2026) [Pakistan](https://www.urdupoint.com/pakistan/) Peoples Party ([PPP](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/ppp.html)) Senator Palwasha Khan asked whether a referendum could abolish the procedure laid down in the Constitution. Senator Palwasha Khan, Minister of State for Law Barrister Aqeel Malik and [PTI](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/pakistan-tehreek-e-insaf.html) leader Shafi Jan participated in a private [tv](https://www.urdupoint.com/tv/) programme. The [PPP](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/ppp.html) senator said referendums had a very [poor](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/poor.html) reputation in the country and asked when political governments had held referendums.

She said that when a procedure was prescribed in the Constitution, could a referendum abolish it? Palwasha Khan said the [PPP](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/ppp.html) had maintained [from](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/from.html) the outset that people should get what they wanted.

She said they had heard that a [Senate](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/senate.html) session was expected [from](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/from.html) 17 [September](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/september.html), adding that discussion on the matter was taking place everywhere except where it should be held. She said the [PPP](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/ppp.html) had not treated the [PTI](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/pakistan-tehreek-e-insaf.html) in the way the [PML-N](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/muslim-league-n.html) had treated it in [Lahore](https://www.urdupoint.com/education/board-of-education-lahore.html).
