FAISALABAD, (APP - UrduPoint / Pakistan Point News - 8th Sep, 2026) Anjuman Insdad-e-Manshiyat (registered) has opened its membership for the 2026-28 term up to September 26 and invited the educated, physically and mentally fit individuals of exemplary character to join the organization and play role for eradication of narcotics use in the society.

According to General Secretary Madam Amina Akram, the interested individuals meeting the prescribed eligibility criteria can apply for membership by the stipulated deadline. She said that the applicants must be physically and mentally fit, educated and possess an exemplary character while illiterate persons would not be eligible to participate in the election process.

She said that after completion of the membership period, nomination [papers](https://www.urdupoint.com/education/past-papers.html) would be available from the office of Anjuman Insdad-e-Manshiyat at Fatima Ward of Allied Hospital-II (DHQ Hospital [Faisalabad](https://www.urdupoint.com/education/board-of-education-faisalabad.html)) on September 28, 2026.

She said that scrutiny of nomination papers, submission of objections and withdrawal of nomination papers would be carried out on September 29.

The final list of candidates would be displayed on September 30 while elections for 2026-28 term would be held on October 02, she added.

She urged eligible and interested individuals to complete the membership process within the stipulated period and participate in the organizational electoral process.
