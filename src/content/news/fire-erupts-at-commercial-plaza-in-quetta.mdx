ISLAMABAD, (APP - UrduPoint / Pakistan Point News - 25th Aug, 2026) A fire that erupted in a commercial plaza on Zarghoon Road was brought under control on Tuesday, with no casualties reported.

According to Director Fire Services, Abdul Haq Achakzai, the fire broke out inside a lawyer's chamber located within the plaza, a private [news](https://www.urdupoint.com/urdu-news) channel reported.

The blaze significantly damaged the lawyer’s chamber and its adjacent sections, Achakzai said.

Following the incident, fire tenders and rescue teams rushed to the spot and extinguished the flames before they could spread to the entire building.

Authorities have launched an investigation to determine the exact cause of the incident.
