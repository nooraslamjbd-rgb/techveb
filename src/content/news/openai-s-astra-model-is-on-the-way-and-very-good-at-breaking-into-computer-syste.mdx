## OpenAI Astra Reaches Critical Cybersecurity Threshold

OpenAI has shared new details about its upcoming frontier model, Astra. According to a report originally covered by TechCrunch, Astra is the company's first large language model to reach its critical cybersecurity threshold.

OpenAI announced that the model will be available soon, though public access to its most potent cyber capabilities will remain strictly limited.

### Autonomous Hacking and Zero-Day Exploits

Internal evaluations reveal that Astra can discover and exploit unknown software flaws without human intervention. In tests, the model achieved a perfect score on ExploitBench, an industry standard for measuring AI hacking skills.

Furthermore, during modified testing conducted by OpenAI engineers, Astra successfully identified and exploited two zero-day vulnerabilities. This autonomous capability places Astra alongside Anthropic's Mythos in terms of cyber risks and capabilities.

### Safety Guardrails and Deployment Strategy

To minimize potential risks, OpenAI is implementing strict safeguards before deployment:

* **Restricted Access:** Advanced offensive features will be gated from general users.
* **Risk Monitoring:** Flagged high-risk accounts will face tighter prompt restrictions.
* **Chain-of-Thought Auditing:** Real-time monitoring will intercept malicious reasoning processes.
* **Harness Upgrades:** Enhanced safety harnesses will detect jailbreak attempts in real time.

While OpenAI claims Astra is its most aligned model to date, third-party security auditors have not yet independently verified these safety assertions.
