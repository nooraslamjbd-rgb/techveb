DUBAI, (UrduPoint / Pakistan Point News / WAM - 27th Aug, 2026) The inaugural edition of “Emirates Loves Palestine” will open on Saturday, 29th [August](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/august.html), at [Dubai](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/dubai.html) Exhibition Centre, Expo City [Dubai](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/dubai.html), in the presence of Sheikh Nahyan bin Mubarak Al Nahyan, Minister of Tolerance and Coexistence. More than 20,000 participants are expected to attend.

The [event](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/event.html) is organised by the “Emirates Loves Palestine” page, in partnership with the UAE-Palestinian Friendship Club and the Palestinian [business](https://www.urdupoint.com/business/prize-bonds.html) Council - [Dubai](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/dubai.html) & Northern Emirates. The [event](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/event.html) forms part of the wider “Emirates Loves” series, which celebrates resident communities in the [UAE](https://www.urdupoint.com/arabic/uae.html) and highlights their cultures, heritage and contributions to society.

The first edition celebrates the Palestinian community in the [UAE](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/uae.html) and aims to deepen social and cultural ties, while showcasing the [bonds](https://www.urdupoint.com/business/prize-bonds.html) of friendship between the Emirati and Palestinian peoples. The [event](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/event.html) will unfold in a community setting that reflects the values of tolerance, coexistence and cultural diversity that define [UAE](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/uae.html) society.

The programme will feature a wide range of artistic and cultural performances highlighting different aspects of Palestinian culture and heritage, including songs, [music](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/music.html), folk arts and comedy shows.

Participating Palestinian artists include Mohammed Assaf and comedian Imad Farajin, alongside several artistic and community figures. The [event](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/event.html) will also include sections dedicated to Palestinian heritage displays, crafts and arts, a platform for homegrown projects by members of the community, and a dedicated area for traditional Palestinian cuisine, offering visitors a broader experience of Palestinian culture.

“Emirates Loves Palestine” reflects the [UAE](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/uae.html)’s approach to promoting tolerance, coexistence and cultural openness, as well as its commitment to providing an inclusive community environment for [all](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/all.html) resident communities, strengthening human connection and bringing cultures closer together.
