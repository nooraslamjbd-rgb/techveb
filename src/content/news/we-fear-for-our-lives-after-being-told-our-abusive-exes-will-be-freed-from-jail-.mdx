Dame Nicole Jacobs, the domestic abuse commissioner for England and Wales, called the scheme "shameful" and said she's had "no assurance that the danger perpetrators pose has been properly considered and assessed with meaningful input from victims".

"Ultimately, we cannot ignore the fact that the early release scheme has caused untold damage to victims' confidence in the justice system," Dame Nicole added.

She said Prime Minister Andy Burnham "must take urgent steps to address this" and called on the probation service to be given "the resources it needs to safely manage more offenders in the community". She also called for additional funding for domestic abuse services to support victims.

A recent report raised serious concerns about electronic tagging, which can be used to monitor offenders' movements in the community. It found almost half of tags were not fitted on time.
