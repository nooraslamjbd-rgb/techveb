DUBAI, (UrduPoint / Pakistan Point News / WAM - 07th Sep, 2026) Dr. Thani bin Ahmed Al Zeyoudi, Minister of Foreign Trade and Chairman of the 15th edition of the AIM Investment Summit 2026, which opened today at the [Dubai](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/dubai.html) [World](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/world.html) Trade Centre, stressed that the [UAE](https://www.urdupoint.com/arabic/uae.html) has established its position as a global [hub](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/hub.html) for trade and investment, underpinned by an open, agile and multi-partnership economy, advanced infrastructure, and a legislative and regulatory environment that supports [business](https://www.urdupoint.com/business/prize-bonds.html), strengthening its ability to turn global transformations into new opportunities for growth.

The summit opened with the participation of leaders, policymakers, institutional investors and innovators, bringing together 25,000 participants [from](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/from.html) 191 countries and organisations and 540 global speakers across 103 keynote, interactive and dialogue sessions.

Al Zeyoudi said the AIM Investment Summit 2026, held under the theme ‘Reshaping Global Prosperity: Opening New Investment Pathways Towards a Sustainable and Inclusive Future’, convenes at a time when the global economy is undergoing rapid transformations that are reshaping the trade and investment landscape, driven by technological development, geopolitical realignments, the acceleration of sustainability efforts and the emergence of new growth centres across regions.

He explained that the [UAE](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/uae.html) prepared early for these transformations and has continued to develop an economic model built on openness and international partnerships, noting that the country’s non-oil foreign trade exceeded AED1.

9 trillion during the first half of 2026.

He added that during 2025, the [UAE](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/uae.html) attracted FDI inflows of $48.3 [billion](https://www.urdupoint.com/business/prize-bonds.html), advancing to ninth place globally among the largest destinations for foreign direct investment, while the cumulative stock of FDI in the country exceeded AED1.17 trillion.

Al Zeyoudi said the next phase will witness rapid shifts in the geography of the global economy, a transition towards technology-led economies and the transformation of sustainability into an investment opportunity.

He underscored the importance of investing in future sectors, foremost among them artificial intelligence, clean energy, climate [technology](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/technology.html), [food](https://www.urdupoint.com/cooking/) and [water](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/water.html) security, and sustainable infrastructure.

He stressed that the [UAE](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/uae.html) offers investors not merely a [market](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/market.html), but a global platform for growth, expansion and [market](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/market.html) access, and emphasised the importance of building long-term partnerships that contribute to the transfer of knowledge and [technology](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/technology.html), create [employment](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/employment.html) opportunities and promote sustainable and inclusive growth.

Al Zeyoudi said the [UAE](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/uae.html) looks forward to continuing its role as a bridge connecting [markets](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/market.html) and economies and opening new pathways for trade, investment and [technology](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/technology.html), stressing that the next phase requires moving [from](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/from.html) talking about the future of investment to “investing in the future”.
