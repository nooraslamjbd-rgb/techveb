## Security Forces Conduct Intelligence-Based Operations

Pakistan security forces successfully neutralized eight terrorists during targeted intelligence-based operations (IBOs) in Balochistan. The military's media wing, Inter-Services Public Relations (ISPR), confirmed the developments in an official press release, as reported by Dawn News.

## Operations Across Key Balochistan Districts

The coordinated raids were carried out across three major districts in the province: Quetta, Nushki, and Khuzdar. Security forces initiated these actions following precise intelligence reports concerning the presence of foreign-sponsored militant elements.

### Operation Details and Recovery

* **Location Coverage:** Targeted sweeps took place simultaneously in Quetta, Nushki, and Khuzdar.
* **Threat Neutralization:** Eight active militants linked to external proxy groups were eliminated.
* **Seizures:** Security forces recovered weapons, ammunition, and operational materials during the raids.

## Official ISPR Statement

According to the ISPR, the operations significantly disrupted foreign-backed terrorist networks attempting to destabilize the region. Security personnel remain committed to eliminating remaining hostile elements and maintaining peace across Balochistan.
