## Senate Committee Takes Action Over Staff Misconduct

According to a report by Dawn News, the Senate Rules of Procedure and Privileges Committee has directed Pakistan International Airlines (PIA) and the Airport Security Force (ASF) to take strict disciplinary measures against employees accused of misbehaving with lawmakers. 

The committee emphasized that uncalled-for conduct by public-facing officials damages the overall credibility of key state institutions.

## Disciplinary Directives Issued to PIA

Chaired by Senator Syed Waqar Mehdi, the panel reviewed a privilege motion submitted by Senator Bilal Ahmed Khan Mandokhail. The motion detailed alleged misbehavior by a PIA cabin crew member on flight PK-326 from Quetta to Islamabad.

Despite earlier recommendations for the official involved to issue an apology, the senator confirmed no contact was made. Consequently, the committee disposed of the motion while directing PIA management to pursue formal departmental action under applicable laws.

## ASF Security Inspection Protocols Questioned

The panel also addressed a separate privilege motion brought by Senator Samina Mumtaz Zehri regarding ASF officials at check-in counters. 

Senator Zehri reported that security personnel conducted a luggage search in an open desk area rather than following standard procedures for private verification of foreign currency. The committee directed ASF authorities to investigate the matter and ensure proper protocol enforcement.
