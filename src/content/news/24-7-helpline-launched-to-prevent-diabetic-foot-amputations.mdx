KARACHI, (APP - UrduPoint / Pakistan Point News - 30th Aug, 2026) A round-the-clock diabetic foot helpline has been launched to provide immediate guidance and referral to millions of people with diabetes developing foot wounds, ulcers and infections.

The helpline, 0330-6666774, was launched by the Baqai Institute of Diabetology and Endocrinology (BIDE) during the three-day International Conference on Diabetes & Diabetic Foot 2026, which concluded in [Karachi](https://www.urdupoint.com/education/board-of-education-karachi.html) on Sunday after bringing together Pakistani and international experts in diabetes and diabetic foot management.
