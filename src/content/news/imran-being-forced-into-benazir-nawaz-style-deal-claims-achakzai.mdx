## Alleged Pressure for Political Compromise

Leader of the Opposition in the National Assembly, Mahmood Khan Achakzai, has claimed that Pakistan Tehreek-e-Insaf (PTI) founder Imran Khan is being coerced into a political agreement. Speaking in Peshawar, Achakzai stated that the proposed deal resembles past compromises offered to former Prime Ministers Nawaz Sharif and Benazir Bhutto.

Achakzai stressed that political stability cannot be achieved through forced agreements, urging authorities to respect democratic principles and judicial independence.

### Opposition Demands Disclosures and Legal Accountability

During his address, Achakzai urged government authorities to immediately disclose the whereabouts of Sohail Afridi. He emphasized that secret detentions and unaccountable state actions undermine public confidence in democratic governance.

### TTAP Alliance Challenges Emergency Measures

Supporting the opposition stance, BNP-Mengal chief Sardar Akhtar Mengal criticized the current administration, asserting that political figures who claim to be framers of the Constitution are actively violating it.

In response to recent administrative actions, the Tehreek Tahafuz-i-Aayin-i-Pakistan (TTAP) alliance has formally challenged governor's rule and emergency declarations before the Peshawar High Court (PHC).
