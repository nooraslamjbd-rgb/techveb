## Sri Lanka Advances to Asian Games Women's Cricket Final

Sri Lanka secured a spot in the Asian Games women's cricket final following a dominant eight-wicket victory over Pakistan in the semi-finals at Korogi Sports Park in Nisshin, Japan.

According to reports from Dawn News, Sri Lanka comfortably chased down a target of 178 runs, finishing the match with 13 balls to spare.

### Athapaththu Leads Sri Lanka to Victory

Sri Lanka captain Chamari Athapaththu anchored the run chase with an explosive unbeaten knock. She scored 83 runs off just 50 deliveries, hitting seven boundaries and six sixes to shatter Pakistan's bowling attack.

Harshitha Samarawickrama provided solid support at the other end, remaining unbeaten on 17 to ensure a smooth finish for her side.

### Pakistan Knocked Out of Gold Medal Race

Led by Fatima Sana, the Pakistan women's cricket team failed to defend their target against Sri Lanka's aggressive batting lineup. The defeat ends Pakistan's campaign for a gold medal at this edition of the Asian Games.

Sri Lanka now moves on to face their final opponent in the championship match, while Pakistan misses out on a podium spot in the women's cricket event.
