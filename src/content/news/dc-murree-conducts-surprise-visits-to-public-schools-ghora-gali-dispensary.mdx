MURREE, (APP - UrduPoint / Pakistan Point News - 25th Aug, 2026) Deputy Commissioner Murree Agha Zaheer Abbas Shirazi paid surprise visits to two public schools and the Num Dispensary in Ghora Gali to inspect public service delivery across the district.

During his [school](https://www.urdupoint.com/education/schools.html) visits, DC Shirazi inspected classrooms, evaluated teaching standards, and met with faculty and students to address operational difficulties.

He directed officers to resolve school infrastructure and administrative issues on a priority basis to ensure a conducive learning environment.

Inspecting the Ghora Gali dispensary, the Deputy Commissioner audited medicine stocks, evaluated patient care, and reviewed overall hygiene protocols inside and around the facility.

Verifying staff attendance registers on site, he issued directives for strict administrative action against absent medical personnel.

He said that upgrading public [education](https://www.urdupoint.com/education/) standards and delivering accessible healthcare remain top priorities for the district administration, emphasizing that administrative negligence will not be tolerated.
