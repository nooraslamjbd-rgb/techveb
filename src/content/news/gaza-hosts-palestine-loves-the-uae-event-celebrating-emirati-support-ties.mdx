GAZA, (UrduPoint / Pakistan Point News / WAM - 30th Aug, 2026) Operation Chivalrous Knight 3 organised the '[Palestine](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/palestine.html) Loves the [UAE](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/uae.html)' [event](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/event.html) in the [Gaza](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/gaza.html) Strip, with broad participation [from](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/from.html) dignitaries and prominent community figures, in an atmosphere combining Palestinian heritage, art, [music](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/music.html) and entertainment.

The [event](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/event.html) expressed feelings of affection and gratitude towards the United [Arab](https://www.urdupoint.com/arabic/) Emirates in appreciation of its continued humanitarian support for the people of [Gaza](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/gaza.html).

The programme included a heritage exhibition and Palestinian bazaar featuring traditional foods, Palestinian dresses and embroidery, handicrafts, tents and a traditional Bedouin majlis, alongside a Palestinian henna corner, giving visitors an opportunity to explore different aspects of Palestinian cultural heritage.

The [event](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/event.html) also featured a range of artistic and heritage performances, including Al-Dihyah and traditional dabke, [music](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/music.html) and children's entertainment, as well as an artistic performance that drew strong audience engagement and created a joyful atmosphere.

Participants expressed their affection and gratitude towards the [UAE](https://www.urdupoint.com/arabic/uae.html) and its people, affirming their appreciation for the country's humanitarian positions and continued support for residents of the [Gaza](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/gaza.html) Strip. Their remarks and participation conveyed messages of loyalty and thanks, highlighting the depth of sentiment between the two peoples.

The [Gaza](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/gaza.html) [event](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/event.html) brought together heritage and humanitarian values, reflecting Palestinians' attachment to their identity and cultural legacy, while providing a platform to express appreciation for the [UAE](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/uae.html)'s continued support for the Palestinian people and the assistance provided to [Gaza](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/gaza.html) residents through Operation Chivalrous Knight 3.

The '[Palestine](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/palestine.html) Loves the [UAE](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/uae.html)' [event](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/event.html) coincided with the 'Emirates Loves [Palestine](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/palestine.html)' [event](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/event.html) held in the [United Arab Emirates](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/united-arab-emirates.html), in a scene reflecting the mutual affection between the Palestinian and Emirati peoples and the depth of the fraternal ties between them.

Together, the two events conveyed a shared message of loyalty, affection and mutual appreciation.
