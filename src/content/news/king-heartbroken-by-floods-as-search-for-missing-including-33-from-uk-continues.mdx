## Royal Family Expresses Deep Sorrow Over Nepal Disaster
King Charles III and Queen Camilla have shared their profound heartbreak following catastrophic flash floods along the Nepal-Tibet border. The Prince and Princess of Wales also voiced their shock, offering thoughts to everyone caught up in the devastating disaster.

## High Casualties and Missing British Tourists
The confirmed death toll has climbed to at least 359, according to Nepali officials. Over 1,300 people remain missing, including 644 international tourists. Among those missing are 33 British citizens, including two teenagers aged 13 and 14.

Key highlights of the ongoing humanitarian crisis include:
* **Rising Casualties:** Over 350 fatalities confirmed alongside widespread structural damage.
* **Missing Foreigners:** Hundreds of tourists, including 33 UK nationals, are unaccounted for.
* **Emergency Relief:** The UK government has pledged £5 million in emergency aid.
* **Secondary Hazards:** Warnings are active for secondary flooding near the Bhote Koshi river.

## Official Response and Rescue Operations
UK authorities and the Foreign Office are working closely with local Nepalese emergency services to locate missing individuals. Officials have urged residents and travelers in the region to remain alert to changing weather conditions as rescue operations proceed.
