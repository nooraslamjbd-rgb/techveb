Jamaat-i-Islami (JI) has officially launched its protest march towards Islamabad, escalating political pressure on the federal government over rising petroleum taxes.

According to a report by Dawn News, party Emir Hafiz Naeemur Rehman addressed a large gathering of supporters at Karachi Cantonment Station on Sunday before departing for the national capital.

## Key Demands and Political Warnings

The central issue driving the protest is the government's petroleum levy, which JI demands must be completely removed to provide economic relief to the public.

During his address, Hafiz Naeemur Rehman issued a clear ultimatum:
* Immediate withdrawal of the heavy petroleum levy on fuel products.
* Warning that failure to comply could turn the movement into a campaign to oust the sitting government.
* Mass mobilization along key transit routes leading to Islamabad.

### Inflation and Economic Strain

Fuel prices in Pakistan have triggered severe economic strain due to compounding inflation and state taxation. While the petroleum levy serves as a key revenue mechanism for the government, citizens and opposition parties view it as an unfair burden on working families.

## What Comes Next?

As the convoy moves toward Islamabad, major transit routes may see increased political activity and administrative security checks. The government's decision to either engage in talks or enforce strict security measures will determine the outcome of the standoff in the coming days.
