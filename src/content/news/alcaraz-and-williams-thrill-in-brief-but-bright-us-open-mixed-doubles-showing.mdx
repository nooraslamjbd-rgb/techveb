## Star-Studded Duo Lights Up Arthur Ashe Stadium

Carlos Alcaraz and Serena Williams teamed up for a captivating mixed doubles appearance at the US Open. Playing in front of a packed Arthur Ashe Stadium with approximately 15,000 fans, the blockbuster pair thrilled the crowd despite a brief run.

Alcaraz, returning from a wrist injury that kept him out since April, expressed sheer joy at sharing the court with the 23-time Grand Slam champion. Williams returned to her home major for the first time since stepping away from tennis in 2022.

### Quarter-Final Exit in Revamped Tournament

The marquee duo secured an impressive opening victory against Great Britain's Lloyd Glasspool and New Zealand's Erin Routliffe (5-3, 4-1). However, their run ended in the quarter-finals after a tight defeat to Italy's Flavio Cobolli and Switzerland's Belinda Bencic, who won 5-4 (7-4), 4-1.

### Format Controversy and Singles Dominance

According to BBC News, the tournament's revamped mixed doubles format sparked debate for prioritizing top singles stars at the expense of established doubles experts. Interestingly, all four specialist doubles pairings were defeated by teams of singles players during the opening rounds.

Despite the early exit, the Alcaraz-Williams partnership provided unforgettable highlights, showcasing high-energy tennis and mutual admiration on court.
