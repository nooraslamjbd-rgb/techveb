CAPITALS, (UrduPoint / Pakistan Point News / WAM - 07th Sep, 2026) [Gold](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/gold.html) [prices](https://www.urdupoint.com/business/prize-bonds.html) nudged lower on Monday.

Spot [gold](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/gold.html) was down 0.5 percent at $4,405.47 per ounce, as of 02:11 GMT, after falling 1 percent on Friday.

US [gold](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/gold.html) futures for [December](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/december.html) delivery were down 0.5 percent at $4,452.20.

Among other metals, spot [silver](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/silver.html) eased 0.2 percent at $66.03 per ounce, platinum lost 0.8 percent at $1,805.53 and palladium declined 0.7 percent to $1,396.08.
