ISLAMABAD, (APP - UrduPoint / Pakistan Point News - 28th Aug, 2026) The [prices](https://www.urdupoint.com/business/prize-bonds.html) of gold remained unchanged in the local market on Friday, with the [price](https://www.urdupoint.com/business/prize-bonds.html) of 24-karat gold per tola holding steady at Rs483,036, according to rates issued by the All [Pakistan](https://www.urdupoint.com/pakistan/) Sarafa Gems and Jewellers Association.

Similarly, the price of 10 grams of 24-karat gold remained unchanged at Rs414,125, whereas 10 grams of 22-karat gold also stayed unchanged at Rs379,628.

In the international market, the price of gold remained unchanged at $4,605 per ounce, the association reported.

Meanwhile, the price of silver per tola remained unchanged at Rs7,509, while the price of 10 grams of silver also stayed unchanged at Rs6,437.

The price of silver in the international market stood unchanged at $70.30 per ounce, the association added.
