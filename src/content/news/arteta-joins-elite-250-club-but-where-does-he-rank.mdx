## Mikel Arteta Reaches 250 Premier League Games with Arsenal

Arsenal manager Mikel Arteta celebrated his 250th Premier League match in charge with a 1-0 victory over Aston Villa at Villa Park. Bukayo Saka secured the win with a second-half goal, extending the Gunners' strong start to the season.

According to BBC News, Arteta is now the 10th manager in Premier League history to reach 250 top-flight matches with a single club. This achievement places him alongside iconic leaders such as Arsene Wenger, Sir Alex Ferguson, and Pep Guardiola.

## Where Arteta Ranks Among Premier League Legends

Reaching 250 matches is impressive, but Arteta's statistical performance is even more notable:

* **Fourth-Best Win Percentage**: Only Pep Guardiola, Sir Alex Ferguson, and Jurgen Klopp hold higher win percentages among managers reaching 250 games with one club.
* **Longest-Serving Current Manager**: Arteta currently stands as the longest-serving manager active in the Premier League.

His tactical discipline and attention to detail have established a clear tactical identity at Emirates Stadium, transforming the Gunners back into title contenders.

## Defensive Solidity Defines Arsenal's Success

Arsenal's victory over Aston Villa showcased their defining trademark under Arteta: defensive excellence. The team conceded zero shots on target during the match, maintaining a record of allowing only three shots on target across their opening two games.

With key rivals undergoing managerial and squad transitions, Arsenal's defensive discipline makes them strong contenders to sustain another championship push this season.
