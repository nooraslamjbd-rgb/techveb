KARACHI, (APP - UrduPoint / Pakistan Point News - 8th Sep, 2026) The Sindh Chief Minister Syed Murad Ali Shah has felicitated the nation on [Pakistan](https://www.urdupoint.com/pakistan/) Navy Day, paying tribute to the Pakistan Navy for its outstanding performance and sacrifices during the 1965 war.

He said that September 8 reminded us of the remarkable performance and sacrifices of the Pakistan Navy in 1965 war.

The Chief Minister in his message said that Pakistan Navy's performance was a historic example of the Pakistan Navy’s courage, professional expertise and excellent strategic planning.

He said, “During the 1965 war, the Pakistan Navy’s 25th Destroyer Squadron successfully carried out an operation against the enemy.”

The Chief Minister paid rich tribute to the martyrs and veterans of the Pakistan Navy, saying that the naval personnel who sacrificed their lives in defence of the motherland were a source of pride for the entire nation.

He said the role of the Pakistan Navy in defending the country’s maritime boundaries was unforgettable.

“Pakistan’s coastline is of immense importance to the country’s defence, trade and economy,” he added.

The Chief Minister Murad Ali Shah said that Sindh’s extensive coastline was an important component of Pakistan’s maritime identity and economy.

The entire coastal belt of Sindh, from [Karachi](https://www.urdupoint.com/education/board-of-education-karachi.html) to Keti Bandar, was a vital national economic asset, he said.

He stressed that safeguarding national interests along the Sindh coast and in the Arabian Sea was a national responsibility.

Murad said that our Navy was playing an important role in protecting all coastal areas of Pakistan.

He said that Navy Day served as a reminder of the nation’s commitment to protecting its maritime heritage and coastal boundaries.

He also paid tribute to the services of Pakistan Navy officers, personnel and their families.
