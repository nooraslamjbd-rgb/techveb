QUETTA, (APP - UrduPoint / Pakistan Point News - 23rd Aug, 2026) Chief Minister Balochistan Mir Sarfraz Bugti has announced that a major road in Duki will be named after Lady Doctor Mahnoor Nasar in acknowledgment of her courage and dedication.

On the directives of the Chief Minister, the Local Government and Rural Development Department issued a formal notification to dedicate the road to Dr. Mahnoor Nasar.

Speaking on the occasion here on Sunday, CM Bugti praised Dr. Mahnoor Nasar’s bravery and sense of duty, describing her as a proud example for the daughters of Balochistan.

He emphasized that recognizing individuals who serve humanity with courage, determination, and compassion is the government’s responsibility.

Sarfraz Bugti noted that Dr. Mahnoor Nasar’s resilience in difficult circumstances was commendable, and naming the road after her was a tribute to her services and valor. He added that such acts of recognition inspired society and highlight the importance of honoring those who stand firm in the face of adversity.
