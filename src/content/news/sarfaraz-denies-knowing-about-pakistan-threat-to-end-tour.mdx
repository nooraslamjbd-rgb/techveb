## Pakistan Coach Sarfaraz Ahmed Addresses Tour Dispute

Pakistan head coach Sarfaraz Ahmed stated that neither he nor his squad had any knowledge of the Pakistan Cricket Board (PCB) threatening to halt their ongoing England tour. The controversy arose during the second Test at Lord's following a broadcast decision by Sky Sports.

Speaking at the end of day two, Sarfaraz clarified that the team remained entirely focused on cricket while officials dealt with political tension off the field.

### Sky Sports Interview Sparks Tension

The dispute began when Sky Sports broadcast an interview with Suleiman and Kasim Khan, the sons of former Pakistan prime minister Imran Khan. In the segment, conducted by former England captain Michael Atherton, Khan's sons voiced concerns regarding their father's imprisonment and safety in Pakistan.

The PCB reportedly warned the England and Wales Cricket Board (ECB) that it might pull out of the Lord's Test and cancel the remaining tour matches if the footage aired. Following advice from the UK Foreign, Commonwealth and Development Office, the ECB permitted play to proceed as scheduled.

### Impact on Players and Media Coverage

According to a report by BBC News, players returned to the field without delay after the lunch interval on day one. Sarfaraz reassured reporters that team morale was intact and the squad was unaware of any cancellation risk while having lunch.

* **Broadcast Censorship:** Public broadcaster PTV omitted the interview segment during its Pakistani broadcast.
* **Official Stance:** Neither the PCB nor the ECB issued formal statements stopping the ongoing series.

The tour continues with players maintaining focus on the pitch despite political distractions off the field.
