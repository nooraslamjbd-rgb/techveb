ISLAMABAD, (APP - UrduPoint / Pakistan Point News - 30th Aug, 2026) Chief Traffic Officer (CTO) Islamabad Kainat Azhar Khan inspected different Police Khidmat Centres and field duty points, directing officers to ensure timely and transparent public services while strengthening traffic enforcement and keeping traffic moving smoothly across the [Federal](https://www.urdupoint.com/education/board-of-education-federal.html) capital.

An Islamabad Traffic Police (ITP) official told APP on Sunday that the CTO Kainat reviewed various services being provided to citizens at the Police Khidmat Centres and checked arrangements made for their facilitation.

She directed the staff to ensure that citizens received timely, transparent and quality services without unnecessary inconvenience.

During her field visits, Kainat also checked the performance of officers deployed on traffic duties and reviewed measures being taken to maintain smooth traffic flow.

She directed field officers to take effective steps to facilitate road users while ensuring uninterrupted movement of traffic.

The CTO Kainat also ordered stronger enforcement of traffic laws and more effective action to prevent violations on the capital's roads.

She stressed that public service and citizens' facilitation remained a top priority of Islamabad Traffic Police, directing field officers to perform their duties responsibly and professionally while maintaining a public-friendly attitude.

Kainat warned that no negligence in providing traffic-related services to citizens would be tolerated and directed officers to maintain high standards both at facilitation centres and in the field.
