## Range Rover Enters the All-Electric Era

Jaguar Land Rover (JLR) has officially unveiled its first fully electric Range Rover, marking a major milestone in the luxury automaker's electrification strategy. The debut comes after a one-year launch delay, supported by extensive infrastructure investments across its West Midlands manufacturing hubs.

## Workforce Transformation and Production

To facilitate the transition to electric production, JLR upskilled 10,500 employees across its facilities. Vehicle assembly is centered in Solihull, where approximately 9,000 staff members underwent specialized EV training.

### Key Manufacturing Upgrades:
- **Solihull Assembly Plant:** Primary site for the new fully electric flagship SUV.
- **Wolverhampton Propulsion Centre:** Manufactures battery packs and electric drive units alongside legacy engines.
- **Regional Upskilling:** Trained 1,500 additional staff members to reinforce the electric supply chain.

## Navigating Financial and Operational Challenges

As reported by BBC News, the launch arrives during a turbulent financial period for the automaker. JLR recently encountered supply chain bottlenecks, recovery from a major cyber attack, and production delays following a fire at a key Norwegian supplier.

Despite first-quarter profits falling to £66 million and planned restructuring affecting under 300 roles, company leadership remains confident. Range Rover Managing Director Martin Limpert stated the vehicle represents a decade of "considered engineering" aimed at securing the brand's long-term future.
