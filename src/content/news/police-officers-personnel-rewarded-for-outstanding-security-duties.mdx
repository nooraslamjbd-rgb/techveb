DERA ISMAIL KHAN, (APP - UrduPoint / Pakistan Point News - 28th Aug, 2026) The police officers and personnel who demonstrated exceptional performance, discipline and professional commitment during their security duties have been awarded commendation certificates and cash prizes.

The awards were distributed by SP Security, Dera Region, Murad Ali Khan, on the special directives of DIG Security Sajjad Ahmed Khan, in recognition of the officials’ dedication and effective performance while performing their duties.

Speaking on the occasion, Murad Ali Khan appreciated the officers and personnel for their sense of responsibility, courage and vigilance, saying their commitment was particularly commendable in the prevailing security situation.

He said that the personnel who performed their duties with distinction were an asset to the police department and that recognition of their efforts would help boost morale, dedication and professional standards within the force.

The SP said the process of recognizing and encouraging officers and personnel showing exceptional performance would continue in future.
