DUBAI, (UrduPoint / Pakistan Point News / WAM - 09th Sep, 2026) His Highness Sheikh Mohammed bin [Rashid](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/rashid.html) Al Maktoum, Vice President, [Prime Minister](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/prime-minister.html) and Ruler of [Dubai](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/dubai.html), met with his teams and a number of senior officials as part of his annual ‘New [Government](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/government.html) Season Meeting 2026- 2027,’ which reviewed the priorities of [government](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/government.html) work along with the key directions and projects planned for the coming phase.

The meeting was held in the presence of H.H. Sheikh Hamdan bin Mohammed bin [Rashid](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/rashid.html) Al Maktoum, Crown Prince of [Dubai](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/dubai.html), Deputy [Prime Minister](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/prime-minister.html) and Minister of Defence; and H.H. Sheikh Ahmed bin Mohammed bin [Rashid](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/rashid.html) Al Maktoum, Second Deputy Ruler of [Dubai](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/dubai.html).

His Highness Sheikh Mohammed bin [Rashid](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/rashid.html) Al Maktoum commended the efforts of his team and the achievements realised over the past period, noting that this work had helped raise the efficiency of [government](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/government.html) operations, speed up the delivery of national priorities, advance services, and support the [UAE](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/uae.html)'s broader development journey.

His Highness directed [government](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/government.html) teams to redouble their efforts throughout the new [government](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/government.html) season, accelerate the delivery of projects and priorities, deepen integration between entities and teams, and continue building work models that are more efficient, agile and forward looking, with a clear focus on [results](https://www.urdupoint.com/education/results.html) and tangible impact for the community.

His Highness Sheikh Mohammed bin [Rashid](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/rashid.html) Al Maktoum said, "With the start of every [government](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/government.html) season, I begin my work by meeting my team. We set out on a new season, carrying renewed energy, strong resolve and ambitions that match our country."

His Highness Sheikh Mohammed bin [Rashid](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/rashid.html) Al Maktoum added: "My message to my team has always been the [same](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/same.html). I want the [UAE](https://www.urdupoint.com/arabic/uae.html) [government](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/government.html) to be the [best](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/best.html) [government](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/government.html) in the [world](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/world.html).”

The meeting also looked back at the key accomplishments of the previous [government](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/government.html) season, alongside upcoming priorities, initiatives and projects across the developmental, social and economic sectors, as well as plans to speed up delivery and strengthen the readiness of [government](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/government.html) entities to adapt to change and carry forward national projects.

Discussions further touched on plans to develop the [Federal](https://www.urdupoint.com/education/board-of-education-federal.html) [government](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/government.html) work system, strengthen coordination across entities, and invest in national talent and emerging technologies, [all](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/all.html) in support of the [UAE](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/uae.html)'s competitiveness and its leading position in advancing the practice of [government](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/government.html) work.

The meeting was also attended by Mohammad bin Abdullah Al Gergawi, Minister of [Cabinet](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/cabinet.html) Affairs, as well as a number of ministers and senior officials.
