## South Africa Secures 2-0 Series Lead Over Namibia

South Africa delivered a dominant all-round performance against Namibia in their recent One Day International (ODI) clash, extending their series lead to 2-0. As reported by ESPN Cricinfo, powerful batting paired with disciplined bowling sealed a massive win for the South African side.

### Esterhuizen Shines with Maiden ODI Century

The highlight of South Africa's innings was a standout performance by Esterhuizen, who registered his maiden ODI century. His brilliant knock anchored the team to a massive total, setting Namibia a target of 336 runs.

Tony de Zorzi provided crucial support at the top order, scoring 79 runs to keep the scoreboard moving rapidly. Their combined efforts placed South Africa in a commanding position early in the match.

### Dominant Bowling Performance Secures Victory

Chasing 336 was always going to be an uphill battle for Namibia, and South Africa's bowlers ensured the opposition never got comfortable:

- **Duan Jansen**: Led the bowling attack with precision, taking a brilliant four-wicket haul.
- **Eathan Bosch**: Kept the pressure mounting by taking three vital wickets.

### Historical Setback for Namibia

Namibia's batting order collapsed under the relentless pressure, leading to their fourth-biggest defeat in ODI history. South Africa's authoritative victory highlights their formidable form across both batting and bowling departments.
