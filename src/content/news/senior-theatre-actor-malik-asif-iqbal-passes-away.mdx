LAHORE, (UrduPoint / Pakistan Point News - 19th Sep, 2026) Senior and renowned [Pakistani](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/pakistan.html) theatre actor Malik Asif Iqbal has passed away after suffering a heart attack. [news](https://www.urdupoint.com/urdu-news) of the sudden demise of another actor known for making audiences laugh and bringing smiles to their faces has caused deep grief and shock in [showbiz](https://www.urdupoint.com/showbiz/) circles, particularly the theatre community. According to family sources, Malik Asif Iqbal suffered a sudden and severe heart attack that proved fatal.

Details of his funeral prayers and burial will be announced soon. It was reported that he had been associated with [Pakistan](https://www.urdupoint.com/pakistan/)’s theatre [industry](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/industry.html) for a long time.

Through his distinctive acting style, dialogue delivery and extempore lines, he earned a special place in the hearts of drama audiences.

During his lengthy career, he performed memorable and lively roles in numerous stage plays. He was known for spontaneity and sharp humour in his performances, which made him extremely popular among theatre-goers.

Fellow stage performers, fans and prominent showbiz personalities have expressed deep sorrow over the senior actor’s sudden death. Artists and theatre performers paid rich tribute to the deceased, saying that Malik Asif Iqbal’s absence could never be filled and that his services to the theatre industry would always be remembered.
