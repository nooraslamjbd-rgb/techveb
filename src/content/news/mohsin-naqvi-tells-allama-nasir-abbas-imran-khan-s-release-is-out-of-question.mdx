The establishment believes PTI’s protest should not be postponed, as it may otherwise be rescheduled for November or December, journalist Muneeb Farooq reports.

LAHORE, (UrduPoint / Pakistan Point News - 19th Sep, 2026) Journalist Muneeb Farooq said [Interior Minister](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/interior-minister.html) Mohsin Naqvi told Allama Nasir Abbas during a meeting that Imran Khan’s release was “out of question.” In his vlog, he said Mohsin Naqvi stated that Imran Khan’s release had never been part of the long march’s agenda.

He said they had offered the best possible option, meetings between Imran Khan and his sisters had begun, and a meeting between [Bushra Bibi](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/bushra-bibi.html) and his family was also taking place. The issue of Imran Khan’s treatment at a hospital was also expected to be resolved.

Allama Nasir asked for a meeting with Imran Khan, but Mohsin Naqvi said that such a meeting could not take place.

> 

Mohsin Naqvi also refused to arrange the meeting.

During the discussion, he said they had stated that relief would also be provided to “Bibi” and that she would be shifted to Bani Gala. He said all of this would happen through the courts, while the government would act as a facilitator.

He added that when the [prime minister](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/prime-minister.html) came, the [Election Commission](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/election-commission.html) issue would also be discussed. Muneeb Farooq reported that Mohsin Naqvi and the establishment believe PTI’s protest should not be postponed.

According to this thinking, if it is postponed now, they will say they intend to come in November or December; therefore, they should be allowed to come so that there can be peace for two years, as happened when they came in 2024. In his vlog, journalist Muneeb Farooq said some people were discussing the matter and several politicians were also posting on [social media](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/social-media.html).

He said that, on one hand, a sit-in protest was approaching, while on the other, Mohsin Naqvi’s speech was being discussed and the government’s shortcomings were being highlighted. Claims that the government was about to leave were now becoming part of media discussions. He said clearly that removing the government would not require a media trial or any special effort.

Once the government’s departure was decided, even a stroke of the pen would not be needed. When those in power wished, the government would fall easily, he said, adding that no one would be affected and any alternative system would emerge accordingly. Farooq said discussions were taking place in media gatherings about what was happening, while influential figures were looking for answers to questions.

Journalists were saying that the government’s work was over, but he reported that this was not the case. When its tenure was actually to end, no media trial would be needed; the government would come to an end on its own.
