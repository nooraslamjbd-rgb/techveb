(UrduPoint / Pakistan Point News / WAM - 31st Aug, 2026) [SINGAPORE](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/singapore.html), 31st [August](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/august.html), 2026 (WAM) [Oil](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/oil.html) [prices](https://www.urdupoint.com/business/prize-bonds.html) rose more than 2% in early trading on Monday.

Brent crude futures rose $2.22, or 2.52 %, to $90.32 a barrel, while US West Texas Intermediate (WTI) crude stood at $85.41 a barrel, up $2.01, or 2.41%.

Oil prices remain supported by concerns over [middle](https://www.urdupoint.com/education/middle-result.html) East supply disruptions and uncertainty surrounding crude flows through the Strait of Hormuz.
