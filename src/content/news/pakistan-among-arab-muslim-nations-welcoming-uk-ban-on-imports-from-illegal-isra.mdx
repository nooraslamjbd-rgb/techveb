## Pakistan and Muslim Nations Endorse UK Trade Ban

Pakistan, alongside seven Arab and Muslim nations, has officially welcomed the United Kingdom's decision to ban imports originating from illegal Israeli settlements in the occupied West Bank.

The diplomatic endorsement was issued through a joint foreign ministry statement following trade measures implemented by the UK, Canada, and France.

### International Reaction and Legal Compliance

British Foreign Secretary Ed Miliband announced the trade restriction, criticizing the Israeli government for turning a blind eye to actions in Palestinian territories.

The joint declaration included Pakistan, Saudi Arabia, Turkiye, Egypt, Indonesia, Jordan, and the UAE. According to reports originally published by Dawn News, these nations highlighted that the policy aligns with international frameworks, including:

* **UN Security Council Resolution 2334 (2016)**
* **International Court of Justice (ICJ) July 2024 Advisory Opinion**

## Growing European Support for Settlement Restrictions

In addition to the UK's decisive trade action, foreign ministers from 12 European countries—including Denmark, Finland, Ireland, Spain, and Sweden—expressed intentions to implement similar import restrictions.

The international coalition emphasized that unchecked settlement expansion severely undermines the viability of a peaceful two-state solution. The diplomatic bloc urged global powers to adopt concrete measures to hold settlement organizations accountable under international law.
