## Saud Shakeel Cites Unforgiving English Conditions After Edgbaston Collapse

Pakistan suffered another major batting collapse on the opening day of the third Test against England at Edgbaston. The visitors were bowled out for a disappointing 133 in just 33.5 overs, marking their fifth consecutive innings failing to cross the 200-run threshold.

Speaking after the match, middle-order batter Saud Shakeel highlighted the severe lateral movement and overcast weather as primary factors behind the team's struggles, as reported by Dawn News.

### Early Damage and Unfavorable Weather

England took full advantage after opting to bowl under heavy cloud cover. Pakistan's top order crumbled rapidly, falling to 26-4 within the first 10 overs. Top-order batters including Saim Ayub, Azan Awais, Abdullah Shafique, and captain Shan Masood were dismissed cheaply before Babar Azam fell for seven.

Saud Shakeel, who top-scored with a fighting 43, admitted that conditions were vastly different from previous series in England:

* **Excessive Movement:** Significant seaming and lateral movement tested the batters early on.
* **Early Pitch Moisture:** Damp morning conditions heavily favored England's fast bowling attack.
* **Adaptability Challenges:** Struggled to navigate tough early overs before conditions settled.

### England Seizes Control on Day 1

Despite a brief fightback from Pakistan's bowlers, England closed Day 1 on 156-4, establishing a 23-run lead. Shakeel acknowledged that while batters must take responsibility, surviving the opening session remains the biggest challenge in English conditions.
