ISLAMABAD/RIYADH, (UrduPoint / Pakistan Point News - 12th Sep, 2026) [Pakistan](https://www.urdupoint.com/pakistan/) has strongly condemned [drone](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/drone.html) attacks on [Saudi Arabia](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/saudi-arabia.html)’s East-West Pipeline. The [Foreign Office](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/foreign-office.html) spokesperson said the pipeline in the [Riyadh](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/riyadh.html) and [Medina](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/medina.html) regions was damaged in the [drone](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/drone.html) attacks, which also [injured](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/injured.html) civilians.

Targeting civilian infrastructure in [Saudi Arabia](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/saudi-arabia.html) is a violation of international law, the spokesperson said. In a statement, the [Foreign Office](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/foreign-office.html) spokesperson said the [drone](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/drone.html) attacks were also an open violation of [Saudi Arabia](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/saudi-arabia.html)’s sovereignty and territorial integrity and posed a serious threat to peace, security and stability in the region.

Pakistan fully supports [Saudi Arabia](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/saudi-arabia.html)’s sovereignty, territorial integrity, security and prosperity and reiterates its complete solidarity with the [Saudi](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/saudi.html) leadership, [government](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/government.html) and brotherly people, the spokesperson said.

Pakistan will continue sincere efforts for lasting peace and stability in the region. Meanwhile, Turkish Foreign Minister Hakan Fidan telephoned [Saudi](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/saudi.html) Foreign Minister Prince Faisal bin Farhan at a time when the East-West [Oil](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/oil.html) Pipeline had been targeted by attacks originating [from](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/from.html) [Iraq](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/iraq.html), while [Saudi Arabia](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/saudi-arabia.html) was also facing attacks by the Houthis.

During the telephone conversation, the two sides reviewed cooperation to promote regional stability, joint efforts to reduce regional tensions and efforts to address the ongoing tensions in the region.
