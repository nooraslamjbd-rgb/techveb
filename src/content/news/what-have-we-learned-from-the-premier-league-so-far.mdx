Brighton's 3-0 win over Arsenal could have a big impact on both teams' seasons

ByEmlyn Begley

BBC Sport journalist

**The Premier League takes a near three-week break as we enter a four-game international window.**

So, what have we learned from the opening five matches of the season?

BBC Sport takes a look.

## Arsenal are beatable
Highlights: Brilliant Brighton batter Arsenal in shock first defeat as champions

Premier League champions [Arsenal](https://www.bbc.co.uk/sport/football/teams/arsenal) were looking fairly unstoppable in the opening weeks of the season.

A win over [Brighton](https://www.bbc.co.uk/sport/football/teams/brighton-and-hove-albion) on Saturday would have given the Gunners a club-record equalling eighth consecutive win in all competitions to start the season.

But that all ended with a 3-0 loss at Amex Stadium on Saturday.

The Gunners had not lost a league game by more than one goal, let alone three, since October 2024.

It was their heaviest defeat in the league since May 2023 when they lost 3-0 to the same opposition.

"That's not going to take away what the team has done and the boys have done in the last six or seven weeks since coming back from the World Cup," said boss Mikel Arteta.

"What we've done, the way we've competed and the amount of games we've won has been remarkable - but this is a big lesson for all of us to understand what it takes to win at this level."

## Maresca hits ground running at Man City
Man City come out on top in eight goal thriller against Sunderland

Many wondered if [Manchester City](https://www.bbc.co.uk/sport/football/teams/manchester-city) would experience a post-Pep Guardiola slump when the legendary boss left after a decade.

Things did not start too well under Enzo Maresca, with the Community Shield defeat by [Arsenal](https://www.bbc.co.uk/sport/football/teams/arsenal).

But they have won all five games in the Premier League - and seven in a row in all competitions - after Sunday's entertaining 5-3 win over [Sunderland](https://www.bbc.co.uk/sport/football/teams/sunderland).

That takes them three points clear of [Arsenal](https://www.bbc.co.uk/sport/football/teams/arsenal).

"In the press conference before the game, I said I expect a very tough game," said Maresca. "Then we also conceded two or three goals but we have learned a lot in these eight weeks and this is good."

## Brighton & Brentford continue to impress
Brentford stay unbeaten as they put three past Chelsea

[Brighton](https://www.bbc.co.uk/sport/football/teams/brighton-and-hove-albion) and [Brentford](https://www.bbc.co.uk/sport/football/teams/brentford), two clubs who have never won a major trophy, have continued to impress this season.

Fabian Hurzeler's [Brighton](https://www.bbc.co.uk/sport/football/teams/brighton-and-hove-albion) enjoyed that 3-0 win over [Arsenal](https://www.bbc.co.uk/sport/football/teams/arsenal) to see them sit third in the table, only two points behind the Gunners.

[Brighton](https://www.bbc.co.uk/sport/football/teams/brighton-and-hove-albion) have won at home against the reigning Premier League champions in each of the last three seasons.

Star player Pascal Gross laughed and said "no" when BBC Sport asked him if Albion were in the title race.

But stranger things have happened...

Meanwhile, just one point below them in the table are Keith Andrews' [Brentford](https://www.bbc.co.uk/sport/football/teams/brentford).

Many tipped the Bees to struggle after the departure of Thomas Frank last summer but if anything they improved under their former set-piece coach Andrews.

And their start to this season - unbeaten after five games and fourth in the table - is showing he is no one-season wonder as a manager.

They go into the break on the back of their most impressive result, a 3-0 home win over [Chelsea](https://www.bbc.co.uk/sport/football/teams/chelsea).

[Leeds](https://www.bbc.co.uk/sport/football/teams/leeds-united), impressing again under boss Daniel Farke, are also on nine points and in a potential Champions League place.

Andoni Iraola's [Liverpool](https://www.bbc.co.uk/sport/football/teams/liverpool) and [Everton](https://www.bbc.co.uk/sport/football/teams/everton) are just below them on goal difference.

## Carrick under pressure?
Cunha's late strike denies Fulham first league win of the season

Seven years apart, United did the same thing twice hoping for different results - appointing a club legend who last managed in Britain in the Championship after a good start as caretaker boss.

In 2019 it was Ole Gunnar Solskjaer and this time it was Michael Carrick.

But this season, after Carrick having a summer to work on his team, the Red Devils have only won once in the league.

Their five points after five league games is their joint fewest at this stage of a Premier League season.

They go into the international break on the back of a 1-1 draw against [Fulham](https://www.bbc.co.uk/sport/football/teams/fulham), and even needed a late deflected Matheus Cunha strike to stop the Cottagers winning their first game all season.

After the Lisandro Martinez own goal that give [Fulham](https://www.bbc.co.uk/sport/football/teams/fulham) the lead, ex-United captain Gary Neville said on Sky Sports: "I've watched [Manchester United](https://www.bbc.co.uk/sport/football/teams/manchester-united) since the age of five and that's the worst 15 minutes I've ever seen.

"Absolutely pathetic. I've never seen such a lack of effort."

United's owners will have almost three weeks to stew on this before they host fellow underachievers [Tottenham](https://www.bbc.co.uk/sport/football/teams/tottenham-hotspur) (more on them soon) on 10 October.
Coventry defeat Forest for first win since Prem return

In 2024 and 2025, the three promoted teams went straight back down - mostly with really low points tallies too.

But that streak came to an end last year when [Sunderland](https://www.bbc.co.uk/sport/football/teams/sunderland) finished seventh to qualify for Europe and [Leeds](https://www.bbc.co.uk/sport/football/teams/leeds-united) came 14th. Only Burnley went down.

And the early weeks of this season indicate we could be in for a similar situation.

[Hull City](https://www.bbc.co.uk/sport/football/teams/hull-city) are eighth after five games, with their 2-1 loss to [Newcastle](https://www.bbc.co.uk/sport/football/teams/newcastle-united) their first defeat.

Ipswich are 11th. And Coventry, after losing the first four games without scoring, beat [Nottingham Forest](https://www.bbc.co.uk/sport/football/teams/nottingham-forest) 1-0 and only sit in the drop zone on goal difference.

## No quick fix at Spurs
Highlights: Villa survive late Spurs scare for first PL win of the season

[Tottenham](https://www.bbc.co.uk/sport/football/teams/tottenham-hotspur) finished 17th the last two seasons, their lowest two finishes since being in the second tier in 1977-78.

But expectations were raised after a summer of big spending - with Spurs splashing out £302m as well as making two high-profile loan signings.

Defence and midfield in particular were overhauled, with new wingers too.

But they are left with one senior striker in Dominic Solanke, who has only scored 12 Premier League goals in over two years at the club.

"I feel they have overpaid for every player they bought this summer - you can't rule them out from being in and around the relegation battle," said TNT pundit and ex-England international Joe Cole.

Saturday's 3-2 loss against [Aston Villa](https://www.bbc.co.uk/sport/football/teams/aston-villa) sees them sit 19th going into the break - despite scoring their first two league goals of the campaign.

"We have to put on the pitch something more than we are putting," said manager Roberto de Zerbi.

"Football is tactical but it is blood, it is fighting, it is first one on the ball. We will suffer until the next game."

## Chelsea are the great entertainers

Many were keen to see what Xabi Alonso could do at [Chelsea](https://www.bbc.co.uk/sport/football/teams/chelsea) this season.

The Spaniard memorably led Bayer Leverkusen to a historic unbeaten German double in 2023-24.

And they made big changes to their squad this summer, with £117m attacking midfielder Morgan Rogers and new first-choice keeper Emiliano Martinez both coming in from [Aston Villa](https://www.bbc.co.uk/sport/football/teams/aston-villa).

There have been 22 goals in their five league games - more coming against them than for them - and they sit 10th.

And that is not even counting the 6-3 win over [Leeds](https://www.bbc.co.uk/sport/football/teams/leeds-united) in the Carabao Cup.

Hugely entertaining for the neutrals - but perhaps not for [Chelsea](https://www.bbc.co.uk/sport/football/teams/chelsea) fans or management.
