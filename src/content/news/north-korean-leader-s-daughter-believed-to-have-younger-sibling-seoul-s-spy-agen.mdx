## Kim Jong Un's Heir Has a Younger Sibling, Intelligence Confirms

South Korea's National Intelligence Service (NIS) revealed new details regarding North Korea's ruling family. According to a recent assessment, Kim Ju Ae, the apparent successor to North Korean leader Kim Jong Un, has a younger sibling whose gender remains unconfirmed.

The intelligence report also addressed long-standing rumors about an older brother. The NIS indicated that while it continues to verify his existence, any older son is likely not in line for supreme leadership.

### Grooming the Next Generation

Kim Ju Ae first stepped into the public spotlight in late 2022 during an intercontinental ballistic missile test. Since then, she has accompanied her father to numerous high-profile political, military, and diplomatic engagements.

North Korean state media has significantly elevated her status, using the Korean title *hyangdo* ("great person of guidance"). This term is historically reserved exclusively for supreme leaders and designated heirs to the Paektu bloodline.

### Key Succession Indicators

- **Primary Successor:** Kim Ju Ae is currently assessed as the main heir.
- **Family Order:** The NIS previously estimated Kim Jong Un had three children.
- **State Media Promotion:** Public appearances signal strong elite endorsement.

As reported by Dawn News, these intelligence updates provide rare insight into Pyongyang's tightly guarded succession plans.
