MURREE, (APP - UrduPoint / Pakistan Point News - 25th Aug, 2026) At least three persons were killed and several others sustained injuries when a passenger bus plunged into a deep ravine in Murree area, on Tuesday, according to Rescue sources quoted by [tv](https://www.urdupoint.com/tv/) channels.

According to initial reports, a bus carrying passengers was passing through Murree area when suddenly, it fell into a deep ravine. As a [result](https://www.urdupoint.com/education/results.html), three persons died on the spot. Several other persons were also injured in the same incident.

Rescue team rushed to the site after receiving information about the incident and shifted the bodies and injured persons to nearby hospital. Authorities have launched an investigation to determine the exact cause of the incident.
