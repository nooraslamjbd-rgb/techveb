ABU DHABI, (UrduPoint / Pakistan Point News / WAM - 29th Aug, 2026) The third edition of the Fatima bint Mubarak [academy](https://www.urdupoint.com/education/academies-in-pakistan.html) [Women](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/women.html)’s Basketball Cup 2026 has begun in [Abu Dhabi](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/abu-dhabi.html), bringing together seven teams [from](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/from.html) the [UAE](https://www.urdupoint.com/arabic/uae.html), [Qatar](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/qatar.html), [Oman](https://www.urdupoint.com/arabic/oman.html), [Kuwait](https://www.urdupoint.com/arabic/kuwait.html), [Jordan](https://www.urdupoint.com/arabic/jordan.html) and [Egypt](https://www.urdupoint.com/arabic/egypt.html).

Held under the patronage of H.H. Sheikha Fatima bint Mubarak, Chairwoman of the General [Women](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/women.html)’s Union, President of the Supreme Council for Motherhood and Childhood, and Supreme Chairwoman of the [Family](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/family.html) Development Foundation, and under the directives of Sheikha Fatima bint Hazza bin Zayed Al Nahyan, Chairwoman of the [board](https://www.urdupoint.com/education/boards-of-education.html) of Directors of the Fatima Bint Mubarak Ladies [Sports](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/sports.html) Academy, the tournament runs [from](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/from.html) 27th to 31st [August](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/august.html).

This year’s edition coincides with Emirati [Women](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/women.html)’s Day and its theme, “We Emerge Stronger and Better”, highlighting the continued [progress](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/progress.html) of Emirati [women](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/women.html) and their growing participation in sport.

The tournament gives female basketball players opportunities to compete, gain experience and develop their skills against teams with different styles of play.

Seven teams are participating: the Fatima bint Mubarak Academy Basketball Team [from](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/from.html) the [UAE](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/uae.html), Al Gharafa [from](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/from.html) [Qatar](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/qatar.html), Salwa Al Sabah and Al-Oyoun [Women](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/women.html)’s [from](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/from.html) [Kuwait](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/kuwait.html), [Salalah](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/salalah.html) [from](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/from.html) [Oman](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/oman.html), Al-Muqawileen Club [from](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/from.html) Jordan, and 6th of [October](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/october.html) [from](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/from.html) [Egypt](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/egypt.html).

The opening day featured three matches. [Kuwait](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/kuwait.html)’s Al-Oyoun [Women](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/women.html)’s defeated Jordan’s Al-Muqawileen Club 70–34, while [Qatar](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/qatar.html)’s Al Gharafa beat [Oman](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/oman.html)’s [Salalah](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/salalah.html) 82–36. [Egypt](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/egypt.html)’s 6th of [October](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/october.html) Club defeated [Kuwait](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/kuwait.html)’s Salwa Al Sabah 87–60.

Dr. Omnia Al Hajri, Vice Chairwoman of the Board of Directors of the Fatima bint Mubarak Ladies [Sports](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/sports.html) Academy, said, “The third edition of this tournament holds particular significance as it coincides with Emirati [Women](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/women.html)’s Day, an occasion on which we reflect on the support Emirati [women](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/women.html) continue to receive and the pivotal role played by H.H. Sheikha Fatima bint Mubarak, Mother of the Nation, in supporting and empowering [women](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/women.html) across various fields.”

She added, “In the [sports](https://www.urdupoint.com/sports/) sector, we continue to create opportunities that enable female athletes to compete and develop their skills. This year’s slogan, ‘We Emerge Stronger and Better’, reflects the ambition of Emirati [women](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/women.html) to continue progressing and achieving more.”

The tournament forms part of the Academy’s efforts to support [women](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/women.html)’s sport by providing more opportunities for female athletes to compete, develop and [exchange](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/exchange.html) experience through regional tournaments.
