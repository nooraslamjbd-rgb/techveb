SUKKUR, (APP - UrduPoint / Pakistan Point News - 31st Aug, 2026) The [Pakistan](https://www.urdupoint.com/pakistan/) Army Selection and Recruitment Centre, Pano Aqil announced induction for Junior Commissioned Officer (Naib Khateeb) and other ranks.

According to handout issued here on Monday, the registration process for the recruitments will remain open from September 1 to November 23.

The statement said that for the post of Junior Commissioned Officer (Naib Khateeb), candidates could obtain the prescribed application form from Army Selection and Recruitment Centres at Gilgit, [Peshawar](https://www.urdupoint.com/education/board-of-education-peshawar.html), D.I. Khan, Muzaffarabad, [Rawalpindi](https://www.urdupoint.com/education/board-of-education-rawalpindi.html), [Hyderabad](https://www.urdupoint.com/education/board-of-education-hyderabad.html), [Lahore](https://www.urdupoint.com/education/board-of-education-lahore.html), [Faisalabad](https://www.urdupoint.com/education/board-of-education-faisalabad.html), [Multan](https://www.urdupoint.com/education/board-of-education-multan.html), [Quetta](https://www.urdupoint.com/education/board-of-education-quetta.html), Khuzdar, Pano Aqil and [Karachi](https://www.urdupoint.com/education/board-of-education-karachi.html) from September 1 to November 23, 2026.

Meanwhile, candidates wishing to apply for the posts of Soldier General Duty, Driver, Soldier Technical, Soldier Clerk, Cook and Sanitary Worker can get themselves registered at their nearest Army Selection and Recruitment Centre or Regimental Centre.

For online registration and further information, candidates have been advised to visit the Pakistan Army's official recruitment website at www.joinpakarmy.gov.pk.
