SANTIAGO, (UrduPoint / Pakistan Point News / WAM - 31st Aug, 2026) Chilean rider Alisa Rios, aboard "Hli Facina Rosa", won the fourth round of the Emirates International Endurance Village 160-km Series during the main [event](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/event.html) held yesterday evening in the suburb of Llaillay, [Chile](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/chile.html), featuring [top](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/top.html) endurance riders across five stages.

The main [event](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/event.html) was held in partnership between the Emirates International Endurance Village, EIEV PULSE, and the Chilean Equestrian Federation under the supervision of the Federation Equestre Internationale (FEI). It coincided with four races ranging between 100 km and 120 km, alongside seven races covering distances between 20 km and 80 km.

Alisa secured the title among seven competing riders, recording a time of 9:01:33 hours.

Nicolas Andres Silva [Ovalle](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/ovalle.html), aboard "Amaranta", won the two-star 120-km international [race](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/race.html) in 6:40:45 hours, whilst Valentina Letelier won the two-star 120-km junior and [young](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/young.html) rider international [race](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/race.html) aboard "CL [Rabat](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/rabat.html)" with a time of 6:40:47 hours.

Pedro Pablo Gomez, aboard "AS Karima", claimed the one-star 100-km international [race](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/race.html) in 5:39:13 hours, while [Sofia](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/sofia.html) Barrientos won the one-star 100-km junior and [young](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/young.html) rider international [race](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/race.html) aboard "SI Vianda" in 5:33:12 hours.

Mohamed Alneyadi, [UAE](https://www.urdupoint.com/arabic/uae.html) Ambassador to [Chile](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/chile.html), and Lara Sawaya, Executive Director of Marketing and Sponsorship at Emirates International Endurance Village, and EIEV PULSE, attended the [race](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/race.html) and crowned the winners alongside several officials.
