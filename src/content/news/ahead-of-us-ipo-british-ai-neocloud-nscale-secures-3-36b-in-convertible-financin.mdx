[Nscale](https://www.nscale.com/), a British neocloud, has secured $3.36 billion in financing ahead of its IPO later this year, the company [announced](https://www.nscale.com/press-releases/pre-ipo-convertible-financing) on Friday. Structured as a convertible note, the massive funding round underscores the staggering capital required to build out AI data centers.

Led by hedge fund Third Point, the new investment includes $2.36 billion available to the company immediately and an additional $1 billion coming from existing investor Nvidia, which Nscale will receive in mid-November. The notes will convert into equity shares once the IPO is completed.

Nscale, which filed its IPO paperwork last week, is expected to be valued at $35 billion on the NYSE, [Financial Times](https://www.ft.com/content/93ba41c5-d777-4733-8738-93a06e8fead1?syn-25a6b1a6=1) reported, and is seeking to raise $3 billion in the offering, according to [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-21/ai-cloud-firm-nscale-is-said-to-seek-up-to-3-billion-in-us-ipo?utm_medium=cpc_search&utm_campaign=NB_ENG_DSAXX_DSAXXXXXXXXXX_EVG_XXXX_XXX_COUSA_EN_EN_X_BLOM_GO_SE_XXX_XXXXXXXXXX&gclsrc=aw.ds&gad_source=1&gad_campaignid=9835680891&gbraid=0AAAAAD9e5yr4ALPfjOcqOp3AX9o0eUgfU&gclid=CjwKCAjwq8PVBhAKEiwA2i3SHYc1lkbMfSdDNJNqYo0Ia3GZpco8tklDAsBdQ0lmq-vYvbLj0MegABoCoqsQAvD_BwE).

Since it was spun out of Australian cryptocurrency mining company Arkon Energy two years ago, Nscale has amassed over $103 billion worth of contracts, according to its [IPO filing](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/2110365/000119312526395475/ck0002110365-20260918.htm).

The company is currently developing several large data center campuses, including in Norway and West Virginia.
