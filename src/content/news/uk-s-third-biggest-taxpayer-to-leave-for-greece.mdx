Billionaire hedge fund manager Chris Rokos, recognized as the UK's third-highest taxpayer, plans to relocate to Greece, according to reports originally broken by Bloomberg and confirmed by BBC News. Rokos intends to open an office in Athens to capitalize on competitive regional tax frameworks.

## Greece's Flat Tax Scheme for High-Net-Worth Individuals

Greece has aggressively positioned itself to attract international capital through generous fiscal incentives. Under current laws, eligible foreigners can access significant benefits:

- A flat annual tax payment of €100,000 (£86,000) on all global foreign-earned income.
- Complete exemption from standard progressive tax structures on overseas revenue.
- Streamlined legal pathways for foreign investment and corporate office setups.

## Economic Impact on the UK Treasury

Rokos contributed £330 million in UK taxes last year, according to The Sunday Times tax list. Dan Neidle, founder of Tax Policy Associates, noted that losing a contributor of this scale severely impacts state revenue—equivalent to funding roughly 4,500 public school teachers.

## Changing Tax Policies and Capital Flight

While UK Treasury officials assert that Britain remains an attractive hub for investment, experts point out that ongoing policy shifts regarding non-domiciled rules create financial unpredictability. Frequent regulatory changes encourage ultra-wealthy individuals to seek tax stability in welcoming international jurisdictions like Greece.
