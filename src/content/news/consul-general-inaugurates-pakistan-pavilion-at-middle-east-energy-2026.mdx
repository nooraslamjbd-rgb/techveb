DUBAI, (APP - UrduPoint / Pakistan Point News - 1st Sep, 2026) Consul General, Hussain Muhammad on Tuesday inaugurated [Pakistan](https://www.urdupoint.com/pakistan/) Pavilion at the 50th edition of [middle](https://www.urdupoint.com/education/middle-result.html) East Energy 2026, being held at Dubai World Trade Centre from September 1–3, 2026.

Middle East Energy is one of the region’s premier energy exhibitions, bringing together more than 35,000 energy professionals and attendees, 1,900 international exhibitors, and participants from over 150 countries.

Organized under the auspices of the Trade Development Authority of Pakistan (TDAP), the Pakistan Pavilion features five (05) Pakistani companies showcasing their products, expertise, and innovative solutions across key energy transition sectors, including power generation, grids and transmission, energy storage and energy management.

Hussain Muhammad expressed pride in Pakistan’s growing presence in the international energy market. He said that the participation of five Pakistani companies under TDAP at this prestigious international exhibition provides an important opportunity to showcase Pakistan’s capabilities in smart energy solutions, renewable technologies, and power infrastructure.

The Consul General noted that the presence of Pakistani products reflects the country’s potential as a competitive player in the global energy sector. He emphasized that participation in such international exhibitions helps Pakistani companies explore new [business](https://www.urdupoint.com/business/prize-bonds.html) opportunities, establish linkages with global partners and further strengthen Pakistan’s footprint in international markets. He also thanked the [UAE](https://www.urdupoint.com/arabic/uae.html) authorities, particularly the Dubai World Trade Centre, for their facilitation and appreciated the efforts of the Commercial Section of the Consulate in making Pakistan’s participation in the exhibition a success.

Assuring the participating companies of the Consulate’s requisite support, the Consul General visited their stalls and interacted with their representatives. The inauguration ceremony was also attended by Ali Zeb Khan, Trade & Investment Counsellor, M. Haroon Chaudhry and Muhammad Saleh, Counsellors, Shabbir Merchant, Chairman, Pakistan Business Council Dubai, representatives of the participating Pakistani companies, and other guests.
