KOHAT, (APP - UrduPoint / Pakistan Point News - 7th Sep, 2026) District Police Officer (DPO) [Kohat](https://www.urdupoint.com/education/board-of-education-kohat.html), SSP Rai Mazhar Iqbal on Monday met with Elite Force personnel to review their operational preparedness and discuss professional matters, appreciating their high morale, courage and unwavering commitment to protecting citizens.

Speaking on the occasion, DPO Rai Mazhar Iqbal paid tribute to the Elite Force personnel for their bravery, resilience and dedication to duty, particularly while performing responsibilities under challenging circumstances. He said the Elite Force remained fully prepared to maintain law and order and protect the lives and property of citizens in every difficult situation.

The DPO said the real strength of the police force rested with its brave and dutiful personnel, who stood on the front lines to ensure public safety and peace, often placing their own lives at risk in the line of duty. He urged them to continue performing their responsibilities with professionalism and commitment.

The Elite Force personnel, on the occasion, assured the DPO of their complete readiness to meet every challenge. They reaffirmed their resolve to discharge their duties with honesty, courage and dedication and make every possible sacrifice for the maintenance of peace and law and order in the district.
