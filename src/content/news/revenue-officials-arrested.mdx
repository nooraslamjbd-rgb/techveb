SARGODHA, (APP - UrduPoint / Pakistan Point News - 29th Aug, 2026) The Anti Corruption Establishment (ACE) on Saturday arrested revenue officers

and two private people in a bribe case in khushab.

The ACE sources said accuse Abid Hussain, service center incharge , Abdul khaliq ,

Girdawar ,Patwari Gohar Hussain with two others deprived a citizen from his property

after making fake documents.

The officials were found guilty in the inquiry who were arrested for further actions.
