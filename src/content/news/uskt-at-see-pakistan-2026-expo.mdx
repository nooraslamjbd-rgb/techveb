SIALKOT, (APP - UrduPoint / Pakistan Point News - 25th Aug, 2026) University of Sialkot (USKT) Vice Chancellor Prof Dr Muhammad Khaleeq-ur-Rehman

participated as a co-host of SEE [Pakistan](https://www.urdupoint.com/pakistan/) 2026 at the [Lahore](https://www.urdupoint.com/education/board-of-education-lahore.html) Expo Centre.

USKT sources said here on Tuesday the SEE Pakistan 2026 emerged as a remarkable

celebration of Pakistan’s innovation, creativity, and entrepreneurial spirit. Organized

through APSUP, the initiative brought together 4,000+ applications, 17 rigorous

rounds, participants from 49 countries, 285 cities, and 647 institutes, culminating

in 100 outstanding start-ups that showcased the immense potential of Pakistani

youth on a global stage.

The USKT was honored to contribute to the prestigious national platform that empowers

young innovators, nurtures entrepreneurial talent, and highlights Pakistan’s growing

presence in the global start-up ecosystem.

The event reflected the passion, creativity, and determination of youth, proving

that Pakistan was ready to lead through innovation and enterprise.
