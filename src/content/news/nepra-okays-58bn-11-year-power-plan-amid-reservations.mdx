## NEPRA Conditionally Approves $58 Billion Energy Roadmap

The National Electric Power Regulatory Authority (NEPRA) has conditionally approved the Integrated System Plan (ISP) 2025, according to a report by Dawn News. The ambitious 11-year strategic document entails an estimated $58 billion investment across Pakistan's power generation and transmission sectors from 2025 to 2035.

Despite the final approval, the 45-page decision highlights deep internal disagreement within the regulator. All three NEPRA authority members, including the chairman, authored over 12 pages of dissenting and advisory notes regarding the framework.

### Governance Concerns and Constitutional Bypass

A primary point of contention centered on bypassing the Council of Common Interests (CCI), the constitutional body responsible for national energy policies. NEPRA members emphasized that technical committees within the power division cannot override or dictate national policy without proper CCI authorization.

### Strategic Project Exclusions

The regulatory approval selectively excluded specific major components from the immediate scope:

- Battery Energy Storage Systems (BESS)
- K-Electric Ltd (KEL) transmission line expansions planned for 2028

Additionally, regulators noted contradictory statements from key administrative bodies, including the Independent System and Market Operator (ISMO) and the Power Planning and Monitoring Company (PPMC), underscoring ongoing governance issues within the sector.
