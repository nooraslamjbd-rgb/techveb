ISLAMABAD, (UrduPoint / Pakistan Point News - 9th Sep, 2026) Anchorperson Muhammad Malik said that [Chief Minister](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/chief-minister.html) [Maryam Nawaz](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/maryam-nawaz-sharif.html) attended her daughter’s graduation ceremony, and the [cost](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/cost.html) of her private aircraft amounted to Rs11.3 [million](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/million.html).

He said on a [television](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/tv.html) programme that Maryam had travelled to [London](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/london.html) on a private aircraft, whose parking fee for just three days was Rs3.5 [million](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/million.html), while fuel costs amounted to Rs7.8 [million](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/million.html), bringing the total [cost](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/cost.html) to Rs11.3 [million](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/million.html).

Similarly, President [Asif Ali Zardari](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/asif-ali-zardari.html) travelled on a private [visit](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/visit.html).

He travelled on a [government](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/government.html) aircraft, whose parking fee amounted to Rs5.4 [million](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/million.html).

It [may](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/may.html) be recalled that President [Asif Ali Zardari](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/asif-ali-zardari.html) has departed [London](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/london.html) for [Pakistan](https://www.urdupoint.com/pakistan/). He stayed in Britain for more than a week. During a conversation with journalists at the [airport](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/airport.html), responding to a question about his health, he said, “By the grace of Allah, I am well.

I had a good time in [London](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/london.html).” [Pakistani](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/pakistan.html) High Commissioner to the [United Kingdom](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/united-kingdom.html) Tepo Usman bid farewell to President [Zardari](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/asif-ali-zardari.html) in Britain. He stayed there for more than a week.
