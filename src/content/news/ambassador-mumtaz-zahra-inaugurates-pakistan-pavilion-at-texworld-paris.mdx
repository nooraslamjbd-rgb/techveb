Pakistan's ambassador to France Mumtaz Zahra Baloch inaugurated the Pakistan Pavilion at the 59th edition of Texworld Paris, where Pakistani exhibitors are showcasing a diverse range of textile and fashion products

ISLAMABAD, (APP - UrduPoint / Pakistan Point News - 31st Aug, 2026) Pakistan's ambassador to France Mumtaz Zahra Baloch inaugurated the [Pakistan](https://www.urdupoint.com/pakistan/) Pavilion at the 59th edition of Texworld Paris, where Pakistani exhibitors are showcasing a diverse range of textile and fashion products.

Welcomed by Frédéric Bougeard, President of Texworld and his team, the ambassador toured the exhibition and engaged with Pakistani exhibitors, reaffirming embassy’s commitment to supporting Pakistani businesses and expanding their footprint in the French and European markets, says a press release received here.

The exhibition continues for next two days, offering valuable opportunities for B2B linkages and new [business](https://www.urdupoint.com/business/prize-bonds.html) partnerships.
