## Ventures Platform Expands Pan-African Reach with $83M Fund

African venture capital firm Ventures Platform has officially closed an oversubscribed $83 million second fund. As reported by TechCrunch, the Pan-African firm is expanding its geographic focus beyond its home market of Nigeria to back early-stage technology founders across the continent.

## Key Focus Sectors and Investment Thesis

Founding partner Kola Aina revealed that Fund II will target sectors addressing essential societal needs. Primary investment areas include:

* **Fintech & Healthcare:** Expanding access to essential financial and medical products.
* **SaaS & Infrastructure:** Software solutions tackling critical regional infrastructure gaps.
* **Artificial Intelligence:** Leveraging AI to alter cost structures and optimize service delivery.

"For us, AI is most interesting when it is not simply a feature, but an enabler of an entirely different cost structure, business model, or market," Aina stated regarding the firm's AI strategy.

## Geographic Mandate and Check Sizes

Building upon its $46 million Fund I raised in 2022, Ventures Platform is widening its geographic reach. Fund II has already backed five startups based in key regional hubs, including Kenya, South Africa, and Egypt.

The firm plans to deploy capital over the next three to four years, writing individual checks up to $3 million for promising early-stage companies.
