LONDON, (UrduPoint / Pakistan Point News / WAM - 31st Aug, 2026) [Manchester United](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/manchester-united.html) got their [Premier League](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/premier-league.html) campaign up and running with a resounding 5–2 victory over [Ipswich](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/ipswich.html) Town at [Old Trafford](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/old-trafford.html) on Matchweek 2.

After trailing early to a Leif Davis strike, United roared back led by captain Bruno Fernandes, who scored a sensational hat-trick and provided an assist.

An own goal by Jacob Greaves and a late effort [from](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/from.html) [Bryan](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/bryan.html) Mbeumo sealed United’s first three points of the season, while Chuba Akpom grabbed a late consolation for [Ipswich](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/ipswich.html).

Chelsea edged out [Brighton](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/brighton.html) 4–3 in a thrilling, high-scoring contest at [Stamford](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/stamford.html) Bridge. The victory moves the Blues to 6 points [from](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/from.html) their opening two matches, while [Brighton](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/brighton.html) remain on 3 points.

Sunderland ground out a narrow 1–0 home victory over Fulham to claim [all](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/all.html) three points in their Matchweek 2 encounter.

Elsewhere in the round, [Leeds](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/leeds.html) United and Brentford shared the spoils in a 1–1 draw.
