BEIJING, (UrduPoint / Pakistan Point News / WAM - 08th Sep, 2026) [China](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/china.html)'s foreign [exchange](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/exchange.html) reserves totalled US$3.4383 trillion at the end of [August](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/august.html) 2026, up US$19.5 [billion](https://www.urdupoint.com/business/prize-bonds.html), or 0.57 percent, [from](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/from.html) the end of [July](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/july.html), official data showed.

The combined effects of [Currency](https://www.urdupoint.com/business/open-market-currency-rates-in-pakistan.html) translation and asset [price](https://www.urdupoint.com/business/prize-bonds.html) changes led to the increase in [China](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/china.html)'s foreign [exchange](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/exchange.html) reserves in [August](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/august.html), the State Administration of Foreign [Exchange](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/exchange.html) said in a statement.

The statement also noted that [China](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/china.html)'s economy remained generally stable and made solid [progress](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/progress.html), with its resilience and vitality supporting the stability of the country's foreign [exchange](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/exchange.html) reserves.
