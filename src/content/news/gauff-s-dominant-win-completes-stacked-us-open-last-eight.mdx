## Coco Gauff Secures Spot in US Open Last Eight

Former champion Coco Gauff delivered a commanding performance at Flushing Meadows, defeating 18-year-old rising star Iva Jovic 6-1, 6-4. The fourth seed demonstrated exceptional composure from the baseline to seal her place in the quarter-finals.

According to BBC News, Gauff's dominant victory completes a historic quarter-final lineup where all top six seeds advanced. This marks the first time since 1998 that the top six women's seeds reached the US Open last eight together, highlighting the current strength in depth across the WTA Tour.

### Key Match Highlights and Ranking Stakes

After an intense physical start with early exchange of breaks, Gauff took total control of the baseline exchanges. The champion capitalized on her grand slam experience to pressure Jovic, who was coming off an exhausting three-hour fourth-round victory.

- **First Set Dominance:** Gauff seized momentum after breaking for 3-1, rolling through the set 6-1.
- **Controlled Second Set:** Consistent baseline play earned Gauff a 6-4 finish to seal the match.
- **World No. 1 Battle:** Gauff could claim the world number one ranking if she wins the tournament and Elena Rybakina exits in the quarter-finals.

### A Star-Studded Women's Quarter-Final Field

The remaining field features five Grand Slam champions, including Gauff, Elena Rybakina, and reigning Olympic champion Zheng Qinwen. Rybakina secured her spot earlier in the day by defeating two-time US Open champion Naomi Osaka.
