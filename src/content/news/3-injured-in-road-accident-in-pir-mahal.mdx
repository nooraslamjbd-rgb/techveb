Pir Mahal, (APP - UrduPoint / Pakistan Point News - 25th Aug, 2026) At least three persons including two students and a driver were injured in a road mishap occurred in Pir Mahal area on Tuesday, according to Rescue sources quoted by T.V channel.

According to initial reports, a van carrying students was on its way when suddenly it turned turtled near Pir Mahal area of Punjab.

As, a [result](https://www.urdupoint.com/education/results.html) two students and a driver sustained injuries in the accident.

Rescue teams rushed to the scene after receiving information about the incident and shifted the injured persons to a nearby hospital for treatment.
