Employees closed the Eiffel Tower on Monday over alleged gender discrimination, ending their strike after an investigation was promised and sparking a fresh political debate in France.

PARIS, (UrduPoint / Pakistan Point News - 8th Sep, 2026) Employees at the Eiffel Tower in the French capital, [Paris](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/paris.html), went on strike to [protest](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/protest.html) the removal of female staff [from](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/from.html) certain areas and their replacement by [male](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/male.html) employees during a private [visit](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/visit.html) by a Hindu religious group. According to a French broadcaster, a delegation of around 100 people [from](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/from.html) the Bochasanwasi Akshar Purushottam Swaminarayan Sanstha visited the Eiffel Tower privately.

In a statement, employees said female workers were instructed to leave their duties and remain out of sight when the delegation arrived. Some [women](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/women.html) were reportedly asked to move to other locations, while [male](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/male.html) employees replaced them in certain areas.

The statement added that female staff were also prevented [from](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/from.html) passing through or crossing certain designated areas while the delegation was present. The employees strongly objected to the instructions, saying that pushing [women](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/women.html) back, replacing them and keeping them out of sight on the basis of gender was unacceptable. The [company](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/company.html) operating the Eiffel Tower confirmed that the Hindu delegation had requested arrangements limiting interaction with [women](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/women.html) during the [visit](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/visit.html).

The [company](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/company.html) acknowledged that such conditions should not have been accepted, saying they were incompatible with its values and the principle of equality between men and [women](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/women.html), and that management was prepared to accept the consequences. Ariel Weil, president of the Eiffel Tower and a Socialist official in [Paris](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/paris.html), told Agence France-Presse (AFP) that restricting the presence of [women](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/women.html) was clearly unacceptable.

He reaffirmed the Eiffel Tower’s commitment to the values of the French Republic and equality between men and [women](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/women.html). Following the controversy, the Hindu religious and social organisation BAPS said it understood the concerns raised by employees and was taking them extremely seriously.

The organisation said the [visit](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/visit.html) had been arranged with the Eiffel Tower management at the start of normal operating hours because of the delegation’s large size, in order to minimise possible disruption to other tourists.

BAPS said that, to its knowledge, no one had been denied entry to the Eiffel Tower.

However, it sincerely apologised if the situation had caused anyone discomfort or inconvenience and said it remained committed to the universal values of mutual respect and service to others. BAPS, formally known as the Bochasanwasi Akshar Purushottam Swaminarayan Sanstha, said it worked to improve society through religious faith, service and global harmony.

The organisation is a registered charitable institution in [India](https://www.urdupoint.com/islam/india-prayers-timings.html) and holds consultative status with the [United Nations](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/united-nations.html) Economic and Social Council. It is involved in various activities around the [world](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/world.html), including humanitarian services and the construction of temples. BAPS also held the religious inauguration ceremony on [Sunday](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/sunday.html) for its first major [temple](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/temple.html) in [France](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/france.html), on the outskirts of [Paris](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/paris.html). Paris Mayor Emmanuel Grégoire said an investigation into the matter would begin as soon as possible.

He said he was aware of the anger among Eiffel Tower employees and considered their indignation justified. France’s Minister for Equality and [Women](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/women.html)’s Rights, Aurore Bergé, also reacted strongly, saying that [women](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/women.html) in [France](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/france.html) are not asked to erase themselves or remain out of sight, and that no belief or religion is above the laws of the Republic. French right-wing presidential candidate Marine Le Pen criticised the incident, saying [France](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/france.html) would never accept any attempt to restrict the presence of [women](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/women.html).

Centrist politician Gabriel Attal also said that in [France](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/france.html), no culture, belief, religion or individual could order [women](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/women.html) where their place in society should be. The Eiffel Tower remained closed throughout Monday because of the [protest](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/protest.html), but an [employee](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/employment.html) representative said it reopened as usual on Tuesday.

According to the Eiffel Tower’s website, the world-famous tourist landmark attracts around 7 [million](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/million.html) visitors each year.
