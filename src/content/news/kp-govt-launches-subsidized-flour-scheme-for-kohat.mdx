PESHAWAR, (APP - UrduPoint / Pakistan Point News - 30th Aug, 2026) Government of Khyber Pakhtunkhwa has launched a Subsidized Flour Scheme in [Kohat](https://www.urdupoint.com/education/board-of-education-kohat.html) district, offering a 20-kilogram bag at a fixed [price](https://www.urdupoint.com/business/prize-bonds.html) of Rs2,520.

In a statement issued here Sunday, the scheme was being implemented through 64 approved dealers across district to provide affordable flour to consumers.

Customers will be required to present their original National Identity Cards and will be allowed to purchase one bag at a time at government set price.

Official banners have been displayed at the outlets of authorized dealers to help residents identify designated sales points.

Residents can report complaints related to weight, quality or price of flour on 0922-9260032, 0336-9261444 and 0336-0977575.

The district administration would monitor to ensure supply of quality flour at prescribed price and to prevent violations of scheme's terms.
