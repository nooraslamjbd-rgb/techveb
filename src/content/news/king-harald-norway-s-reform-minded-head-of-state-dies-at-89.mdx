## King Harald V Passes Away at Age 89

Norway’s reform-minded monarch, King Harald V, passed away peacefully at Oslo University Hospital at 6:35 am on Friday, August 28. He was 89 years old and served as Norway's head of state for over three decades.

The royal palace announced the news with deep sadness, lowering the official flag at the Oslo palace to half-mast. Europe's oldest reigning monarch had been hospitalized since August 17 due to haemolytic anaemia, a condition involving abnormal destruction of red blood cells.

## Crown Prince Haakon Ascends the Throne

Following royal succession protocols, King Harald’s 53-year-old son, Crown Prince Haakon, automatically succeeds his father as the King of Norway. 

Members of the royal family, including Queen Sonja, Crown Prince Haakon, and Princess Mette-Marit, were present at the hospital bedside during the King's final moments.

## Global Leaders Express Condolences

International leaders extended heartfelt sympathies to the people of Norway and the royal family:

* **Prime Minister Shehbaz Sharif** offered condolences on behalf of Pakistan, praising the late King's lifetime of dedicated public service.
* **President Asif Ali Zardari** paid tribute to King Harald's efforts in strengthening bilateral relations between Pakistan and Norway.
