FAISALABAD, (APP - UrduPoint / Pakistan Point News - 1st Sep, 2026) District Sports Department has arranged Chief Minister (CM) Sports Gala at Al-Fatah Sports Complex in connection with celebration of [Pakistan](https://www.urdupoint.com/pakistan/)’s Independence Day.

The event featured competitions in both Under-16 and senior categories.

In Under-16 category, Taha Bin Fahid secured first position while Huzaifa Bin Fahid claimed second and Shahroz finished third position.

In senior category, Ahsan Akhtar took first position while Samad Khan came second and Muhammad Ahmad secured third position.

District Sports Officer [Faisalabad](https://www.urdupoint.com/education/board-of-education-faisalabad.html) Madam Sajida witnessed the gala as chief guest and later she distributed medals and trophies among the winning athletes.

Mr. Tahir Mehmood Mann, Secretary of the District Squash Association; Fahid Ali; Mr. Ashfaq Khan; Aamer [islam](https://www.urdupoint.com/islam/), international badminton player; Sardar Pervaiz Akhtar, international player and Secretary of Divisional Squash Association; were also present on the occasion along with a large number of players and [sports](https://www.urdupoint.com/sports/) enthusiasts.
