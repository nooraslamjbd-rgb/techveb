## Key Takeaways

- Iran President Pezeshkian offered to restore the June interim peace agreement.
- Tehran demands the US honor signed commitments before halting further military actions.
- Escalation followed recent US strikes on Larak Island and Iranian strikes in Jordan.
- Free navigation in the Strait of Hormuz remains conditional on US compliance.
- President Trump indicated recent strikes do not signal full-scale war.

## Tehran Signals Readiness for Diplomacy

Iranian President Masoud Pezeshkian announced that Tehran is prepared to immediately fulfill its obligations under the June interim agreement, provided Washington honors its commitments. Speaking at the Shanghai Cooperation Organisation (SCO) Summit in Bishkek, Pezeshkian emphasized that war serves no nation's interest and advocated for a negotiated solution.

### Escalation Precedes Diplomatic Overtures

The offer comes shortly after direct military exchanges between both nations, marking a severe escalation in regional tensions:

- **US Military Actions:** Targeted strikes conducted against Iran's Larak Island.
- **Iranian Counter-Attacks:** Retaliatory missile strikes launched against two US air bases in Jordan.
- **Strait of Hormuz Impact:** Tehran reiterated that unhindered navigation through the crucial oil waterway depends on US adherence to agreement terms.

## Global Leadership Reactions

Iranian Foreign Minister Abbas Araghchi reinforced Pezeshkian's statements, urging Washington to align with previously signed commitments to put negotiations back on track.

In response, US President Donald Trump stated that while there will be a response to Iranian strikes, the military actions do not indicate a return to full-scale conflict.
