LAHORE, (UrduPoint / Pakistan Point News - 12th Sep, 2026) A motion has been submitted in the [Punjab Assembly](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/punjab-assembly.html) seeking an immediate debate over the alleged use of the [Punjab government](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/government-of-punjab.html)’s official plane for [Chief Minister](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/chief-minister.html) [Maryam Nawaz](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/maryam-nawaz-sharif.html)’s private [visit](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/visit.html) to [London](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/london.html). The adjournment motion was submitted by [opposition](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/opposition.html) member Waqas Mahmood Mann.

The motion states that, according to [media](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/media.html) reports, [Chief Minister](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/chief-minister.html) [Maryam Nawaz](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/maryam-nawaz-sharif.html) travelled to [London](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/london.html) to attend her daughter’s graduation ceremony and used the [Punjab government](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/government-of-punjab.html)’s official plane for the trip.

It claims that approximately Rs12.5 [million](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/million.html) [from](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/from.html) the public exchequer was spent on the aircraft’s fuel, parking and other related expenses. Waqas Mahmood Mann demanded that if reports regarding the use of the official plane and expenditure [from](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/from.html) the public exchequer are correct, the [government](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/government.html) should present before the House the legal and administrative approvals, details of the expenses and sources of payment. The motion maintains that the matter raises important questions regarding the alleged personal use of public resources, financial discipline and the exercise of authority.

It therefore calls for the issue to be taken up for immediate debate in the House instead of being dealt with through routine proceedings. Meanwhile, the [Punjab government](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/government-of-punjab.html) has denied that the [Chief Minister](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/chief-minister.html)’s [London](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/london.html) [visit](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/visit.html) was paid for [from](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/from.html) the public exchequer.

Provincial [Information Minister](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/information-minister.html) Uzma Bukhari said [Maryam Nawaz](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/maryam-nawaz-sharif.html) paid [all](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/all.html) expenses for the plane used for the [London](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/london.html) [visit](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/visit.html) [from](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/from.html) her own pocket. The central question is now how the official aircraft was used and its expenses paid if the plane was government-owned, while the [government](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/government.html) maintains that the trip did not burden the public exchequer.

Further clarification is expected during debate on the adjournment motion in the [Punjab Assembly](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/punjab-assembly.html), when details regarding the use of the official plane, the actual expenses and proof of payment [may](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/may.html) emerge.
