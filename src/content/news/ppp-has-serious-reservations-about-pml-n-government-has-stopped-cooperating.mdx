LAHORE, (UrduPoint / Pakistan Point News - 10th Sep, 2026) [Pakistan](https://www.urdupoint.com/pakistan/) Peoples Party leader Sharmila Faruqui said that the party had serious reservations about the [PML-N](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/muslim-league-n.html) [government](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/government.html) and had stopped cooperating with it, adding that the [government](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/government.html) was unable to handle anything.

Speaking on a Hum [news](https://www.urdupoint.com/urdu-news) programme, she said the [PPP](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/ppp.html) had serious reservations about the [PML-N](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/muslim-league-n.html) [government](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/government.html) and had withdrawn its cooperation. The [government](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/government.html) had planned to convene a [National Assembly](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/national-assembly.html) session on 21 [September](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/september.html), but that is no longer happening either.

The [government](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/government.html)’s problem is that it is unable to handle matters in any direction. Bilawal Bhutto has said that issues related to the failure of the [government](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/government.html) system and the provinces should be discussed in [Parliament](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/parliament.html) so that politicians can be held accountable.

> 

PTI leader Shaukat Yousafzai said that the [PTI](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/pakistan-tehreek-e-insaf.html) did not need to bring any machinery, as the people of [Pakistan](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/pakistan.html) would join the [long march](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/long-march.html).

He said there was no threat to the [government](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/government.html) because its support rested elsewhere.

The [long march](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/long-march.html) was not being held for just one purpose; the country’s economy had been destroyed and the country was not functioning. He said everyone had the right to peaceful [protest](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/protest.html), not just the [PTI](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/pakistan-tehreek-e-insaf.html).

They would have to account for the use of [government](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/government.html) resources against the people. He said the arrest warrants issued today were intended to increase pressure. “Let them arrest us; [Sohail Afridi](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/sohail-afridi.html) is not afraid, and no one else is afraid either.” Furthermore, [PTI](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/pakistan-tehreek-e-insaf.html) [Khyber Pakhtunkhwa](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/khyber-pakhtunkhwa.html) President Junaid Akbar said while speaking on a [Samaa](https://www.urdupoint.com/tv/news-channels/samaa-tv-live.html) News programme that [Rana Sanaullah](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/rana-sanaullah.html) was a political worker and they respected him, but he did not believe Rana had made the statement about armed groups sincerely.

“I want to ask whether these are the [same](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/same.html) reports that said [Rana Sanaullah](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/rana-sanaullah.html) was linked to the drug mafia, that he had a major network and that heroin had been planted in his [vehicle](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/vehicle.html). Such reports had previously emerged against [Rana Sanaullah](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/rana-sanaullah.html), and a similar report is appearing today,” he said.
