## Pakistan vs Sri Lanka: Saad Baig Makes Debut in Tri-Series Opener

Wicket-keeper-batter Saad Baig was handed his international debut for Pakistan as Sri Lanka won the toss and chose to bowl first in the opening match of the ODI tri-series. The encounter is currently underway at the Rawalpindi Cricket Stadium, as reported by Dawn News.

### Saad Baig Earns Maiden Cap

Former Pakistan Under-19 captain Saad Baig secured his place in the national playing XI following impressive domestic performances. Baig earned consistent attention from selectors after scoring over 1,000 runs during the 2025-26 Quaid-e-Azam Trophy, Pakistan's premier first-class tournament.

### Match Setup and Conditions

Sri Lanka's decision to field first leverages early conditions at the Rawalpindi venue. Both teams are utilizing this tri-series opener to test tactical strategies and solidify squad combinations ahead of upcoming international commitments.

* **Match Type:** One-Day International (ODI Tri-Series)
* **Venue:** Rawalpindi Cricket Stadium
* **Toss Result:** Sri Lanka elected to bowl first
* **Key Player Debut:** Saad Baig (Pakistan)
