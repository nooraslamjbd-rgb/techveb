RIYADH, (UrduPoint / Pakistan Point News / WAM - 31st Aug, 2026) Jasem Mohamed Albudaiwi, Secretary General of the Gulf Cooperation Council (GCC), has strongly condemned the Iranian aggression against the United [Arab](https://www.urdupoint.com/arabic/) Emirates involving a [drone](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/drone.html), which was intercepted by air defence systems over the [UAE](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/uae.html)’s territorial waters.

He affirmed that the [attack](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/attack.html) constitutes a dangerous escalation and a flagrant violation of the [UAE](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/uae.html)’s sovereignty, security and stability, as well as a direct threat to the safety of citizens and residents.

In a statement issued today, Albudaiwi stressed that GCC countries stand united alongside the [UAE](https://www.urdupoint.com/arabic/uae.html) in confronting anything that threatens its security, sovereignty and territorial integrity.

He also called on [Iran](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/iran.html) to immediately halt such attacks and hostile acts and to bear full responsibility for their repercussions, which could undermine the security and stability of the region.
