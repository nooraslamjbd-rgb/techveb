[Umer Jamshaid](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/team/umer-jamshaid.html) Published September 02, 2026 | 09:30 AM

BERLIN, (UrduPoint / Pakistan Point News / WAM - 02nd Sep, 2026) Two men were killed, apparently by gunfire, at a factory [site](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/site.html) in [Hamburg](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/hamburg.html)'s St Pauli district, [police](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/police.html) said.

Witnesses heard what sounded like gunshots and called emergency services, a [police](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/police.html) operations centre spokesman said.

Several patrol [cars](https://www.urdupoint.com/cars/) were sent to the scene on Lagerstrasse near Sternschanze railway station late on Tuesday, where officers found the two men. Both [died](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/died.html) [from](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/from.html) their injuries at the scene.

Police launched a search involving more than two dozen patrol cars.
