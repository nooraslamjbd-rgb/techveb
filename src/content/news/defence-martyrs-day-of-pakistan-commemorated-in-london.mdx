LONDON, (APP - UrduPoint / Pakistan Point News - 8th Sep, 2026) [Pakistan](https://www.urdupoint.com/pakistan/) High Commission in London on Monday held a ceremony to commemorate Defence and Martyrs Day, paying tribute to the armed forces and the sacrifices of those who laid down their lives in defence of the country.

Former Chairman Joint Chiefs of Staff Committee General (retd) Zubair Mahmood Hayat attended the ceremony as the guest of honour, which was also attended by British parliamentarians, senior UK government officials, ambassadors, defence attaches, media representatives and a large number of friends of Pakistan.

Speaking on the occasion, High Commissioner of Pakistan to the UK Tipu Usman paid tribute to the armed forces and martyrs, saying the courage and determination demonstrated by Pakistan during Marka-e-Haq had once again showcased the capability and resolve of the country’s defence forces.

He said the armed forces, under the civilian and military leadership and with the unwavering support of the people, had demonstrated their determination to safeguard the country’s sovereignty. He described the success of Operation Bunyanum Marsoos as another significant chapter in Pakistan’s military history, saying it had also revitalised the spirit of the nation.

The high commissioner also highlighted Pakistan’s role in promoting regional peace and stability, referring to its internationally recognised mediation efforts in the [middle](https://www.urdupoint.com/education/middle-result.html) East and the Makkah Joint Defence Agreement with Saudi Arabia and Türkiye as manifestations of the country’s commitment to regional security.

He said Pakistan remained committed to further strengthening defence and security cooperation with the United Kingdom in the broader interest of regional and international peace.

The ceremony also reaffirmed Pakistan’s political, moral and diplomatic support for the people of Kashmir facing human rights violations in [Indian](https://www.urdupoint.com/islam/india-ramadan-calendar-sehar-aftar-timings.html) Illegally Occupied Jammu and Kashmir.

Participants expressed the resolve that the nation would remain united with the Pakistan Defence Forces in confronting threats to the country and protecting its sovereignty at all costs.

The distinguished guests also paid glowing tributes to the dedication, professionalism and sacrifices of Pakistan’s armed forces, reaffirming the enduring [bond](https://www.urdupoint.com/business/prize-bonds.html) between the Pakistani community and the country’s national institutions.
