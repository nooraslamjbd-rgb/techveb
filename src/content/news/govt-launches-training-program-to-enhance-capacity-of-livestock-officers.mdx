PESHAWAR, (APP - UrduPoint / Pakistan Point News - 29th Aug, 2026) A three-day training program was launched under the department of livestock, fisheries and cooperatives, aimed at enhancing the professional capacity and promoting effective teamwork among officers and officials.

On the first day of the training program here Saturday, Secretary Livestock, Fisheries and Cooperatives, Khyber Pakhtunkhwa, Zareef-ul-Mani, addressed the focal persons and relevant team members.

He highlighted the project’s vision, key objectives, and the importance of teamwork, coordination, a sense of responsibility, and professional competence for its successful implementation.

The Secretary emphasized that [mobile](https://www.urdupoint.com/mobile/) veterinary clinics were not merely vehicles, but rather an integrated system designed to deliver modern veterinary services to livestock farmers in remote areas.

He stated that the success of the initiative depended on effective teamwork, timely service delivery, and transparent monitoring.

During the three-day training program, the participants will receive practical training focused on improving professional skills, team building, communication, field management, digital monitoring and the effective delivery of veterinary services.
