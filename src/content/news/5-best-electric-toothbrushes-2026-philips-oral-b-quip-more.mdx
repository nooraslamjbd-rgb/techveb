The best electric toothbrush reduces plaque and gingivitis better than a manual one, encourages you to brush long enough, and prevents overzealous scrubbing (it’s a thing--just ask my dentist).

I didn’t always love to brush my teeth, but over the years, I’ve come to appreciate the oral-health ritual: two minutes of foamy, mindless maintenance twice a day (or more) that keeps the dental hygienist off my back.

Investing in preventative dental care can save you thousands of dollars (and a lot of discomfort) in the long run.

If you have a permanent retainer from orthodontics, an electric device can deal with the associated plaque buildup far more effectively than a manual toothbrush.

And a worthy electric toothbrush doesn’t have to cost a fortune.

We’ve tested dozens, including budget-friendly basic options and high-tech models packed with pressure sensors and multiple cleaning modes.

Each brush gets at least two weeks of use--longer if we’re verifying battery claims--so we can assess performance, comfort, and overall value.
