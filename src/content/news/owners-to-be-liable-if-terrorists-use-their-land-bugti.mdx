Balochistan Chief Minister Mir Sarfraz Bugti has inaugurated major digital governance initiatives for the provincial police while issuing a stern warning to property owners regarding terrorist activities.

Speaking at the Central Police Office, CM Bugti announced the rollout of 16 digital applications aimed at modernizing public safety and police services across the province.

## Major Digital Initiatives for Public Safety

To improve transparency and public service delivery, the Balochistan government upgraded eight existing police applications and introduced eight new digital tools.

Key features of the digital initiative include:
* **Women Safety Application:** Allows users to trigger immediate emergency alerts to police and family members with a single click.
* **Martyr Welfare Portal:** Offers direct communication and welfare assistance to families of fallen police personnel.
* **Public Service Integration:** Integrates Khidmat Markaz facilities, hotel tracking, and streamlined access to the criminal justice system.

The provincial government also announced an upcoming awareness campaign targeting female students, working women, and travelers to maximize app adoption.

## Strict Security Warnings for Property Owners

Alongside the technological upgrades, CM Bugti issued a strong warning to vineyard and property owners in Mastung. Landowners will be treated as criminal facilitators if their private property is used by terrorists to attack security personnel or government officials.

Reaffirming the sacrifice of the Pakistan Army, Frontier Corps, and police force, the Chief Minister emphasized that private property cannot be used as a cover for anti-state activities.
