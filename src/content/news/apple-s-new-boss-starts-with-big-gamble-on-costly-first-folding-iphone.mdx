## Apple Unveils the Foldable iPhone Duo

John Ternus made his major product debut during his second week as Apple chief executive, taking the stage to introduce the company's first foldable smartphone—the iPhone Duo. Succeeding former CEO Tim Cook, Ternus announced what analysts call the most significant design evolution in the iPhone's nearly 20-year history.

## Design, Features, and Pricing

The iPhone Duo adopts a book-style form factor designed to seamlessly blend the capabilities of Apple's mobile and tablet lineups.

### Key Product Details
* **Form Factor:** Opens into a small iPad display and folds into a wider iPhone profile.
* **Starting Price:** $2,000 (£1,999 in the UK), scaling up to $3,200 for higher storage tiers.
* **Software Experience:** Offers intuitive, fluid transitions optimized for dual-screen usage.

## Redefining the Foldable Smartphone Market

During the keynote, Ternus stated that Apple specifically aimed to overcome design flaws seen in rival products. He noted that existing foldable devices often feel bulky, like "two phones stuck together," which reduces the effective usable screen space.

Before taking over the chief executive role, Ternus spent several years leading Apple's hardware engineering division, where he directly managed the development of the foldable project. Industry analysts from research firms FDM and Creative Strategies noted that his promotion was strategically timed to coincide with this critical hardware launch.
