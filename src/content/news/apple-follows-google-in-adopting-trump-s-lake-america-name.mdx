## Apple and Google Update Lake Ontario to 'Lake America'

Following a recent U.S. executive order, Apple Maps has joined Google Maps in renaming Lake Ontario to "Lake America" for users based in the United States. According to reports originally published by TechCrunch, the update aligns digital maps with changes in federal databases managed by the U.S. Interior Department.

### Regional Display Differences

The name change is geographically restricted to U.S. audiences. Users accessing Apple Maps within Canada will continue to see "Lake Ontario," while users in other international regions will see both names displayed simultaneously.

### MapQuest Resists the Naming Shift

While major tech giants complied with the federal directive, legacy navigation service MapQuest opted out. MapQuest publicly stated it would retain the traditional name to prioritize user familiarity, mirroring its past decision regarding the Gulf of Mexico.

### Surge in MapQuest App Downloads

MapQuest's stance against the geographic renaming sparked widespread interest. Downloads for the classic navigation app surged rapidly, pushing MapQuest to the top position on the U.S. iOS App Store charts.
