ABU DHABI, (UrduPoint / Pakistan Point News / WAM - 28th Aug, 2026) President His Highness Sheikh Mohamed bin Zayed Al Nahyan sent a message of condolences to His Majesty King Haakon VIII of [Norway](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/norway.html), over the death of King Harald V.

His Highness Sheikh Mohammed bin [Rashid](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/rashid.html) Al Maktoum, Vice President, [Prime Minister](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/prime-minister.html) and Ruler of [Dubai](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/dubai.html), and His Highness Sheikh Mansour bin Zayed Al Nahyan, Vice President, Deputy [Prime Minister](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/prime-minister.html), and Chairman of the Presidential [Court](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/court.html), also sent similar messages of condolences to His Majesty King Haakon VIII.
