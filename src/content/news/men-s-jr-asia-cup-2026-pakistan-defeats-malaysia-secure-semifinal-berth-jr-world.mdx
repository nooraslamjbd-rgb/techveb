ISLAMABAD, (APP - UrduPoint / Pakistan Point News - 8th Sep, 2026) In a crucial Pool B clash at the Men’s Junior Asia Cup 2026, the [Pakistan](https://www.urdupoint.com/pakistan/) Junior Hockey Team showcased a dominant performance, thrashing Malaysia 5–2.

With this commanding win, Pakistan maintains its unbeaten streak in the tournament, recording their third consecutive victory, said a press release.

The game got off to a tense start as Malaysia’s Muhammad Salmi struck in the 9th minute to give his side an early lead. However, Pakistan launched a blistering second-half comeback, dismantling the Malaysian defense. Pakistan's Nadeem Khan scored a brilliant field goal to draw level at 1–1 in 32nd minutes. Abdullah Sami Rana found the back of the net to put Pakistan ahead 2–1 in 36th minute.

Waleed Rana stole the show with back-to-back goals in 39th & 40th minutes of the game, firing Pakistan into a commanding 4–1 lead. Ali Hamza added a fifth goal in 41st minutes to complete a devastating scoring spell.

Malaysia’s Gunaselan Dhirash pulled one back in the 54th minute, but Pakistan safely managed the closing minutes to seal an emphatic 5–2 victory.

Pakistan Hockey Federation (PHF) President Mohyuddin Ahmad Wani congratulated the junior squad and team management on their outstanding performance, expressing full confidence that the team will maintain this winning momentum in the knockout stages.
