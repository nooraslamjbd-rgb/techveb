## Devastating Floods Strike Nepal-Tibet Border

A catastrophic flood disaster along the Nepal-Tibet border has claimed at least 359 lives in Nepal, according to an official statement by Nepal Police reported by Dawn News. Emergency teams are battling harsh terrain as disaster relief operations continue across the region.

Search and rescue efforts are intensifying as authorities estimate that around 1,000 people remain unaccounted for across affected districts.

### Cross-Border Impact and Casualties

The natural disaster has impacted communities on both sides of the border:

- **Nepal Death Toll:** Officially confirmed at 359 by local authorities.
- **Tibet Casualties:** Chinese state media reported a major mudslide at a border hub, resulting in 3 confirmed deaths.
- **Combined Toll:** Total cross-border fatalities have reached at least 362 individuals.

## Emergency Response and Rescue Challenges

Emergency personnel, law enforcement, and local volunteers are conducting active search operations in high-risk zones. Widespread mudslides and destroyed infrastructure continue to severely obstruct access to remote villages.

Authorities warn that the death toll may rise further as rescue teams reach previously isolated settlements along the border region.
