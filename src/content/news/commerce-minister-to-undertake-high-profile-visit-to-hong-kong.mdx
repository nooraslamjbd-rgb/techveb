ISLAMABAD, (APP - UrduPoint / Pakistan Point News - 8th Sep, 2026) [Federal](https://www.urdupoint.com/education/board-of-education-federal.html) Minister for Commerce Jam Kamal Khan will undertake a high-profile official visit to Hong Kong from September 8–11th 2026 to represent [Pakistan](https://www.urdupoint.com/pakistan/) at the Belt and Road Summit.

During the visit, the Minister will deliver a special address, participate in key summit sessions and hold high-level meetings with senior leaders and officials from Hong Kong and various other countries, said a release issued here on Tuesday.

He will also engage with leading [business](https://www.urdupoint.com/business/prize-bonds.html) organisations, investors and the Pakistani community to promote trade, attract investment and strengthen Pakistan’s economic relations with international partners.
