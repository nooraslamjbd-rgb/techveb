## Bruno Fernandes Targeted in Cheshire Home Break-In

Manchester United captain Bruno Fernandes became the latest Premier League star to suffer a home burglary while away on international match duties. The break-in occurred on Wednesday evening while the 32-year-old midfielder was in Italy for United's Champions League fixture against Como.

According to reports first published by BBC Sport, Fernandes' family was not present at his Cheshire residence during the incident. Police responded swiftly, with intruders remaining inside the property for less than five minutes before officers arrived on the scene.

## Pattern of Footballer Burglaries in Cheshire

This incident highlights an ongoing security concern for professional athletes living in North West England. Several high-profile footballers in the same affluent Cheshire neighborhood have faced similar targeted break-ins while competing in away fixtures:

- **Ruben Dias:** The Manchester City defender's home was burgled in January during a Champions League match against Galatasaray.
- **Jack Grealish & Raheem Sterling:** Both experienced high-value home break-ins during live matches in recent seasons.
- **Riyad Mahrez:** Targeted in a similar fashion during his tenure in the Premier League.

Manchester United confirmed they are actively supporting Fernandes and his family following the security breach. Cheshire Police have been contacted regarding the ongoing investigation.

## Match Context and Captaincy Status

The burglary rounded off a difficult trip for the Portuguese international. Manchester United suffered a heavy 4-1 defeat to Como, after which Fernandes publicly criticized tactics employed by manager Michael Carrick. Despite the post-match comments, club officials confirmed Fernandes will not face disciplinary action and retains his role as team captain.
