EL ALAMEIN, Egypt, (UrduPoint / Pakistan Point News / WAM - 07th Sep, 2026) The [UAE](https://www.urdupoint.com/arabic/uae.html) National Pavilion kicks off tomorrow at the [El Alamein](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/el-alamein.html) International Airshow 2026, taking place at [El Alamein](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/el-alamein.html) International [Airport](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/airport.html), Matrouh Governorate, [Egypt](https://www.urdupoint.com/arabic/egypt.html), [from](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/from.html) 8 to 10 [September](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/september.html) 2026.

Launched in 2024, [El Alamein](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/el-alamein.html) International Airshow serves as a strategic regional platform connecting aerospace, defence and space stakeholders across [Africa](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/africa.html) and the [middle](https://www.urdupoint.com/education/middle-result.html) East. Supported by key Egyptian stakeholders including the Ministry of Defence, the Egyptian Air Force, the Ministry of Civil Aviation, the Egyptian Space Agency and EgyptAir, the three-day [event](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/event.html) brings together governments, armed forces, civil aviation authorities, space agencies and [industry](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/industry.html) across 125,000 square metres of exhibition space, featuring more than 250 exhibitors [from](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/from.html) 50 countries.

Supported by the [UAE](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/uae.html) Ministry of Defence (MOD) and the Tawazun Council for Defence Enablement (Tawazun), and organised by ADNEC Group, a Modon [company](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/company.html), the pavilion positions the [UAE](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/uae.html)'s national defence sector at the centre of one of the [world](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/world.html)'s leading [industry](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/industry.html) gatherings.

Spanning 871 square metres, the [UAE](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/uae.html) Pavilion brings together a group of leading Emirati companies operating across defence, aerospace, maintenance and advanced [technology](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/technology.html), including EDGE Group, Generation 5 Holding, [Abu Dhabi](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/abu-dhabi.html) Aviation and its group companies, and Multi Level Group, offering visitors a comprehensive view of the [UAE](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/uae.html)'s expanding industrial and technological capabilities.

EDGE Group is participating in the [UAE](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/uae.html) Pavilion with a range of advanced multi-domain defence capabilities. The Group’s display will span autonomous systems, uncrewed aviation, precision effects and ammunition, including the SHADOW 25 and SHADOW 50 loitering munitions, HT-100 unmanned helicopter, SKYKNIGHT air defence missile system, DESERT STING precision-guided glide weapons, THUNDER and AL TARIQ precision-guided munitions, 155mm artillery ammunition, and MK 80 series aerial munitions, The showcase reflects EDGE’s growing portfolio of technologies developed to address evolving operational requirements across multiple domains.

Generation 5 Holding is taking part in the [UAE](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/uae.html) Pavilion, reflecting its growing presence across the defence, aerospace and advanced [technology](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/technology.html) sectors and the industrial and technological capabilities it has developed. The [company](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/company.html)'s participation supports its strategy to expand across regional and international [markets](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/market.html) and to establish new strategic partnerships, providing a platform to engage with [government](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/government.html) and military entities, specialised companies and operators, and to explore cooperation in development, manufacturing, technical integration, and research and development.

AD Aviation Group is exhibiting at the [UAE](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/uae.html) Pavilion through its portfolio of companies — [Abu Dhabi](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/abu-dhabi.html) Aviation (ADA), GAL, AMMROC and Maximus Air — presenting the integrated capabilities and expertise of the Group across the aviation sector. The Group's participation as part of the [UAE](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/uae.html) National Pavilion reflects its ambition to expand its international footprint and strengthen partnerships in key [markets](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/market.html).

[Egypt](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/egypt.html)'s strategic position connecting the [Middle East](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/middle-east.html) and [Africa](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/africa.html) makes the Airshow an important platform to explore new opportunities for collaboration and growth.

GAL is showcasing display models of two of the platforms it represents: the F-16, a compact, high-performance multirole fighter recognised for its agility and versatility across air-to-air and air-to-surface missions, and the Rafale, a twin-engine "omnirole" fighter designed to perform a wide spectrum of missions across air, land and maritime environments within a single sortie.

AMMROC is presenting a scale model of its Al Ain [MRO](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/mauritanian-ouguiya.html) Facility, a comprehensive military aviation maintenance, repair and overhaul centre spanning a one-square-kilometre [site](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/site.html) with more than 140,000 square metres of gross floor area, five [MRO](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/mauritanian-ouguiya.html) hangars and over 30 back shops, supporting more than 35 fixed-wing and rotary aircraft types for the [UAE](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/uae.html) Armed Forces, regional air forces and international partners. The [company](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/company.html) is also displaying a model of the C-130 Hercules, reflecting its role as a Lockheed Martin-approved C-130 Hercules Service Centre for the [Middle East](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/middle-east.html) and North [Africa](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/africa.html), providing depot-level maintenance, engineering, component, modification and upgrade support for the platform.

Maximus Air is featuring a model of its Antonov AN-124-100, the Group's strategic heavy-lift aircraft, capable of carrying up to 120 tonnes of payload across 850 cubic metres of cargo hold space. Designed for oversized and mission-critical cargo, the aircraft supports governments, defence organisations and commercial customers with safe, flexible and rapid solutions for complex and time-critical operations worldwide.

ADNEC Group is participating in the [El Alamein](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/el-alamein.html) International Air Show to showcase [Abu Dhabi](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/abu-dhabi.html)’s position as a leading global [hub](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/hub.html) for the defence, security, and aerospace industries, while promoting its portfolio of internationally recognised events, including the International Defence Exhibition (IDEX), Naval Defence and Maritime Security Exhibition (NAVDEX), Unmanned Systems Exhibition and Conference (UMEX), Simulation and Training Exhibition (SimTEX), and the International Exhibition for National Security and Resilience (ISNR).

Visitors can explore the [UAE](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/uae.html) Pavilion's exhibits and engage with the participating companies at Stand C70 throughout the three-day [event](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/event.html), [from](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/from.html) 8 to 10 [September](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/september.html) 2026. Leading experts and specialists [from](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/from.html) several organisations will be at the [event](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/event.html), allowing visitors to engage with [industry](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/industry.html) professionals, discuss the latest advancements and innovations, and gain valuable insights into the [UAE](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/uae.html)'s rapidly evolving manufacturing and [technology](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/technology.html) sectors.

The pavilion will also serve as a platform for fostering collaboration, knowledge [exchange](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/exchange.html), and new [business](https://www.urdupoint.com/business/prize-bonds.html) opportunities between [UAE](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/uae.html) companies and international stakeholders.
