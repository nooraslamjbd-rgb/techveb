## Smart Surveillance Leads to Arrest in Lahore Assault Case

Lahore police have arrested a man accused of torturing and injuring a young woman riding a scooty on Mozang Link Road, as reported by Dawn News. The suspect, identified as Ghulam Mustafa, was apprehended following a targeted law enforcement operation.

### PSCA Camera Network Key to Identifying Suspect

Law enforcement utilized smart city surveillance infrastructure to track down the offender. Analysts combed through data from dozens of Punjab Safe Cities Authority (PSCA) CCTV cameras positioned across the city.

* **Location:** Mozang Link Road, Lahore
* **Suspect Identified:** Ghulam Mustafa
* **Technology Used:** PSCA Smart City CCTV Network
* **Status:** Sent behind bars under criminal charges

### High-Tech Tracking Meets Traditional Investigation

By integrating digital camera feeds with standard investigative tactics, police quickly established the suspect's route and identity. Authorities confirmed that Mustafa has been formally nominated in a criminal case and remains in custody as legal proceedings move forward.
