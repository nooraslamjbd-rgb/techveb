## Key Witness Notices Issued in Mir Raza Investigation

A judicial commission probing the death of 25-year-old Mir Raza Ali has formally issued notices to his business partner, employees, and close associates. The witnesses are instructed to record their official statements on September 15.

### Business Partners and Staff Summoned

According to details reported by Dawn News, those summoned include Ahsan Anis Shamsi, a 30 percent partner in Ali's Karachi eatery, Wafflix. Additionally, the eatery's manager Sherry and accountant Muhammad Umair Khan were issued notices alongside associates Asad Ali Butt, Usman Buzai, and Hassan Tanoli.

Tanoli, currently residing in Australia, has been granted permission to record his testimony remotely via Zoom. Investigating Officer DSP Siraj Lashari is coordinating with court bailiffs to ensure proper service of notices.

## Background and Discrepancies in Police Probe

Mir Raza Ali was reported missing in July before his body was discovered with a gunshot wound in Gulistan-i-Jauhar, Karachi. While law enforcement initially attempted to frame the incident as a suicide, the victim's family accused police of concealing facts.

* **July:** Mir Raza Ali found dead in Gulistan-i-Jauhar.
* **Post-Mortem Discrepancies:** Police Surgeon Dr. Summaiya Syed flagged inconsistencies between photographs and medical findings.
* **August 6 Court Order:** Exhumation approved and an eight-member medico-legal board formed for a second autopsy.
