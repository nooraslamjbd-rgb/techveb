SARGODHA, (APP - UrduPoint / Pakistan Point News - 9th Sep, 2026) A local court sentenced a woman to life imprisonment and imposed a fine of Rs400,000 for murdering her husband, following effective investigation and prosecution by Tarkhanwala Police, Sargodha Police said on Wednesday.

According to police, the court convicted Nazia Bibi in case No. 434/25 registered under Section 302 of the [Pakistan](https://www.urdupoint.com/pakistan/) Penal Code after the prosecution successfully established her involvement in the murder of her husband, Muhammad Saleem.
