ISLAMABAD, (APP - UrduPoint / Pakistan Point News - 11th Sep, 2026) The [Federal](https://www.urdupoint.com/education/board-of-education-federal.html) Investigation Agency (FIA) Islamabad Zone has advised dual nationals and overseas Pakistanis travelling to [Pakistan](https://www.urdupoint.com/pakistan/) to ensure they carry valid [travel](https://www.urdupoint.com/travel/) documents and meet all applicable immigration requirements before starting their journey.

An FIA official told APP on Friday that dual nationals travelling to Pakistan should carry a valid Pakistani passport, National Identity Card for Overseas Pakistanis (NICOP), or Pakistan e-Visa where applicable.

The FIA clarified that Pakistani nationals working abroad could return to Pakistan even if their CNIC had expired, provided they possessed other required travel documents in accordance with applicable regulations.

It advised passengers to verify relevant travel and immigration requirements before boarding their flights and ensure that their documents were in order.

The agency urged travellers to check the validity of their passports and make sure their identity documents were up to [date](https://www.urdupoint.com/islam/today-islamic-date.html) and acceptable for travel.

Travel informed, travel prepared, the FIA said, advising passengers to complete necessary document checks before beginning their journey to avoid inconvenience.
