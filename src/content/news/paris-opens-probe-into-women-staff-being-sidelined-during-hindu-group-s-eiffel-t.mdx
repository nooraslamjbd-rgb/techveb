## Paris Authorities Launch Investigation Into Eiffel Tower Gender Controversy

Paris city authorities have initiated a formal investigation following allegations that female staff at the Eiffel Tower were sidelined during a private visit by a Hindu religious delegation. The incident sparked intense employee protests and union backlash, resulting in a brief closure of the iconic French monument.

### Background of the Sidelining Incident

According to reports from Dawn News, a delegation of approximately 100 individuals associated with the Bochasanwasi Akshar Purushottam Swaminarayan Sanstha (BAPS) visited the landmark over the weekend. During the private tour, female employees of both the landmark and outside contractors were instructed to step out of sight to limit interactions with the group.

### Official Responses and Apologies

Staff union representatives strongly condemned the directives, characterizing them as sex-based discrimination. SETE, the operating company managing the Eiffel Tower, acknowledged that the delegation requested restricted interactions with female staff and conceded that management should have denied the request.

* **City Stance:** Paris official Emmanuel Gregoire stated that employee anger is legitimate and promised a thorough investigation into the matter.
* **Delegation Statement:** BAPS issued an apology on social media platform X, stating the visit was arranged early to avoid disruption and expressing regret for any inconvenience caused.

The ongoing investigation aims to establish full accountability and reinforce non-discrimination policies at public French landmarks.
