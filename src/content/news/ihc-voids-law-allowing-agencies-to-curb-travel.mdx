## IHC Strikes Down Arbitrary Passport Control List Rules

The Islamabad High Court (IHC) has issued a major ruling declaring a key provision of the Passport Rules, 2021, unconstitutional. The voided section previously allowed government agencies to place citizens on the Passport Control List (PCL) without proper legal safeguards.

This legal milestone reinstates constitutional guarantees regarding citizen rights and freedom of movement, severely limiting unchecked state agency oversight over individual travel.

### Key Highlights of the High Court Ruling

Under the struck-down rule, government departments could arbitrarily request travel bans without identifying a competent authority or following transparent procedures.

* **Constitutional Rights:** The court emphasized that freedom of movement is a fundamental right.
* **Lack of Safeguards:** The previous rule failed to provide legal remedies for individuals placed on the PCL.
* **Agency Power Limits:** Government bodies can no longer unilaterally recommend travel restrictions.

### Impact on Governance and Due Process

According to a report by Dawn News, this verdict addresses longstanding concerns over administrative abuse and arbitrary watchlists. By requiring proper legal authority and procedures, the ruling ensures greater accountability in national administrative processes.

Moving forward, the federal government must realign its passport regulation frameworks with constitutional standards to protect citizens from unlawful travel restrictions.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Why did the IHC void the Passport Control List rule?

The Islamabad High Court ruled it unconstitutional due to lack of legal safeguards and missing competent authority definitions.

### What was the impact of the Passport Rules 2021 provision?

It allowed government agencies to restrict citizens' travel without due legal process or transparent oversight.

### Can Pakistani agencies still place citizens on the PCL arbitrarily?

No, the court ruling strikes down arbitrary travel restrictions without formal legal justification and proper procedure.
