## India Elects to Bowl First in Series Opener Against West Indies

India has won the toss and chosen to bowl first in the opening One Day International (ODI) match against the West Indies. The decision highlights India's strategy to exploit early pitch conditions while setting up a controlled run chase.

### Naman Dhir Earns Maiden International Cap

Promising allrounder Naman Dhir has officially made his ODI debut for the Indian national team. Dhir's inclusion adds versatility to the squad, bolstering both the middle-order batting and offering useful bowling options.

### Tactical Lineup Adjustments

According to coverage by ESPN Cricinfo, the team management made distinct team selection choices to balance the attack:

* **Kuldeep Yadav Recalled:** The premier wrist-spinner makes his return to strengthen India's slow bowling attack.
* **Mohammed Siraj Rested:** Fast bowler Mohammed Siraj has been left out of the playing eleven for this fixture.
* **Triple Allrounder Balance:** The team features three designated allrounders—Ravindra Jadeja, Nitish Kumar Reddy, and debutant Naman Dhir.

This tactical lineup provides India with deep batting lower down the order while giving the captain dynamic bowling variations across the 50 overs.
