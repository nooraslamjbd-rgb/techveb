## Balochistan CM Bugti Orders AI-Based Merit Recruitment

Balochistan Chief Minister Sarfraz Bugti has strictly prohibited the illegal sale of public sector jobs, mandating automated AI systems to ensure transparent recruitment across provincial departments. Addressing a secretaries’ committee meeting, the Chief Minister warned that severe legal consequences await any official involved in hiring malpractice.

To safeguard youth employment rights, Bugti ordered the immediate implementation of an approved AI-driven policy for recruitment in Grade 5 and higher positions. 

### Key Provisions of the AI Recruitment Framework

- **Automated Testing:** Local testing centers will conduct AI-assisted evaluations for Grade 5+ posts.
- **Secretariat Support:** The Chief Minister’s Secretariat will assist local centers to maintain strict oversight.
- **Zero Political Influence:** The provincial government declared zero tolerance for political pressure or nepotism.

## Forest Department Probe and Governance Reforms

Alongside the AI implementation, CM Bugti initiated a formal inquiry into alleged recruitment irregularities in the forest department. High-ranking officials, including provincial ministers Mir Zahoor Ahmed Buledi and Mir Shoaib Nosherwani, attended the session to align on digital governance standards.

According to a report by Dawn News, the government machinery remains united in leveraging technology to modernize public administration and eliminate administrative corruption.
