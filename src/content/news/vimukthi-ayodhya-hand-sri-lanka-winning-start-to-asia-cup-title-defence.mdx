## Sri Lanka Women Kick Off Asia Cup Title Defence with Dominant Victory

Defending champions Sri Lanka began their Women's Asia Cup campaign on a high note, securing a resounding nine-wicket victory over the United Arab Emirates (UAE).

### Stellar Bowling Restricts UAE to 79

Sri Lanka's bowling unit dominated from the outset after putting UAE in to bat. Spinners Vimukthi and Ayodhya led the attack, delivering disciplined spells to dismantle the UAE batting lineup completely.

- **Vimukthi:** Picked up 3 crucial wickets to break partnership momentum.
- **Ayodhya:** Claimed 3 wickets, applying constant pressure on the middle order.

Backed by sharp fielding, the dual bowling effort dismissed UAE for just 79 runs.

### Athapaththu Leads Easy Run Chase

Chasing a target of 80 runs, Sri Lanka made quick work of the run chase. Star performer Chamari Athapaththu led from the front with a polished, unbeaten 48 runs, steering her side to victory with comfortable ease.

Sri Lanka reached the target with nine wickets remaining, signaling a strong start to their tournament defence. *(Source: ESPN Cricinfo)*
