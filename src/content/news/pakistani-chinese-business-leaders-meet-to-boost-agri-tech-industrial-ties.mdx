RAWALPINDI, (APP - UrduPoint / Pakistan Point News - 25th Aug, 2026) The [Rawalpindi](https://www.urdupoint.com/education/board-of-education-rawalpindi.html) Chamber of Commerce and Industry (RCCI) organized a high-level business-to-business (B2B) session with a visiting Chinese commercial delegation to explore joint ventures, technology transfer, and expanded bilateral trade across key industrial and agricultural sectors.

Chaired by RCCI President Usman Shaukat along with Senior Vice President Khalid Farooq Qazi, the meeting convened corporate leaders from both nations to evaluate bilateral investment opportunities.

Primary areas of focus included aquatic feed attractants, poultry processing, cold chain logistics, circular layer farming, veterinary active pharmaceutical ingredients (APIs), industrial biotechnology, enzymes, probiotics, automated livestock equipment, functional premixes, precision nutrition, smart manufacturing, and phytogenic antibiotic alternatives, says a press release issued on Tuesday.

The Pakistani [business](https://www.urdupoint.com/business/prize-bonds.html) community was represented by key industry executives, including DSK Poultry CEO Dr. Saeed Ahmad, SLK Corporate Advisors CEO Shaista Gilani, CVF-V20 representative Haseeb Khan, and IBI representative Daniyal Asif.

The incoming delegation featured representatives from leading Chinese enterprises and industry bodies, including the Beijing Egg Product Process and Selling Association, Shandong Weidu Agri-tech Company Limited, Shanxi Zhaoyi Bio Company Limited, Hainan Kunyuan Life Tech Company Limited, Huamei Ceramics Private Limited, and Ningbo Cold Chain Duoduo Company Limited.

RCCI leadership reaffirmed the chamber’s ongoing commitment to building structured enterprise linkages between Pakistani and Chinese firms to accelerate industrial modernization and deepen bilateral economic cooperation.
