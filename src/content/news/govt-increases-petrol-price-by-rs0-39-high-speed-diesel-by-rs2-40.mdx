## Key Takeaways

- Petrol (MS-92) price increased by Rs0.39 per litre
- High-speed diesel (HSD) price increased by Rs2.40 per litre
- New prices effective from August 26, 2026 as per OGRA notification

The Government of Pakistan has increased the price of petrol (MS-92) by Rs0.39 per litre and high-speed diesel (HSD) by Rs2.40 per litre, according to notifications issued by the Oil and Gas Regulatory Authority (OGRA).

The revised ex-depot prices will be effective from August 26, 2026. The price adjustments are based on international oil price movements and exchange rate fluctuations.
