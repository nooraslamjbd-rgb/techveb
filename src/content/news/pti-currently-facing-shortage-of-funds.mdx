Journalist Muneeb Farooq says overseas remittances of Rs2.5 million to Rs3 million sent for PTI Secretariat expenses were not processed by the State Bank and were returned.

ISLAMABAD, (UrduPoint / Pakistan Point News - 12th Sep, 2026) Journalist Muneeb Farooq has said that the State [Bank](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/bank.html) did not process remittances sent by [PTI](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/pakistan-tehreek-e-insaf.html) supporters overseas, and the [money](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/money.html) was returned.

In his vlog, Farooq said it was being reported that [PTI](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/pakistan-tehreek-e-insaf.html) currently had no [money](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/money.html) at [all](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/all.html). These funds were used to pay the [rent](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/rent.html) of the [PTI](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/pakistan-tehreek-e-insaf.html) Secretariat and employees’ salaries, but the State [Bank](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/bank.html) did not process them and they were returned because they had been flagged.

He added that if people in [Khyber Pakhtunkhwa](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/khyber-pakhtunkhwa.html) still provide funds to [PTI](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/pakistan-tehreek-e-insaf.html).

Farooq said that, naturally, not everything is noticed, raising questions about how long protests could continue.

He said [PTI](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/pakistan-tehreek-e-insaf.html) was facing a [shortage](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/shortage.html) of funds because overseas supporters who used to send Rs2.5 [million](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/million.html) to Rs3 [million](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/million.html) for salaries at the [PTI](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/pakistan-tehreek-e-insaf.html) Secretariat had their remittances returned after the State [Bank](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/bank.html) did not process them.

Local businesspeople had also received the message that funding [PTI](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/pakistan-tehreek-e-insaf.html) could create problems for them. Meanwhile, important revelations and claims have emerged about the country’s political and economic situation, including reports of behind-the-scenes meetings between [government](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/government.html) figures and [PTI](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/pakistan-tehreek-e-insaf.html) leaders.

According to the details, anchorperson Muneeb Farooq said in a Geo [news](https://www.urdupoint.com/urdu-news) programme that [PTI](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/pakistan-tehreek-e-insaf.html) Chairman Barrister Gohar had held three to four meetings with [Interior Minister](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/interior-minister.html) Mohsin Naqvi during the current month, one of which was exceptionally long.

In this regard, [PTI](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/pakistan-tehreek-e-insaf.html) leaders were allegedly told that if a [protest](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/protest.html) was not held on [September](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/september.html) 27, [Imran Khan](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/imran-khan.html) could be shifted to another, better hospital instead of PIMS Hospital.
