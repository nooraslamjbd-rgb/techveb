## Massive Flood Devastates Nepal-Tibet Border Region

A catastrophic flood caused by a massive glacier collapse has triggered severe devastation along the Nepal-Tibet border. The deluge of ice, mud, and heavy debris swept through mountain villages, resulting in over 900 casualties and thousands missing.

## Urgent Rescue Operations at Hydropower Tunnels

According to reports from Dawn News, emergency teams are focusing on reaching roughly 933 workers believed to be trapped inside six major hydropower tunnels, including the Chilime Hydropower Plant in Rasuwa.

### Key Relief Efforts Underway:
* **Heavy Equipment Deployment:** Earth-moving machinery and excavators are clearing dense rock and mud around tunnel entrances.
* **International Collaboration:** Technical experts from China and India have joined local Nepalese soldiers overnight.
* **Humanitarian Aid:** The Red Cross estimates over 90,000 individuals have been affected by the disaster.

## Climate Change and Infrastructure Damage

Officials and climate scientists link this unprecedented Himalayan deluge directly to accelerating global climate change. Authorities continue night-and-day operations despite challenging weather conditions to reach survivors and assess critical energy infrastructure damage.
