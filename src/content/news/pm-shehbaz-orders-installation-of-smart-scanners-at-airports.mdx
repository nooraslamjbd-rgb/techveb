## Modernizing Airport Customs: PM Directs Smart Scanner Deployment

In a key move to update national infrastructure, Prime Minister Shehbaz Sharif has directed the installation of advanced "smart scanners" across all international airports in Pakistan. The initiative aims to streamline customs procedures and significantly improve travel convenience for international passengers.

According to an official statement reported by Dawn News, the premier issued these instructions during a high-level review meeting on customs enforcement in Islamabad. Senior leadership, including Deputy Prime Minister Ishaq Dar, Law Minister Azam Nazeer Tarar, and Information Minister Attaullah Tarar, attended the session.

### Benefits of Automated Airport Scanning

Integrating smart scanning technology into airport customs operations is expected to deliver substantial improvements:

- **Accelerated Passenger Clearance:** Automated checks reduce reliance on manual baggage searches, shortening queues during peak flight schedules.
- **Advanced Threat Detection:** Modern AI-assisted scanners identify restricted items, uncollected duties, and contraband with high precision.
- **Transparent Border Operations:** Digital baggage screening reduces human interference, promoting greater efficiency and revenue compliance.

### Upgrading Border Security Standards

This directive is part of a broader federal policy aimed at modernizing economic and border management. By adopting smart technologies at international airports, Pakistan seeks to align its customs enforcement with global aviation standards and facilitate seamless international travel.
