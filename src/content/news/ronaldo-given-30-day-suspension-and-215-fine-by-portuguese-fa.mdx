**Cristiano Ronaldo has been given a 30-day suspension and fined 255 euros (£215) by the Portuguese Football Federation after leaving his country's international camp last month.**

The 41-year-old captain [walked out of Portugal's Nations League squad](https://www.bbc.co.uk/sport/football/articles/cm9dw7ddwgzwo) in September after being an unused substitute against Norway and then finding out he would not start against Denmark.

Ronaldo said [in a lengthy statement](https://www.bbc.co.uk/sport/football/articles/cmd7qn48q13lo) he had left because Portugal coach Jorge Jesus twice "broke his word", accusing him of an "absolute breach of trust".

It is not clear which matches or activities the suspension would cover, or whether time already served under a provisional suspension would count towards the 30-day sanction.

The Al-Nassr forward was provisionally suspended on 9 October. If the suspension is backdated to that date, he will be available to play in his country's next match - against Denmark on 14 November.

Portugal boss Jesus denied making any agreement with Ronaldo but has [left the door open for his return.](https://www.bbc.co.uk/sport/football/articles/c3dxwry8vydvo)

Ronaldo accepted accountability and [apologised to his team-mates and fans](https://www.bbc.co.uk/sport/football/articles/cmd7qn48q13lo) for his actions, before adding he deserved to be punished.

Pedro Proenca, president of the FPF, told Portuguese outlet Record there is a "strong desire" to have Ronaldo back with the national team once the matter is resolved.

Ronaldo, who has won the Ballon d'Or five times, has scored 146 goals in 234 Portugal appearances - both men's world records - helping them win Euro 2016 and the two Nations League titles.
