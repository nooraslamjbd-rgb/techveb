## Senate Debates Pakistan Fuel Supply Security

Concerns over Pakistan's national fuel reserves sparked intense debate in the Senate on Friday, with lawmakers questioning petrol availability for December. According to a report by Dawn News, political leaders expressed deep worries regarding energy stability amid escalating geopolitical conflicts.

PPP Senator Sherry Rehman explicitly raised alarms over upcoming petrol supply levels. She questioned whether the country's current strategic reserves could withstand potential global market shocks heading into the final month of the year.

## Geopolitical Tensions Raise Regional Supply Fears

Opposition Leader Raja Nasir Abbas further highlighted the risks posed by heightening tensions in the Middle East. He warned that expanding regional strife could directly destabilize maritime shipping corridors, leading to potential delays and severe shortages in domestic oil imports.

### Impact on Domestic Fuel Markets

- Heightened vulnerability to international crude price spikes.
- Disruption risks along critical ocean trade routes.
- Growing public panic over potential fuel rationing.

## Government Reassures Public on Petrol Availability

Despite the alarms raised in the upper house, the government maintains that national energy supplies remain fully secure. Minister of State for Finance and Revenue Bilal Azhar Kayani stated officially that Pakistan faces no immediate fuel shortage issues.

Kayani clarified that import pipelines and reserve levels remain adequate to satisfy nationwide energy demand through December and beyond.
