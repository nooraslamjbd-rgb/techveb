## Marriyum Aurangzeb Defends CM Maryam Nawaz

Punjab Senior Minister Marriyum Aurangzeb publicly defended Chief Minister Maryam Nawaz following mounting opposition criticism regarding private jet usage and recent security remarks.

Speaking on social media platform X, Aurangzeb claimed that political opposition is targeting the Chief Minister due to her rising performance metrics and public popularity across the region.

### Funding Clarification for London Travel

Addressing public concerns about state expenditure, Aurangzeb reiterated that CM Maryam Nawaz personally covered all expenses for her private trip to London.

The Chief Minister traveled abroad to attend her daughter's graduation ceremony and returned to Lahore earlier this week, as reported by Dawn News.

### Controversy Surrounding Security Remarks

The ministerial defense follows political backlash triggered by Maryam Nawaz's statements suggesting Punjab faces greater security threats from neighboring domestic provinces than foreign nations.

In response, the Khyber Pakhtunkhwa (KP) Assembly suspended standard agenda items to pass a majority resolution demanding constitutional action under Article 6 against the Chief Minister.

### Rejection of Derogatory Political Rhetoric

Aurangzeb strongly condemned derogatory and sexist remarks directed at Maryam Nawaz by KP legislative members during assembly proceedings.

She emphasized that using offensive rhetoric against women violates Pashtun cultural traditions and accused political opponents of using divisive language to destabilize inter-provincial harmony.
