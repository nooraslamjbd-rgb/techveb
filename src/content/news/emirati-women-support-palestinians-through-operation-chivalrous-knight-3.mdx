ABU DHABI, (UrduPoint / Pakistan Point News / WAM - 28th Aug, 2026) Emirati [women](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/women.html) continue to make valuable contributions to humanitarian and relief efforts through Operation Chivalrous Knight 3, supporting the Palestinian people in the [Gaza](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/gaza.html) Strip.

Working across various areas of the operation, they have contributed to healthcare and relief efforts, supported Palestinian children, [women](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/women.html) and families, and participated in initiatives aimed at alleviating the suffering of [Gaza](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/gaza.html)'s population.

Emirati [women](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/women.html) have embodied the [UAE](https://www.urdupoint.com/arabic/uae.html)'s deeply rooted values of compassion, giving and solidarity, demonstrating their ability to play an active role in the most challenging humanitarian circumstances alongside the teams of Operation Chivalrous Knight 3.

This participation builds on Emirati [women](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/women.html)'s longstanding record of giving and achievement, reinforcing their contribution to humanitarian action and reflecting the [UAE](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/uae.html)'s enduring commitment to providing assistance to those in need worldwide.
