## Aaqib Javed Sends Strong Warning to Recalled Pakistan Players

Pakistan national selector Aaqib Javed has delivered a stern warning to recalled cricketers, including opener Imam-ul-Haq. He emphasized that future selection hinges on performance and visible effort during critical international tours.

## A Strategic Overhaul for Pakistan Cricket

According to a report by ESPN Cricinfo, the recent changes in squad composition serve as a direct message to both the players and the team management. Selection committee member Aaqib Javed made it clear that passive performances will no longer be tolerated on major foreign assignments.

### Key Expectations from Recalled Players

Javed highlighted that returning players must actively raise their standards to remain competitive at the international level:

* **Higher Standards:** Elevated levels of fitness and competitive drive on important tours.
* **Visible Effort:** Clear proof of dedication and intensity in high-pressure match situations.
* **Accountability:** Total alignment between management expectations and ground results.

The selection panel's aggressive restructuring aims to build a resilient squad capable of consistently winning overseas series. Players are now required to justify their spots through sustained work rate and top-tier performances.
