QUETTA, (APP - UrduPoint / Pakistan Point News - 28th Aug, 2026) The Regional Meteorological Center [Quetta](https://www.urdupoint.com/education/board-of-education-quetta.html) has predicted mainly hot and humid weather across most districts of Balochistan during the next 48 to 72 hours.

According to the weather report hot conditions are expected in Nokkundi, Washuk, Dalbandin, Kharan, Panjgur, Noshki, Sibi, Dera Bugti, and parts of Awaran, Kech, Khuzdar districts, Naseerabad division and surrounding areas.

Partly cloudy conditions are likely in Zhob, Sherani, Barkhan, Musakhail, and coastal areas, while gusty winds may occur in most parts of the province.

Dalbandin recorded the maximum temperature at 42.0°C, whereas Kalat and Ormara registered the lowest at 31.0°C. Other notable readings included Lasbella at 37.0°C, Khuzdar at 37.0°C, Quetta at 36.0°C, Uthal at 36.5°C, Pasni at 33.5°C, Jiwani at 31.5°C, and Gwadar at 31.5°C.

The forecast highlights that hot and humid conditions will dominate most districts, while northern and coastal regions may experience partly cloudy weather accompanied by occasional gusty winds.
