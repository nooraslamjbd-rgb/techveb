NICOSIA, (UrduPoint / Pakistan Point News / WAM - 31st Aug, 2026) At least eight people [died](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/died.html) and 18 were missing ‌after a ferry carrying around 270 passengers and crew capsized off North [Cyprus](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/cyprus.html) on [Sunday](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/sunday.html), leaving survivors clinging to its overturned hull.

The privately owned ferry left the port of [Kyrenia](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/kyrenia.html) at 12:30 pm local time and immediately started taking on [water](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/water.html) for an unknown reason, authorities said.

It was heading for the Turkish port of Tasucu in the eastern ​Mediterranean.

Survivors reported the ferry moving erratically, with its last hard impact with the [water](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/water.html)'s surface [breaking](https://www.urdupoint.com/urdu-news) the bow of the two-hulled vessel, ​allowing [water](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/water.html) to seep in. The boat was carrying 267 people, including a crew of eight, while 241 people ⁠were rescued.

The disaster occurred just off the coast of [Kyrenia](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/kyrenia.html), a popular holiday resort for both locals and tourists.
