## Legal Challenge Filed Against M-1 Motorway Closure

A petition has been submitted to the Islamabad High Court (IHC) challenging the sudden blockade of the Islamabad-Peshawar Motorway (M-1). According to a report by Dawn News, a local lawyer filed the plea to address growing public hardship caused by transit restrictions.

### Impact on Emergency Services and Litigants

The petitioner emphasized that the unexpected highway closure severely impacts public mobility. Key concerns raised in the petition include:

- Blocked movement for emergency medical vehicles and first responders.
- Restricted travel access for litigants attending mandatory court hearings.
- Severe disruptions for legal professionals moving between key administrative cities.

### Government Measures Ahead of PTI Protests

The federal government initiated the motorway closures earlier this week to restrict public gatherings. These measures were put in place to prevent supporters of Pakistan Tehreek-e-Insaf (PTI) from launching a political rally into the capital.

The petitioner has urged the IHC to issue directions to restore public access on the M-1 motorway immediately, ensuring basic constitutional rights to free movement are protected.
