## PCB Lodges Protest Over Sky Sports Broadcast

The Pakistan Cricket Board (PCB) has formally lodged a complaint with British broadcaster Sky Sports after an interview featuring the sons of former Prime Minister and cricket legend Imran Khan was telecast during the first day of the second Test against England at Lord's, as reported by Dawn News.

The interview aired during a rain-delayed lunch interval and highlighted serious health concerns regarding Imran Khan, who remains in custody. His sons expressed fears over his deteriorating eyesight and overall health status.

## Threat of Match Delay and ECB Intervention

According to reports from ESPN Cricinfo and The Guardian, the PCB raised strong objections before the broadcast and warned that the Pakistani team might refuse to return for the afternoon session in protest.

While Sky Sports briefly delayed the airing to conduct discussions, the network ultimately broadcast the full interview. Following mediation assistance from the England and Wales Cricket Board (ECB), Pakistan's players returned to the field on schedule at 2:40 PM local time, preventing match disruption.

## Global Reaction and Domestic Blackout

Despite the controversy on international feeds, Pakistan's state broadcaster, PTV, did not air the interview domestically. Meanwhile, Australian captain Pat Cummins joined international sports figures in publicly supporting medical care calls for Imran Khan on social media.
