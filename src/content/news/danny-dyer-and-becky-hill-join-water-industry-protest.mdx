## Celebrity Stars Join Kent Water Ownership Protest

Prominent actor Danny Dyer, TV personality Dani Dyer, and pop singer Becky Hill joined environmental activists in Kent to demand public ownership of the UK water industry. Organized by campaign group Surfers Against Sewage, the demonstration at Leysdown followed a petition signed by over 315,000 citizens calling for structural reform.

Speaking to BBC News, Dyer strongly criticized current coastal water quality, labeling the situation a dangerous disgrace. Singer Becky Hill echoed his concerns, stating that finding human waste while open-water swimming was unacceptable and calling on families to stand up for cleaner seas.

### Escalating Calls for Structural Reform

Industry insiders have backed the call for systemic change. Cliff Roney, a GMB union activist with over 40 years of industry experience, warned that without returning water infrastructure to public hands, the UK faces an impending national catastrophe.

### Sector Promises £104bn Upgrade and AI Integration

In response to public outrage, water utility representatives outlined upcoming investment initiatives:

- **Record Investment Strategy:** Water UK confirmed a £104 billion investment programme designed to halve storm overflow spills and upgrade 1,700 wastewater facilities.
- **Tripled Funding:** Companies plan to triple investment over the next five years to secure national water supplies.
- **AI Innovation:** Southern Water highlighted its £1.5 billion Clean Rivers and Seas Plan, which leverages artificial intelligence and nature-based solutions in areas like Whitstable to monitor and reduce spills.

Despite these technological commitments, campaigners insist that long-term accountability requires complete renationalization of the UK water network.
