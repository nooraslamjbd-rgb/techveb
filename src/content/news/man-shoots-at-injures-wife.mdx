LAHORE, (APP - UrduPoint / Pakistan Point News - 19th Sep, 2026) The [woman](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/women.html) was shot at and [injured](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/injured.html) by her husband on a domestic issue in Shahdara area of [Lahore](https://www.urdupoint.com/education/board-of-education-lahore.html) on Saturday.

According to rescue officials, the man shot at his 55-year-old [wife](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/wife.html) Hamania in the head and injured her.

Rescue personnel provided the first aid to the injured woman and shifted her to Mayo Hospital in a critical condition.

The [police](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/police.html) also reached the spot and started investigation.
