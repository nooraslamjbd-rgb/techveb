## Australia Clinch Series Victory with Dominant Chase

Australia Women secured a decisive series victory following a sensational batting display led by Georgia Voll. Chasing down a competitive target, Australia made light work of the task thanks to an exceptional top-order performance.

Georgia Voll anchored the innings with a brilliant 131 not out, showing impressive shot selection and composure throughout her knock. She was joined at the top by Phoebe Litchfield, and together the duo shared a match-defining 162-run partnership for the first wicket.
