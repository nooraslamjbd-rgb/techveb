LAHORE, (APP - UrduPoint / Pakistan Point News - 8th Sep, 2026) Punjab Chief Minister Maryam Nawaz Sharif has paid rich tribute to the officers and personnel of the [Pakistan](https://www.urdupoint.com/pakistan/) Navy for protecting the country’s maritime frontiers and expressed profound respect for its martyrs and Ghazis.

In her special message on Naval Day, she said the Pakistan Navy had repeatedly demonstrated exceptional professional capabilities and proved its superiority over the enemy through its remarkable performance on different occasions.

The CM said September 8 held historic significance in the country’s naval history, as the Pakistan Navy had successfully conducted a landmark operation and demonstrated its operational superiority, adding that the Pakistan Navy submarine Ghazi had also made history in naval warfare in the [Indian](https://www.urdupoint.com/islam/india-ramadan-calendar-sehar-aftar-timings.html) Ocean, reflecting the courage, determination and professional expertise of its personnel.

CM Maryam Nawaz said the Pakistan Navy was playing an important role in promoting and protecting maritime trade. In the prevailing global situation, she said, the Navy had contributed significantly to ensuring the uninterrupted movement of Pakistani-flagged merchant vessels despite tensions around the Strait of Hormuz. She said the Pakistan Navy was also effectively contributing to maritime diplomacy through its commendable public-service initiatives and international engagements.

The CM said the induction of modern naval assets, including advanced submarines and cruise and ballistic missile systems, had further strengthened the Pakistan Navy’s capabilities and enhanced its standing internationally. She prayed that the Pakistan Navy would continue to uphold its glorious traditions and maintain its outstanding legacy of service and sacrifice for the country.
