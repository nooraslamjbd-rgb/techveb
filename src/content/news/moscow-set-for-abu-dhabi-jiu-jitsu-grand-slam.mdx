MOSCOW, (UrduPoint / Pakistan Point News / WAM - 29th Aug, 2026) [Moscow](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/moscow.html) is set to host the [Abu Dhabi](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/abu-dhabi.html) Jiu-Jitsu Grand Slam on 29th and 30th [August](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/august.html) as part of the 2026-2027 season.

The two-day tournament at Dynamo [sports](https://www.urdupoint.com/sports/) [academy](https://www.urdupoint.com/education/academies-in-pakistan.html) will feature 1,220 [male](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/male.html) and female athletes [from](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/from.html) 32 countries, including 85 black belts.

The [event](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/event.html) brings together competitors [from](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/from.html) a wide range of jiu-jitsu schools and styles, offering athletes the chance to compete for medals, test themselves against elite [opposition](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/opposition.html) and gain international experience.

Brazilian black belt Jonathan Silva said, "Every match is different when you compete at an [event](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/event.html) of this size. There are no easy fights at black belt level. Having athletes [from](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/from.html) 32 countries makes the competition more diverse, and that is what we look for as athletes.

"

Tareq Al Bahri, General Manager of the [Abu Dhabi](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/abu-dhabi.html) Jiu-Jitsu Pro Association, said the [Abu Dhabi](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/abu-dhabi.html) Jiu-Jitsu Grand Slam has established itself as a major [event](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/event.html) on the international [Calendar](https://www.urdupoint.com/islam/islamic-calendar.html).

He said, "Every stop brings together champions [from](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/from.html) different schools, and this is one of the key strengths of the [Abu Dhabi](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/abu-dhabi.html) Jiu-Jitsu Grand Slam. [Moscow](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/moscow.html) has its own character, while this year's participation reflects the confidence of athletes and [academies](https://www.urdupoint.com/education/academies-in-pakistan.html) in the [event](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/event.html)."

He added that organisers are looking forward to two days of high-level competition befitting the tournament's status.

Following the [Moscow](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/moscow.html) leg, the Grand Slam series will move to [Shanghai](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/shanghai.html), [China](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/china.html), on 17th and 18th [October](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/october.html), continuing the 2026-2027 season.
