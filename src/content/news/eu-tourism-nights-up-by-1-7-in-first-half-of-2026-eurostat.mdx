BRUSSELS, (UrduPoint / Pakistan Point News / WAM - 02nd Sep, 2026) In the first half of 2026, there were 1.321 [million](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/million.html) overnight stays in tourist accommodations across the EU, a 1.7% increase compared with the first 6 months of 2025 (1.299 [million](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/million.html)), according to figures released by Eurostat, the statistical office of the [European Union](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/european-union.html).

At country level, [Ireland](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/ireland.html) (+14.6%), [Malta](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/malta.html) (+9.9%) and [Slovakia](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/slovakia.html) (+5.9%) had the highest increases in the [share](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/share.html) of overnight stays in tourism accommodation in the first 6 months of this year compared with the [same](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/same.html) period of last year. Meanwhile, 9 countries recorded a fall, with the highest being in [Cyprus](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/cyprus.html) (-7.7%) and [Romania](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/romania.html) (-6.7%).

Foreign visitors (non-residents of the country, EU and non-EU) accounted for almost half (48.

9%) of [all](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/all.html) overnight stays in the first half of 2026, with large differences among EU countries. The biggest [share](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/share.html) of foreign overnight stays at the beginning of the year was recorded in [Malta](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/malta.html) (95.2%), [Cyprus](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/cyprus.html) (92.6%) and [Luxembourg](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/luxembourg.html) (87.7%).

By contrast, foreign guests accounted for less than a quarter of overnight stays in [Germany](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/germany.html) (18.5%), [Poland](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/poland.html) (19.8%) and [Romania](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/romania.html) (23.0%).

The rise in nights spent by foreign visitors in the first half of 2026 (+2.5% compared with the first half of 2025) was almost 3 times as much as the growth in nights spent by domestic visitors (+0.9%).
