WASHINGTON/TEHRAN, (UrduPoint / Pakistan Point News - 9th Sep, 2026) Tensions between the [United States](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/united-states.html) and [Iran](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/iran.html) in the Strait of Hormuz have reached an extremely dangerous stage. [Iran](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/iran.html) claims it targeted 10 vessels in response to a US [attack](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/attack.html), while the [United States](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/united-states.html) had earlier confirmed destroying five Iranian [oil](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/oil.html) tankers.

Following the latest clashes, crude [oil](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/oil.html) [prices](https://www.urdupoint.com/business/prize-bonds.html) in global [markets](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/market.html) exceeded $100 per barrel. According to [Iran](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/iran.html)’s Islamic Revolutionary Guard Corps, the targeted vessels included two US warships and eight [oil](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/oil.html) tankers.

US officials, however, denied that their naval vessels had been damaged. The US military described its operation against the Iranian tankers as a response to Iranian missile attacks on its warships. Videos showing five Iranian [oil](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/oil.html) tankers catching [fire](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/fire.html) in the US [attack](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/attack.html) were also released.

Washington says additional Iranian tankers could be targeted in response to attempts by [Iran](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/iran.html) to [attack](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/attack.html) US warships. Rising tensions in the Strait of Hormuz have shaken global energy [markets](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/market.html). Brent crude rose above $100 per barrel, reaching that level for the first time since [July](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/july.html).

According to a report, Brent increased by approximately 3.7% to $101.55 per barrel. Experts say that if [oil](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/oil.html) shipments through the Strait of Hormuz remain disrupted for an extended period, global [petrol](https://www.urdupoint.com/business/petroleum-prices-in-pakistan.html), diesel, shipping and industrial costs could rise further, leading to higher global inflation, particularly in the prices of basic [food](https://www.urdupoint.com/cooking/) items. A large quantity of the [world](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/world.html)’s [oil](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/oil.html) passes through the Strait of Hormuz, meaning any major disruption there could be felt worldwide.

According to Reuters, after the latest escalation in fighting, [oil](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/oil.html) shipments through the strait at one point fell [from](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/from.html) 8 to 9 [million](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/million.html) barrels per day to approximately 2 [million](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/million.html) barrels, raising serious concerns about global supplies. Iran has warned that it could declare a wider “no-go zone” in the maritime area using sea mines, potentially creating further difficulties for global shipping.

The Islamic Revolutionary Guard Corps has also [threatened](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/threatened.html) to expand the scope of its response if further attacks take place. Recent events indicate that the ongoing US-Iran war is no longer limited to ground or air operations.

Sea routes, [oil](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/oil.html) tankers and critical global energy infrastructure have also become direct parts of the conflict. The situation has become more complicated after Iran-backed Houthi forces in [Yemen](https://www.urdupoint.com/arabic/yemen.html) attacked energy centres in [Saudi Arabia](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/saudi-arabia.html), while fighting between [Saudi Arabia](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/saudi-arabia.html) and the Houthis is also intensifying again. The Strait of Hormuz is considered one of the [world](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/world.html)’s most important energy routes.

Continued attacks and disruptions to commercial shipping could push global [oil](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/oil.html) prices even higher. Experts have already warned that if key energy facilities or maritime routes remain affected for a prolonged period, crude [oil](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/oil.html) could reach $120 per barrel or even higher. The latest developments have reinforced concerns that the Strait of Hormuz is no longer merely a regional battlefield, as its effects could extend to the global economy, inflation, energy prices and international trade.
