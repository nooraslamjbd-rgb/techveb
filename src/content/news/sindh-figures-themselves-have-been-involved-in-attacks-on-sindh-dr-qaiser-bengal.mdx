KARACHI, (UrduPoint / Pakistan Point News - 12th Sep, 2026) Economist Dr Qaiser Bengali has said that calls to break up [Sindh](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/sindh.html) are not new and that figures [from](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/from.html) [Sindh](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/sindh.html) themselves have been involved in attacks on the province.

Addressing a seminar in [Karachi](https://www.urdupoint.com/education/board-of-education-karachi.html), he said [all](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/all.html) political parties were involved in the imposition of the petroleum levy, while under the Constitution, the provinces have a right to GST. Dr Qaiser Bengali said General Sales Tax was transferred to the [Federal](https://www.urdupoint.com/education/board-of-education-federal.html) [government](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/government.html) with the support of political parties and was later returned to the provinces. The economist said influential people in [Sindh](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/sindh.html) compromise and that local figures have been involved in attacks on the province.

He said [Sindh](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/sindh.html) was separated [from](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/from.html) the Bombay Presidency after a major struggle, while the [Sindh](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/sindh.html) [Assembly](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/assembly.html) later voted to support the establishment of One Unit. According to Qaiser Bengali, it is not enough to view [Sindh](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/sindh.html)'s history merely through the lens of external invaders; the role of local political, social and influential groups must also be examined in its historical context.

He said [Sindh](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/sindh.html)'s history had also been shaped during different periods by political conflicts, struggles for power and the links between local elements and external forces. Qaiser Bengali said an impartial and critical study of history was essential to understand [Sindh](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/sindh.html)'s past, so that events could be viewed in the light of facts and historical evidence rather than emotions.
