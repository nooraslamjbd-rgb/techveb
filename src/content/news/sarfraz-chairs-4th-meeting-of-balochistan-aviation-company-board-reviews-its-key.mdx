QUETTA, (APP - UrduPoint / Pakistan Point News - 9th Sep, 2026) Chief Minister Balochistan Mir Sarfraz Bugti presided over the fourth meeting of the Board of Directors of Balochistan Aviation Company on Wednesday reviewing key administrative, financial and operational matters of the institution.

The chief minister directed officials to align the company with modern aviation requirements, enhancing its operational and administrative capacity to play a more effective role in expanding air connectivity and facilities across the province.

He emphasized the need for transparency, professional management, and effective monitoring, instructing that a clear mechanism be devised to ensure timely implementation of approved decisions.

During the session, the [board](https://www.urdupoint.com/education/boards-of-education.html) discussed the company’s performance, management issues, operational challenges, and future strategy. Several important decisions and proposals were approved, while resolutions from the previous board meeting were formally ratified.

The meeting was attended by Chief Secretary Balochistan Shakeel Qadir Khan, Finance Secretary Jahangir Khan, Director General Balochistan Aviation Company Captain Ali Azad, members of the board, and other relevant officials.
