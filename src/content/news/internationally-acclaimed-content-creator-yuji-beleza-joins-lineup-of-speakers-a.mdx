DUBAI, (UrduPoint / Pakistan Point News / WAM - 29th Aug, 2026) The Organising Committee of the [Arab](https://www.urdupoint.com/arabic/) [Media](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/media.html) Summit 2026 has announced that internationally acclaimed content creator Yuji Beleza will join the lineup of speakers participating in the Summit, taking place in [Dubai](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/dubai.html) [from](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/from.html) 15th to 17th [September](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/september.html).

His participation reflects the emergence of a new generation of content creators who have redefined the concept of content creation and transformed digital platforms into spaces that transcend borders, cultures and languages.

Held under the patronage of His Highness Sheikh Mohammed bin [Rashid](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/rashid.html) Al Maktoum, Vice President and [Prime Minister](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/prime-minister.html) and Ruler of [Dubai](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/dubai.html), and under the directives of H.H. Sheikh Ahmed bin Mohammed bin [Rashid](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/rashid.html) Al Maktoum, Second Deputy Ruler of [Dubai](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/dubai.html) and Chairman of the [Dubai](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/dubai.html) [Media](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/media.html) Council, the upcoming edition of the Summit will bring together more than 400 distinguished speakers [from](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/from.html) 23 countries.

They will take part in more than 175 panel discussions and interactive sessions, with around 10,000 [media](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/media.html) professionals and [industry](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/industry.html) stakeholders expected to be in attendance.

Beleza’s participation is particularly significant given the way his style exemplifies the transformation brought about by [social media](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/social-media.html) and digital platforms in amplifying [media](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/media.html) influence.

He has built a vast digital community spanning multiple continents and cultures. More than 20 [million](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/million.html) people follow him across major [social media](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/social-media.html) platforms, while his [YouTube](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/youtube.html) channel has surpassed one [billion](https://www.urdupoint.com/business/prize-bonds.html) views, highlighting the global reach achieved by his content. He is also followed by around 7.4 [million](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/million.html) people on [Instagram](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/instagram.html), while his TikTok audience exceeds 10 [million](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/million.html) followers.

Beleza’s participation comes as part of the [Arab](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/arab.html) Youth [Media](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/media.html) Forum, which forms part of the [Arab](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/arab.html) [Media](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/media.html) Summit, and reflects the Summit’s efforts to showcase pioneering examples of content creation [from](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/from.html) across the [world](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/world.html) and offer a comprehensive perspective on the transformations shaping the [media](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/media.html) landscape.

The [Arab](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/arab.html) [Media](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/media.html) Summit continues to bring together prominent [media](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/media.html) personalities, thought leaders, content creators, and influential figures [from](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/from.html) around the [world](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/world.html), in line with its vision of convening in [Dubai](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/dubai.html) experiences that represent both the present and future of the [industry](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/industry.html) while fostering dialogue that keeps [pace](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/pace.html) with the rapid shifts filtering through [media](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/media.html), [technology](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/technology.html), and the creator economy.
