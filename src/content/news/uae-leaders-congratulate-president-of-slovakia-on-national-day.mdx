ABU DHABI, (UrduPoint / Pakistan Point News / WAM - 01st Sep, 2026) President His Highness Sheikh Mohamed bin Zayed Al Nahyan has sent a congratulatory message to President Peter Pellegrini of [Slovakia](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/slovakia.html) on the occasion of his country's National Day.

His Highness Sheikh Mohammed bin [Rashid](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/rashid.html) Al Maktoum, Vice President, [Prime Minister](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/prime-minister.html) and Ruler of [Dubai](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/dubai.html), and His Highness Sheikh Mansour bin Zayed Al Nahyan, Vice President, Deputy [Prime Minister](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/prime-minister.html) and Chairman of the Presidential [Court](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/court.html), dispatched similar messages to President Pellegrini and to [Prime Minister](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/prime-minister.html) Robert Fico.
