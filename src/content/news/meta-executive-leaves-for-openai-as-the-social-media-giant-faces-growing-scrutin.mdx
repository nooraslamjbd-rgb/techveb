## Sandhya Devanathan Transitions from Meta to OpenAI

Sandhya Devanathan, Meta's Vice President for India and Southeast Asia, has resigned after more than a decade at the social media giant to join OpenAI, according to a report by TechCrunch. 

Devanathan will be based in Singapore and report to Kiran Mani, OpenAI's Managing Director for the Asia-Pacific region. Her move highlights OpenAI's aggressive talent acquisition strategy across emerging technology hubs.

### Expanding OpenAI's Footprint in Asia-Pacific

In her new executive role, Devanathan will lead consumer growth, enterprise adoption, strategic partnerships, regulatory affairs, and operational strategy across Southeast Asia and Australia.

Her appointment comes shortly after OpenAI named former Uber executive Prabhjeet Singh as its India head. Over the past two years, the AI firm has established a strong physical presence across Tokyo, Seoul, Singapore, Sydney, and Delhi.

## Management Shifts and Regulatory Pressure at Meta

Following Devanathan's exit, Meta India Managing Director Arun Srinivas will report directly to Benjamin Joe, Meta's Vice President for Asia-Pacific.

Devanathan’s departure coincides with growing regulatory scrutiny facing Meta in India. Authorities in New Delhi have recently raised sharp concerns regarding content moderation policies, child safety, and platform oversight.
