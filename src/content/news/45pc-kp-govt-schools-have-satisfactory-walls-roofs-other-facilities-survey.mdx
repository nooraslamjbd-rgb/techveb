PESHAWAR, (APP - UrduPoint / Pakistan Point News - 11th Sep, 2026) A province-wide assessment of 34,265 government schools has found that the condition of walls and roofs is fully satisfactory in only 45 percent of educational institutions, while 26 percent have been rated unsatisfactory.

The survey, covering schools in all 36 districts of Khyber Pakhtunkhwa, showed that the condition of walls and roofs was partially satisfactory in another 29 percent of schools, highlighting the need for greater attention to the upkeep of [school](https://www.urdupoint.com/education/schools.html) buildings.

The assessment found comparatively better conditions for doors, which were satisfactory in 79 percent of schools, while 16 percent were partially satisfactory and five percent were declared unsatisfactory.

Windows were in satisfactory condition in 63 percent of schools, partially satisfactory in 30 percent, and unsatisfactory in seven percent.

The availability and condition of lights and fans were satisfactory in 65 percent of schools, partially satisfactory in 22 percent and unsatisfactory in 13 percent.

Writing boards were among the better-maintained facilities, with 84 percent of schools rated satisfactory, 12 percent partially satisfactory, and four percent unsatisfactory.

The survey also assessed cleanliness, finding classroom cleanliness satisfactory in 61 percent of schools, partially satisfactory in 22 percent and unsatisfactory in 17 percent.

The condition of school grounds was rated satisfactory in 57 percent of institutions, partially satisfactory in 25 percent and unsatisfactory in 18 percent.
