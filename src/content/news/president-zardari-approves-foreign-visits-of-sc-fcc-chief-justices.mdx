## President Approves Foreign Travel for Chief Justices

President Asif Ali Zardari has officially approved ex-Pakistan leaves and issued No-Objection Certificates (NOC) for the judicial heads of Pakistan. The formal approval allows the top judges to proceed with upcoming foreign visits.

The decision was made following formal advice submitted by the Prime Minister, adhering to standard executive protocols for constitutional officeholders.

## Clearances Granted to SC and FCC Leaders

The travel clearances apply to two key leaders of the nation's judiciary:

* **Chief Justice of Pakistan (CJP):** Yahya Afridi
* **Federal Constitutional Court (FCC) Chief Justice:** Aminuddin Khan

Both chief justices received official clearance to travel abroad for their respective scheduled international commitments.

## Administrative Protocols and Official Statement

Under established state procedure, top judicial officers must secure executive NOCs and approved leave prior to undertaking international travel. The official notification was formally released by the Presidency late Monday evening, as reported by Dawn News.
