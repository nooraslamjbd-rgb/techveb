ISLAMABAD, (APP - UrduPoint / Pakistan Point News - 9th Sep, 2026) The Supreme Court has granted final relief to Zaitoon Bibi of [Dera Ismail Khan](https://www.urdupoint.com/education/board-of-education-dera-ismail-khan.html) in an inheritance property dispute after and directed implementation of its 2022 judgment.

A three-member bench comprising Justice Muneeb Akhtar, Justice Malik Shahzad and Justice Salahuddin Panhwar announced the judgment, authored by Justice Salahuddin Panhwar.

The apex court also rejected the opposing party’s application seeking review of the order.

The court expressed serious concern over non-compliance with judicial orders, observing that the judicial system rests on confidence, discipline and respect for the sanctity of court orders and that compliance with judicial directions was mandatory in all circumstances.

It observed that treating a concession granted by a court as merely a procedural formality was regrettable.

According to the judgment, a conditional adjournment had been granted in the review proceedings on March 4, 2024.

At the subsequent hearing on September 12, another adjournment was sought, following which the review petition was dismissed for non-prosecution.

The court held that recalling the order dismissing the review petition would effectively amount to recalling its March 4, 2024 order.

The Supreme Court observed that treating the court’s indulgence as a mere procedural formality by litigants or their counsel amounted to disregarding the seriousness and sanctity of judicial orders.

According to the case record, Zaitoon Bibi’s father died in 1976 and she had been deprived of her share in the inherited property since then.

The Supreme Court directed implementation of its 2022 judgment and dismissed the application filed by the opposing party. The review petition had already been dismissed in 2024.
