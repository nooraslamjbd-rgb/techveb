## Islamabad Court Retains Terrorism Charges in High-Profile Case

An Anti-Terrorism Court (ATC) in Islamabad has disposed of a petition seeking the removal of terrorism charges against human rights lawyer Imaan Mazari and her husband, Hadi Ali Chattha. The legal case relates to the alleged removal of security barriers that were placed to safeguard the visiting English cricket team.

### Court Demands Explanation from Prison Authorities

During proceedings, the court raised concerns over prison officials failing to produce both accused individuals in person despite explicit court orders. Following a brief adjournment, jail authorities submitted a formal explanation detailing the reasons for their non-production.

## Key Background of the Security Breach Incident

- **Incident:** Alleged removal of official security barricades along a high-security route.
- **Key Figures:** Human rights advocate Imaan Mazari and defense lawyer Hadi Ali Chattha.
- **Forum:** Islamabad Anti-Terrorism Court (ATC).

According to reporting by Dawn News, defense lawyers argued that the actions did not fall under terrorism statutes and requested the charges be dropped. However, the ATC disposed of the plea, allowing the case to proceed under existing legal provisions.

### Future Legal Strategy

The court instructed prison administrators to comply with upcoming production orders to prevent further procedural delays. The defense counsel is reviewing alternative legal options within the judicial framework.
