## PIMS Refutes CT Scan Failure Reports

The Pakistan Institute of Medical Sciences (PIMS) in Islamabad has officially clarified that its CT scan machine remains fully functional. The statement follows media reports suggesting that jailed PTI founder and former Prime Minister Imran Khan was unable to undergo a scheduled heart scan due to equipment failure.

### Diagnostic Services Remain Operational

According to PIMS management, radiology and diagnostic facilities continue to operate without interruption. Officials emphasized that technical services remain active to support both emergency and routine patient diagnostics.

- **Facility Status:** CT scan machine is operational
- **Location:** PIMS Hospital, Islamabad
- **Source:** Dawn News

### Context Behind the Medical Visit

Imran Khan was brought to PIMS for a routine medical examination last week. Despite rumors alleging that critical diagnostic scans could not be performed due to hardware issues, PIMS authorities clarified that the necessary equipment remained ready and functional throughout.
