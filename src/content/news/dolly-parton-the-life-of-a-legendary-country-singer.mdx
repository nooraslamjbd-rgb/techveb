## Legendary Country Musician Dolly Parton Dies at 80

Iconic country singer and songwriter Dolly Parton has passed away at the age of 80, according to an official announcement shared by her family on social media. The news has sent waves of grief across the global music industry and among millions of devoted fans worldwide.

## Family Shares Heartfelt Announcement

The tragic update was confirmed by Parton's nephew, Brian Seaver, in an emotional video message posted to her official accounts, as originally reported by BBC News. 

"Sadness lies with us, not with Dolly," Seaver expressed during the video tribute. "Dolly lived in the light and is in the arms of Jesus, surely."

### A Legacy That Shaped American Music

Dolly Parton rose from humble beginnings in rural Tennessee to become one of the most influential figures in entertainment history. Over a career spanning six decades, she crafted timeless hits such as "Jolene," "9 to 5," and "I Will Always Love You."

Beyond her musical achievements, Parton was widely celebrated for her business acumen and extensive philanthropic work, notably through her Imagination Library child literacy program. Her lasting impact as a singer, songwriter, entrepreneur, and humanitarian remains unmatched in pop culture.
