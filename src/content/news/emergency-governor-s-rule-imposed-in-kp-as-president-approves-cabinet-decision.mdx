## Federal Government Imposes Emergency and Governor's Rule in KP

In a significant governance transition, the federal government of Pakistan has imposed Emergency and Governor's Rule in Khyber Pakhtunkhwa (KP). The decision was finalized following presidential approval of a federal cabinet recommendation, according to Dawn News.

### Constitutional Grounds and Administrative Changes

The measure was taken under two specific constitutional clauses. Emergency rule was invoked under Article 232, while Governor's Rule was enforced via Article 234.

The cabinet's summary highlighted concern regarding the provincial administration's deliberate disengagement from state-level security and counter-terrorism measures. Following the mandate, Dr Muhammad Fakhr Alam was appointed as the new Chief Secretary for KP to lead the provincial bureaucracy.

## Political Backlash and Response

The move drew immediate opposition from political figures. PTI Chairman Barrister Gohar Khan condemned the action as undemocratic, stating that peace cannot be achieved by suppressing provincial autonomy.

In contrast, Governor Faisal Karim Kundi called on provincial stakeholders to cooperate, framing the move as a critical step toward restoring regional development and public safety.

### Key Impact Summary

- Federal oversight takes effect across provincial security operations.
- Administrative authority shifts to the central executive apparatus.
- New leadership aims to address escalating security and governance challenges.
