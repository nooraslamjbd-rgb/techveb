LAHORE, (UrduPoint / Pakistan Point News - 19th Sep, 2026) Renowned stage actor and comedian Asif Iqbal died of a heart attack, according to his family. He suffered a heart [attack](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/attack.html) that proved fatal.

News of his sudden death caused deep grief and sorrow in [showbiz](https://www.urdupoint.com/showbiz/) circles. Asif Iqbal had been associated with theatre for a long time and earned a special place in viewers’ hearts through his distinctive acting style.

During his career, he performed memorable roles in numerous stage dramas.

His performances were marked by spontaneity and humour, which made him extremely popular among theatre lovers. Asif Iqbal began his career in stage dramas as an assistant director with renowned actor and director Afzal Ahmad.

He was simultaneously an actor, director and playwright. Fellow artists, fans and [showbiz](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/showbiz.html) personalities expressed deep grief over his death. Paying tribute to him, artists said that his services would always be remembered. According to the deceased’s [family](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/family.html), details regarding his funeral [prayer](https://www.urdupoint.com/islam/prayer-timings.html) and burial will be announced later.
