ISLAMABAD, (APP - UrduPoint / Pakistan Point News - 6th Sep, 2026) At the directions of the Prime Minister of Pakistan, the government of [Pakistan](https://www.urdupoint.com/pakistan/) dispatched 100 tons of humanitarian assistance to the [Federal](https://www.urdupoint.com/education/board-of-education-federal.html) Democratic Republic of Nepal on Sunday to support relief efforts following the recent devastating floods and landslides.

The humanitarian assistance, coordinated by the National Disaster Management Authority (NDMA), was sent by a chartered flight from Islamabad to Kathmandu. The relief consignment comprised tents, blankets, mats, hygiene kits and medicines for the affected communities.

A send-off ceremony was held at Islamabad International Airport, where Federal Minister for Parliamentary Affairs Dr. Tariq Fazal Chaudhry attended as chief guest, along with the Ambassador of Nepal to Pakistan and senior officials from the Ministry of Foreign Affairs, NDMA and other concerned organizations.

The assistance reflects Pakistan’s solidarity with the government and people of Nepal during this difficult time.
