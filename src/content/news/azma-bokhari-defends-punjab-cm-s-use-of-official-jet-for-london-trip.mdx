LAHORE: Punjab Chief Minister Maryam Nawaz’s use of the provincial government’s jet during her recent private visit to London has triggered a fresh controversy, with opposition leaders questioning the use of an official aircraft for a family event.

CM Maryam travelled to London last week to attend the graduation ceremony of her daughter Mahnoor and returned to Lahore on Wednes­day. The government insists the CM bore all expenses of the trip.

Responding to criticism that the CM used the official jet for a private visit, Punjab Inf­ormation Minister Azma Bokhari rejected the allegation. “Absolutely incorrect. The Punjab chief minister paid all expenses for her London trip out of her own pocket,” she said in a post on X on Thursday.

During her stay in London, CM Maryam also faced questions about the debate over the [creation of new provinces](https://www.dawn.com/news/2028912). However, she avoided gi­­ving a detailed response, saying she would speak on the issue after ret­ur­ning home and holding consultations.

> Says Maryam Nawaz bore all expenses after opposition calls out use of govt resources for private visit

Asked about her earlier statement that Punjab was facing a threat of terrorism from its provincial borders, she said: “Punjab is facing a threat from incompetence.”

Opposition leaders, meanwhile, continued to target the chief minister over her use of the official aircraft. Former federal minister and PTI leader Moonis Elahi, in a [post](https://x.com/MoonisElahi/status/2097994151128162536) on X, linked the controversy to the rising cost of fuel.

The 2019-manufactured Gulf­stream G500, reportedly valued at around $38 million to $42m — more than Rs10 billion — has remained under scrutiny ever since reports emerged about its acquisition by the provincial government.

**Mahnoor’s nikkah on Saturday**

Meanwhile, CM Maryam Nawaz’s daughter Mahnoor is set to [tie the knot](https://www.dawn.com/news/2028851/maryam-nawazs-daughter-mahnoor-to-tie-the-knot-on-saturday) on Saturday, with her nikkah ceremony scheduled to be held at the Sharif family’s residence in Jati Umra, a family source told Dawn on Thursday.

According to the source, Mah­noor’s marriage has been arranged with a member of a prominent business family. The ceremony is expec­ted to be a private family affair, with only close relatives and members of the extended family attending.
