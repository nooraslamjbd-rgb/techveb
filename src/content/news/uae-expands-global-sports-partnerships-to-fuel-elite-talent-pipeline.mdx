ABU DHABI, (UrduPoint / Pakistan Point News / WAM - 30th Aug, 2026) The [UAE](https://www.urdupoint.com/arabic/uae.html) is strategically scaling up its high-profile alliances with premier international [sports](https://www.urdupoint.com/sports/) federations and elite global institutions to fast-track home-grown talent onto podiums worldwide. Capitalising on state-of-the-art infrastructure and a stacked [Calendar](https://www.urdupoint.com/islam/islamic-calendar.html) of world-class events, the nation continues to deepen ties across the sporting spectrum.

Football leads the push through grassroots [academies](https://www.urdupoint.com/education/academies-in-pakistan.html) featuring [Manchester City](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/manchester-city.html), [Barcelona](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/barcelona.html), and [Real Madrid](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/real-madrid.html), Alongside LaLiga and the MENA region's sole Lega Serie A office in [Abu Dhabi](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/abu-dhabi.html). Beyond [football](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/football.html), the Department of Culture and Tourism – [Abu Dhabi](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/abu-dhabi.html) continues to expand major rights deals with both the NBA and UFC, complemented by golf partnerships across the DP [World](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/world.html) Tour and specialized [sports](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/sports.html) science monitoring via [Hamilton](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/hamilton.html) [Sports](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/sports.html) Science for national rugby squads.

Similar international expansion drives in modern pentathlon and [badminton](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/badminton.html) ensure a broad, multi-sport developmental base.

Underlining the core vision behind these strategic tie-ups, Sheikh Suhail bin Butti Al Maktoum highlighted that ongoing alliances serve as a critical foundation to identify talent and "build future champions."

Framing the practical impact for athletes, Dr. Huda Al Matroushi noted that hosting global events offers invaluable "direct exposure to elite international players," driving technical standard across [all](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/all.html) age groups.

By combining world-class coaching, physiological monitoring, and high-level competition, [sports](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/sports.html) authorities in the [UAE](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/uae.html) are cementing a sustainable, future-proof sporting ecosystem.
