LAHORE, (APP - UrduPoint / Pakistan Point News - 7th Sep, 2026) Provincial Minister for Health and Emergency Services Punjab Khawaja Salman

Rafique, on Monday paid rich tribute to the [Pakistan](https://www.urdupoint.com/pakistan/) Air Force (PAF) for its bravery,

sacrifices and services to the nation.

In his message on Air Force Day, he said that Pakistan Air Force created an

immortal history of courage and valor during the 1965 war. He said the enemy's

attempt to attack Pakistan under the cover of darkness was foiled by the brave

pilots of the PAF, who defended the motherland without caring for their lives.

He recalled the historic achievement of Squadron Leader Muhammad Mahmood

Alam (MM Alam), who shot down five [Indian](https://www.urdupoint.com/islam/india-ramadan-calendar-sehar-aftar-timings.html) aircraft and created a remarkable

chapter in the history of Pakistan Air Force.

The provincial minister said the PAF continued to serve the country during peacetime

as well as on the war front. He lauded its role in relief activities and in protecting

the country's borders during natural disasters, including earthquakes and floods.

Khawaja Salman Rafique said the nation paid rich tribute to the martyrs and

veterans of the Pakistan Air Force, adding that their sacrifices remained a source

of pride for the entire nation.
