## Maldives President Dr. Mohamed Muizzu Begins Pakistan Visit

Maldives President Dr. Mohamed Muizzu arrived in Islamabad on Tuesday for a landmark three-day official visit to Pakistan. He was warmly received after accepting a formal invitation from Pakistan Prime Minister Shehbaz Sharif, state-run Radio Pakistan reported.

### Historical Milestone in South Asian Bilateral Ties

This high-profile trip represents a major diplomatic development, marking the first time a Maldivian president has visited Pakistan in 11 years. Furthermore, the visit coincides with the 60th anniversary of formal diplomatic relations between the two South Asian nations, originally established in 1966.

### Key Areas of Bilateral Cooperation

During his time in Islamabad, President Muizzu will meet with top Pakistani leadership to review state-level ties and explore fresh avenues for mutual collaboration. Primary topics on the agenda include:

- **Economic Exchange:** Boosting trade partnerships and bilateral commercial investment.
- **Regional Stability:** Enhancing joint cooperation on key South Asian security and diplomatic issues.
- **Cultural Ties:** Deepening historical partnerships and public sector educational exchanges.

As reported by Dawn News, both governments aim to leverage this visit to reinforce long-standing fraternal relations and advance strategic interests across multiple sectors.
