## Major Hospital Safety Overhaul in Punjab

In response to the tragic incident that claimed the lives of 14 infants at the Pakistan Institute of Medical Sciences (PIMS) in Islamabad, the Punjab government has enacted major safety measures across public-sector teaching hospitals.

The Specialised Healthcare & Medical Education Department has directed Emergency Services Department (ESD) Punjab Secretary Dr. Rizwan Naseer to deploy Rescue 1122 personnel to all government teaching hospitals.

### Standardizing Emergency Response Protocols

According to an official letter from the health secretary, Rescue 1122 will help establish a unified emergency command structure. The assigned emergency officers will work alongside hospital emergency, fire-safety, and security teams.

Key responsibilities for emergency officers include:
- Conducting routine inspections of fire extinguishers and life-safety systems.
- Monitoring emergency exit accessibility.
- Standardizing emergency preparedness protocols across tertiary care facilities.

### Technical Working Group Formed for Neonatal Units

Alongside safety deployments, the Punjab government has established an 11-member Technical Working Group (TWG). Comprising senior medical teachers and doctors, the group is tasked with creating comprehensive Standard Operating Procedures (SOPs) specifically for nursery and neonatology units.

This proactive policy shift, as reported by Dawn News, aims to prevent future emergency management failures and safeguard patient lives across the province's tertiary healthcare network.
