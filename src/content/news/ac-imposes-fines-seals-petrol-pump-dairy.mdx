HYDERABAD, (APP - UrduPoint / Pakistan Point News - 7th Sep, 2026) Assistant Commissioner in City taluka imposed a fine on a [petrol](https://www.urdupoint.com/business/petroleum-prices-in-pakistan.html) pump and sealed a dairy in Al Rahim Center area here on Monday while his counterpart in Qasimabad taluka carried out an anti encroachment drive.

According to details, AC City Syed Noor Hussain checked the quantity of fuel being sold at Hussaini Petrol Pump, adjacent to Al Rahim Centre.

During the inspection, the quantity of fuel dispensed was checked and found to be significantly below the prescribed standard.

Taking action against the violation, the petrol pump was fined with Rs115,000 penalty and sealed in accordance with the relevant rules.

Likewise, Hussain also sealed Dawood Dairy for overcharging on milk and yogurt besides imposing a fine of Rs30,000.

AC Qasimabad taluka Hataf Siyal conducted an anti-encroachment drive in various areas of Qasimabad.

He oversaw removal of illegal encroachment and obstructions on public spaces.
