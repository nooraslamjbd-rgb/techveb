## India Women Secure Historic Asian Games Cricket Gold

The Indian women's cricket team delivered a dominant performance to claim the gold medal at the Asian Games. Led by stellar contributions from Smriti Mandhana, Shafali Verma, and Richa Ghosh, India completely outplayed Sri Lanka in a one-sided final clash.

### Spinners Dominate the Title Match

India's bowling attack proved unstoppable as Sri Lanka folded for just 69 runs during their run chase. Remarkably, all ten Sri Lankan wickets fell to Indian spinners, demonstrating total command over the pitch conditions and opposition batters.

### Key Highlights of the Final

- **Massive Margin**: India clinched the championship with a decisive 147-run victory.
- **Dominant Bowling**: Indian spin bowlers achieved a rare feat by taking all 10 wickets in the innings.
- **Batting Setup**: Early momentum from Mandhana, Shafali, and Ghosh laid a formidable foundation.
