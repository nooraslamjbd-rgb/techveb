DOHA, (UrduPoint / Pakistan Point News / WAM - 31st Aug, 2026) The State of [Qatar](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/qatar.html) has strongly condemned renewed Iranian attacks targeting [Jordan](https://www.urdupoint.com/arabic/jordan.html) with missiles and the United [Arab](https://www.urdupoint.com/arabic/) Emirates with a [drone](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/drone.html).

In a statement carried by the [Qatar](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/qatar.html) [news](https://www.urdupoint.com/urdu-news) Agency on Monday, [Qatar](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/qatar.html)'s Ministry of Foreign Affairs said the attacks were a serious violation of the sovereignty and territorial integrity of the two countries, as well as a breach of international law and the [United Nations](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/united-nations.html) Charter.

The statement warned that the Islamic Republic of [Iran](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/iran.html)'s continued attacks represented a dangerous escalation that could complicate de-escalation efforts and undermine diplomatic and political initiatives aimed at restoring security and stability.

The State of [Qatar](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/qatar.html) called for an immediate and complete halt to [all](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/all.html) military actions and attacks threatening regional security, and urged [all](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/all.html) sides to avoid steps that could widen the conflict.

It also called for a serious return to dialogue and negotiations, as well as adherence to understandings reached through diplomatic efforts.

Doha reaffirmed its full solidarity with the [UAE](https://www.urdupoint.com/arabic/uae.html) Hashemite and the Kingdom of Jordan and said it supported measures taken by the two countries to protect their sovereignty and security.
