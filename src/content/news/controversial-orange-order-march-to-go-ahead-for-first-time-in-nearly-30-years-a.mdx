## High Court Clears Controversial Drumcree Parade

A controversial Orange Order parade is set to proceed in a nationalist area of Northern Ireland for the first time in nearly three decades. Following intense late-night legal proceedings, the High Court ruled early Sunday morning that the Parades Commission's approval stands.

The verdict allows the parade to march along Garvaghy Road, a location historically associated with severe civil unrest during the 1990s. Specific restrictions remain in place to manage public safety during the event.

## Late-Night Drama in Belfast Courts

The legal battle concluded at 02:15 BST on Sunday morning, just hours before the scheduled procession. Justice Michael Humphreys noted the extraordinary timing and context surrounding the emergency hearings.

### Notable Quotes from the Ruling

* **Justice Michael Humphreys:** "This court cannot substitute its own view or anyone else's view for that of the commission."
* **Lady Chief Justice Siobhan Keegan:** Described the last-minute legal challenge as "unprecedented."
* **Judge Smyth:** Encouraged involved parties to "reach an agreement" and use the law constructively for the community.

## Impact on Regional Stability

The court emphasized that the matter holds fundamental importance for Northern Ireland's wider society. Information supplied by the Police Service of Northern Ireland (PSNI) played a key role during the final hours of deliberation.

According to report details originally sourced from BBC News, the final ruling allows the event to go forward while placing emphasis on adherence to public order regulations.
