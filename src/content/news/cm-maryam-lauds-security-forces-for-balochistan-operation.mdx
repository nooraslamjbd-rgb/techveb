LAHORE, (APP - UrduPoint / Pakistan Point News - 9th Sep, 2026) Punjab Chief Minister Maryam Nawaz Sharif has commended the security forces for their successful intelligence-based operation in Kharan and Washuk districts of Balochistan.

The chief minister expressed gratitude over the successful operation and paid tribute to the professional capabilities of the security forces for eliminating 11 terrorists.

She lauded the security forces for their courage, professionalism and effective action against terrorism, saying their successful operations reflect their commitment to protecting the country.
