## Fatal Incident and Fire at Alberta Military Base

A serious incident at the Wainwright military base in Alberta, Canada, has left multiple people dead and several others injured, according to official government statements released late Saturday.

Canadian Defence Minister David McGuinty confirmed the tragic outcome, expressing deep sadness over the casualties sustained during the event on the western Canadian base.

### Major Fire Reported at CFB Wainwright

Prior to the official defense statement, Prime Minister Mark Carney posted on X (formerly Twitter) that a major fire had broken out following a severe event at the facility. Emergency services were deployed immediately to suppress the flames and treat injured personnel.

* **Location:** CFB Wainwright, Alberta, Canada
* **Reported Casualties:** Confirmed fatalities and multiple injuries
* **Current Status:** Fire containment and emergency response under way

### Official Response and Ongoing Investigation

Military leadership and local authorities have secured the affected zone within the base. Investigations are underway to determine the cause of the explosion and subsequent fire.
