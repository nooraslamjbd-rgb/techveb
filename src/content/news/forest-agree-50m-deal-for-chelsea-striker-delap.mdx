[**Nottingham Forest**](https://www.bbc.co.uk/sport/football/teams/nottingham-forest) **have agreed a club-record fee worth around £50m to sign** [**Chelsea**](https://www.bbc.co.uk/sport/football/teams/chelsea) **striker Liam Delap.**

The 23-year-old is expected to undergo a medical and sign a long-term contract at the City Ground this week.

Delap's move comes a year after he joined [Chelsea](https://www.bbc.co.uk/sport/football/teams/chelsea) from [Ipswich Town](https://www.bbc.co.uk/sport/football/teams/ipswich-town) for £30m amid competition from [Manchester United](https://www.bbc.co.uk/sport/football/teams/manchester-united) and [Everton](https://www.bbc.co.uk/sport/football/teams/everton).

However, [Chelsea](https://www.bbc.co.uk/sport/football/teams/chelsea) made him available for sale this summer after he scored just one Premier League goal in a previous campaign disrupted by shoulder and hamstring injuries.

Forest have already signed defender Ousmane Diomande from Sporting for £34m, alongside free agents, Austrian midfielder Xaver Schlager and goalkeeper Steve Benda.

They sold England midfielder Elliot Anderson to [Manchester City](https://www.bbc.co.uk/sport/football/teams/manchester-city) for a club-record £116m and striker Taiwo Awoniyi to [Coventry City](https://www.bbc.co.uk/sport/football/teams/coventry-city) for £9m.

Delap's arrival would provide competition for strikers Chris Wood and Igor Jesus, who failed to find the target in Forest's 1-0 defeat by [Leeds](https://www.bbc.co.uk/sport/football/teams/leeds-united) in their home Premier League opener on Saturday.

[Chelsea](https://www.bbc.co.uk/sport/football/teams/chelsea) have also signed two strikers this summer in Emmanuel Emegha from Strasbourg and Danny Welbeck from [Brighton](https://www.bbc.co.uk/sport/football/teams/brighton-and-hove-albion).

Delap came up through Derby County's youth system before joining [Manchester City](https://www.bbc.co.uk/sport/football/teams/manchester-city) in 2019.

After helping City win the FA Youth Cup in the 2019-20 campaign, he scored 24 goals in 20 Premier League 2 matches in 2020-21 as they won the title.

In September 2020 the striker scored on his senior City debut in a 2-1 League Cup win against [Bournemouth](https://www.bbc.co.uk/sport/football/teams/afc-bournemouth), and three days later made his Premier League debut in a 5-2 defeat by Leicester.

Injuries played a part in limiting further first-team opportunities, and between 2022 and 2024 Delap spent time on loan in the Championship with Stoke, Preston North End and [Hull City](https://www.bbc.co.uk/sport/football/teams/hull-city).

In the summer of 2024 he was sold for £20m by City to Ipswich and scored 12 goals in 37 Premier League games for the newly promoted side.
