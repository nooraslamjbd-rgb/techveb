SUKKUR, (APP - UrduPoint / Pakistan Point News - 19th Sep, 2026) The mandatory requirement of Juvenile Card for appearing in the Medical and Dental Colleges Admission Test (MDCAT) 2026 in Sindh has been relaxed, SIBA Testing Services (STS) announced on Saturday.

According to a press release issued by [Sukkur](https://www.urdupoint.com/education/board-of-education-sukkur.html) IBA, the decision has been taken as per the letter issued by the Health Department, [Government](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/government.html) of Sindh.

The MDCAT Committee, constituted with the approval of the Provincial [Cabinet](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/cabinet.html), Government of Sindh, unanimously approved the relaxation to facilitate the participation of eligible candidates in the MDCAT-2026, [scheduled](https://www.urdupoint.com/business/prize-bonds-draw-schedule.html) to be conducted by SIBA Testing Services (STS) on Sunday, [September](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/september.html) 20, 2026.

However, it has been clarified that applicants who do not possess a Juvenile Card must bring their original photographic Matriculation or Intermediate mark-sheet to the test centre for verification purposes.
