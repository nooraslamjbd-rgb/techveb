## Government Slashes Fuel Prices Across Pakistan

In a recent economic update, the Government of Pakistan announced a price reduction for petroleum products following recommendations from the Oil and Gas Regulatory Authority (OGRA) and the Ministry of Energy (Petroleum Division), as reported by Dawn News.

### Updated Petrol and Diesel Rates

Under the official ex-depot price notification, the price of Petrol (MS-92) has been reduced by Rs 1.70 per litre. Concurrently, High-Speed Diesel (HSD) witnessed a price drop of Rs 3.12 per litre, bringing noticeable relief to transport and industrial sectors.

Key highlights of the fuel rate adjustment:
- **Petrol (MS-92):** Decreased by Rs 1.70 per litre.
- **High-Speed Diesel (HSD):** Decreased by Rs 3.12 per litre.
- **Regulatory Body:** Official pricing approved by OGRA and the Petroleum Division.

### Impact on Consumers and Transport

The price adjustments aim to ease inflation pressures on consumers and reduce transport expenses across the country. Lower diesel costs are expected to slightly curb supply chain and freight charges for key commodities.
