ABU DHABI, (UrduPoint / Pakistan Point News / WAM - 10th Sep, 2026) The Central [Bank](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/bank.html) of the United [Arab](https://www.urdupoint.com/arabic/) Emirates (CBUAE) participated in the Second BRICS Finance Ministers and Central [Bank](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/bank.html) Governors Meeting, which concluded today in [Mumbai](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/mumbai.html), Republic of [India](https://www.urdupoint.com/islam/india-prayers-timings.html).

The CBUAE delegation was headed by Ebrahim Obaid Ebrahim Al Zaabi, Assistant [Governor](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/governor.html) for Monetary Policy and Financial Stability.

The meeting reviewed the objectives of the BRICS financial track and the [progress](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/progress.html) achieved by its respective working groups. Participants also approved the group's joint statement and announced the launch of the BRICS Centre for Central [Bank](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/bank.html) Capacity Building, an initiative aimed at strengthening cooperation and facilitating the [exchange](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/exchange.html) of expertise among the central banks of member states.
