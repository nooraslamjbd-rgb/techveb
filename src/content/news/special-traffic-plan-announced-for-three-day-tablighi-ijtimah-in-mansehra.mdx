MANSEHRA, (APP - UrduPoint / Pakistan Point News - 8th Sep, 2026) The Mansehra Traffic Police have finalized a special traffic plan for the three-day Tablighi gathering [scheduled](https://www.urdupoint.com/business/prize-bonds-draw-schedule.html) to be held at Nokot and Sabir Shah on the directives of District Police Officer Mansehra Muhammad Azhar Khan.

Under the plan, participants and groups arriving from different areas have been assigned specific routes and parking areas.

The Traffic Police have urged all participants to park their vehicles only at the designated locations and avoid parking on narrow roads and gathering routes to ensure smooth traffic flow.

According to the traffic plan, groups arriving from Bhirkan, Mansehra City and Lasan Nawab will enter Nokot Road through Tarha via Bhirkan Road and use the designated parking area on the Nokot side.

Groups coming from Oghi, Khawajgan, Tanawal and Darband will approach the gathering through Khawajgan Chowk and park their vehicles in the designated Khawajgan parking area.

Meanwhile, groups arriving from Shinkiari, Siran Valley, Bijnah, Dhodial and Battal will use the Bijnah Interchange before proceeding towards Khawajgan Road and parking at the designated locations.

The Traffic Police said vehicles would not be allowed to proceed beyond the barriers installed along the routes. Parking at places other than the designated areas has also been prohibited.

The police have appealed to participants to follow the instructions of traffic personnel and cooperate with the authorities to prevent congestion and inconvenience to thousands of people attending the gathering.

The Traffic Police said public cooperation would be essential for maintaining safe and smooth traffic movement throughout the three-day event.
