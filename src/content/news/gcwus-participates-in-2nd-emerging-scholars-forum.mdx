SIALKOT, (APP - UrduPoint / Pakistan Point News - 25th Aug, 2026) Government College Women University Sialkot (GCWUS) participated in 2nd International Emerging Scholars Forum (IESF-2026), a release of the varsity said.

The Department of Economics, Government College Women University Sialkot (GCWUS) is proud to serve as a Co-Host of the 2nd International Emerging Scholars Forum (IESF-2026), hosted by the Institute of Agricultural and Resource Economics (IARE), University of Agriculture [Faisalabad](https://www.urdupoint.com/education/board-of-education-faisalabad.html).

The forum brings together emerging scholars and researchers from around the world to promote research, innovation, academic collaboration, and sustainable agri-food systems.

The Department of Economics on this prestigious international platform, fostering knowledge exchange and strengthening global research collaboration.
