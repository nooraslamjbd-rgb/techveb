Now, the official vivo Malaysia website has confirmed the phone’s launch, albeit with a different design.

vivo Malaysia has announced that the V80 Lite 5G will launch in the country on September 3.

The company has also confirmed that the phone will pack a 10,000mAh battery.

The company has also revealed the design of the upcoming smartphone.

It will be available in black, green, and pink color options and features a flat frame and flat rear panel.

The rear houses a square-shaped camera module with a 50MP primary sensor.

Interestingly, the teaser that surfaced earlier this week showed the vivo V80 Lite with a curved display.

This suggests that the company could offer different designs for the phone in different markets.

The Indian variant is expected to be a rebranded version of the recently launched vivo S2.
