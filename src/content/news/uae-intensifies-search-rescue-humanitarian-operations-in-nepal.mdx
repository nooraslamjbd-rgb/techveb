ABU DHABI, (UrduPoint / Pakistan Point News / WAM - 09th Sep, 2026) The [UAE](https://www.urdupoint.com/arabic/uae.html) continues its humanitarian efforts in [Nepal](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/nepal.html), supporting search and rescue and humanitarian relief operations and delivering urgent assistance to those affected, in coordination with the relevant Nepalese authorities, as part of its response to the impact of the severe flooding that has hit the country.

The [UAE](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/uae.html) Search and Rescue Team continues its field operations in Rasuwa District, using advanced search and surveying technologies, including drones for aerial imaging, surveys and three-dimensional mapping. The [technology](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/technology.html) supports more efficient search and rescue operations and helps accelerate efforts to locate [missing persons](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/missing-persons.html) and access affected areas.

As part of its humanitarian relief efforts, the [UAE](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/uae.html) has so far dispatched four relief aircraft to [Nepal](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/nepal.html) carrying a total of 186 tonnes of humanitarian and relief aid, including tents, [food](https://www.urdupoint.com/cooking/), shelter supplies, essential items, power generators and logistical equipment.

This has been complemented by the procurement of essential supplies [from](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/from.html) the local Nepalese [market](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/market.html) to help accelerate the delivery of aid to those affected.

The [UAE](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/uae.html) has also deployed a specialised search and rescue team [from](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/from.html) [Dubai](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/dubai.html) [Police](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/police.html), equipped with advanced devices, equipment and technologies, to support the Nepalese authorities in search and rescue operations, locating [missing persons](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/missing-persons.html) and assessing affected sites and areas.

Hammoud Al Afari, Director of [UAE](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/uae.html) Relief Operations, said that [UAE](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/uae.html) teams continue their missions in coordination with the relevant Nepalese authorities, deploying [all](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/all.html) available resources and technical capabilities to support search and rescue operations and humanitarian relief efforts amid challenging field conditions.
