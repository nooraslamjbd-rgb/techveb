(UrduPoint / Pakistan Point News / WAM - 29th Aug, 2026) [DUBAI](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/dubai.html), 29th [August](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/august.html), 2026 (WAM) — [UAE](https://www.urdupoint.com/arabic/uae.html) President His Highness Sheikh Mohamed bin Zayed Al Nahyan and His Highness Sheikh Mohammed bin [Rashid](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/rashid.html) Al Maktoum, Vice President, [Prime Minister](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/prime-minister.html) and Ruler of [Dubai](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/dubai.html), met today at Al Marmoom in [Dubai](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/dubai.html).

During the meeting, Their Highnesses engaged in cordial discussions and reviewed a number of issues related to national and citizens’ affairs, as well as efforts to support the [UAE](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/uae.html)’s development journey and fulfil the aspirations of its people towards a more prosperous future.

The meeting was attended by His Excellency Sheikh Mohammed bin Hamad bin Tahnoon Al Nahyan, Advisor to the [UAE](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/uae.html) President, along with a number of ministers and senior officials.
