DUBAI, (UrduPoint / Pakistan Point News / WAM - 09th Sep, 2026) H.H. Sheikh Ahmed bin Saeed Al Maktoum, Chairman of [Dubai](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/dubai.html) Holding, witnessed the signing of an AED5 [billion](https://www.urdupoint.com/business/prize-bonds.html) construction contract between [Dubai](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/dubai.html) Holding and [China](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/china.html) State Construction Engineering Corporation [middle](https://www.urdupoint.com/education/middle-result.html) East (CSCEC ME) for the delivery of the Group’s new headquarters and Jumeirah Residences Emirates Towers.

The building contract represents the single largest [Dubai](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/dubai.html) Holding has awarded to [date](https://www.urdupoint.com/islam/today-islamic-date.html), reflecting the scale of capital the Group continues to deploy into projects that support [Dubai](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/dubai.html)'s long-term growth.

Set to become one of [Dubai](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/dubai.html)'s most distinctive corporate landmarks, the new headquarters will bring the Group's businesses together in a workplace built around collaboration, sustainability and [employee](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/employment.html) wellbeing, while opening a prime new address in one of the city's most sought-after commercial locations. The building is [scheduled](https://www.urdupoint.com/business/prize-bonds-draw-schedule.html) to open in 2029.

The award will also advance the delivery of Jumeirah Residences Emirates Towers, comprising 754 branded residences across two towers in the [same](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/same.html) location, adding a striking new presence to the city’s luxury residential [market](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/market.html).

Mohamed bin Hadi Al Hussaini, Minister of State for Financial Affairs; and Ou Boqian, Consul General of the People's Republic of [China](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/china.html) in [Dubai](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/dubai.html) and the Northern Emirates, also attended the signing ceremony alongside Amit Kaushal, Group Chief Executive Officer of [Dubai](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/dubai.html) Holding; Tian Sanchuan, Chairman of CSCEC ME and senior representatives [from](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/from.html) both organisations.

Sheikh Ahmed bin Saeed said, “Dubai’s success has been built on a clear vision for the future, the confidence to invest with purpose and the discipline to turn ambition into [progress](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/progress.html). We continue to build [from](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/from.html) a position of strength, committing capital to assets that support economic growth and create value for our people, communities and future generations.

For more than two decades, [Dubai](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/dubai.html) Holding has been an important contributor to the emirate’s transformation, enabling economic activity equivalent to 30% of [Dubai](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/dubai.html)’s GDP. This investment reflects the [same](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/same.html) long-term ambition: to keep setting new standards for what a global city can achieve, and to strengthen [Dubai](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/dubai.html) as a global [hub](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/hub.html) for [business](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/business.html), investment and talent.”

Designed by Skidmore, Owings & Merrill (SOM), the globally renowned architecture, engineering and urban planning practice behind many of the [world](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/world.html)’s most recognisable buildings, [Dubai](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/dubai.html) Holding’s new headquarters is designed as a highly connected environment.

Defined by its distinctive circular form, the office will be organised around a central open-air atrium and landscaped courtyard, with expansive terraces designed to encourage collaboration and the [exchange](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/exchange.html) of ideas.

Employee wellbeing, sustainability, and [technology](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/technology.html) are central to the design.

Reflecting the Group’s commitment to sustainable development, the new headquarters has been designed to support resource efficiency, enhance the workplace experience and create a future-focused environment for [Dubai](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/dubai.html) Holding's people, with ambitions to meet leading international sustainability and wellbeing standards while supporting a lower-carbon future.

Amit Kaushal said, “This commitment reflects the scale at which [Dubai](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/dubai.html) Holding continues to invest for the future. We have a clear strategy for growth and a disciplined approach to capital deployment, focused on strengthening our portfolio, advancing high-quality assets and creating sustainable long-term value for the Group and the city.

Our new headquarters marks the next phase in our evolution. Built to world-class design principles, it sets a new benchmark for prime office space in [Dubai](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/dubai.html) and is designed for the people who will shape our future, today and in the years to come. Jumeirah Residences Emirates Towers carries the [same](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/same.html) ambition in another form, adding a residential landmark to one of [Dubai](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/dubai.html)'s most prominent districts and adding further strength to our real estate portfolio.”

Tian Sanchuan said, “We are honoured to have been awarded these landmark projects by [Dubai](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/dubai.html) Holding, reflecting the mutual trust and confidence between our two organisations. CSCEC ME’s journey in [Dubai](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/dubai.html) has been made possible by its exceptional [business](https://www.urdupoint.com/business/prize-bonds.html) environment, founded on openness, transparency, fairness and opportunity.

These landmark developments reflect the resilience and strength of [Dubai](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/dubai.html)’s economy and reinforce our confidence in its continued growth. We remain committed to [Dubai](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/dubai.html) and are proud to contribute to the development of this remarkable city and its ambitious future.”

CSCEC ME will also deliver Jumeirah Residences Emirates Towers, a flagship residential development by Meraas in one of [Dubai](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/dubai.html)'s most established districts. Designed by SCDA Architects, the development will combine contemporary architecture with Jumeirah’s renowned Arabian hospitality and service expertise, alongside a curated collection of lifestyle, wellness and leisure experiences.

This development will benefit [from](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/from.html) direct connectivity to some of the most prominent [business](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/business.html), cultural and lifestyle destinations in [Dubai](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/dubai.html). Handover is scheduled for 2030.

Operating in the [UAE](https://www.urdupoint.com/arabic/uae.html) since 2003, CSCEC ME has delivered more than 110 large-scale projects across the Gulf, spanning residential, commercial, hospitality and infrastructure. It is supported by the global capabilities of its parent [company](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/company.html), [China](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/china.html) State Construction Engineering Corporation, one of the largest construction and investment groups in the [world](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/world.html).
