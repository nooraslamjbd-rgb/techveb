ISLAMABAD, (UrduPoint / Pakistan Point News - 11th Sep, 2026) [Pakistan](https://www.urdupoint.com/pakistan/) Tehreek-e-Insaf Chairman Barrister Gohar Khan has said that an [agreement](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/agreement.html) was reached with Jamiat Ulema-e-Islam-Fazl chief Maulana Fazlur Rehman on forming a joint [opposition](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/opposition.html) in the [Senate](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/senate.html) and [National Assembly](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/national-assembly.html). Speaking to journalists after meeting Maulana Fazlur Rehman in the [Federal](https://www.urdupoint.com/education/board-of-education-federal.html) capital, Barrister Gohar said the [opposition](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/opposition.html) would adopt a joint strategy inside [Parliament](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/parliament.html) and [all](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/all.html) political parties would be taken into confidence on important national issues. He said discussions were held with Maulana Fazlur Rehman regarding a joint [opposition](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/opposition.html), adding that an [agreement](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/agreement.html) on establishing a joint [opposition](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/opposition.html) in the [Senate](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/senate.html) and [National Assembly](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/national-assembly.html) had already been reached.

He said today’s meeting was the first regarding the joint [opposition](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/opposition.html) and had been encouraging. The [PTI](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/pakistan-tehreek-e-insaf.html) chairman said it had been decided to take [all](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/all.html) parties into confidence on national issues.

He added that an All-Parties Conference ([APC](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/apc.html)) would be convened on the issue of provinces and other important matters. Consensus consultations would also be held through the joint [opposition](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/opposition.html) platform regarding [Assembly](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/assembly.html) sessions and prisoners’ issues. Barrister Gohar said the future course of action would be decided through the united [opposition](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/opposition.html) platform and meetings of the joint [opposition](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/opposition.html) would continue in the future. On the other hand, JUI Secretary-General Maulana Abdul Ghafoor Haideri said they welcomed the [opposition](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/opposition.html)’s arrival.

He said the consultative meeting also discussed convening an All-Parties Conference, while various important matters, including the creation of new provinces in the country, came under consideration. Maulana Abdul Ghafoor Haideri said the [APC](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/apc.html) would discuss the wave of unrest in [Balochistan](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/balochistan.html) and [Khyber Pakhtunkhwa](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/khyber-pakhtunkhwa.html), terrorism and other issues.

He said efforts would be made for the [opposition](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/opposition.html) to play its role in addressing the problems currently facing the country.
