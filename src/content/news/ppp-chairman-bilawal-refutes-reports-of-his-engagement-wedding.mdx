## Bilawal Bhutto Dismisses Viral Marriage Reports

Pakistan Peoples Party (PPP) Chairman Bilawal Bhutto-Zardari has officially denied ongoing social media rumors regarding his engagement and wedding. Speaking in a video statement, the PPP leader clarified that no such personal event has taken place and dismissed claims linking him to European royalty.

"Social media has gotten my baat pakki, engagement, and wedding done," Bilawal stated, adding that he does not know any "Princess Gloria." Speculative reports had previously claimed that President Asif Ali Zardari had traveled to Liechtenstein to finalize a royal match. Bilawal emphasized that any real decision regarding his life would be announced directly by his family.

### Shifting Focus to Critical Public Matters

Bilawal contended that the viral social media ruckus was intentionally created to distract the public's attention from pressing national challenges. He urged media platforms and the public to redirect their attention to actual governance issues.

- **PIMS Hospital Incident:** Bilawal highlighted the tragic fire at the Pakistan Institute of Medical Sciences in Islamabad that claimed the lives of 14 infants.
- **Provincial Autonomy:** He criticized administrative failures and strongly rejected proposals aiming to divide Sindh into smaller administrative units.

According to reports from Dawn News, Bilawal reaffirmed that discussions on provincial governance belong inside legislative assemblies, such as the Sindh Assembly, rather than being debated on social media platforms.
