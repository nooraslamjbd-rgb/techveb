## Celtic's European Disaster in Austria

Celtic experienced one of the most painful European exits in their modern history after collapsing against LASK at the Raiffeisen Arena. Despite holding a comfortable four-goal aggregate lead, the Scottish champions surrendered their advantage in a chaotic second half, eventually succumbing to a 5-4 aggregate loss after extra time.

According to BBC News, the defeat echoes previous European disappointments, including last season's exit to Kairat Almaty. However, the nature of this second-half collapse has left fans and pundits questioning the squad's resilience under pressure.

### Martin O'Neill Takes Full Responsibility

Speaking after the final whistle, Celtic manager Martin O'Neill offered no excuses for his side's capitulation.

"I'm responsible for this one," O'Neill stated. "Without a doubt. Naturally it took me a long time to get over the Uefa Cup final in 2003 but this was majorly disappointing."

O'Neill acknowledged the gap between expectations and reality, adding that Celtic remains "a million miles from the finished article."

### Financial Fallout and Transfer Window Impact

The consequences of this exit extend far beyond the pitch. Missing out on the UEFA Champions League group stage means Celtic will forgo tens of millions in prize money and broadcasting revenue.

* **Financial Loss:** Loss of guaranteed Champions League group stage windfall.
* **Transfer Pressure:** Limited budget to finalize key targets before the deadline.
* **Domestic Focus:** Rebuilding momentum for the Scottish Premiership title defense.

Celtic must now regroup quickly to navigate the remaining transfer window and refocus on their domestic campaign.
