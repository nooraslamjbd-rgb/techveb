Reading English became difficult for him, and a video went viral on social media.

KARACHI, (UrduPoint / Pakistan Point News - 12th Sep, 2026) [Sindh](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/sindh.html) [Assembly](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/assembly.html) parliamentary leader Muhammad Shabbir Qureshi faced difficulty [reading](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/reading.html) an [English](https://www.urdupoint.com/dictionary/) [resolution](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/resolution.html) presented to pay tribute to Quaid-e-Azam [Muhammad Ali Jinnah](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/muhammad-ali-jinnah.html), while a video of the incident went viral on [social media](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/social-media.html).

According to [media](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/media.html) reports, Muhammad Shabbir Qureshi, an MPA elected [from](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/from.html) [Karachi](https://www.urdupoint.com/education/board-of-education-karachi.html)’s Keamari, presented the [resolution](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/resolution.html) in English.

When he read out its text, he struggled with the language, prompting members seated in the [assembly](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/assembly.html) to laugh at his English.

> 

However, he eventually managed to read the [resolution](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/resolution.html).

A [resolution](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/resolution.html) paying tribute to Quaid-e-Azam was subsequently passed by the [Sindh](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/sindh.html) [Assembly](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/assembly.html). Members supporting the [PPP](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/ppp.html), MQM-Pakistan, Jamaat-e-Islami and [PTI](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/pakistan-tehreek-e-insaf.html) presented the [resolution](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/resolution.html). It highlighted [Pakistan](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/pakistan.html)’s struggle under Quaid-e-Azam’s historic leadership and paid tribute to his efforts for the establishment of [Pakistan](https://www.urdupoint.com/pakistan/).
