AL AIN, (UrduPoint / Pakistan Point News / WAM - 01st Sep, 2026) Bahraini Al-Nassr opened the second round of the International Youth Volleyball Tournament by securing their second consecutive victory, defeating Qatari Al-Rayyan 3–0.

The tournament, organised by Al Ain Club under the patronage of the Department of Culture and Tourism – [Abu Dhabi](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/abu-dhabi.html), in strategic partnership with the [Abu Dhabi](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/abu-dhabi.html) [sports](https://www.urdupoint.com/sports/) Council, and in technical cooperation with the [UAE](https://www.urdupoint.com/arabic/uae.html) Volleyball Federation, is being held at Khalifa bin Zayed [Sports](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/sports.html) Hall in Al Ain.

In the “Kuwaiti clash,” Al-Qadsia defeated Burgan 3–0, securing their first victory in the tournament after losing in the opening round to Qatari Al-Rayyan.

The third match witnessed strong competition that extended into the fifth set, with Qatari Al-Arabi securing victory over Al Ain 3–2.

With these results, Bahraini Al-Nassr continued their strong start with a perfect record [from](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/from.html) two matches, while Kuwaiti Al-Qadsia and Qatari Al-Arabi joined the winners’ [circle](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/circle.html), with competition among the clubs continuing in the coming rounds to achieve more positive [results](https://www.urdupoint.com/education/results.html) and move closer to competing for the [top](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/top.html) positions.

The organising committee confirmed that the competition is witnessing strong technical performances [from](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/from.html) the participating clubs, reflecting the importance of the tournament in providing youth players with opportunities to gain experience and exposure in a competitive atmosphere among Gulf clubs.

Wednesday will be a rest day for the participating teams, along with an honoring ceremony and an [exchange](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/exchange.html) of commemorative shields, ahead of the resumption of the competition on Thursday with three matches: Al Ain against Bahraini Al-Nassr, followed by Qatari Al-Rayyan against Kuwaiti Burgan, and finally Qatari Al-Arabi against Kuwaiti Al-Qadsia.
