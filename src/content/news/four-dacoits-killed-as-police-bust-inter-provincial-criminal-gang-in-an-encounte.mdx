PESHAWAR, (APP - UrduPoint / Pakistan Point News - 30th Aug, 2026) City Police busted a notorious six-member dacoit network on Sunday, killing four high-profile robbers during a police encounter within jurisdiction of Yakkatoot police station.

According to CCPO Mian Saeed Ahmed, the inter-provincial network operated across [Peshawar](https://www.urdupoint.com/education/board-of-education-peshawar.html), [Malakand](https://www.urdupoint.com/education/board-of-education-malakand.html), Dir, [Swat](https://www.urdupoint.com/education/board-of-education-swat.html), [Rawalpindi](https://www.urdupoint.com/education/board-of-education-rawalpindi.html), Islamabad, Punjab, and [Karachi](https://www.urdupoint.com/education/board-of-education-karachi.html) using sophisticated tactics.

The killed criminals were identified infamous Waqar alias Major, a gang leader, Sahil alias Saud Mohmand, Ijaz alias Pehlawan, Hafeez alias Charra, Gran Wali, and Ali alias Sheena, all of whom were wanted for numerous serious offenses across the country.

Two other members of the gang managed to escape.

The gang had been actively carrying out organized crime across Khyber Pakhtunkhwa, Punjab, Sindh, and Islamabad for the past 15 years.

The suspects frequently impersonated security and intelligence personnel by wearing counterfeit uniforms, utilizing Wi-Fi jammers, wireless communication devices, and modern weaponry to gain entry into homes under the guise of search operations before looting cash, gold, and valuable items at gunpoint, CCPO told.
