ABU DHABI, (UrduPoint / Pakistan Point News / WAM - 02nd Sep, 2026) Lana Zaki Nusseibeh, [UAE](https://www.urdupoint.com/arabic/uae.html) Minister of State, received a copy of the credentials of Marie Audouard, Ambassador Extraordinary and Plenipotentiary of the Republic of [France](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/france.html) to the United [Arab](https://www.urdupoint.com/arabic/) Emirates.

Nusseibeh wished the new Ambassador success in the performance of her duties and affirmed the [UAE](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/uae.html)’s commitment to expanding cooperation with the Republic of [France](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/france.html) in a manner that advances the shared interests of both countries and their friendly peoples.

For her part, Marie Audouard commended the [UAE](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/uae.html)’s distinguished regional and international standing under the wise leadership of President His Highness Sheikh Mohamed bin Zayed Al Nahyan, and expressed her aspiration to further strengthen and develop bilateral relations across various fields.
