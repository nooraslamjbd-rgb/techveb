MIRPURKHAS, (APP - UrduPoint / Pakistan Point News - 7th Sep, 2026) An art event titled “Colour of Courage” was organised on Monday in [Mirpurkhas](https://www.urdupoint.com/education/board-of-education-mirpurkhas.html) to mark Defence Day of [Pakistan](https://www.urdupoint.com/pakistan/), providing students an opportunity to showcase their artistic talent and express their patriotic sentiments. The event was arranged by AJG Studio in collaboration with the Sindh Culture, Tourism, Antiquities and Archives Department.

Students from various schools and educational institutions participated enthusiastically in the event, which was attended by prominent [business](https://www.urdupoint.com/business/prize-bonds.html) and social personalities, Head of Department at Shaheed Allah Buksh Soomro University of Art, Design and Architecture Jamshoro Fazal Achakzai Kashif, university students, All Private [school](https://www.urdupoint.com/education/schools.html) Management Association President Faisal Khanzai, [Suzuki](https://www.urdupoint.com/cars/maker/suzuki.html) Tharparkar Motors owner Riaz Sheikh and other distinguished guests.

Speaking to the media, AJG Studio Founder, Director and Pride of Performance awardee Abdul Jabbar Gul said the initiative aimed to provide young people with a platform to express their creativity through fine arts while paying tribute to the sacrifices and services of the Armed Forces of Pakistan.

He also held an interactive question-and-answer session with students and appreciated the contributions of AJG Studio team members, including Matanat Hussain, Maryam Zahid Ali, Junaid Jutt, Abdul Rehman Alamgir, Hazik Ali, Faraz Jabbar, Bilal Jabbar, Abdul Sattar, Ashir Laghari, Sana Laghari, Muhammad Wasim, Abid Khalil Ansari and others.

The participating students displayed a variety of artworks and took part in creative activities centred on patriotism and national spirit.

At the conclusion of the event, the organisers expressed gratitude to the distinguished guests, students and all participants for their presence and enthusiastic involvement, describing the initiative as an important platform for nurturing creativity and patriotic values among the younger generation.
