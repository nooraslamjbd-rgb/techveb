CAPITALS, (UrduPoint / Pakistan Point News / WAM - 09th Sep, 2026) Major European clubs made strong starts to their 2026–27 UEFA Champions League campaigns on Tuesday evening, with [Manchester City](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/manchester-city.html), [Real Madrid](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/real-madrid.html), [Borussia Dortmund](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/borussia.html), and Real Betis [all](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/all.html) securing maximum points in their opening matchday fixtures.

Manchester City kicked off their campaign with a 2-0 away victory against FC [Porto](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/porto.html) at the Estádio do Dragão. Striker Erling Haaland provided the breakthrough for the [English](https://www.urdupoint.com/dictionary/) side immediately after the restart, opening the scoring in the 47th minute before doubling the advantage in the 90th minute to seal the win.

At the [Santiago](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/santiago.html) Bernabéu, [Real Madrid](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/real-madrid.html) earned a 2-1 home win over Italian side [Inter Milan](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/inter-milan.html). Early strikes [from](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/from.html) Kylian Mbappé in the 14th minute and Federico Valverde in the 23rd minute put the hosts in control. Inter's Carlos Augusto pulled a goal back in the 77th minute, but the Spanish side held on to secure [all](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/all.html) three points.

Borussia [Dortmund](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/dortmund.html) edged past Spanish side Villarreal 3-2 at Signal Iduna Park in a high-scoring encounter.

Real Betis matched that scoreline, capturing a 3-2 away victory against French outfit [Lille](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/lille.html).
