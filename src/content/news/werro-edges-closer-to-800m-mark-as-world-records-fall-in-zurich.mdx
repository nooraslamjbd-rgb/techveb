## Audrey Werro Delivers Historic 800m Performance in Zurich

Swiss European champion Audrey Werro nearly made track and field history at the Zurich Diamond League. In front of a crowd of 25,000 spectators, the 22-year-old athlete clocked an impressive time of 1:53.70 in the women's 800 meters.

This performance stands as the third-fastest outdoor 800m run in track history. Werro finished just 0.42 seconds behind Jarmila Kratochvilova’s long-standing world record of 1:53.28, established 43 years ago.

### Pacing Lights and Fierce Competition

During the final stretch, Werro ran almost shoulder-to-shoulder with the infield pacing lights set to world-record speed. Although she narrowly missed the mark, Werro shaved another tenth of a second off her previous personal best.

The race featured strong competition, with world 400m hurdles champion Femke Broeders-Bol finishing second during her debut 800m season. Meanwhile, British Olympic champion Keely Hodgkinson withdrew prior to the event to recover from recent racing exertions.

### Ruthless Consistency on the World Stage

Werro's transition to full-time training after completing her studies has yielded remarkable results. She now holds four of the top-10 fastest 800m times in history, all set during this summer season.

"I am so proud to be able to run those sub-1:54 times," Werro stated after the race, expressing strong confidence in her future track career.
