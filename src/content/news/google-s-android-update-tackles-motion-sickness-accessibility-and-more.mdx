## Key Takeaways

- Google introduced Motion Assist to reduce motion sickness while using Android phones in vehicles.
- Guided Vision combines Gemini AI and camera guidance for blind and low-vision users.
- Motion Assist uses interactive bubble overlays similar to Apple's Vehicle Motion Cues.
- Guided Vision works on devices running Android 9+ where Gemini is available.
- Updates include AI memory aids for lost items and new Google Messages options.

Google has officially announced a new wave of Android updates aimed at improving accessibility, utility, and personalization across its ecosystem. As reported by TechCrunch, the latest rollout introduces AI-powered features designed to assist users in daily tasks and combat vehicle-induced motion sickness.

## Motion Assist: Reducing Vehicle Motion Sickness
One of the standout features is Motion Assist, an innovative tool tailored for users who experience motion sickness while viewing screens in moving vehicles.

* **Visual Cue Overlay:** Adds a customizable bubble overlay on the screen that moves responsively with the vehicle’s motion, bridging the sensory gap between sight and physical movement.
* **Customization & Controls:** Users can adjust bubble shape, color, and transparency, or set the feature to activate automatically via Quick Settings.
* **Availability:** Targeted for devices running Android 17.

## Guided Vision: AI-Powered Accessibility with Gemini
Designed for blind and low-vision users, Guided Vision leverages Google Gemini AI alongside the device's camera.

### Key Capabilities of Guided Vision
* Reads fine text on food labels and restaurant menus in low-light environments.
* Identifies everyday household objects accurately.
* Provides real-time spoken prompts to help users position, pan, or center the camera correctly.

Guided Vision is rolling out to phones running Android 9 and newer in regions where Gemini is supported.

## Enhanced Daily Utility and Personalization
Google is also integrating Gemini to assist users with tracking personal belongings and offering expanded personalization features in Google Messages. These updates reflect Google’s ongoing effort to combine practical AI features with core mobile accessibility.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is Google Motion Assist on Android?

Motion Assist is an Android feature designed to reduce vehicle motion sickness by displaying visual bubble overlays that sync with vehicle movement.

### How does Guided Vision work on Android?

Guided Vision uses Gemini AI and the phone camera to provide real-time audio guidance and describe surroundings for visually impaired users.

### Which Android versions support Guided Vision and Motion Assist?

Guided Vision supports Android 9 and newer where Gemini is supported, while Motion Assist targets Android 17.
