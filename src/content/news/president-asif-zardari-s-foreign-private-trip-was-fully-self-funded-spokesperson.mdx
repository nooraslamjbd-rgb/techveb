ISLAMABAD, (UrduPoint / Pakistan Point News - 12th Sep, 2026) The spokesperson for the President’s House has clarified that President [Asif Zardari](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/asif-ali-zardari.html)’s private foreign [visit](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/visit.html) was funded entirely [from](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/from.html) his personal resources.

According to a statement posted by the President’s House on X, the spokesperson said the recent foreign [visit](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/visit.html) was wholly private and that [all](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/all.html) expenses were personally borne by President [Asif Ali Zardari](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/asif-ali-zardari.html).

According to the spokesperson, the president also privately chartered an aircraft for the [visit](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/visit.html).

All aircraft-related expenses, including those for the crew, parking and accommodation, as well as other associated costs, were paid by the president [from](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/from.html) his own pocket. Meanwhile, a major debate is also underway over the expenses of [Punjab](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/punjab.html) [Chief Minister](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/chief-minister.html) [Maryam Nawaz Sharif](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/maryam-nawaz-sharif.html)’s private [visit](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/visit.html) to [London](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/london.html).

In her response on X, [Punjab](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/punjab.html) [Information Minister](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/information-minister.html) Azma Bukhari said she had already stated, as [information minister](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/information-minister.html) and with full responsibility, that the [chief minister](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/chief-minister.html) would pay [all](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/all.html) expenses herself.

She said political orphans and “U-tumors” had been busy “eating dollars” over the [chief minister](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/chief-minister.html)’s [London](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/london.html) [visit](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/visit.html) for several days. [Chief Minister](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/chief-minister.html) [Maryam Nawaz Sharif](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/maryam-nawaz-sharif.html) had made it clear before departing for [London](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/london.html) that she would bear [all](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/all.html) expenses of the [visit](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/visit.html) herself.

However, even before she returned home [from](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/from.html) [London](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/london.html), those fond of criticism began spreading propaganda without knowing the facts. The expenses would have been estimated and paid after the [visit](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/visit.html) concluded.

Everything in the [government](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/government.html) system is handled according to legal rules and procedures: an estimate is prepared and payment is made through a challan. It is not EasyPaisa or JazzCash, where the account can be presented at the press of a button before the journey has even ended.

The [visit](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/visit.html) had not yet been completed when the propaganda began. Those who themselves squandered [billions](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/billion.html) of rupees [from](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/from.html) the national exchequer on luxuries are now putting on a cheap act of pretending to be blameless, she said.

Those whose own reputations are covered in stains of [corruption](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/corruption.html) should be ashamed of making allegations against others, even half-asleep. Get ready once again to face embarrassment and humiliation—just wait a little longer, she added.
