(UrduPoint / Pakistan Point News / WAM - 08th Sep, 2026) [MUSCAT](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/muscat.html), 8th [September](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/september.html), 2026 (WAM) – His Majesty Sultan Haitham bin Tarik of [Oman](https://www.urdupoint.com/arabic/oman.html) and President Paul Kagame of the Republic of [Rwanda](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/rwanda.html) held an official session of talks at Al Hosn Palace in [Salalah](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/salalah.html) on Monday.

Oman [news](https://www.urdupoint.com/urdu-news) Agency (ONA) said the discussions focused on cooperation and investment in mining, renewable energy, [food](https://www.urdupoint.com/cooking/) security, land ports, logistics and healthcare, with the aim of strengthening investment partnership between the two countries in a manner that meets the aspirations of their peoples and serves their mutual interests.
