ISLAMABAD, (APP - UrduPoint / Pakistan Point News - 7th Sep, 2026) Inspector General of Police (IGP) Islamabad Syed Ali Nasir Rizvi on Monday paid glowing tribute to the “Shaheens” of the [Pakistan](https://www.urdupoint.com/pakistan/) Air Force (PAF) on Air Force Day, saluting their courage, professionalism and outstanding role in defending the country’s skies.

An official told APP that in his message marking September 7, Air Force Day, the IGP Rizvi said the brave personnel of the PAF had always demonstrated exceptional courage, bravery and professional excellence in the defence of Pakistan.

He said Air Force Day reminded the nation of the unforgettable sacrifices made in defence of the homeland, adding that the sacrifices of PAF martyrs and war veterans remained a guiding light for the nation.

Rizvi said the nation was breathing in a free atmosphere today because of the sacrifices rendered by its martyrs.

“The entire nation salutes the guardians of Pakistan’s airspace,” he said, adding that the PAF had always played an outstanding role in the defence of the country.

IGP Rizvi said the services of the Pakistan Air Force towards national defence and security were unforgettable and would always be remembered with pride.

He said Air Force Day strengthened the spirit of patriotism and the resolve to defend the homeland.

IGP Rizvi also saluted all PAF officers, personnel, martyrs and their families, paying tribute to their services and sacrifices for Pakistan.
