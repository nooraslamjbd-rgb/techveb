It has been clearly stated that Maryam Nawaz will personally bear the expenses of her London visit, while those using the Punjab chief minister’s statement to spread hatred among provinces should apologize themselves, says the senior provincial minister.

LAHORE, (UrduPoint / Pakistan Point News - 12th Sep, 2026) The verbal war over [Punjab](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/punjab.html) [Chief Minister](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/chief-minister.html) [Maryam Nawaz](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/maryam-nawaz-sharif.html)’s [visit](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/visit.html) to [London](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/london.html), use of an aircraft and political statements has intensified, with [Pakistan](https://www.urdupoint.com/pakistan/) [Muslim](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/muslim.html) League-Nawaz ([PML-N](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/muslim-league-n.html)) central leader and [Punjab](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/punjab.html) Senior Provincial Minister [Marriyum Aurangzeb](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/maryam-aurangzeb.html) also joining in and strongly criticizing her opponents. According to details, [Marriyum Aurangzeb](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/maryam-aurangzeb.html) made it clear in a strong reaction on [social media](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/social-media.html) that the real reason behind the current uproar is neither an aircraft nor a personal [visit](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/visit.html), but opponents’ panic over [Maryam Nawaz](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/maryam-nawaz-sharif.html)’s growing popularity among the public and her outstanding performance. She said it had been clearly announced that [Maryam Nawaz](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/maryam-nawaz-sharif.html) would pay [all](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/all.html) expenses for the [London](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/london.html) [visit](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/visit.html) [from](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/from.html) her own pocket.

She added that those using the [Punjab](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/punjab.html) [chief minister](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/chief-minister.html)’s statement to create hatred among the provinces and division among the nation should themselves apologize to the public.

> 

Marriyum Aurangzeb strongly criticized statements by the [Khyber Pakhtunkhwa](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/khyber-pakhtunkhwa.html) [government](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/government.html) spokesperson and [PTI](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/pakistan-tehreek-e-insaf.html) leadership, saying their low-level remarks were actually a continuation of [PTI](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/pakistan-tehreek-e-insaf.html)’s old tradition of using foul language against [women](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/women.html).

Unfortunately, she said, [Khyber Pakhtunkhwa](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/khyber-pakhtunkhwa.html) is controlled by elements completely unfamiliar with the great traditions of proud Pashtuns who respect mothers, sisters and daughters. The [Punjab](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/punjab.html) senior provincial minister said in strong terms that while terrorists blow people to pieces with guns and [suicide](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/suicide.html) jackets, their political facilitators use language as a [weapon](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/weapon.html) to insult people, particularly [women](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/women.html).
