## CJP Yahya Afridi Emphasizes District Judiciary Reforms

Chief Justice of Pakistan (CJP) Yahya Afridi has urged immediate structural reforms to empower district courts, calling them the "backbone" of the nation's legal system. Speaking at an official judicial conference in Islamabad, CJP Afridi stressed that public confidence in state institutions relies heavily on frontline legal operations.

He noted that public trust is neither inherited nor proclaimed. Instead, it must be earned through judicial integrity, competence, fairness, and institutional support.

### Key Administrative Overhauls Introduced

To address historical delays and funding bottlenecks, CJP Afridi announced major operational changes through the National Judicial Policy Making Committee (NJPMC):

- **21-Day Approval Mandate:** A strict 21-day limit has been implemented for evaluating and disbursing money from the Access to Justice Fund.
- **Provincial Liaison Officers:** Additional secretaries will be stationed in each province to bridge communication between high courts, district courts, and the NJPMC.
- **Budget Efficiency:** Accelerating administrative reviews will prevent project abandonments caused by inflation and cost overruns.

### Addressing Disconnect in Remote Districts

During visits to remote areas such as Chitral, Gwadar, Sadiqabad, Bahawalnagar, and Nagarparkar, CJP Afridi observed a clear disconnect between local courts and central policymaking bodies, as reported by Dawn News.

The new measures aim to streamline resource allocation, ensuring that judges in remote parts of Pakistan receive the operational support necessary to deliver timely justice.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Why did CJP Afridi call the district judiciary the backbone of justice?

Because for most citizens, district courts serve as the primary and initial point of contact with Pakistan's justice system.

### What is the National Judicial Policy Making Committee (NJPMC)?

The NJPMC is a statutory body created in 2002 to coordinate judicial policy and allocate development funds to lower courts.

### What reforms were introduced for judicial funding approvals?

A strict 21-day timeline was instituted for approving projects and disbursing finances from the Access to Justice Fund.
