The opposition alliance will meet on September 15 to make key decisions and review preparations and progress on the long march, its spokesperson said.

ISLAMABAD, (UrduPoint / Pakistan Point News - 11th Sep, 2026) Spokesperson for the Tehreek Tahaffuz Aain [Pakistan](https://www.urdupoint.com/pakistan/) Akhunzada Hussain Yousafzai said that assurances had been given to [Pakistan](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/pakistan.html) Tehreek-e-Insaf ([PTI](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/pakistan-tehreek-e-insaf.html)) regarding its participation in the [long march](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/long-march.html). Speaking to journalists in [Islamabad](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/islamabad.html), Akhunzada Yousafzai said the [long march](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/long-march.html) had been announced by the [Chief Minister](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/chief-minister.html) of [Khyber Pakhtunkhwa](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/khyber-pakhtunkhwa.html), who would [lead](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/lead.html) it.

He said the [opposition](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/opposition.html) leadership was travelling to [Karachi](https://www.urdupoint.com/education/board-of-education-karachi.html) for the death anniversary of [Ataullah Mengal](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/ataullah-mengal.html), and a proposal had been made to hold the Tehreek Tahaffuz Aain [Pakistan](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/pakistan.html) meeting in [Karachi](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/karachi.html) as well. The spokesperson said the [opposition](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/opposition.html) [alliance](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/alliance.html) would meet on [September](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/september.html) 15, where important decisions would be made.

Preparations for and [progress](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/progress.html) on the [long march](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/long-march.html) would also be reviewed.
