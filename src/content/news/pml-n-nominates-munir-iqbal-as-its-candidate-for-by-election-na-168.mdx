BAHAWALPUR, (APP - UrduPoint / Pakistan Point News - 30th Aug, 2026) [Pakistan](https://www.urdupoint.com/pakistan/) Muslim League-N has nominated, Malik Munir Iqbal as its candidate to contest

NA-168 by-election [Bahawalpur](https://www.urdupoint.com/education/board-of-education-bahawalpur.html) seat that was vacant after the demise of his father,

Malik Iqbal Channar.

The Channar family has confirmed that official ticket of Pakistan Muslim League-N for

contesting NA-168 by-election Bahawalpur seat has been issued to Malik Munir Iqbal

Channar. The by-election will be held on September 27.
