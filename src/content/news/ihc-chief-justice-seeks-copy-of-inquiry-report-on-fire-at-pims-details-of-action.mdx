## Islamabad High Court Demands PIMS Fire Inquiry Details

The Islamabad High Court (IHC) has officially summoned the inquiry report submitted to Prime Minister Shehbaz Sharif regarding the devastating fire at the Pakistan Institute of Medical Sciences (PIMS). Chief Justice Sardar Muhammad Sarfaraz Dogar directed authorities to submit complete details of actions taken against responsible officials.

### Judicial Scrutiny of Hospital Management

During hearings for petitions filed by lawyer Qasim Iqbal Jalali, Chief Justice Dogar described the incident as a "terrible tragedy" highlighting the tragic loss of 14 children. The petitioner argued against bureaucrat-led inquiry committees, questioning whether officials could fairly investigate their own department.

Key points raised during the court proceeding include:
- **Accountability Concerns:** Petitioners argued that internal departmental inquiries fail to fix clear responsibility.
- **Legal Authority:** The Chief Justice questioned the specific legal basis under the Inquiry Act to order a separate judicial commission.
- **Executive Domain:** The court noted that administrative commissions fall primarily within executive domain rather than court mandates.

### Government Actions and Suspensions

According to court submissions, immediate administrative steps were initiated following the incident. Additional Attorney General Rashid Hafeez informed the court that the health secretary and key hospital administration officials were suspended immediately after the tragedy.

As reported by Dawn News, the court continues to examine accountability measures to ensure proper legal oversight without overstepping judicial boundaries into administrative functions.
