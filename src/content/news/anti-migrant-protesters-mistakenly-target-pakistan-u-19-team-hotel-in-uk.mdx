## Anti-Immigration Demonstrators Target Pakistan U-19 Team Hotel

Anti-immigration protesters in southern England mistakenly targeted a hotel hosting Pakistan’s U-19 cricket squad after confusing them with asylum seekers, according to British media reports cited by Dawn News.

The incident took place outside a Marriott Hotel in Cosham, a northern suburb of Portsmouth. Demonstrators massed at the venue following unverified reports on social media about a coach carrying young Asian men.

### Misinformation Triggers Gathering Outside Team Hotel

Tensions surrounding immigration have escalated across Britain in recent years, particularly regarding small boat arrivals across the English Channel. Social media speculation quickly turned into a physical demonstration outside the players' accommodations.

- **Target Location:** Marriott Hotel, Cosham (Portsmouth)
- **Affected Party:** Pakistan U-19 National Cricket Team
- **Primary Cause:** Unverified social media rumors mistaking athletes for migrants

### Intervention and Crowd Dispersion

A local councillor from the Reform UK party arrived at the venue and helped disperse the crowd after verifying that the individuals were members of Pakistan's youth cricket team.

Hampshire Police confirmed that officers attended the scene after being notified of a gathering of around 30 people. Authorities reassured the crowd that their concerns were unfounded, leading to a peaceful dispersion with no offences identified or arrests made.
