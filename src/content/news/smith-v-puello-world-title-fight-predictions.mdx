## Dalton Smith Defends World Title Against Alberto Puello

Dalton Smith is set to make the first defense of his WBC light-welterweight title against Dominican Republic fighter Alberto Puello on home soil in Sheffield. Smith, unbeaten in 19 professional fights, faces a tough challenge at the Utilita Arena.

Smith returns to the ring following his impressive knockout victory over Subriel Matias in January. Puello, a former champion with 25 wins and just one career loss, looks to reclaim top status in the division.

## Expert Predictions for Smith vs. Puello

Boxing insiders and former world champions provided their tactical analysis to BBC Sport ahead of the marquee matchup:

### Key Predictions
* **Chantelle Cameron:** Expects a highly competitive fight resulting in a majority decision victory for Smith.
* **Darren Barker:** Believes Smith's right hand will prove decisive against Puello's southpaw stance, forecasting a decision or late stoppage.
* **Carl Frampton:** Notes that both fighters possess technical boxer-puncher styles, making for a high-level tactical clash.

## High Stakes in Sheffield

A victory for Smith keeps his goal of headlining a major event at Sheffield Wednesday's Hillsborough stadium alive. Meanwhile, Puello brings experience and defensive skill as he aims to secure another world title.
