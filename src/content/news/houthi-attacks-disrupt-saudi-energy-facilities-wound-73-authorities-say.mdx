## Houthi Strikes Disrupt Saudi Energy Infrastructure

Operations at multiple critical energy sites in Saudi Arabia were suspended on Tuesday following drone and missile strikes launched by Yemen's Houthi rebels. According to a report by Dawn News, Saudi authorities described the strikes as a dangerous escalation targeting key civilian and industrial infrastructure across southern cities, including Abha, Khamis Mushait, Jazan, and Najran.

The foreign ministry confirmed that 73 civilians, including women and children, sustained injuries. Emergency crews and firefighting units were deployed immediately to control blazes and secure facility perimeters.

## Impact on Global Energy Markets

The supply disruptions immediately rattled international markets amid existing Middle East tensions. The financial impact was immediate:

* **Brent Crude:** Surged by $1 to reach $98 per barrel.
* **US Crude Futures:** Rose over $2 to trade at $93.65 per barrel.
* **Aramco Jazan Facility:** Unconfirmed reports indicate potential damage currently under technical assessment.

## Saudi Defense Response and Escalation Risks

The Saudi Energy Ministry stated that approved emergency operational plans were activated to protect personnel and resume production safely. Meanwhile, the foreign ministry reiterated that Riyadh will take all necessary actions to safeguard its sovereignty and secure international shipping lanes against rebel threats.
