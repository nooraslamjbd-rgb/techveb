RAWALPINDI, (APP - UrduPoint / Pakistan Point News - 10th Sep, 2026) A training workshop was held at Police Headquarters [Rawalpindi](https://www.urdupoint.com/education/board-of-education-rawalpindi.html) on Thursday to enhance the professional capacity of investigation officers.

According to the Police spokesperson, investigation officers from the Head of Investigation Unit (HIU) and Malkhana Moharrars of police stations attended the workshop.

Assistant District Public Prosecutor Mureed Raza delivered lectures on the investigation of murder and other important cases, covering the arrest of accused persons, collection of evidence, prosecution and shortcomings in investigation.

The participants were also briefed on modern, coordinated and scientific methods of investigation.

The speakers highlighted the importance of effective investigation in bringing accused persons to justice and ensuring justice for citizens.
