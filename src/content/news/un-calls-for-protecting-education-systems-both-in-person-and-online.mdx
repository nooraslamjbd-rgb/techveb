NEW YORK, (UrduPoint / Pakistan Point News / WAM - 10th Sep, 2026) [UN](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/united-nations.html) Secretary-General António Guterres called on [all](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/all.html) parties to conflict must stop and prevent attacks on schools and other [education](https://www.urdupoint.com/education/) institutions, meet their obligations under international law, and hold perpetrators accountable.

''Countries need to invest in [education](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/education.html) for every child, fully endorse and implement the Safe Schools Declaration, and support the Global Coalition to Protect [Education](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/education.html) [from](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/from.html) [Attack](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/attack.html).

We must work together to prepare and protect [education](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/education.html) systems — both in person and online — particularly for girls and children in vulnerable situations. And we need to safely reintegrate children and youth associated with armed forces and groups back into schools and communities, said Guterre in a message on the Occasion of the International Day to Protect [Education](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/education.html) [from](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/from.html) [Attack](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/attack.html), observed on 9 [September](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/september.html) every year.

The [United Nations](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/united-nations.html), he added, offers a range of tools to support countries in these efforts, including through UNESCO’s practical guidance and policy support.

''[Education](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/education.html) is a passport to a better future for every child and [young](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/young.html) person. It is also a fundamental human right. But exercising that right is becoming increasingly dangerous.''

In the past two years, ne noted, more than 8,500 attacks worldwide targeted schools, students and teachers, unleashing a wave of deadly violence, abductions and trauma.

All States, he affirmed, have an obligation to ensure the right to [education](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/education.html) for every child — and the safety of schools and places of learning.

Together, let’s keep children safe as they learn and build the peaceful futures they deserve, he urged.

The International Day to Protect [Education](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/education.html) [from](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/from.html) [Attack](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/attack.html) is observed annually on [September](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/september.html) 9 to raise global awareness about the urgent need to safeguard schools, students, and teachers during armed conflict.
