DUBAI, (UrduPoint / Pakistan Point News / WAM - 29th Aug, 2026) In the presence of Sheikh Nahyan bin Mubarak Al Nahyan, Minister of Tolerance and Coexistence, and Abeer AlRamahi, Ambassador of the State of [Palestine](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/palestine.html) to the [UAE](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/uae.html), the “Emirates Loves Palestine” page organised the first edition of its community [event](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/event.html) celebrating the Palestinian community in the [UAE](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/uae.html).

Held at [Dubai](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/dubai.html) Exhibition Centre, Expo City [Dubai](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/dubai.html), “Emirates Loves Palestine” was organised in partnership with the UAE-Palestinian Friendship Club and the Palestinian [Business](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/business.html) Council in [Dubai](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/dubai.html) and the Northern Emirates.

The [event](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/event.html) attracted more than 50,000 people [from](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/from.html) the Palestinian community, the Emirati community and [UAE](https://www.urdupoint.com/arabic/uae.html) residents, in an atmosphere that celebrated Palestinian culture and heritage and reflected the [bonds](https://www.urdupoint.com/business/prize-bonds.html) of affection and connection between the Emirati and Palestinian peoples.

Sheikh Nahyan bin Mubarak said: "It gives me great pleasure to be with you at this wonderful fraternal gathering, which represents the largest gathering of the Palestinian community in the [world](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/world.html), and which clearly embodies the sincere feelings of affection that the United [Arab](https://www.urdupoint.com/arabic/) Emirates holds for [Palestine](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/palestine.html) and its brotherly people."

He added: "In this gathering, which expresses the [UAE](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/uae.html)'s [love](https://www.urdupoint.com/poetry/love-poetry.html) for [Palestine](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/palestine.html), we send together a message of pure support and affection.

The '[UAE](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/uae.html) Loves [Palestine](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/palestine.html)' [event](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/event.html) also embodies the [UAE](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/uae.html)'s approach to promoting the values of tolerance, coexistence, and cultural openness, and its keenness to provide a welcoming community environment for the various communities residing on its soil… For decades, the [UAE](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/uae.html) has hosted a Palestinian community that has always been an active and giving part of the fabric of its society, and its sons and daughters have contributed effectively to the [march](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/march.html) of development and [progress](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/progress.html) across various sectors."

He said: "The [UAE](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/uae.html) has always stood by you, and the [UAE](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/uae.html) remains firmly committed to continuing its humanitarian efforts to support the Palestinian people, placing support for our brothers and the relief of their suffering at the forefront of its priorities, and at the heart of its diplomatic and humanitarian actions."

He affirmed: "The [UAE](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/uae.html) has emerged as the largest donor of humanitarian aid to the [Gaza](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/gaza.html) Strip, and has set a distinguished example of solidarity and mutual support, reflecting its historic and steadfast commitment to standing by the Palestinian people and supporting them at various levels."

He added: “On this occasion, I also extend my thanks to the Embassy of the State of [Palestine](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/palestine.html) in the [United Arab Emirates](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/united-arab-emirates.html), to the Emirati Palestinian Friendship Club, and to the Palestinian [Business](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/business.html) Council in [Dubai](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/dubai.html) and the Northern Emirates, for their sponsorship of this celebration and their efforts in making it a success.”

He said: “This gathering confirms that the relations between the [United Arab Emirates](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/united-arab-emirates.html) and [Palestine](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/palestine.html) are deeply rooted and ever renewing relations, resting on firm fraternal bonds between the two peoples, and on a shared belief in the values of peace, coexistence, and human solidarity… We are certain that today's gathering represents a new link in a continuous chain of meetings and events that strengthen these bonds, and that embody the [love](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/love.html), mutual support, and solidarity that unite the two brotherly peoples.”

The [event](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/event.html) featured a diverse cultural, artistic and heritage programme, including songs, [music](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/music.html), folk performances and traditional showcases that reflected the richness of Palestinian heritage and the diversity of its cultural and artistic expression.

A number of Palestinian artists took part in the [event](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/event.html), including Mohammed Assaf and comedian Imad Farajin, alongside a distinguished group of cultural and community figures.

Together, they shared with the audience a vibrant celebration of Palestinian culture and its deep civilisational and human legacy.

Visitors also had the opportunity to explore different aspects of Palestinian heritage through dedicated sections for traditional [food](https://www.urdupoint.com/cooking/), heritage displays, crafts and home-based projects by members of the community, as well as a range of community activities and artistic performances designed for families and individuals.

The [event](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/event.html) embodied the deep-rooted social and human ties between the Emirati and Palestinian peoples. It also provided a community platform for Palestinians in the [UAE](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/uae.html) to introduce their culture, heritage and contributions within a society that celebrates cultural diversity and upholds the values of tolerance, coexistence and human connection.

The [event](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/event.html) was organised in cooperation with the UAE-Palestinian Friendship Club, which works to strengthen community and cultural engagement between the two peoples and support initiatives that showcase Palestinian culture and heritage. The Palestinian [Business](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/business.html) Council in [Dubai](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/dubai.html) and the Northern Emirates also supported the [event](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/event.html) as part of its role in strengthening connections among Palestinian [business](https://www.urdupoint.com/business/prize-bonds.html) leaders and entrepreneurs in the [UAE](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/uae.html), and highlighting their economic and social contributions.

The [event](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/event.html) also honoured a number of prominent Palestinian figures in recognition of their contributions, achievements and role in serving the community, while highlighting the success stories of members of the Palestinian community in the [UAE](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/uae.html).

The honourees included Dr. Nader Darwich,Founder, Knee & [sports](https://www.urdupoint.com/sports/) Medicine Center & [consultant](https://www.urdupoint.com/education/consultants-in-pakistan.html) Orthopaedic and Knee Surgeon, Qusay Mohamed Ahmed Al Ghusain, Businessman & [board](https://www.urdupoint.com/education/boards-of-education.html) Member, Emirati-Palestinian Friendship Club, Dr. Abeer Ismail Mohamed Al-Najjar, Professor of [Media](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/media.html) & Journalism, American University of [Sharjah](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/sharjah.html), Eng. Ahmad Adnan Zaki Saffarini, Chairman, Adnan Saffarini Engineering Consultants, Eng. Fares Moh'd Said Mustafa Abubaker, Engineer – SEE Institute, Eng. Fouad Abdulhadi Mohammed Mashal, CEO, Al Barakah International Investment Group, Salam Ezzat Hussein Abu Shihab, Journalist & [Media](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/media.html) Professional, Al Khaleej, and Farouk Khalil Hafez Toukan, Chairman, Target [Jordan](https://www.urdupoint.com/arabic/jordan.html) [Palestine](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/palestine.html) Securities.

Coinciding with “Emirates Loves Palestine” in [Dubai](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/dubai.html), the [Gaza](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/gaza.html) Strip hosted “Palestine Loves the UAE” in cooperation with Operation Chivalrous Knight 3, celebrating the humanitarian ties between the two peoples and Palestinian culture and heritage.

The [Dubai](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/dubai.html) [event](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/event.html) featured a live broadcast [from](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/from.html) [Gaza](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/gaza.html), connecting attendees at [Dubai](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/dubai.html) Exhibition Centre with Palestinians in the Strip in a scene that reflected the depth of the human bonds between the Emirati and Palestinian peoples.

Members of the Palestinian community are an active part of the [UAE](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/uae.html)’s diverse social fabric, contributing across economic, cultural and social fields and strengthening the ties of friendship, cooperation and communication between the Emirati and Palestinian peoples.

“Emirates Loves Palestine” reflects the [UAE](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/uae.html)’s approach to celebrating the communities that live on its land, recognising their cultures and contributions, and reinforcing a society built on tolerance, openness and respect for cultural diversity. It further strengthens the [UAE](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/uae.html)’s position as a global model for coexistence and communication among peoples and cultures.
