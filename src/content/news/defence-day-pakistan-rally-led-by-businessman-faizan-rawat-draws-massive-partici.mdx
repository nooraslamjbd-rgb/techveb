KARACHI, (APP - UrduPoint / Pakistan Point News - 6th Sep, 2026) A grand Defence Day of [Pakistan](https://www.urdupoint.com/pakistan/) rally was held in [Karachi](https://www.urdupoint.com/education/board-of-education-karachi.html) on the occasion of Defence Day, led by prominent [business](https://www.urdupoint.com/business/prize-bonds.html) leader Faizan Rawat. A large number of citizens from different parts of the city participated in the rally, including members of the business community, youth, women, senior citizens and children.

The participants expressed their deep [love](https://www.urdupoint.com/poetry/love-poetry.html) for Pakistan and demonstrated a strong spirit of national unity. The atmosphere of Karachi resonated with slogans of “Pakistan Zindabad” and “Pakistan Armed Forces Zindabad,” while the participants reaffirmed their unwavering commitment to the security, sovereignty and defence of the country.

Addressing the rally, business leader Faizan Rawat said that there would be no compromise whatsoever on Pakistan’s security and sovereignty, adding that the nation was ready to sacrifice its body, mind and wealth for the country.

He said that the enemy should not remain under any misconception, as defending Pakistan is the foremost responsibility of every citizen. “Anyone who looks at Pakistan with hostile intentions will face a united response from the entire nation.

The people of Pakistan stand shoulder to shoulder with the armed forces for the security and defence of the country,” he said. Women, senior citizens, children and young people participating in the rally waved national flags and expressed their love for Pakistan.

At the conclusion of the rally, participants offered special prayers for the security, progress and prosperity of Pakistan. The gathering reaffirmed its commitment to national unity and patriotism amid resounding slogans of “Pakistan Zindabad” and “Pakistan Armed Forces Zindabad.”
