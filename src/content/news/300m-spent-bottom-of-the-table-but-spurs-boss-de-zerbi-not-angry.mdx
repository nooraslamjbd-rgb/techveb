## Tottenham Face Early Season Crisis Despite Major Rebuild

Tottenham Hotspur manager Roberto De Zerbi insists there is "no panic" despite his side falling to the bottom of the Premier League table. A 2-0 defeat to Newcastle, featuring second-half goals from Anthony Elanga and Yoane Wissa, marked Spurs' second consecutive loss of the new campaign.

The disappointing start comes despite Tottenham spending over £300 million during the summer transfer window. Supporters have grown increasingly frustrated, having witnessed only three home league victories across their past 23 matches.

### De Zerbi Urges Patience for New-Look Squad

Addressing post-match concerns, De Zerbi emphasized that the performance prior to Newcastle's opening goal was encouraging. He urged fans to remain patient as the club integrates several high-profile signings.

* **Major Transfers:** Midfielders Sandro Tonali (£100m) and Mateus Fernandes (£85m) joined during the summer.
* **Current Form:** Tottenham sit 20th out of 20 teams with zero goals scored in two matches.
* **Managerial Record:** De Zerbi has recorded four wins, two draws, and four losses in his first 10 matches in charge across all competitions.

"We are disappointed for the result and our fans," De Zerbi told reporters. "We are working to be a great team. When we become a team, we can change the story of the last two seasons for Tottenham."
