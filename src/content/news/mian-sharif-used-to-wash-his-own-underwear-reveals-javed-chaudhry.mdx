LAHORE, (UrduPoint / Pakistan Point News - 12th Sep, 2026) Renowned columnist and analyst [Javed Chaudhry](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/javed-chaudhry.html) has made some unusual and interesting revelations about the life of Mian Muhammad Sharif, the elder of the Sharif [family](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/family.html), [Pakistan](https://www.urdupoint.com/pakistan/) [Muslim](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/muslim.html) League-Nawaz President Mian [Nawaz Sharif](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/nawaz-sharif.html) and [Prime Minister](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/prime-minister.html) [Shehbaz Sharif](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/shahbaz-sharif.html)’s father.

Speaking on one of his programmes, [Javed Chaudhry](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/javed-chaudhry.html) said that despite owning a vast [business](https://www.urdupoint.com/business/prize-bonds.html) empire and wielding considerable influence, Mian Muhammad Sharif was extremely simple in his daily life.

> 

He revealed that Mian Sharif, the grandfather of [Punjab](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/punjab.html) [Chief Minister](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/chief-minister.html) [Maryam Nawaz](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/maryam-nawaz-sharif.html), was so committed to simplicity that he never had anyone else clean his shoes; instead, he polished them himself until his death.

He was also so simple that he washed his own undergarments throughout his life and never felt embarrassed by it.
