ISLAMABAD, (UrduPoint / Pakistan Point News - 11th Sep, 2026) Consultations between the [government](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/government.html) and the [opposition](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/opposition.html) have begun over the appointment of the [Chief Election Commissioner](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/chief-election-commissioner.html) and two members.

A key [opposition](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/opposition.html) meeting was held in [Opposition](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/opposition.html) Leader Raja [Nasir](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/nasir.html) Abbas’s [chamber](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/chamber.html), attended by [Mahmood Khan Achakzai](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/mahmood-khan-achakzai.html), Barrister Gohar and Salman Akram Raja.

The meeting held detailed discussions on the [September](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/september.html) 27 [protest](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/protest.html) and directed [Pakistan](https://www.urdupoint.com/pakistan/) Tehreek-e-Insaf ([PTI](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/pakistan-tehreek-e-insaf.html)) to make full preparations for the [event](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/event.html).

It was decided to contact [all](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/all.html) political parties and convene an [All](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/all.html) Parties Conference ([APC](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/apc.html)) over the country’s deteriorating situation. A meeting of the Tehreek Tahafuz-e-Aain will be held on [September](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/september.html) 15 to review preparations for the [APC](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/apc.html).

According to sources, the [opposition](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/opposition.html) leader has replied to the [prime minister](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/prime-minister.html)’s letter and requested a [date](https://www.urdupoint.com/islam/today-islamic-date.html) and time for consultations. [Opposition](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/opposition.html) leader [Mahmood Khan Achakzai](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/mahmood-khan-achakzai.html) responded to the letter after consultations with [opposition](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/opposition.html) leaders.
