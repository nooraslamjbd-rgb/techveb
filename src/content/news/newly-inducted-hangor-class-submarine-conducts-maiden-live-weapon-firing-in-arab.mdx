## Hangor-Class Submarine Completes Maiden Weapon Test

The Pakistan Navy has successfully executed the maiden live weapon firing of its newly inducted Hangor-class submarine in the Arabian Sea, according to a statement released by the Inter-Services Public Relations (ISPR).

This milestone marks a significant step forward in Pakistan's naval defense posture and operational readiness in strategic maritime zones.

### Strategic Defense Cooperation with China

The Hangor-class submarine arrived at Karachi Port in June, representing a major highlight of the deepening defense collaboration between Pakistan and China. Under the bilateral agreement, China is assisting in building multiple advanced submarines to modernize the Pakistan Navy fleet.

### Enhanced Maritime Capabilities

The successful live weapon trial demonstrates the vessel's combat capability and stealth integration.

- **Operational Readiness:** Proves target engagement capabilities in deep sea conditions.
- **Bilateral Tech Transfer:** Highlights ongoing military technology exchange between Islamabad and Beijing.
- **Regional Balance:** Strengthens deterrence and security in the North Arabian Sea.

According to reports originally published by Dawn News, the successful live-fire exercise confirms that the submarine is fully operational and integrated into naval fleet strategies.
