The Pakistan Stock Exchange (PSX) experienced a notable downturn during intraday trading on Tuesday, with the benchmark KSE-100 index dropping over 1,900 points. The market plummeted by 1.10 percent to 171,731.76 points from its previous close of 173,636.07, as reported by Dawn News.

## Primary Factors Driving the PSX Decline
Financial experts point to a confluence of geopolitical instability and rising energy costs as the main catalysts for the market selloff.

### Escalating Geopolitical Tensions
Prolonged friction between the United States and Iran, alongside security incidents targeting Saudi Arabia's energy facilities, has unsettled global markets. These regional dynamics have driven broad profit-taking among investors seeking safety.

### Surging Global Oil Prices
The conflict in the Middle East triggered sharp increases in international crude benchmarks:

* **Brent Crude Futures**: Surged over 2 percent to reach $99.00 per barrel.
* **US WTI Crude**: Increased by 3.2 percent to hit $94.41 per barrel.

Rising oil prices add significant pressure to emerging economies like Pakistan, which rely heavily on imported fuel.

## Macroeconomic Indicators Offer a Cushion
Despite current volatility, market analysts from AKD Securities highlight that Pakistan's macroeconomic foundation remains relatively stable. Recent taps into the international Eurobond market and strengthened economic indicators offer policymakers a cushion against external shocks, mitigating long-term structural risks for local investors.
