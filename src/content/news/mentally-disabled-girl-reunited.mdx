LAHORE, (APP - UrduPoint / Pakistan Point News - 30th Aug, 2026) Naseerabad Police reunited a mentally disabled girl with her parents.

Police said on Sunday that Ayesha (6 years) had come to Children's

Hospital from Okara district for a check-up, where she went missing.

The parents called 15 regarding the girl's disappearance, which the

DIG Operations took notice of and directed steps to immediately

search for the girl.

On the direction of DIG Operations, Naseerabad Police found the

girl from Main Ferozepur Road and handed her over to her parents.
