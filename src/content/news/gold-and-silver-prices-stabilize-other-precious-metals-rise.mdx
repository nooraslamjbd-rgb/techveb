LONDON, (UrduPoint / Pakistan Point News / WAM - 10th Sep, 2026) [Gold](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/gold.html) and [silver](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/silver.html) [prices](https://www.urdupoint.com/business/prize-bonds.html) have entered a choppy stabilization phase, while other precious metals like platinum show relative strength amid shifting [Federal](https://www.urdupoint.com/education/board-of-education-federal.html) Reserve rate expectations and resilient US economic data.

Spot [gold](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/gold.html) was largely unchanged at $4,396.69 per ounce, while US [gold](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/gold.html) futures for [December](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/december.html) delivery fell 0.4% to $4,440.80.

As for other precious metals, spot [silver](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/silver.html) was steady at $67.30 per ounce, while platinum rose 0.1% to $1,897.57 and palladium gained 0.2% to $1,355.44.
