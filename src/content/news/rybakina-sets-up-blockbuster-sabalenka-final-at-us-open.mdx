## Rybakina Secures World No. 1 with US Open Final Spot

Elena Rybakina demonstrated remarkable grit by rallying from a set down against American fourth seed Coco Gauff to book her place in the US Open final. The Kazakh second seed secured a 3-6, 6-4, 6-4 victory, silencing the partisan crowd and setting up a showdown against top seed Aryna Sabalenka.

Regardless of Saturday's result, Rybakina is guaranteed to end Sabalenka's 99-week reign at the top of the WTA rankings when they officially update on Monday.

### Sabalenka Overpowers Pegula to Continue Title Defense

Earlier in the day, Aryna Sabalenka displayed immense power, defeating home favorite Jessica Pegula 7-5, 6-2. Sabalenka is attempting to become only the third woman in the Open era to win three consecutive US Open singles titles.

- **First Set:** Sabalenka broke late in a tight opening set to capture momentum.
- **Second Set:** The top seed controlled the tempo from the baseline to close out the match comfortably.

### Historic Top-Two Seeds Showdown

This marquee matchup marks the first time since 2013 that the top two women's seeds will contest the US Open final, mirroring the legendary clash between Serena Williams and Victoria Azarenka.

As reported by BBC News, Rybakina is now chasing her third Grand Slam title following victories at Wimbledon in 2022 and the Australian Open in January. Sabalenka, meanwhile, aims to cap off her Grand Slam season with a historic victory in Flushing Meadows.
