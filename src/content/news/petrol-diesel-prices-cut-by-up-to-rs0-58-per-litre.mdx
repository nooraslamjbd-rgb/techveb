ISLAMABAD, (APP - UrduPoint / Pakistan Point News - 29th Aug, 2026) The Oil and Gas Regulatory Authority (OGRA) has reduced the ex-depot [prices](https://www.urdupoint.com/business/prize-bonds.html) of [petrol](https://www.urdupoint.com/business/petroleum-prices-in-pakistan.html) and high-speed diesel (HSD) for August 29 to 31, 2026.

According to a press release issued by the Petroleum Division on Friday, the revised prices were notified under the [Federal](https://www.urdupoint.com/education/board-of-education-federal.html) government’s revised petroleum pricing mechanism.

The [price](https://www.urdupoint.com/business/prize-bonds.html) of high-speed diesel (HSD) has been reduced by Rs0.17 per litre, from Rs371.61 to Rs371.44 per litre.

The price of Motor Spirit (petrol) has been reduced by Rs0.58 per litre, from Rs342.60 to Rs342.02 per litre.

The revised prices will remain effective from August 29 to August 31, 2026.
