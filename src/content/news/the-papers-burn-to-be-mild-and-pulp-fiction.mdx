**The prime minister's first encounter in-person with US President Donald Trump makes nearly all the front pages, with many focusing on the comments between the two about the controversial Chagos Islands deal which would see the UK hand sovereignty of the islands to Mauritius.**

[The Daily Mirror, external](https://www.mirror.co.uk/news/politics/burnham-trump-meeting-un-assembly-37691091) says Burnham "emerged with credit" after what it called a "tricky meeting", where Trump described the deal as "terrible". [The i Paper, external](https://inews.co.uk/news/politics/burnham-warns-ai-sets-collision-course-america-4784183) described the meeting as "magnanimous". [The Daily Telegrah's, external](https://www.telegraph.co.uk/world-news/2026/09/22/trump-tells-burnham-look-again-at-terrible-chagos-deal/) headline says "Burnham bows to Trump on Chagos" after the prime minister agreed to "review" the deal.

The strength of the El Nino event developing in the tropical Pacific is reported to be "Godzilla-level" in [the Guardian, external](https://www.theguardian.com/environment/2026/sep/21/super-el-nino-temperature-record). It quotes figures from climate scientists who say more than 450,000 people are projected to die from droughts, wildfires or flooding caused by the weather phenomenon in the next six months.

The former chairman of John Lewis Sir Charlie Mayfield has told [the Times, external](https://www.thetimes.com/uk/politics/article/ex-john-lewis-boss-talking-will-help-unemployment-x2czjf5x8) that what he calls the "culture of fear" preventing managers from talking to staff if they are off sick, must end. Sir Charlie, who now advises the government, tells the paper he wants to "change the norms" of people emailing or texting their employer if they can't go to work, so both sides feel more able to talk about sickness and develop "return to work plans" together.

[The Sun, external](https://www.thesun.co.uk/royals/40463696/piers-morgan-earl-spencer-book-destroyed-lies/) highlights the former Daily Mirror Editor Piers Morgan's demand for Princess Diana's brother Earl Spencer's book to be withdrawn from sale, because Morgan was wrongly accused in it of being behind the publication of intrusive photos. The main headline is "Pulp fiction."

[The Daily Mail, external](https://www.dailymail.com/sciencetech/article-16151203/sexiest-male-voices-women-barack-obama.html) reports research which shows there is a "sweet" spot" when it comes to the vocal pitch that women find attractive. The study, led by a team at Durham University, found that voices which are too high, or too low, lose their appeal. Those judged most attractive were in the "moderate pitch range" and researchers said that was similar to the voices of former US President Barack Obama and the actor Gerard Butler.
