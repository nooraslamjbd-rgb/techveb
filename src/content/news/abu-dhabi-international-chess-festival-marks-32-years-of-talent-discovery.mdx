ABU DHABI, (UrduPoint / Pakistan Point News / WAM - 30th Aug, 2026) Reaching its 32nd edition, the [Abu Dhabi](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/abu-dhabi.html) International Chess Festival has consolidated its position as a global tournament, attracting over 2,400 players [from](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/from.html) across the [world](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/world.html), including nearly 300 Emirati competitors.

The [event](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/event.html) gains added momentum as the [UAE](https://www.urdupoint.com/arabic/uae.html) prepares to host the [World](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/world.html) Chess Olympiad 2028 in [Abu Dhabi](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/abu-dhabi.html) for the second time, following the historic 1986 edition. First held in 1991, the festival has grown [from](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/from.html) a local initiative into a staple on the International Chess Federation (FIDE) [Calendar](https://www.urdupoint.com/islam/islamic-calendar.html), regularly drawing [top](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/top.html) global Grandmasters alongside local talent such as [GM](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/general-motors.html) [Salem](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/salem.html) Abdul Rahman and WGM Rouda Al Serkal.

Highlighting the [event](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/event.html)'s global reach, Hussein Abdullah Al Khouri, Chairman of the [Abu Dhabi](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/abu-dhabi.html) Chess Club and Head of the Higher Organising Committee, noted that sustained backing [from](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/from.html) the [Abu Dhabi](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/abu-dhabi.html) [sports](https://www.urdupoint.com/sports/) Council has been pivotal, stating, "Our international reputation has contributed to attracting the [world](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/world.html)'s elite champions to compete.

"

Underlining the festival's strategic role ahead of upcoming global events, Taryam Matar Taryam, Chairman of the [Sharjah](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/sharjah.html) Cultural and Chess Club, added that the tournament serves as a vital platform "to develop players' expertise and support the nation's preparations to host the [World](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/world.html) Chess Olympiad 2028."

Emphasising the infrastructure and legacy behind the tournament, Saeed Ahmed Al Khouri, Executive Director of the [Abu Dhabi](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/abu-dhabi.html) Chess Club, stated that the [event](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/event.html) "reflects the prestigious status of the [UAE](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/uae.html) in hosting and organising major international sporting events according to the highest global standards."
