CAIRO, (UrduPoint / Pakistan Point News / WAM - 02nd Sep, 2026) Egyptian President Abdel-Fattah El-Sisi welcomed [Chinese](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/china.html) President [Xi Jinping](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/xi-jinping.html) at [Cairo](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/cairo.html) International [Airport](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/airport.html) on Tuesday, marking the start of Xi’s official [visit](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/visit.html) to [Egypt](https://www.urdupoint.com/arabic/egypt.html).

Xi’s 1-3 [September](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/september.html) [visit](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/visit.html) is part of a wider international tour.

He is expected to hold talks with El-Sisi on bilateral relations, as well as regional and international issues of mutual concern.

The [visit](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/visit.html) is aimed at strengthening the two countries' comprehensive strategic partnership and expanding cooperation in political, economic and development fields. The two leaders are also expected to discuss regional developments.
