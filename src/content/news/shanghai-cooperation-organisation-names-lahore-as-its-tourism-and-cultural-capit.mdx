## Lahore Named SCO Tourism and Cultural Capital for 2026-27

The Shanghai Cooperation Organisation (SCO) has officially designated Lahore as its Tourism and Cultural Capital for the 2026-27 period. The decision was formally declared during the 26th meeting of the SCO Council of Heads of State held in Bishkek, Kyrgyzstan, as reported by Dawn News.

Prime Minister Shehbaz Sharif attended the summit, representing Pakistan as it assumes the rotating chairmanship of the regional body. Pakistan's theme for its presidency focuses on 'Turning Vision into Action: Connectivity, Innovation and Shared Prosperity.'

### Boosting Regional Tourism and Heritage

Since 2022, the SCO title of Tourism and Cultural Capital rotates annually among member countries. The designation aims to showcase cities that represent the historical and cultural essence of the country holding the SCO presidency.

This title helps foster stronger people-to-people ties, economic cooperation, and international tourism across member nations. The foundational decision to select Lahore was originally endorsed in April during a meeting of SCO tourism heads in Bishkek.

### Historical Context of SCO Cultural Capitals

Lahore joins a distinguished list of regional hubs that have held this status:

- **Varanasi, India** (2022-23)
- **Almaty, Kazakhstan** (2023-24)
- **Qingdao, China** (2024-25)
- **Cholpon-Ata, Kyrgyzstan** (2025-26)

This recognition highlights Lahore's extensive historical legacy, vibrant arts scene, and architectural heritage on a global platform.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Why was Lahore named the SCO Tourism and Cultural Capital?

Lahore was selected to showcase its rich cultural heritage and promote people-to-people contacts and tourism across SCO member states.

### What is the theme of Pakistan's SCO presidency?

The theme defined by PM Shehbaz Sharif is 'Turning Vision into Action: Connectivity, Innovation and Shared Prosperity'.

### Which cities held the SCO Cultural Capital title previously?

Previous cities include Varanasi (2022-23), Almaty (2023-24), Qingdao (2024-25), and Cholpon-Ata (2025-26).
