## Craig Bellamy Urges Wales to Elevate Performance After Lisbon Defeat

Wales manager Craig Bellamy expressed pride in his squad's defensive resilience but demanded greater composure following a tight 1-0 loss to Portugal in the UEFA Nations League.

Despite missing several key players due to injury, a stubborn Welsh side pushed the hosts in Lisbon before succumbing to a deflected strike from Joao Felix.

### Tactical Resistance and Late Drama

Portugal controlled much of the possession at the Jose Alvalade Stadium, where legendary forward Cristiano Ronaldo saw a goal ruled out for offside by VAR.

Wales endured long stretches without the ball, yet displayed remarkable discipline. In the closing stages, youngsters Brennan Johnson and Lewis Koumas both came close to snatching an equalizer.

### Key Match Highlights

- **Defeated Winner**: Joao Felix's deflected shot proved to be the match-winner for new Portugal boss Jorge Jesus.
- **VAR Intervention**: Cristiano Ronaldo was denied his 980th top-flight goal due to an offside call.
- **Welsh Resurgence**: Brennan Johnson and Lewis Koumas created clear chances late in the second half.

### Bellamy's Post-Match Reflection

Acknowledging the quality of Portugal's £1.4bn squad, Bellamy remained optimistic about his team's trajectory. "It was a difficult game for us, but I'm beyond proud," Bellamy noted, praising the energy shown by players lacking regular club minutes.

Wales will host Portugal in the return Nations League fixture in Cardiff this November, as reported by BBC News.
