BEIJING, (UrduPoint / Pakistan Point News / WAM - 27th Aug, 2026) Profits of [China](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/china.html)'s industrial firms above designated size increased 17.6 percent year on year during the first seven months of 2026, data [from](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/from.html) the National Bureau of Statistics showed Thursday.

These industrial firms with an annual main [business](https://www.urdupoint.com/business/prize-bonds.html) revenue of at least 20 [million](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/million.html) [Yuan](https://www.urdupoint.com/business/currency-rate/yuan-to-pkr.html) (about US$2.95 [million](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/million.html)) saw their combined profits reach 4.58 trillion yuan in the January-July period, according to the data.
