Cotton is not merely an agricultural crop or economic commodity for Pakistan but also an important part of the country’s cultural heritage, Pakistan’s Ambassador to France Mumtaz Zahra Baloch said while addressing the event.

PARIS, (UrduPoint / Pakistan Point News - 12th Sep, 2026) A special conference was held in [Paris](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/paris.html) to highlight the thousands-year history of [cotton](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/cotton.html) cultivation and the [textile](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/textile.html) [industry](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/industry.html) in [Pakistan](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/pakistan.html), its cultural heritage and its role in the global fashion [industry](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/industry.html). Titled “The Thread of [Cotton](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/cotton.html): Thousands of Years of [Cotton](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/cotton.html) Culture in [Pakistan](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/pakistan.html),” the conference was held at the Embassy of [Pakistan](https://www.urdupoint.com/pakistan/) in [Paris](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/paris.html) and attended by diplomats, UNESCO officials, representatives of the French [textile](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/textile.html) [industry](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/industry.html), fashion designers and experts. Addressing the [event](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/event.html), [Pakistan](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/pakistan.html)’s Ambassador to [France](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/france.html) Mumtaz Zahra Baloch said that the history of [cotton](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/cotton.html)’s discovery and cultivation in ancient [Pakistan](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/pakistan.html) dates back approximately 7,000 years.

She said farmers, spinners and weavers in the region had built a strong [textile](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/textile.html) tradition over centuries, which later played a key role in laying the foundations of the global [textile](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/textile.html) [industry](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/industry.html). The [Pakistani](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/pakistan.html) ambassador said that when [cotton](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/cotton.html) reached different continents, it was not merely a valuable trading commodity; it also carried knowledge, skills, craftsmanship and a tradition of cultural [exchange](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/exchange.html) among different civilizations. Mumtaz Zahra Baloch said that [textiles](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/textile.html) and garments remain a central part of [Pakistan](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/pakistan.html)’s industrial production and [exports](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/exports.html) and that the sector provides [employment](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/employment.html) to a large portion of the country’s industrial workforce.

From spinning mills in [Faisalabad](https://www.urdupoint.com/education/board-of-education-faisalabad.html) to export houses in [Karachi](https://www.urdupoint.com/education/board-of-education-karachi.html) and [Lahore](https://www.urdupoint.com/education/board-of-education-lahore.html), the [textile](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/textile.html) [industry](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/industry.html) holds a central position in [Pakistan](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/pakistan.html)’s economy. She said [cotton](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/cotton.html) is not merely an agricultural crop or economic commodity for [Pakistan](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/pakistan.html) but also an important part of the country’s cultural heritage.

Centuries-old traditions of spinning, weaving, embroidery and [textile](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/textile.html) craftsmanship remain part of [Pakistan](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/pakistan.html)’s cultural identity. The ambassador said these living traditions constitute intangible cultural heritage, whose preservation receives special attention [from](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/from.html) UNESCO.

They include knowledge, skills and craftsmanship passed down [from](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/from.html) one generation to the next. Frederika Sundberg, founder of M. Sundberg and president of Cercle des Amis du [Pakistan](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/pakistan.html), also addressed the conference.

She shared her expert views on [Pakistan](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/pakistan.html)’s [textile](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/textile.html) sector and reviewed the thousands-year history of [cotton](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/cotton.html) in [Pakistan](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/pakistan.html), [from](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/from.html) the Indus Valley to the modern [textile](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/textile.html) [industry](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/industry.html). She also highlighted the export potential of [Pakistani](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/pakistan.html) [textile](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/textile.html) products in global [markets](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/market.html) and said the country’s [textile](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/textile.html) sector had significant scope for further expansion internationally. French archaeologist Catherine Jarrige, former co-director of the French Archaeological Mission in the Indus Valley, highlighted evidence of [cotton](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/cotton.html) cultivation in ancient [Pakistan](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/pakistan.html).

She said archaeological evidence indicated that [cotton](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/cotton.html) was cultivated, spun and woven in the Indus Valley civilisation approximately 7,000 years ago. The conference was organised under the Embassy of [Pakistan](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/pakistan.html)’s public and economic diplomacy initiative to highlight the historical and contemporary links between [Pakistan](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/pakistan.html), [cotton](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/cotton.html) cultivation, the [textile](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/textile.html) [industry](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/industry.html) and cultural heritage.

The [event](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/event.html) showcased both [Pakistan](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/pakistan.html)’s thousands-year-old [textile](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/textile.html) tradition and the importance of the [textile](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/textile.html) sector to the country’s economy, [exports](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/exports.html) and the global fashion [industry](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/industry.html) today.
