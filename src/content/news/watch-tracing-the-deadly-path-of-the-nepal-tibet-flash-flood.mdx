## Satellite Tech Maps Deadly Nepal-Tibet Flash Flood

A catastrophic flash flood has devastated the border region between Nepal and Tibet, leaving hundreds dead and hundreds more missing. Torrential water flows destroyed entire towns and swept away critical bridge infrastructure.

To understand the full scope of the disaster, investigative journalists at BBC Verify utilized advanced satellite imagery and video verification techniques to map the precise trajectory of the surge.

### Open-Source Tech Exposes Scale of Destruction

Disaster verification teams analyzed high-resolution satellite captures alongside user-generated content from social media to track the water flow. Key findings from the imagery analysis include:

- Complete submersion and structural destruction of remote border settlements.
- Severe damage to major transit routes, including washed-away bridges.
- Landslides triggered by rapid water accumulation along steep mountain valleys.

### The Growing Importance of OSINT in Disaster Relief

Modern open-source intelligence (OSINT) and geospatial satellite tracking have become vital tools for assessing hard-to-reach crisis zones. When landslides block ground access, remote sensing provides rapid situational awareness for international aid efforts.

The original investigation was reported by Merlyn Thomas and produced by Aisha Sembhi for BBC News, featuring graphic analysis by Mesut Ersoz and verification by a specialized data team.
