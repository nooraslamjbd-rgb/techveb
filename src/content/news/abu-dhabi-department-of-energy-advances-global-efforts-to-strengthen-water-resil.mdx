STOCKHOLM, (UrduPoint / Pakistan Point News / WAM - 28th Aug, 2026) The [Abu Dhabi](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/abu-dhabi.html) Department of Energy (DoE) took part in [World](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/world.html) [Water](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/water.html) Week 2026 in [Stockholm](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/stockholm.html), reinforcing the emirate’s role as a trusted global partner and a leader in advancing [water](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/water.html) resilience and integrated [water](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/water.html) and energy governance.

Led by Dr. Abdullah Humaid Al Jarwan, Chairman of the Department of Energy, the delegation brought together senior energy-sector leaders [from](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/from.html) the [UAE](https://www.urdupoint.com/arabic/uae.html).

Throughout the week, the delegation met with governments, regulators, international organisations, academic institutions and [industry](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/industry.html) leaders to explore practical partnerships that strengthen the resilience of [water](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/water.html) and energy systems and support sustainable economic development.

World [Water](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/water.html) Week is one of the [world](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/world.html)’s leading forums for [water](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/water.html) policy and international cooperation, bringing together decision-makers [from](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/from.html) across the globe to address the critical challenges shaping the future of [water](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/water.html) security.

Through its participation, DoE showcased [Abu Dhabi](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/abu-dhabi.html)’s integrated approach to governance, regulation and long-term planning, and how these efforts are strengthening [water](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/water.html) resilience, safeguarding essential services and supporting sustainable growth.

Dr. Abdullah Humaid Al Jarwan participated in the high-level ministerial panel, Shaping the Future Through [Water](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/water.html), where he shared [Abu Dhabi](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/abu-dhabi.html)’s experience of more than 50 years in building a resilient [water](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/water.html) system based on source diversification, efficient resource use, governance and innovation.

He said, “It is important not to equate [water](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/water.html) abundance with [water](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/water.html) security. The difference lies in [water](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/water.html) resilience. Resilience means building a system with the capabilities to make effective use of available resources and diversify them, making the system safe, sustainable and affordable.”

He also participated in the session, "[Water](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/water.html) Resilience by Design: Protecting People and Enabling [Progress](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/progress.html)", which explored the importance of embedding resilience across [water](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/water.html) and energy systems through integrated planning, governance and investment, strengthening system readiness and the ability to maintain essential services under different conditions.

The department also presented the [Abu Dhabi](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/abu-dhabi.html) [Water](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/water.html) and Energy Resilience Framework. The framework integrates policy, regulation, infrastructure planning and innovation, providing a practical model for strengthening the long-term resilience of [Abu Dhabi](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/abu-dhabi.html)’s [water](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/water.html) and energy sectors.

Commenting on the participation, Dr. Abdullah Humaid Al Jarwan said, “Abu Dhabi continues to advance an integrated, forward-looking approach to [water](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/water.html) and energy resilience, built on long-term planning, progressive regulation, innovation and high-impact international partnerships. As [water](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/water.html) challenges intensify, the priority must be to move beyond dialogue and translate resilience into practical, implementable solutions that enhance system readiness, reliability and sustainability, while safeguarding vital services and supporting sustainable economic growth.

”

He added, “World [Water](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/water.html) Week in [Stockholm](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/stockholm.html) is an important platform to deepen cooperation with our international partners, [exchange](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/exchange.html) expertise and broaden joint action on solutions and policies that can respond to future challenges. We will carry this momentum into [Abu Dhabi](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/abu-dhabi.html) [Water](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/water.html) and Energy Week 2026, bringing together decision-makers, regulators, investors, [technology](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/technology.html) leaders and experts to build on international dialogue through practical partnerships and initiatives that deliver tangible impact, further strengthening [Abu Dhabi](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/abu-dhabi.html)’s role as an active global partner in shaping a more resilient and sustainable future for [water](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/water.html) and energy.”

As part of its programme in [Stockholm](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/stockholm.html), the department held a series of meetings, [media](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/media.html) interviews and engagements with partners to [exchange](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/exchange.html) expertise, explore areas of cooperation and advance dialogue on [water](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/water.html) and energy resilience and integrated planning.

In collaboration with the Ministry of Foreign Affairs, the department also hosted a stakeholder reception at its booth, bringing together leaders, experts and partners to [exchange](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/exchange.html) perspectives, explore opportunities for collaboration and [share](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/share.html) [Abu Dhabi](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/abu-dhabi.html)’s experience in strengthening [water](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/water.html) resilience.

The gathering also continued to build momentum towards the 2026 [United Nations](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/united-nations.html) [Water](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/water.html) Conference, which [Abu Dhabi](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/abu-dhabi.html) will host in [December](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/december.html).

During the reception, Dr. Abdullah Humaid Al Jarwan said, “Water has no boundaries, and neither should solutions. What works should be scaled, and today we have the opportunity to expand proven solutions and turn them into reality. Partnership is the key, and innovation is what will differentiate us.”

From [Stockholm](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/stockholm.html), Dr. Al Jarwan invited partners [from](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/from.html) around the [world](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/world.html) to come to [Abu Dhabi](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/abu-dhabi.html) in [December](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/december.html) to bring their toughest challenges and [best](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/best.html) solutions, and to work together to deliver tangible [results](https://www.urdupoint.com/education/results.html), alongside the 2026 [United Nations](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/united-nations.html) [Water](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/water.html) Conference and [Abu Dhabi](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/abu-dhabi.html) [Water](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/water.html) and Power Week.

DoE’s participation further underscored the emirate’s growing role as a global convening platform for governments, regulators, investors, innovators and research institutions working to advance more resilient [water](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/water.html) and energy systems.

The conversations and partnerships developed in [Stockholm](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/stockholm.html) will continue at [Abu Dhabi](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/abu-dhabi.html) [Water](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/water.html) and Power Week later this year, creating a clear pathway [from](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/from.html) international dialogue to practical collaboration and implementation.

Through sustained international engagement, the department is helping position [Abu Dhabi](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/abu-dhabi.html) as a global centre for regulatory excellence, policy innovation and international cooperation, while contributing to wider efforts to build more resilient and sustainable [water](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/water.html) and energy systems worldwide.
