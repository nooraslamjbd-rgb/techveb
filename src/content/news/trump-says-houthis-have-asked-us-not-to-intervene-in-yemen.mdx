United States President Donald Trump said on Saturday that Yemen’s Iran-allied Houthi fighters, who have seized a [vital shipping corridor](https://www.dawn.com/news/2028876) linking Europe and Asia, have asked Washington not to intervene.

The Houthis have [taken control](https://www.dawn.com/news/2029275/houthi-forces-overrun-red-sea-chokepoints) of Yemen’s entire Red Sea coast after capturing three [strategic islands](https://www.dawn.com/news/2028986/key-port-falls-as-houthis-eye-red-sea-gateway) in a lightning offensive.

Saudi Arabia, the world’s biggest oil exporter, has relied on the area ever since Iran’s Hormuz blockade choked off its shipments via the Gulf.

“The Houthis called us and they don’t want to fight with us,” Trump told reporters on a visit to Dublin, Ireland.

“They don’t want us to go after them.”

He added that “everything’s gonna work out fine and dandy,” in the region.

Trump also mentioned having spoken to Saudi Crown Prince Mohammed bin Salman, whom he described as a “friend”.

According to US outlet [_Axios_](https://www.axios.com/2026/09/11/houthis-yemen-saudi-trump-mbs-strikes), the Saudi crown prince called Trump twice on Thursday to request US strikes on the Houthis, but the American president declined.

The Houthis also [targeted Saudi Arabian oil facilities](https://www.dawn.com/news/2029319/saudi-arabia-says-iraqi-drones-forced-temporary-closure-of-crucial-oil-pipeline) this week with a massive drone and missile attack.

## Yemeni govt forces hit Houthis in Mocha

In Yemen, the government forces said on Saturday they have struck Houthi fighters in the strategic Red Sea city of Mocha.

Men gesture in a screen grab from a video released on Sept 11, 2026 and said to show Khaled bin Walid camp after Houthis captured the city of Mocha in Taiz Province. — Al-Masirah TV/Handout via Reuters

Following a week-long offensive, the Houthis on Friday captured Yemen’s Red Sea coast, cementing their hold on the [Bab al-Mandab strait](https://www.dawn.com/news/2028876), a vital artery for Saudi Arabian oil exports as the Strait of Hormuz remains closed amid the US-Iran war.

“The armed forces destroyed vehicles and weapons and killed members of the Houthi terrorist militia backed by the Iranian regime,” near the port of the city of Mocha, the government news agency _Saba_ reported.

The spokesman for the Yemeni government forces, Majed Abdullah al-Nazili, said they have also struck the Houthis’ “vehicles and members” on the Mocha-Dhubab road, 11 kilometres from the city, according to _Saba_.

Nazili said on Friday they had begun shelling areas in Mocha, and called on citizens not to use the Taiz-Mocha and Mocha-Dhubab roads.

The Houthis [seized control of Mocha](https://www.dawn.com/news/2029146) on Thursday, with their attack driving many to flee to Aden, where the internationally recognised government is based.

On Friday, Houthi media said two Saudi strikes [hit Mocha’s airport](https://www.dawn.com/news/2029101), while a local official said the city’s seaport was hit by six missiles of unknown origin.

The Houthi offensive, which [started last week](https://www.dawn.com/news/2027458), has killed more than 500 people, according to sources on both sides.

It has also displaced about 46,000 people, the UN migration agency said on Friday.

It is part of renewed hostilities in Yemen’s civil war that began with the group’s [takeover of the capital Sanaa](http://www.dawn.com/news/1151506) in 2014.
