## Tragic PIMS Islamabad Fire Highlights Safety Crisis

A devastating fire in the gynaecology ward at Islamabad’s Pakistan Institute of Medical Sciences (PIMS) claimed the lives of fourteen infants. The tragedy has once again exposed severe vulnerabilities in public healthcare infrastructure.

According to reports originally published by Dawn News, fire safety negligence and poor enforcement of building codes remain widespread across healthcare institutions in Pakistan.

## Recurrent Hospital Fire Incidents in Pakistan Since 2010

Hospital fires in Pakistan are rarely isolated events. Over the past decade, recurring disasters have pointed toward systemic failure in safety compliance:

* **Lack of Fire Extinguishers**: Many public facilities lack operational emergency equipment.
* **Faulty Electrical Wiring**: Overloaded circuits often ignite catastrophic blazes in intensive care units.
* **Blocked Emergency Exits**: Poor architectural design frequently impedes swift evacuation procedures.

### Key Factors Driving Fire Hazards in Medical Facilities

1. Inadequate regular safety audits by government authorities.
2. Absence of trained staff for disaster management and emergency evacuations.
3. Insufficient installation of modern automated fire suppression systems.

## Urgent Need for Building Safety Reforms

Experts emphasize that until strict safety laws and automated fire suppression systems are mandated nationwide, public buildings and hospitals will remain high-risk zones for preventable fire disasters.
