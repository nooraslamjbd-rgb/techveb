ISLAMABAD, (UrduPoint / Pakistan Point News - 11th Sep, 2026) A petition filed by [National Assembly](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/national-assembly.html) member Sher Afzal Marwat seeking the removal of [Sohail Afridi](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/sohail-afridi.html) as [chief minister](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/chief-minister.html) and the restoration of Ali Amin Gandapur as [chief minister](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/chief-minister.html) has been fixed for [hearing](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/hearing.html) in the [Federal](https://www.urdupoint.com/education/board-of-education-federal.html) Constitutional [Court](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/court.html).

The case challenging [Sohail Afridi](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/sohail-afridi.html)’s [election](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/election.html) as [chief minister](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/chief-minister.html) has been [scheduled](https://www.urdupoint.com/business/prize-bonds-draw-schedule.html) for 14th [September](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/september.html). Sher Afzal Marwat had filed the petition against [Sohail Afridi](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/sohail-afridi.html), also seeking Ali Amin Gandapur’s restoration as [chief minister](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/chief-minister.html). Marwat maintains that [Sohail Afridi](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/sohail-afridi.html) was nominated by convicted former [prime minister](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/prime-minister.html) [Imran Khan](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/imran-khan.html).
