ABU DHABI, (UrduPoint / Pakistan Point News / WAM - 28th Aug, 2026) The [UAE](https://www.urdupoint.com/arabic/uae.html) Youth Muay Thai Team has returned [from](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/from.html) the 2026 [Arab](https://www.urdupoint.com/arabic/) Muay Thai Youth Championship in [Libya](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/libya.html) with 11 medals, including two [gold](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/gold.html), six [silver](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/silver.html) and three [bronze](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/bronze.html), across age categories [from](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/from.html) 10 to 17 years.

The performance reflects the readiness of the [UAE](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/uae.html)'s next generation of Muay Thai athletes and the sustained preparation programmes delivered by the [UAE](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/uae.html) Muay Thai and Kickboxing Federation across youth age groups and [weight](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/weight.html) categories.

Mohammed Al Shaari won [gold](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/gold.html) in the 16–17 years, 67 kg category, while Aziz Al Hammadi claimed [gold](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/gold.html) in the 14–15 years, 60 kg division.

Silver medals went to Faisal Shehab in the 16–17 years, 51 kg category; Youssef Abu Maali in the 14–15 years, 42 kg category; Zayed Al Marzouqi in the 10–11 years, 34 kg category; Ahmed Abu Jaish in the 14–15 years, 75 kg category; Rakan Ahmed in the 14–15 years, 63.5 kg category; and Ammar Abu Jaish in the 12–13 years, 48 kg category.

Bronze medals were won by Mohammed Bilal in the 14–15 years, 54 kg category; Saeed Amer in the 14–15 years, 48 kg category; and Mohammed Abu Maali in the 14–15 years, 40 kg category.

Ali Khouri, [board](https://www.urdupoint.com/education/boards-of-education.html) Member of the [UAE](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/uae.html) Muay Thai and Kickboxing Federation, said, "This achievement reflects the Federation's vision of building a sporting generation capable of representing the [UAE](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/uae.html) with distinction at [Arab](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/arab.html) and international events."

He added that winning 11 medals demonstrates the success of the Federation's preparation and development programmes for [young](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/young.html) athletes.

Khouri said, "We are proud of the athletes who raised the [UAE](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/uae.html) flag high in [Libya](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/libya.html) and proved that the future of the sport is in trusted hands. These [results](https://www.urdupoint.com/education/results.html) are a motivator to keep working and investing in our youth categories as we prepare for continental and [world](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/world.html) championships."

He also thanked the [Arab](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/arab.html) Muay Thai Federation, under the leadership of Abdullah Saeed Al Neyadi, for its efforts in organising the championship and supporting [young](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/young.html) talent across the region.

The [UAE](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/uae.html) Muay Thai and Kickboxing Federation reaffirmed its commitment to building on the achievement through upcoming international competitions.
