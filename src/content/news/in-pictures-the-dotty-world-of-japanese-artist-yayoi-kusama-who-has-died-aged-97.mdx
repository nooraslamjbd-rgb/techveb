## Renowned Artist Yayoi Kusama Passes Away at 97

Japanese contemporary artist Yayoi Kusama, whose signature polka dots and mesmerizing installations captivated millions worldwide, has died at the age of 97, as reported by BBC News.

Kusama emerged as a pioneering force during the pop art movement of the 1960s. Over her multi-decade career, she became one of the most recognizable and popular figures in modern art history.

### The Visionary Behind Infinity Mirror Rooms

Inspired by childhood hallucinations of lights and repetitive patterns, Kusama translated her personal experiences into groundbreaking art. Her iconic **Infinity Mirror Rooms** used mirrors, lights, and shapes to create illusions of endless space.

Key milestones of her illustrious career include:
- Representing Japan at the Venice Biennale in the 1990s, cementing her global breakthrough.
- Major retrospectives at world-class institutions, including MoMA in New York, Tate Modern in London, and the National Museum of Modern Art in Tokyo.
- Interactive exhibits like the 2017 Hirshhorn Museum installation, where visitors applied over 800,000 polka dot stickers.

### A Lasting Cultural Legacy

From massive public sculptures to immersive light installations, Kusama brought avant-garde art into mainstream culture. Her exhibitions consistently drew record-breaking crowds from Tokyo and New York to London and Melbourne, leaving behind an indelible mark on global visual culture.
