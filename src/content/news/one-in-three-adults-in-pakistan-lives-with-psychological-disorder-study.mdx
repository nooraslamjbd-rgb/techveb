## 1 in 3 Adults in Pakistan Affected by Mental Disorders

According to Pakistan's first nationally representative mental health survey, nearly one in three adults across the country lives with a diagnosable mental and behavioural disorder. The landmark study highlights a critical public health crisis impacting millions of citizens.

### Key Findings from the Landmark PPS Study

The research was conducted by leading experts representing the **Pakistan Psychiatric Society (PPS)** and reported by *Dawn News*. As the largest and most comprehensive mental health study in the country's history, it offers unprecedented insight into the population's psychological well-being.

* **High Prevalence:** Approximately 33% of the adult population suffers from psychological conditions.
* **National Representation:** This survey marks the first time data was collected representatively across diverse demographics in Pakistan.
* **Public Health Impact:** Researchers warn that unaddressed psychological disorders pose severe socio-economic challenges.

### Urgent Need for Healthcare Reforms

The findings underscore an urgent need for structural improvements in Pakistan's healthcare infrastructure. Mental health advocacy, accessible psychiatric care, and public awareness campaigns are vital to addressing this nationwide issue.
