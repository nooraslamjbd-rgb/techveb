LOS ANGELES, (UrduPoint / Pakistan Point News / WAM - 31st Aug, 2026) More than 20 people [may](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/may.html) be missing or unaccounted for following a flash [flood](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/flood.html) in [Grand Canyon](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/grand-canyon.html) National Park in the US state of Arizona, authorities said [Sunday](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/sunday.html).

The "significant flash-flood [event](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/event.html)" occurred in Bright Angel Canyon and the Phantom Ranch area on Saturday, the US National Park Service said in a [news](https://www.urdupoint.com/urdu-news) release.

Park officials said they are continuing evacuation and response efforts in coordination with the Arizona Department of Public Safety, while working to account for individuals who [may](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/may.html) have been in the affected area.

The flash [flood](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/flood.html) caused significant damage to infrastructure throughout Bright Angel Canyon. Nearly [all](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/all.html) footbridges spanning Bright Angel Creek were destroyed, preventing hikers [from](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/from.html) crossing the creek, according to park officials.
