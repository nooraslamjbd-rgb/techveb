In a private TV programme, the Jamaat-e-Islami emir said the government team claims it is trapped in the IMF programme and cannot abolish the levy, though proposals have been presented that could make its removal possible.

ISLAMABAD, (UrduPoint / Pakistan Point News - 8th Sep, 2026) Jamaat-e-Islami [Pakistan](https://www.urdupoint.com/pakistan/) Emir Hafiz Naeemur Rahman has said that if the [government](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/government.html) reduces [petrol](https://www.urdupoint.com/business/petroleum-prices-in-pakistan.html) prices, that is fine; otherwise, his party has a much bigger plan. Speaking on a private [television](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/tv.html) programme, Hafiz Naeemur Rahman said the [government](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/government.html) had [scheduled](https://www.urdupoint.com/business/prize-bonds-draw-schedule.html) negotiations after 2 days.

He said Jamaat-e-Islami had staged sit-ins against the increase in [petrol](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/petrol.html) [prices](https://www.urdupoint.com/business/prize-bonds.html) and the levy, adding that removing the levy and some taxes could reduce the [price](https://www.urdupoint.com/business/prize-bonds.html) of [petrol](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/petrol.html) by Rs106. The Jamaat-e-Islami emir said global prices were a separate matter, but the [government](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/government.html) had imposed the levy to hide its failure.

He said the [government](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/government.html) collected Rs1.567 trillion in petroleum levy in one year. He said increasing the [petrol](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/petrol.html) [price](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/price.html) by Rs12 last night was a great injustice, adding that the levy could have been reduced.

“If they reduce the [petrol](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/petrol.html) [price](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/price.html), that is fine; otherwise, we have a much bigger plan,” Hafiz Naeemur Rahman said. He also said it was incorrect for people who apparently opposed the [government](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/government.html) and criticised Jamaat-e-Islami’s sit-in to criticise the [protest](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/protest.html).

He said the party wanted the [opposition](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/opposition.html) to join its [protest](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/protest.html). He said the levy had no connection with the [price](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/price.html) of [petrol](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/petrol.html) and that the party’s demand was its abolition. He added that the [protest](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/protest.html) was solely for the people’s issue and not for personal or political gain.

The Jamaat-e-Islami emir said that if the [government](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/government.html) accepted the party’s demands, that would be fine; otherwise, it would use [all](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/all.html) available options.

He said the [government](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/government.html) team had stated that it was caught in the [IMF](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/imf.html) programme and could not abolish the levy. He said the [government](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/government.html) team had explained that the [IMF](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/imf.html) forced it to meet its targets, adding that some proposals had been presented under which the levy could be abolished.

He said reducing the policy rate by 3 percent would have an impact of more than Rs1.6 trillion. Hafiz Naeemur Rahman said [ANP](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/awami-national-party.html)’s Mian Iftikhar had joined Jamaat-e-Islami’s sit-in on the day it began, while Barrister Gohar had also supported the party in the [protest](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/protest.html).

He said [Sohail Afridi](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/sohail-afridi.html) had addressed the party’s sit-in in [Hyderabad](https://www.urdupoint.com/education/board-of-education-hyderabad.html) during his [visit](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/visit.html) to [Sindh](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/sindh.html). He said [all](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/all.html) political parties would be asked to support the issue, as it was a public concern. He said Jamaat-e-Islami supported the creation of administrative units, as this was part of its manifesto.

However, he said the condition for creating administrative units was that they should be based on public confidence and the Constitution. The Jamaat-e-Islami emir said broad consultation was necessary for creating administrative units. He questioned why the matter had been initiated in [Sindh](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/sindh.html) without a national dialogue when there was sensitivity over the issue there.
