KARACHI, (UrduPoint / Pakistan Point News - 12th Sep, 2026) [Muttahida Qaumi Movement](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/mqm.html) ([MQM](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/mqm.html)) [Pakistan](https://www.urdupoint.com/pakistan/) Chairman [Khalid Maqbool](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/khalid-maqbool.html) Siddiqui has said that they migrated to Quaid-e-Azam’s [Pakistan](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/pakistan.html) but were pushed into Bhutto’s [Pakistan](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/pakistan.html).

Addressing a ceremony in [Karachi](https://www.urdupoint.com/education/board-of-education-karachi.html), [Khalid Maqbool](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/khalid-maqbool.html) Siddiqui said the motherland is only [Pakistan](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/pakistan.html), while provinces are merely administrative units and nothing more. He said declaring the division of provinces treason is the greatest treason, adding that [all](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/all.html) political parties are now echoing their voice.

He said [Sindh](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/sindh.html)’s urban areas have suffered the greatest exploitation.

The APMSO manifesto mentioned the province, and it has now become the voice of the entire country, he added. He said [MQM](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/mqm.html)’s first demand is that provinces should be formed across the country based on population.

He said, “Give us our province and we will implement Article 140-A ourselves.” He said they were not allowed to table a [resolution](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/resolution.html) against Sindhudesh in the [Sindh](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/sindh.html) [Assembly](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/assembly.html), adding that there is no need to present a lengthy argument today about why provinces should be created.

Khalid Maqbool said they had migrated to Quaid-e-Azam’s [Pakistan](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/pakistan.html) but were pushed into Bhutto’s [Pakistan](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/pakistan.html). “First conduct a census and then honestly prepare voter lists. [Karachi](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/karachi.html)’s population today is more than 20 [million](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/million.html). A state that cannot conduct a census can conduct demographic analysis,” he said.
