## France Shuts Down Karachi Consulate After 50 Years

The Consulate General of France in Karachi is permanently closing its doors after 50 years of diplomatic service in Pakistan.

According to a report by Dawn News, outgoing French Ambassador to Pakistan Nicolas Galey officially announced the shutdown during a farewell event organized by the Board of Management of the Quaid-i-Azam House Museum and the Karachi Council of Foreign Relations.

### Impact on Consular Services and Diplomacy

For five decades, the Karachi consulate served as a vital bridge for bilateral relations, commerce, and visa processing in southern Pakistan. The permanent closure represents a major operational shift in French diplomatic representation.

Key updates regarding the closure include:
- Centralization of official diplomatic functions at the French Embassy in Islamabad.
- Realignment of consular services for residents of Sindh and Balochistan.
- Restructuring of bilateral economic and cultural engagements in the region.

### Centralized Operations in Islamabad

Following the closure, Pakistani citizens seeking diplomatic, visa, or administrative assistance from the French government will need to direct their queries through the primary embassy in Islamabad. While physical operations end in Karachi, both nations aim to maintain diplomatic ties through centralized channels.
