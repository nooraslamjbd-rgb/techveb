## Devastating Missile Strikes Hit Ukrainian Publishing Infrastructure

A series of targeted Russian missile strikes has destroyed over 12 million books across Ukraine, severely disrupting preparations for the upcoming school year. According to reporting by BBC News, major publishing houses and printing facilities near Kyiv suffered catastrophic damage, eliminating vital educational resources.

Local media estimates reveal that nearly one-third of all books printed in Ukraine this year have been completely destroyed. The widespread ruin leaves educational institutions struggling to supply essential textbooks for millions of returning students.

### Destruction of Vital Educational Textbooks

At the Konvi printing plant on the outskirts of Kyiv, a single overnight missile attack ruined a production run of 1.3 million textbooks. This single batch accounted for 10% of the entire nation's annual schoolbook supply. Commercial Director Viktoria Haidai emphasized that water damage from firefighting efforts and exposure to the elements rendered remaining materials completely unsalvageable.

* **Facility Damage:** Over 35 publishing facilities damaged nationwide in recent weeks.
* **Logistics Loss:** Major publishing houses lost central distribution warehouses storing massive book inventories.
* **Cultural Impact:** Strikes also destroyed individual stalls at Kyiv's historic Pochaina second-hand book market.

### Cultural Heritage Under Siege

Ukrainian industry leaders argue that these systematic attacks specifically aim to dismantle the country's cultural identity and educational continuity. Beyond textbooks, historical libraries, art galleries, film studios, and museums across Ukraine continue to face severe destruction.

Despite the overwhelming losses, local publishers, writers, and volunteers remain resilient, working to clear rubble and rebuild critical printing capabilities.
