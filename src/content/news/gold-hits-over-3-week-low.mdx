CAPITALS, (UrduPoint / Pakistan Point News / WAM - 02nd Sep, 2026) [Gold](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/gold.html) fell on Wednesday to its ‌lowest in more than three weeks.

Spot [gold](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/gold.html) was down 0.6 percent at $4,304.01 per ounce by 00:17 GMT, its lowest since 7th [August](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/august.html).

US [gold](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/gold.html) futures for [December](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/december.html) delivery fell 1 percent to $4,350.80.

Among other metals, spot [silver](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/silver.html) lost 1 percent to $63.60 per ounce, platinum edged 1 percent lower to $1,722.23 and palladium fell 1.4 percent to $1,292.21.
