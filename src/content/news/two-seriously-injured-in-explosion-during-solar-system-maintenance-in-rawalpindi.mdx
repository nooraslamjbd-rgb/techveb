 Published August 29, 2026 | 11:10 PM

RAWALPINDI, (APP - UrduPoint / Pakistan Point News - 29th Aug, 2026) At least, two people sustained serious burn injuries after an electrical explosion reportedly occurred during the maintenance of a solar power system at a house near Bat Chowk, National Park Road, in Gulistan Colony, Rawalpindi.

According to Rescue 1122, three emergency vehicles immediately reached the scene and provided first aid to the injured before shifting them to Holy Family Hospital(HFH), [Rawalpindi](https://www.urdupoint.com/education/board-of-education-rawalpindi.html), in critical condition.

Rescue officials said that 45-year-old Kifayatullah while 30 year old Mubeen sustained burns injuries both victims are reported to be in critical condition.

Rescue 1122 advised citizens to take proper safety precautions while maintaining solar systems and other electrical equipment to prevent such incidents.
