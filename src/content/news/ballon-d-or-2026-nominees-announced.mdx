(UrduPoint / Pakistan Point News / WAM - 08th Sep, 2026) [LONDON](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/london.html), 8th [September](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/september.html) (WAM) — [France](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/france.html) [Football](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/football.html) magazine, in cooperation with the Union of European [Football](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/football.html) Associations (UEFA), today announced the shortlist of nominees for the 2026 Ballon d'Or awards, marking the final stage of voting ahead of the awards ceremony [scheduled](https://www.urdupoint.com/business/prize-bonds-draw-schedule.html) to take place in [London](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/london.html) on [October](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/october.html) 26.

The shortlist features 30 nominees for the Men's Ballon d'Or and 30 for the [Women](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/women.html)'s Ballon d'Or, alongside nominees for [best](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/best.html) [male](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/male.html) and female goalkeeper, [best](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/best.html) [young](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/young.html) player, [best](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/best.html) [coach](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/coach.html), and club of the year across both categories.

The men's shortlist includes high-profile figures such as [Spain](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/spain.html)'s Lamine Yamal, [France](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/france.html)'s Kylian Mbappé, [Spain](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/spain.html)'s Rodri, [France](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/france.html)'s Ousmane Dembélé, [Brazil](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/brazil.html)'s Vinícius Júnior, [Argentina](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/argentina.html)'s Lionel Messi, and [Norway](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/norway.html)'s Erling Haaland.

[Paris Saint-Germain](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/psg.html) emerged as the most-represented club among the Men's Player nominees.

The announcement drew widespread attention following the absence of several prominent players, including [Brazil](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/brazil.html)'s Raphinha, while [Portugal](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/portugal.html)'s Cristiano Ronaldo was omitted for the fourth consecutive year. Lionel Messi returned to the candidate list after missing out on the previous two editions.

Marking its 70th anniversary, the Ballon d'Or ceremony is being hosted in [London](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/london.html) for the first time. The accolades are determined by an international panel of journalists representing the [top](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/top.html) 100 FIFA-ranked nations for the men's awards and [top](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/top.html) 50 for the [women](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/women.html)'s awards, with voting based on individual performance, team contributions, and sportsmanship.
