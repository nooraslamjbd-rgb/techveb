## Protests Inflict Rs120 Billion Daily Economic Loss

Pakistan's Finance Minister Muhammad Aurangzeb has delivered a stark warning regarding the severe financial toll of political instability. In a televised message, he highlighted research findings showing that political protests and sit-ins cost the country around Rs120 billion daily.

Aurangzeb categorized these repeated disruptions as "self-inflicted pain" that directly damages national financial recovery and stability efforts.

### Impact on the Planned PTI March

The warning arrived ahead of a planned protest march to Islamabad by the Pakistan Tehreek-e-Insaf (PTI) on September 27. The federal government has firmly stated it will take necessary measures to stop illegal blockades and prevent public disruption.

### Broader Economic Consequences

Frequent street demonstrations lead to business closures, broken supply chains, diminished tax collections, and lower daily wage earnings for millions. Financial experts stress that continuous road blockades alienate foreign investors and hinder economic growth.

According to coverage by Dawn News, government officials urge political stakeholders to prioritize national economic stability over political confrontation.
