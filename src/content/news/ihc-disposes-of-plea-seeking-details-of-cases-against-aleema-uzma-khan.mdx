ISLAMABAD, (APP - UrduPoint / Pakistan Point News - 7th Sep, 2026) The Islamabad High Court (IHC) on Monday disposed of a petition seeking details of cases registered against Aleema Khan and Uzma Khan after a report containing the requisite information was submitted before the court.

Justice Shahrukh Arjumand heard the petition seeking details of cases registered against the two sisters.

According to the report submitted before the court, three cases were registered against Aleema Khan and Uzma Khan with Islamabad Police, while one inquiry against both sisters was pending with the [Federal](https://www.urdupoint.com/education/board-of-education-federal.html) Investigation Agency (FIA).

The government counsel informed the court that, according to the information available till [date](https://www.urdupoint.com/islam/today-islamic-date.html), the three cases and one FIA inquiry were the only matters pending against them.

Advocates Khalid Yousaf Chaudhry and Shaheena Shahab appeared on behalf of Aleema Khan, while Usman Ghumman represented the state.

The petition had been filed by Shahraiz Khan in October 2024 during the arrest of the two sisters. After the report was submitted, the court disposed of the petition.
