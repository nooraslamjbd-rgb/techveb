## Falcons Dominance Secures CPL Final Spot

In a dramatic turn of events in the Caribbean Premier League (CPL), Antigua & Barbuda Falcons advanced to the tournament final following an extraordinary bowling performance. Star spin duo Shadab Khan and Moqim dismantled the Guyana Amazon Warriors, skittling them out for a mere 76 runs.

## Match Highlights: Warriors Collapse for 76

The Amazon Warriors struggled to find momentum against a relentless Falcons attack. Shadab Khan and Moqim took control early, applying immense pressure through accurate spells and quick wickets.

Key highlights from the match include:
* **Low Score:** Guyana Amazon Warriors were bundled out for just 76 runs.
* **Spin Impact:** Shadab and Moqim proved unplayable, sharing crucial middle-order wickets.
* **Final Berth:** The victory officially guaranteed the Falcons a direct entry into the CPL final.

## What's Next in the CPL Play-offs?

Despite the heavy loss, the tournament is not completely over for the Guyana Amazon Warriors. As reported by ESPN Cricinfo, Guyana will now face the Jamaica Kingsmen in the second qualifier on September 18. This upcoming fixture gives the Warriors a final opportunity to redeem themselves and setup a rematch in the CPL title clash.
