SEJONG, (UrduPoint / Pakistan Point News / WAM - 31st Aug, 2026) The Republic of Korea's industrial output remained flat in [July](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/july.html) [from](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/from.html) a month earlier due to a base effect and a strike in the [automobile](https://www.urdupoint.com/cars/makers.html) [industry](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/industry.html), data showed Monday, while retail sales lost ground and facility investment advanced.

Industrial production remained unchanged in [July](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/july.html) [from](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/from.html) a month earlier, according to data [from](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/from.html) the Ministry of Data and Statistics.

Industrial output fell 0.5 percent and 0.4 percent in [April](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/april.html) and [May](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/may.html), respectively, before rebounding 2.4 percent in [June](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/june.html).

Output in the mining and manufacturing sector, a key pillar of the economy, edged up 0.2 percent on the back of the electronic component segment, which increased 20.7 percent. The output in the automobile [industry](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/industry.html), on the other hand, fell 4.

5 percent.

Service sector output, however, contracted 1.3 percent [from](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/from.html) a month earlier in [July](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/july.html). It was the sharpest drop since 1.7 percent posted in [February](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/february.html) 2022.

Output in the information and communication segment rose 3.5 percent, while that in the finance and insurance segment decreased 4.8 percent.

Retail sales, a gauge of private spending, fell 2.4 percent over the period, led mainly by a decline in durable goods, including [cars](https://www.urdupoint.com/cars/), amid high consumer [prices](https://www.urdupoint.com/business/prize-bonds.html).

The report showed that sales of semidurable goods, such as clothes, fell 1.4 percent, while sales of durable goods slid 7.7 percent. Sales of nondurable goods, including cosmetics, slipped 0.1 percent.
