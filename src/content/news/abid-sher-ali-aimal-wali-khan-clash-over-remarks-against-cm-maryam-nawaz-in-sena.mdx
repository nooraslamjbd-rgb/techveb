ISLAMABAD, (UrduPoint / Pakistan Point News - 9th Sep, 2026) A clash broke out between Senator [Abid Sher Ali](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/abid-sher-ali.html) and Aimal Wali Khan during a [Senate](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/senate.html) session, with footage of the incident emerging. [Abid Sher Ali](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/abid-sher-ali.html) said remarks against “our chief minister” could not be allowed.

Meanwhile, [Awami National Party](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/awami-national-party.html) Central President Senator Aimal Wali Khan spoke at a meeting of the [Senate](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/senate.html) Standing Committee on Human Rights about extrajudicial killings and fake [police](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/police.html) encounters.

Aimal Wali Khan said the state was not implementing the decisions of the higher courts. He termed the activities of the CCD and CTD alarming.

He said [police](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/police.html) claimed to act according to the law, asking, “Which law allows [firing](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/firing.html) a pistol in the nape?” He questioned who had given [police](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/police.html) the authority to declare someone liable to be killed, adding that the [same](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/same.html) story was released every time after an accused was killed. The clash between [Abid Sher Ali](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/abid-sher-ali.html) and Aimal Wali Khan then took place during the session.

> 

Later, speaking to the [media](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/media.html) outside [Parliament](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/parliament.html), [Abid Sher Ali](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/abid-sher-ali.html) said Aimal Wali Khan had used inappropriate language against [Maryam Nawaz Sharif](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/maryam-nawaz-sharif.html) and made several other remarks.

He said Aimal Wali Khan had “thundered against Punjab” and expressed hatred for [Maryam Nawaz](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/maryam-nawaz-sharif.html)’s projects. He said the remarks were out of context and that Aimal Wali Khan had tried to [attack](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/attack.html) [Maryam Nawaz](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/maryam-nawaz-sharif.html) two or three times, adding, “I will not allow it at [all](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/all.html).” Responding to Aimal Wali Khan’s statement that “the [government](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/government.html) is frustrated,” [Abid Sher Ali](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/abid-sher-ali.html) said, “We are not aggressive; his mental state should be [fine](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/fine.html).”
