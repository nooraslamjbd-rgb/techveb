ISLAMABAD, (APP - UrduPoint / Pakistan Point News - 28th Aug, 2026) Prime Minister Shehbaz Sharif on Friday expressed deep grief and sorrow over the passing of King Harald V of Norway.

"On behalf of the Government and people of Pakistan, I extend our heartfelt condolences to the Royal Family and the people of Norway", the prime minister said in a post on his official X handle.

He said as Norway mourned this profound loss, the people of [Pakistan](https://www.urdupoint.com/pakistan/) stood in solidarity with the bereaved family and our Norwegian friends.

"His Majesty will be remembered for his lifetime of dedicated service to his nation. Our thoughts and prayers are with the people of Norway at this difficult time," the prime minister added.
