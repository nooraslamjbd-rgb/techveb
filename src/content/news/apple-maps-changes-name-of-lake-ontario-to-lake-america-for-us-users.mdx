## Apple Maps Adopts 'Lake America' for US Users

Apple has updated its web version of Apple Maps to display "Lake America" instead of "Lake Ontario" for users in the United States. As reported by Dawn News, the update aligns with revisions in the US Geographic Names Information System (GNIS) following an executive order by President Donald Trump.

Alphabet's Google Maps made a similar change for US users prior to Apple's update, highlighting how major technology companies are adjusting to domestic policy directives.

## Regional Display Differences

To manage international sensitivities, tech platforms use location-based display logic:

* **United States:** Displays "Lake America"
* **Canada:** Retains the historic title "Lake Ontario"
* **Rest of the World:** Displays both names concurrently

Apple previously used this tiered display approach after the Trump administration ordered the Gulf of Mexico renamed to the Gulf of America in early 2025.

## Political Context and Pushback

The executive order governs federal usage within the United States but does not bind international bodies or foreign governments. Canadian Prime Minister Mark Carney and New York Governor Kathy Hochul both publicly rejected the name change, opting to maintain the name used for over 400 years.

Lake Ontario is shared between the two nations, with roughly 52 percent of its surface area located in Canada and the remaining portion bordering New York state.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Why did Apple Maps rename Lake Ontario to Lake America?

Apple updated the name for US users to align with the US Geographic Names Information System following an executive order by President Donald Trump.

### Will Canadian users see the name Lake America on Apple Maps?

No, users in Canada will continue to see Lake Ontario, while international users will see both names displayed.

### Has Google Maps also changed Lake Ontario's name?

Yes, Google Maps previously updated the body of water's name to Lake America for US users.
