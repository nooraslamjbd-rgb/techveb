West Indies Women delivered a disciplined performance to defeat Zimbabwe Women in the opening match of their T20I series. The victory was built on a strong collective effort from the bowling attack and outstanding fielding, as reported by ESPN Cricinfo.

## Fletcher's Historic Fielding Record

Leg-spinner Afy Fletcher produced a memorable performance, making significant contributions with both ball and field. She claimed two key wickets to disrupt Zimbabwe's batting rhythm.

In addition to her bowling spell, Fletcher etched her name in the history books by taking four catches. This set a new record for the highest number of catches taken by a West Indies player in a single women's T20I match.

## All-Round Effort Secures Victory

All-rounder Claxton and her fellow bowlers provided strong support throughout the innings. The unit bowled tight lines, effectively restricting Zimbabwe's scoring options and forcing mistakes.
