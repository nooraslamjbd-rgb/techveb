## Kate Hudson Set for 'How to Lose a Guy in 10 Days' Sequel

More than two decades after captivating audiences worldwide, Kate Hudson is preparing to return for a sequel to the iconic romantic comedy *How to Lose a Guy in 10 Days*, according to reports from BBC News.

### A Nostalgic Return to a Romantic Comedy Classic

The original 2003 hit grossed nearly $180 million at the global box office and solidified Hudson and co-star Matthew McConaughey as one of cinema's most beloved screen duos. Beyond its box office success, the film left a lasting cultural mark, particularly through Hudson's famous yellow silk Carolina Herrera gown.

This upcoming project aligns with Hollywood's growing trend of reviving classic early-2000s titles for modern audiences, following similar announcements such as *The Devil Wears Prada 2*.

### Key Highlights and Career Milestones

* The original 2003 release earned nearly $180 million globally.
* Hudson and McConaughey previously reunited for the 2008 feature *Fool's Gold*.
* McConaughey has frequently praised their natural chemistry and shared humor during filming.

Hudson recently scored her second Academy Award nomination for her role in *Song Sung Blue* (2025), following her first nomination for *Almost Famous* in 2001. Representatives for both Hudson and McConaughey have been approached for comment regarding production details and final casting.
