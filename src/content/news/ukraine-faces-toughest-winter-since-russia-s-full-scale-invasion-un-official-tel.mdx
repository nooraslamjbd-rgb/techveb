## UN Warns of Ukraine's Severe Winter Crisis

Ukraine is entering its toughest winter since Russia's full-scale invasion began, according to UN official Alexander De Croo in an interview with BBC News. The warning comes as Russia escalates its air campaign, deploying advanced military tech to devastate Ukraine's critical energy grid and civilian infrastructure.

### Advanced Jet-Powered Drones Change Air Defense Dynamics

Russian forces have deployed faster, jet-powered suicide drones in round-the-clock attacks across Ukraine, particularly targeting Kyiv. These upgraded unmanned aerial vehicles (UAVs) move at significantly higher speeds, making interception increasingly difficult for air defense systems.

Targeted infrastructure includes:
* Petrol stations and fuel storage hubs
* Energy generation facilities and distribution grids
* Commercial facilities, including shopping centers and warehouses

### Casualties Mount Amid Escalating Infrastructure Strikes

Recent strikes have caused severe civilian casualties across several regions. A daytime Russian drone attack on a shopping mall in Pavlohrad killed five people and injured 67. Similar air strikes hit commercial centers in Zaporizhzhia and Sumy earlier in the week.

In response, Ukrainian forces continue to execute long-range drone strikes deep inside Russian territory. These retaliatory operations primarily target Russian oil, gas, and military distribution logistics.

With temperature drops looming, humanitarians worry that destroyed energy infrastructure will leave millions without power and heating during extreme sub-zero weather.
