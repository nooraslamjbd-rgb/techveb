## Defense Modernization and Indigenous Production at POF

Chief of Army Staff (COAS) Field Marshal Asim Munir visited the Pakistan Ordnance Factory (POF) facilities in Wah to evaluate operational capabilities, export prospects, and contributions to the national defense industrial base. As reported by Dawn News via the Inter-Services Public Relations (ISPR), the visit highlighted Pakistan's commitment to defense technological self-reliance.

During his inspection, military leadership observed live operational tests and trials of locally designed weapons and ammunition. The demonstrations underscored POF's strategy to replace foreign dependencies with indigenous military technology.

### Industrial Expansion and Legislative Reforms

The army chief received a comprehensive briefing on the corporatisation framework under the DIPRA Ordinance. This structural shift aims to modernize factory management, boost export capabilities, and integrate commercial best practices into military production.

### Upgradation through New Industrial Plants

To expand production capacity, POF is currently integrating two new state-of-the-art industrial plants. Key highlights of the initiative include:

- **Targeted Operations:** New plants will begin commercial and military production by the end of the year.
- **Technological Upgrades:** Modern assembly lines will accelerate production of advanced munitions.
- **Export Potential:** Increased output aims to serve both domestic security needs and international export markets.

The visit concluded with COAS reiterating support for continuous technological innovation within Pakistan's defense sector.
