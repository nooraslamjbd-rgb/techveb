## Mason Crane Earns Surprise England Recall for Pakistan Tri-Series

Leg-spinner Mason Crane has been recalled to the England squad after eight years for the upcoming One-Day International (ODI) tri-series in Pakistan, which also features Sri Lanka. As reported by BBC News, the 29-year-old previously earned his lone Test cap in 2018 against Australia in Sydney.

### Key Squad Changes and Injury Updates

The 16-man squad, captained by Harry Brook, welcomes back Sam Curran and Adil Rashid following their recovery from recent injuries. Uncapped Sussex players James Coles and Henry Crocombe have also earned their first call-ups to the national side.

However, regular white-ball captain Jos Buttler remains sidelined due to a hamstring injury sustained recently. All-rounder Jacob Bethell is also recovering from knee surgery, while Brydon Carse stays absent amid an ongoing investigation.

### Strategic Preparation for World Cup Action

England will utilize this tri-series as critical preparation ahead of their upcoming white-ball commitments and the 50-over World Cup in southern Africa next year. Each team in the tri-series will face off twice, with the top two teams advancing to the final.

White-ball head coach Brendon McCullum will be supported by an updated coaching staff including Jeetan Patel, Mike Yardy, David Saker, and Carl Hopkinson during the tour.
