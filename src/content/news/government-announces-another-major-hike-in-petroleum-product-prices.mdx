Petrol has been reduced by Rs5.58 per litre, while diesel has become Rs4.18 per litre more expensive.

ISLAMABAD, (UrduPoint / Pakistan Point News - 8th Sep, 2026) OGRA has announced a major increase in petroleum product prices. According to a notification issued by the Petroleum Division, the [price](https://www.urdupoint.com/business/prize-bonds.html) of [petrol](https://www.urdupoint.com/business/petroleum-prices-in-pakistan.html) has been reduced by Rs5.58 per litre, while diesel has been increased by Rs4.18 per litre. The new [price](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/price.html) of [petrol](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/petrol.html) has been fixed at Rs364.35 per litre, while diesel will [cost](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/cost.html) Rs385.95 per litre.

The new [prices](https://www.urdupoint.com/business/prize-bonds.html) will take effect [from](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/from.html) midnight tonight.
