## Mohammad Abbas Reaches Milestone at Lord's

Pakistan fast bowler Mohammad Abbas achieved a major career milestone by taking a five-wicket haul at the 'Home of Cricket'. The 36-year-old seamer posted impressive second-innings figures of 5-57 in 22 overs, dismissing England for 208 on the fourth morning.

Abbas had previously come close to the honour with three separate four-wicket hauls at Lord's. Resuming on day four, he wasted little time, quickly removing overnight batter Dan Lawrence to complete his five-wicket milestone.

## England Dominates Fourth-Innings Defence

Despite Abbas's bowling performance, Pakistan faced a daunting fourth-innings target of 389 runs. England's pace attack quickly capitalized on the seam-friendly pitch conditions:

- **Ollie Robinson:** Took two wickets in four balls, removing Azan Awais lbw and bowling Abdullah Shafique for a duck.
- **Jofra Archer:** Express fast bowler Archer dismissed Pakistan captain Babar Azam to leave the visitors reeling at 14-3.

## Match Outlook and Historical Context

Pakistan recovered to 53-3 at lunch on day four but still required 336 runs for an improbable victory. The highest successful fourth-innings run chase at Lord's remains 344-1, achieved by the West Indies in 1984.

According to Dawn News, England remains in a commanding position to close out the match and secure a Test series win.
