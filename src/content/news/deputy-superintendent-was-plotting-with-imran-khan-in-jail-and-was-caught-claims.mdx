ISLAMABAD, (UrduPoint / Pakistan Point News - 12th Sep, 2026) [Pakistan](https://www.urdupoint.com/pakistan/) [Muslim](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/muslim.html) League-Nawaz Senator [Nasir](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/nasir.html) Butt said that no move by jailed [Imran Khan](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/imran-khan.html) could now succeed and that there was no possibility of any deal with him.

He said [all](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/all.html) of [Imran Khan](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/imran-khan.html)’s efforts and planning had failed badly, leaving [court](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/court.html) proceedings as the only option. In an interview with [ARY](https://www.urdupoint.com/tv/news-channels/ary-news-live.html) [news](https://www.urdupoint.com/urdu-news), [Nasir](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/nasir.html) Butt strongly criticized [Imran Khan](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/imran-khan.html), saying there was no question of holding talks with someone who had not done even a single good thing for the country and nation.

He said various plans were being made [from](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/from.html) inside the [jail](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/jail.html) and that the former deputy superintendent of the [jail](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/jail.html) had previously been found involved in such conspiracies.

> 

He claimed that [Imran Khan](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/imran-khan.html) had made every effort to use medical grounds to first get himself shifted to a hospital and then move [from](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/from.html) there to [Bani](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/bani.html) Gala, but the plan had failed.

He said that if any [doctor](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/doctor.html) had colluded to prepare a fabricated or fake medical report, the truth would soon emerge. Just as the former deputy superintendent had come under the law’s grip, the [doctor](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/doctor.html) who prepared the false report would also be exposed and face legal action.
