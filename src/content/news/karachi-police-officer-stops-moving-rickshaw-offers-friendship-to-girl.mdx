KARACHI, (UrduPoint / Pakistan Point News - 19th Sep, 2026) A video has gone viral on social media showing a police officer allegedly stopping a rickshaw on a busy road in [Karachi](https://www.urdupoint.com/education/board-of-education-karachi.html) and offering friendship to a girl sitting inside, raising serious questions about the performance and ethical standards of the police administration and law-enforcement agencies.

According to [ARY](https://www.urdupoint.com/tv/news-channels/ary-news-live.html) [news](https://www.urdupoint.com/urdu-news), the officer allegedly forcibly stopped the rickshaw in the [middle](https://www.urdupoint.com/education/middle-result.html) of the road and began speaking to the girl. The viral video apparently shows the officer engaging in unrelated conversation and allegedly making a friendship proposal to her.

As soon as the rickshaw stopped, another girl inside discreetly switched on her [mobile](https://www.urdupoint.com/mobile/) phone camera and recorded the officer’s alleged inappropriate remarks and actions on the spot.

> 

The police officer seen in the viral video is reportedly posted at Aziz Bhatti Police Station.

The video sparked widespread public outrage after appearing on social media and drew attention from public and journalistic circles. Citizens have urged the authorities to take immediate notice of the alleged incident of police misconduct and harassment of women and to initiate strict disciplinary and legal action against the officer.
