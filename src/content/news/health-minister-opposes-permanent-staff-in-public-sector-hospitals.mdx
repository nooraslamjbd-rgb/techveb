## Key Takeaways

- Suggests hiring private sector CEOs with full hiring and firing authority.

- Permanent positions blamed for litigation and lack of administrative accountability.
- Inquiry revealed PIMS gynae ward fire spread in just two minutes.

Following a fatal fire at the Pakistan Institute of Medical Sciences (PIMS), Health Minister Mustafa Kamal has proposed significant administrative reforms for government-run hospitals. According to a report by Dawn News, the minister advocated for eliminating permanent government positions in public healthcare facilities.

### Shift to Private Sector Leadership and Contract Hiring
Under the proposed reform plan, all public hospital staff would be employed on performance-based contracts rather than permanent scale positions. Key aspects of the proposal include:

- **Private Sector Leadership**: Appointing experienced CEOs from the private sector to manage hospital operations.
- **Administrative Authority**: Granting CEOs full authority over hiring, firing, and budget management.
- **Performance Accountability**: Establishing strict contract-based evaluations to replace permanent civil service tenures.

Minister Kamal stated that permanent tenures often lead to operational stagnation and legal complications when addressing underperformance.

### PIMS Fire Incident Prompts Urgent Scrutiny
The policy proposal comes in the wake of a tragic fire in the PIMS Mother and Child Health Centre gynae ward, which claimed the lives of 14 newborn babies. Inquiry findings indicated that the fire engulfed the ward in just 120 seconds after the initial spark.

By introducing private-sector governance, the ministry aims to streamline operations, enforce strict safety compliance, and improve overall patient care across public medical institutions.
