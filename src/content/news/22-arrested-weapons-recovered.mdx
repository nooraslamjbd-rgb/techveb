SARGODHA, (APP - UrduPoint / Pakistan Point News - 29th Aug, 2026) Police after launching a massive crackdown arrested 22 accused, including 11

proclaimed offenders, here on Saturday.

According to a police spokesman, raiding teams of different police stations raided

different localities and arrested 22 criminals which were wanted to police in cases,

including robbery and dacoity.

The accused also included three members of motorcycle lifting gang

and recovered valuables.

Police after launching another crackdown against illegal weapon holders

across the district and arrested five accused with weapons.
