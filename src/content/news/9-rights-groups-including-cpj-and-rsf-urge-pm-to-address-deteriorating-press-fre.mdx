## Global Rights Groups Address Pakistan's Press Freedom Crisis

Nine prominent human rights and press freedom organizations have addressed a joint letter to Prime Minister Shehbaz Sharif, raising alarms over Pakistan's rapidly deteriorating media environment. International bodies including the Committee to Protect Journalists (CPJ) and Reporters Without Borders (RSF) highlighted an escalating state crackdown on independent reporting.

### Key Concerns Raised in the Joint Letter

According to a report by Dawn News, the coalition detailed several severe challenges facing media personnel across the country:

- **Targeting of Journalists:** A sharp increase in enforced disappearances, detentions, and arbitrary criminal inquiries.
- **Legal & Cyber Harassment:** Misuse of the Prevention of Electronic Crimes Act (PECA), 2016, and aggressive probes by the National Cyber Crime Investigation Agency (NCCIA).
- **Digital & Network Censorship:** Imposition of localized internet shutdowns and telecommunications blackouts to impede live coverage.
- **Reporting Limits:** New rules mandating special authorization for international journalists reporting outside three major cities, along with bans in Azad Jammu and Kashmir (AJK).

### Call for Immediate Government Reforms

The rights organizations emphasized that maintaining public order and national security should not be used as broad justifications to silence critical press coverage. The coalition urged Prime Minister Sharif to secure the safe return of missing journalists, drop unwarranted cybercrime investigations, and protect freedom of expression.
