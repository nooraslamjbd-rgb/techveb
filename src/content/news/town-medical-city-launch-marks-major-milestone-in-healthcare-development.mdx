PESHAWAR, (APP - UrduPoint / Pakistan Point News - 30th Aug, 2026) Town Medical City formally marked the beginning of its development journey with a launching ceremony here bringing together prominent political, healthcare, [business](https://www.urdupoint.com/business/prize-bonds.html) and development personalities.

Former Governor Khyber Pakhtunkhwa Ghulam Ali, former Health Minister Inayat Ullah, CEO Buraq Builders Ali Haider Babakhel and CEO HMAK Anwar Khan were among the distinguished guests attending the ceremony.

The project is being led by Muhammad Ajmal Khan, Project Owner of Town Medical City, whose vision is focused on developing modern healthcare infrastructure and providing improved medical facilities to the community.

Speaking on the occasion, CEO FK Marketing Farhan Khan Khalil said the launch of Town Medical City was a significant milestone and an important step towards creating a healthier and brighter future, while highlighting the importance of modern, accessible and well-planned healthcare facilities to meet the growing needs of the community.

Muhammad Ajmal Khan also thanked the distinguished guests and participants for attending the ceremony and supporting the vision behind the project.

The ceremony provided an opportunity for stakeholders to reaffirm their commitment to the development of healthcare infrastructure and to celebrate the vision of Town Medical City as a prospective contribution to the region’s healthcare and development landscape.
