AMMAN, (UrduPoint / Pakistan Point News / WAM - 31st Aug, 2026) [Jordan](https://www.urdupoint.com/arabic/jordan.html) on Monday condemned an Iranian [drone](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/drone.html) [attack](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/attack.html) that targeted the United [Arab](https://www.urdupoint.com/arabic/) Emirates, which was intercepted by air defence systems over Emirati territorial waters.

The Ministry of Foreign and Expatriates Affairs described the [attack](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/attack.html) as a flagrant violation of [UAE](https://www.urdupoint.com/arabic/uae.html) sovereignty and a threat to its security, stability and territorial integrity.

The ministry called it a serious escalation and a blatant breach of international law and the [United Nations](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/united-nations.html) Charter.

In a statement carried by Jordan [news](https://www.urdupoint.com/urdu-news) Agency, the ministry affirmed Jordan's absolute solidarity with the [UAE](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/uae.html) and its full support for any measures it takes to protect its sovereignty, security and the safety of its citizens and residents.
