EL ALAMEIN, Egypt, (UrduPoint / Pakistan Point News / WAM - 08th Sep, 2026) The [UAE](https://www.urdupoint.com/arabic/uae.html) national pavilion officially opened at the [El Alamein](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/el-alamein.html) International Airshow 2026, which runs until 10 [September](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/september.html) at [El Alamein](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/el-alamein.html) International [Airport](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/airport.html) in [Egypt](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/egypt.html)’s Matrouh Governorate.

The pavilion attracted significant interest [from](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/from.html) official delegations, decision-makers, [industry](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/industry.html) experts, specialists and visitors on the opening day of the exhibition.

As the largest national pavilion at the [event](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/event.html), spanning 871 square metres, the [UAE](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/uae.html) pavilion made a strong impression on the first day, showcasing the latest capabilities, solutions and technologies developed by Emirati companies across the defence, aviation, aerospace, aircraft maintenance and advanced [technology](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/technology.html) sectors. The display highlights the rapid [progress](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/progress.html) of the [UAE](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/uae.html)’s national industries and their growing competitiveness in regional and international [markets](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/market.html).

The pavilion is supported by the Ministry of Defence and the Tawazun Council, and organised by ADNEC Group, a Modon [company](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/company.html). It brings together a distinguished group of leading [UAE](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/uae.html) companies, including EDGE Group, Generation 5, [Abu Dhabi](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/abu-dhabi.html) Aviation Group and its subsidiaries, and Multi Level Group.

The pavilion welcomed a number of senior officials and military delegations [from](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/from.html) the [Arab](https://www.urdupoint.com/arabic/) Republic of [Egypt](https://www.urdupoint.com/arabic/egypt.html), the Kingdom of [Bahrain](https://www.urdupoint.com/arabic/bahrain.html), and the Kingdom of [Morocco](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/morocco.html) on the opening day. Visiting delegations explored the advanced capabilities, technologies and solutions showcased by participating [UAE](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/uae.html) companies, gaining insight into the significant achievements of the [UAE](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/uae.html)’s defence, aviation, aerospace and advanced [technology](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/technology.html) industries.

The strong momentum generated on the first day was reflected in several key indicators. The pavilion welcomed 1,037 visitors, while participating [UAE](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/uae.html) organisations and companies held 59 meetings with international delegations, partners and stakeholders. These included eight meetings by EDGE Group, four by Generation 5, 10 by [Abu Dhabi](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/abu-dhabi.html) Aviation Group, and six by Multi Level Group. In addition, 15 meetings were held to promote IDEX and NAVDEX 2027, 10 meetings focused on UMEX and SimTEX, and six meetings supported the promotion of ISNR. The day’s activities culminated in the signing of a Memorandum of Understanding between [Abu Dhabi](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/abu-dhabi.html) Aviation and Air [Cairo](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/cairo.html), further demonstrating the pavilion’s role as a platform for partnership-building, [business](https://www.urdupoint.com/business/prize-bonds.html) development and international collaboration.

Participating companies are showcasing a diverse range of advanced systems, solutions and products that reflect the [UAE](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/uae.html)’s industrial and technological expertise. Displays include capabilities in aviation, aircraft maintenance, repair and overhaul ([MRO](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/mauritanian-ouguiya.html)), autonomous systems, unmanned aviation, and other specialised technologies. The pavilion provides an integrated platform for presenting [UAE](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/uae.html) innovations while facilitating discussions on future partnerships and cooperation opportunities with international participants.

The [UAE](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/uae.html)’s participation is particularly significant given the growing prominence of the [El Alamein](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/el-alamein.html) International Airshow as a regional platform bringing together leading organisations [from](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/from.html) the aviation, aerospace and defence sectors across [Africa](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/africa.html) and the [middle](https://www.urdupoint.com/education/middle-result.html) East. The exhibition covers 125,000 square metres and features more than 250 exhibitors [from](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/from.html) 50 countries, alongside representatives [from](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/from.html) governments, armed forces, civil aviation authorities, space agencies and specialised industrial organisations.

The strong turnout at the [UAE](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/uae.html) pavilion on the opening day underscores the importance of the [UAE](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/uae.html)’s participation and its role in creating new channels of engagement between national companies and international counterparts. The [event](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/event.html) provides valuable opportunities to explore commercial and investment prospects, while encouraging the [exchange](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/exchange.html) of knowledge and expertise across key industries.

ADNEC Group is also participating in the exhibition to promote [Abu Dhabi](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/abu-dhabi.html)’s position as a leading global [hub](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/hub.html) for the defence, security, aviation and aerospace sectors, while highlighting its portfolio of internationally renowned events, including IDEX, NAVDEX, UMEX, SimTEX and ISNR.

The [UAE](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/uae.html) pavilion will continue welcoming visitors at Stand C70 until 10 [September](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/september.html), providing [UAE](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/uae.html) companies with a platform to engage with international delegations, experts and [industry](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/industry.html) professionals, explore new [business](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/business.html) opportunities, and strengthen the presence of [UAE](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/uae.html) industries in global [markets](https://www.urdupoint.com/en/latest-news/market.html).
