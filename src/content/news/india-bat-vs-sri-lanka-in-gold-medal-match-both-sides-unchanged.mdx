## India Opts to Bat First in High-Stakes Final

India won the toss and decided to bat first against Sri Lanka in the blockbuster gold-medal match. Both management groups opted to stick with their winning combinations, fielding completely unchanged playing XIs for this crucial summit clash.

### Rematch of the 2023 Asian Games Final

This high-octane encounter serves as a direct rematch of the 2023 Asian Games final. In their previous historic meeting, India secured the gold medal by defeating Sri Lanka by 19 runs in a hard-fought contest.

### Key Match Highlights

- **Toss Decision:** India won the toss and chose to bat.
- **Squad Status:** Both sides maintain unchanged playing XIs.
- **Historical Context:** India defeated Sri Lanka by 19 runs in the 2023 final.

### Tactical Outlook and Strategy

By keeping identical lineups, both squads are relying heavily on team stability and recent momentum. Setting a formidable first-innings target will be India's primary objective, while Sri Lanka aims to restrict early scoring through disciplined bowling in the opening overs.
