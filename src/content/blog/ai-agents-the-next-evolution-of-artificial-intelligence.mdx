They break tasks into subtasks and adapt their approach.</p><h2>Current Capabilities</h2><p>Cursor, Claude Code, Devin, and research agents handle real tasks autonomously.</p><h2>Multi-Agent Systems</h2><p>Specialized agents collaborating on complex tasks represent the cutting edge.</p><h2>Challenges</h2><p>Reliability, cost, and safety remain significant challenges.
