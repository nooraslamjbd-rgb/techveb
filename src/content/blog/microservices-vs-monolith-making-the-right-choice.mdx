<h2>The Architecture Decision</h2><p>Neither is inherently better. The right choice depends on team and product.</p><h2>Monolith</h2><p>Simple deployment, easy debugging, fast to develop.</p><h2>Microservices</h2><p>Independent deployment, technology diversity, fault isolation.</p><h2>Start with Monolith</h2><p>Extract services only when you have clear reasons.</p><h2>Warning</h2><p>Don't adopt microservices because they are trendy. The complexity is significant.</p>
