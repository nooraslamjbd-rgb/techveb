<h2>Esports Reaches New Heights</h2><p>Esports continues its explosive growth in 2026, with global revenues surpassing $3 billion and viewership rivaling traditional sports. Prize pools have reached unprecedented levels, and esports athletes are becoming mainstream celebrities.</p><h2>Biggest Prize Pools</h2><p>The International (Dota 2) continues to lead with its crowdfunded prize pool model. League of Legends World Championship and the Valorant Champions Tour offer massive guaranteed prize pools. The Fortnite World Cup returned with a $50 million prize pool, attracting hundreds of thousands of competitors.</p><h2>Rising Games</h2><p>While League of Legends, CS2, and Dota 2 remain esports giants, newer titles are making waves. Valorant has established a robust global circuit, and Street Fighter 6's fighting game community has grown dramatically. Marvel Rivals esports is the breakout story of 2026.</p><h2>Infrastructure Investment</h2><p>Major sports franchises, entertainment companies, and venture capital firms continue investing billions into esports organizations, venues, and broadcasting infrastructure. Purpose-built esports arenas now exist in every major city worldwide.</p><h2>The Athlete Experience</h2><p>Professional gamers now benefit from team houses, coaching staffs, sports psychologists, nutritionists, and physical fitness programs. The profession is increasingly treated with the same seriousness as traditional sports, with player unions and standardized contracts emerging.</p>
