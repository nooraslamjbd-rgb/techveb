<h2>Giving Back</h2><p>Contributing to open source improves skills, builds reputation, and benefits the community. Getting started is easier than most think.</p><h2>Finding Projects</h2><p>Look for projects you already use. Check for good first issue labels. Start with documentation, tests, or bug fixes. Build familiarity before tackling complex issues.</p><h2>Making Your First PR</h2><p>Fork the repo, create a branch, make changes, write clear commit messages, and open a well-documented pull request. Follow the project contribution guidelines exactly.</p><h2>Code Review</h2><p>Respond to feedback constructively. Make requested changes promptly. Learning from reviews accelerates growth. Be patient with maintainers who volunteer their time.</p><h2>Long-Term Impact</h2><p>Consistency matters more than quantity. Regular small contributions build trust and reputation. Becoming a maintainer is a natural progression for dedicated contributors.</p>
