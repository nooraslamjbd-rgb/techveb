<h2>The FGC in 2026</h2><p>The Fighting Game Community (FGC) has never been healthier. Street Fighter 6 continues to dominate with its accessible yet deep gameplay, while Tekken 8 and other titles bring fresh energy to the community. The FGC's grassroots spirit persists even as the genre reaches new mainstream audiences.</p><h2>Street Fighter 6's Impact</h2><p>Street Fighter 6's Drive System made fighting games more accessible without sacrificing depth. Modern controls attracted millions of new players, while classic controls satisfied veterans. The game's World Tour mode provided a unique single-player experience that taught fighting game fundamentals.</p><h2>Tekken 8 and Beyond</h2><p>Tekken 8 introduced the Heat System, adding aggressive gameplay options and new combo routes. The game's story mode delivered one of the best narratives in fighting game history. Fatal Fury: City of the Wolves marked SNK's triumphant return to the competitive scene.</p><h2>Netcode Revolution</h2><p>Rollback netcode has become the standard, transforming online fighting games from a frustrating experience to a genuinely competitive one. Players can now practice and compete online with latency that feels nearly like local play.</p><h2>Community and Culture</h2><p>Fighting game tournaments like EVO, Combo Breaker, and CEO continue to grow, with EVO 2026 breaking attendance records. The community's emphasis on sportsmanship, self-improvement, and in-person competition remains its defining strength.</p>
