<h2>Quantum Enters the Real World</h2><p>Quantum computing is transitioning from research to practical applications. IBM, Google, and startups build systems solving real problems.</p><h2>Hardware</h2><p>IBM Osprey exceeds 400 qubits. Error correction advances make larger quantum systems possible. Cloud access democratizes experimentation.</p><h2>Applications</h2><p>Drug discovery, financial optimization, logistics routing, and materials science are early use cases.</p><h2>Software</h2><p>Qiskit, Cirq, and PennyLane provide quantum programming frameworks.</p><h2>Timeline</h2><p>Fault-tolerant quantum computing remains years away. Near-term value comes from hybrid approaches.</p>
