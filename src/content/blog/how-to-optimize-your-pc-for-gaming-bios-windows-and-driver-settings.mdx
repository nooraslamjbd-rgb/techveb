<h2>Extract Every Last Frame</h2><p>Building a gaming PC is only half the battle. Properly optimizing your BIOS, Windows settings, and drivers can extract 10-20% additional performance from the same hardware. These tweaks cost nothing but can meaningfully improve your gaming experience.</p><h2>BIOS Optimization</h2><p>Enable XMP (Intel) or EXPO (AMD) profiles to run your RAM at rated speeds--this alone can provide 5-10% performance improvement in CPU-bound games. Enable Resizable BAR (ReBAR) for improved GPU communication. Set your primary display to your GPU rather than integrated graphics.</p><h2>Windows Settings</h2><p>Enable Game Mode to prioritize gaming processes. Disable unnecessary startup programs to free resources. Set your power plan to High Performance or Ultimate Performance. Disable hardware-accelerated GPU scheduling if you experience stuttering (test both on/off). Turn off mouse acceleration in pointer settings.</p><h2>Driver Optimization</h2><p>Keep GPU drivers updated--NVIDIA and AMD release game-ready drivers for major releases. Use DDU (Display Driver Uninstaller) for clean driver installations when upgrading. Consider using NVIDIA's or AMD's specific driver branches for best stability rather than always using the latest beta.</p><h2>Monitor Calibration</h2><p>Enable your monitor's highest refresh rate in display settings. Ensure G-Sync/FreeSync is enabled in both NVIDIA/AMD control panel and monitor OSD. Calibrate your monitor's color settings for accurate, vibrant visuals. These display optimizations ensure you see every frame your hardware produces.</p>
