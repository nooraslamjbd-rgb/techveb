Grows near consumers. Uses 95% less water.</p><h2>Tech Stack</h2><p>LED grow lights, IoT sensors, AI optimization, and robotics automate farming.</p><h2>Challenges</h2><p>High energy costs, limited crop variety, and capital intensity.</p><h2>Future</h2><p>Advancing LED efficiency and cheaper renewable energy expand capabilities.</p>
