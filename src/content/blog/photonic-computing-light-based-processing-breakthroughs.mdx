<h2>Computing with Light</h2><p>Photonic computing uses photons instead of electrons for dramatically faster processing.</p><h2>Advantages</h2><p>Speed of light processing. Massive parallelism. Low heat generation. EMI immunity.</p><h2>Current State</h2><p>Silicon photonics established for data center interconnects. Lightmatter builds photonic AI accelerators.</p><h2>Applications</h2><p>AI inference, data center interconnects, quantum networking, signal processing.</p><h2>Challenges</h2><p>Integration with existing electronics, manufacturing costs, and limited programmability.</p>
