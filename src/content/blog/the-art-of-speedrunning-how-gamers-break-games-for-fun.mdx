<h2>Racing Against the Clock</h2><p>Speedrunning transforms gaming from an experience to be savored into a race to be won. The community's dedication to finding the fastest possible routes, exploiting glitches, and mastering frame-perfect inputs is both impressive and entertaining.</p><h2>Categories and Rules</h2><p>Speedruns come in various categories: Any% (finish the game by any means), 100% (complete everything), Glitchless (no exploits allowed), and Randomizer (shuffled game elements). Each category creates fundamentally different experiences and requires different skills.</p><h2>Notable Achievements</h2><p>Super Mario 64 has been beaten in under 14 minutes through decades of route optimization. The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time Any% sits at under 10 minutes using a sequence break discovered in 2024. These records represent thousands of hours of collective research and practice.</p><h2>The Technology</h2><p>Speedrunners use specialized tools like LiveSplit for timing, practice ROMs for route testing, and memory inspection tools for glitch research. TAS (Tool-Assisted Speedruns) use frame-by-frame input optimization to discover theoretical speed limits.</p><h2>Community and Culture</h2><p>Speedrun marathons like Games Done Quick raise millions for charity while showcasing incredible runs. The community's collaborative approach to discovering new strats and glitches creates a unique culture of shared achievement and friendly competition.</p>
