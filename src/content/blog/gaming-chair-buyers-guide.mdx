## Why Ergonomics Matter

A well‑designed chair keeps your spine neutral, reducing the risk of chronic back pain. In 2024, a study by the American Chiropractic Association found that 73 % of gamers who sat in ergonomically certified chairs reported fewer aches compared to those in generic office chairs. The key is a combination of adjustable seat height, lumbar support, and armrest positioning.

- **Seat Height & Depth**: A chair that lets you keep your feet flat on the floor and knees at a 90‑degree angle promotes circulation.  
- **Lumbar Support**: Adjustable lumbar pillows that fit the natural lumbar curve can cut lower‑back strain by up to 40 % (Cleveland Clinic data).  
- **Armrests**: Adjustable, padded armrests that can tilt or swivel help maintain shoulder alignment, especially during long editing sessions.

## Materials That Matter

High‑quality upholstery and frame construction are the backbone of durability.

| Material | Pros | Cons |
|----------|------|------|
| **PU Leather** | Smooth, easy to clean, premium look. | Can crack over time; less breathable. |
| **Microfiber** | Breathable, resistant to spills, soft feel. | May show sweat stains. |
| **Nylon Mesh** | Excellent airflow, lightweight. | Less support for heavy users. |
| **Eco‑Foam** | Sustainable, good contouring. | May compress faster than memory foam. |

Secretlab’s Titan Evo 2025 uses a blend of PU leather and a carbon‑fiber frame, giving it a sturdy feel while keeping the weight down to 29 kg.
