<h2>Building APIs That Last</h2><p>Well-designed APIs are intuitive, consistent, and scalable.</p><h2>Naming</h2><p>Use nouns not verbs. /users/123/posts. Plural nouns consistently.</p><h2>Status Codes</h2><p>200 success, 201 creation, 400 bad request, 404 not found.</p><h2>Pagination</h2><p>Cursor-based for large datasets. Include metadata.</p><h2>Versioning</h2><p>URL versioning is simplest. Be consistent and document.</p>
