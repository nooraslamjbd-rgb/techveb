Autonomous vehicles need sub-millisecond responses.</p><h2>Architecture</h2><p>Edge data centers, on-device processing, and fog computing create a compute hierarchy.</p><h2>Use Cases</h2><p>Autonomous vehicles, retail analytics, predictive maintenance, CDN caching.</p><h2>Hybrid Future</h2><p>Edge and cloud complement each other for latency and scale.</p>
