<h2>Choose the Right Tool</h2><p>SQL and NoSQL serve different needs.</p><h2>SQL Strengths</h2><p>ACID transactions, relational integrity, mature tooling. PostgreSQL handles most cases.</p><h2>NoSQL Strengths</h2><p>Horizontal scaling, flexible schemas, high write throughput.</p><h2>Polyglot</h2><p>Use multiple databases: PostgreSQL for transactions, Redis for caching, Elasticsearch for search.</p><h2>Default to PostgreSQL</h2><p>Unless you have a specific reason not to.</p>
