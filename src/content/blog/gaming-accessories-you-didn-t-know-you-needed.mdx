<h2>Quality of Life Upgrades</h2><p>Beyond the essential peripherals, several gaming accessories can significantly improve your experience in ways you might not expect. These quality-of-life upgrades address common frustrations and enhance comfort during extended gaming sessions.</p><h2>Monitor Arms</h2><p>A dual monitor arm frees up desk space and positions your displays at the perfect height and angle. The Ergotron LX and AmazonBasics monitor arm provide smooth adjustment and clean cable management. This simple upgrade transforms your desk setup and improves ergonomics.</p><h2>Desk Mat</h2><p>A large desk mat (900x400mm) provides a smooth, consistent surface for your mouse while protecting your desk. It reduces mouse noise, eliminates the need for a separate mouse pad, and creates a cleaner aesthetic. The /r/MouseReview community recommends the Glorious Extended and SteelSeries QcK Heavy.</p><h2>USB Hub</h2><p>A powered USB 3.0 hub adds convenient ports for charging controllers, connecting peripherals, and transferring data. Position it within arm's reach to avoid fumbling behind your PC. Some hubs include additional features like SD card readers and ethernet passthrough.</p><h2>Cable Management</h2><p>Cable raceways, velcro ties, and under-desk cable trays eliminate the spaghetti mess behind your setup. This not only looks better but makes it easier to swap peripherals and clean your workspace. Spend 30 minutes on cable management--it's worth it.</p><h2>Blue Light Glasses</h2><p>While the science is debated, many gamers report reduced eye fatigue with blue light filtering glasses. At minimum, enabling your monitor's built-in blue light filter and following the 20-20-20 rule provides real benefits for extended gaming sessions.</p>
