<h2>Strategy Gaming's Golden Age</h2><p>Strategy gaming is experiencing a remarkable renaissance in 2026. From massive 4X epics to intense real-time tactics, the genre offers depth and intellectual challenge that few other gaming categories can match.</p><h2>4X Space Epics</h2><p>Civilization VII continues the franchise's legendary run with deeper diplomacy and improved AI. Stellaris' latest expansion adds unprecedented scale to its galaxy-spanning empire management. Stars Beyond, a new IP, combines 4X mechanics with survival elements in a procedurally generated universe.</p><h2>Real-Time Strategy Returns</h2><p>The RTS genre is making a strong comeback. Stormgate's second season has refined its competitive balance, while Zero-K and Beyond All Reason provide free open-source alternatives with massive community-driven battles. Age of Empires V modernizes the classic formula beautifully.</p><h2>Tactical Excellence</h2><p>XCOM 3 (rumored) would join the excellent Phoenix Point and the recent Jagged Alliance 3 in the tactical combat space. Warhammer 40K: Dawn of War 4 delivers brutal real-time tactics in the grim darkness of the far future.</p><h2>Indie Strategy Gems</h2><p>Don't overlook indie strategy titles. Cobalt Core offers roguelike strategy in space, Against the Storm blends city-building with roguelike elements, and Into the Breach's spiritual successors continue to innovate in the tactical puzzle space.</p>
