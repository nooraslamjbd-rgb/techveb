<h2>Finally Playing Together</h2><p>Cross-platform gaming has become the norm rather than the exception in 2026. Most major multiplayer games support full crossplay, removing the platform barriers that once fragmented gaming communities.</p><h2>Where Crossplay Is Standard</h2><p>Fortnite, Call of Duty, Apex Legends, Rocket League, Minecraft, and most live-service games support full crossplay across PlayStation, Xbox, Switch, and PC. The feature has become so expected that games without crossplay face significant community backlash.</p><h2>Remaining Holdouts</h2><p>Some competitive games restrict crossplay to maintain ranked integrity. Platform-exclusive titles by definition lack crossplay. Technical challenges around input-based matchmaking and anti-cheat differences continue to complicate crossplay implementation in some genres.</p><h2>Progression and Purchases</h2><p>Cross-progression (sharing save data across platforms) and cross-purchase (buying once, playing everywhere) are growing but not universal. Epic Games and some publishers have led the way, but many games still silo purchases by platform.</p><h2>The Business Impact</h2><p>Crossplay benefits players but complicates platform holder strategies. Sony has been the most resistant, likely because PlayStation's market dominance means less incentive to open their ecosystem. However, regulatory pressure and player demand continue to push toward universal crossplay.</p>
