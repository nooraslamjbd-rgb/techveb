You do not need to spend a fortune to get a great smartphone in 2026. Budget phones have improved dramatically, offering features that were flagship-exclusive just a year or two ago.

We tested 15 smartphones under $300 to find the absolute best options. Here are our top picks.

## Our Testing Methodology

Every phone was tested over two weeks with real-world usage:
- Camera quality in various lighting conditions
- Battery life under heavy and light usage
- Performance in everyday tasks and gaming
- Display quality for media consumption
- Build quality and durability

## Best Overall: Samsung Galaxy A56

**Price:** $279 | **Rating:** 9.2/10

The Galaxy A56 strikes the best balance of features, performance, and value. Its 6.7-inch AMOLED display is stunning, the camera system is versatile, and Samsung's software support is class-leading.

**Pros:**
- Excellent 120Hz AMOLED display
- Versatile triple camera system
- 5 years of software updates
- IP67 water resistance

**Cons:**
- Charger not included
- Plastic back

## Best Camera: Google Pixel 8a

**Price:** $249 | **Rating:** 9.0/10

Google's computational photography magic trickles down to the Pixel 8a. In our tests, it consistently produced the best photos in this price range, especially in challenging lighting.

**Pros:**
- Best-in-class camera
- Clean Android experience
- 7 years of updates
- AI-powered photo features

**Cons:**
- 60Hz display (not 120Hz)
- Smaller battery than competitors

## Best Battery: OnePlus Nord CE 4

**Price:** $229 | **Rating:** 8.8/10

With a massive 5,500mAh battery and 80W fast charging, the Nord CE 4 easily lasts two full days. It also includes a surprisingly good camera and smooth 120Hz display.

**Pros:**
- Two-day battery life
- Fast 80W charging
- Good performance
- Value for money

**Cons:**
- Camera struggles in low light
- No wireless charging

## Best for Gaming: Poco X7 Pro

**Price:** $259 | **Rating:** 8.7/10

Powered by the MediaTek Dimensity 8400, the Poco X7 Pro handles demanding games with ease. The 120Hz display and vapor chamber cooling make it a gaming powerhouse.

**Pros:**
- Excellent gaming performance
- 120Hz AMOLED display
- Good cooling system
- Stereo speakers

**Cons:**
- MIUI can be bloated
- Camera is average

## Best Design: Nothing Phone (3a)

**Price:** $299 | **Rating:** 8.9/10

The Nothing Phone (3a) stands out with its unique Glyph interface and transparent design. Beyond aesthetics, it offers solid performance and a clean software experience.

**Pros:**
- Unique, eye-catching design
- Clean Nothing OS
- Good OLED display
- Solid camera

**Cons:**
- Limited Glyph functionality
- Average battery life

## Final Verdict

For most people, the **Samsung Galaxy A56** offers the best overall package. But if camera quality is your priority, go for the **Pixel 8a**. If battery life matters most, the **OnePlus Nord CE 4** is unbeatable.

Whatever you choose, budget smartphones in 2026 prove that great technology does not need to come with a premium price tag.
