- The best all-round choice is a premium over-ear pair with adaptive ANC and 30+ hours of battery life.

Active noise cancellation has matured. In 2026 the difference between a $99 pair and a $349 pair is real but narrower than before, so the best buy depends on where you listen. Prices and ratings below are indicative editorial picks.

## How we rank noise-cancelling headphones

We test on four priorities:

1. **ANC effectiveness** — how well a pair handles engine drone, office chatter and city noise.
2. **Comfort** — weight, clamp pressure and pad material, critical for hours of wear.
3. **Sound quality** — clarity, bass balance and a natural, non-fatiguing signature.
4. **Battery and practicality** — run time, charging speed, a wired option and call microphones.

## The picks, ranked

### 1. Best overall: premium over-ear

The flagship of the category pairs **adaptive ANC** that adjusts to your environment with **plush memory-foam pads** and a **40+ hour battery**. Call quality is excellent thanks to a well-tuned microphone array. If you can stretch your budget, this is the headphone that disappears on your head.

### 2. Best value: mid-range over-ear

The value pick delivers **strong ANC** and **comfortable fit** at roughly half the flagship price. Battery life around 35 hours and a decent mic make it a superb everyday choice for the office and commute. You give up some ANC depth and a little refinement in tuning, but nothing huge.

### 3. Best budget: entry ANC

Budget over-ears now include **solid basic ANC** for flights and commutes. They can't match the flagship on isolation depth or build, and calls are adequate rather than excellent, but for the price they are an easy recommendation for occasional travellers who want to block engine drone.

### 4. Best travel: extreme endurance

Built for long-haul trips, this pair adds **deeper ANC**, a **foldable carry** and a huge **50+ hour battery** with fast charging. Comfort is tuned for all-day wear. If you fly often, the combination of endurance and isolation justifies the premium.

## How much noise will ANC really block?

Set realistic expectations. ANC handles **steady low-frequency rumble** — planes, trains, buses, fans and air conditioning — extremely well. It is much weaker against **voices, keyboards and sudden sharp sounds**. A tight seal and well-fitting pads provide the passive isolation that complements the electronics, so fit is not optional.

## Buying checklist

- **Match ANC to your use**: travel and open offices justify the best ANC; a quiet home does not.
- **Check the microphone**: call quality varies far more than people expect; read call-specific tests if you work remotely.
- **Prioritise comfort**: the best ANC in the world is wasted if you can't wear it for a full flight.
- **Look for a wired mode and fast charging**: both are practical safety nets for long trips.
